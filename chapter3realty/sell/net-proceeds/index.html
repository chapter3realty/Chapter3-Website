Selling a home

What it costs to sell a house in South Carolina, and what you keep
Every cost on a Myrtle Beach-area sale, line by line, with a calculator for your own home.

  • $3,500for each 1 percent of commission

By Devin Day, Operations Officer · Reviewed by Tim Nash, Broker-in-Charge · Updated November 12, 2026

On a $350,000 Horry County sale with a mortgage to release, the fees in state law come to $1,320. The commission you agree is the big cost.

The short answer

On the middle Horry County home, a $350,000 sale with a mortgage to release, the fees in state law come to $1,320, under half of 1 percent. The commission you agree with your agent is the biggest selling cost: $3,500 for each 1 percent.

Your mortgage payoff comes out of the same check. With a 5 percent commission, as an example, and $175,000 still owed, you keep about $155,500 before your attorney's fee and any HOA charges.

Live in another state? About 6 in 10 Horry County condo units have their tax bill mailed outside South Carolina. When your permanent home is outside the state, the buyer must hold back part of the price for state income tax.

For a 2026 sale it is 5.21 percent of your gain if you sign the state's gain affidavit. Without the affidavit, it is 5.21 percent of the price less your selling costs. A main home whose gain is inside the federal exclusion has no hold-back. Our capital gains page works it out.

Who pays what when you sell a house in South Carolina?

On the state REALTORS' standard contract, you pay the deed stamps, your mortgage payoff, the fee you agreed with your agent and your own attorney. The buyer pays both title insurance policies.

You pay

  • The deed preparation
  • The deed stamps and the deed recording
  • Your mortgage payoff, and recording its release
  • Property tax for the months you owned the home
  • The fee you agreed with your own agent
  • Your own attorney

The buyer pays

  • Both title insurance policies, the owner's and the lender's
  • The title exam
  • Inspections, the appraisal and any survey
  • The buyer's attorney

The side the contract ticks paysYou pay if no box is ticked

  • The association's fees for its papers
  • The association's letter of what is owed
  • Transfer fees
  • A capital or working-capital payment to the association

Each side pays its own costs unless both agree otherwise in writing. The form also tells both sides they are responsible for getting legal advice before they sign.

What does the closing attorney charge the seller for?

A South Carolina attorney must supervise every home closing in the state. If the buyer has a loan for a home they will live in, the buyer chooses the closing attorney.

An attorney who represents only the buyer does not advise you. You may refuse to sign a settlement statement you do not agree with. The attorney charges on your side of the closing statement can include three things:

  • The deed preparationPreparing a deed for someone else is the practice of law, so a lawyer prepares your deed. On the standard contract, that cost is yours.
  • A payoff handling feeIn a standard South Carolina closing, the buyer's attorney typically prepares the settlement statement. Under a South Carolina Bar ethics opinion, when that attorney also sends your mortgage payoff, they may add a reasonable payoff handling fee to your side.
  • Your own attorney, if you hire oneOn the standard contract, your own attorney's fee is your cost.

These charges differ from one attorney's office to the next, so this page prints no figure for them.

How much are SC deed stamps at your price?

Deed stamps, the state's fee on a deed that many people call the transfer tax, are $1.85 for each $500 of the price, or part of $500. The seller owes them.

The county also charges $15 to record the deed and $10 to record the release of a paid-off mortgage.

We read the county's recorded deeds to find four prices. Three are for homes other than condos: the middle sale, and the prices 1 in 4 sold below and above. The last row is the middle condo. Find the price nearest yours.

What HOA fees does a seller pay at closing?

The association's charges for the sale go to the side the contract ticks, and to you if no box is ticked. Your regular dues are split as of the closing day instead, like the property tax.

  • No cap in state lawSouth Carolina has no cap on what an association charges for its resale papers or its letter of what is owed. A transfer fee covenant that makes an owner pay a third party on each sale is not binding if it was recorded after February 1, 2012. A fee paid to the association itself is still allowed.
  • Unpaid dues come out of your moneyUnpaid HOA fees are usually a lien on the home, and they come out of the seller's money at closing. We look for them before closing. On a condo sale, state law requires your unpaid association charges to be paid out of the price, after past-due taxes and recorded mortgages.
  • Special assessmentsFor a special assessment approved before closing, the contract has two boxes: you pay all of it, or only the part due in the year of closing. One approved after closing is the buyer's. Our special assessments page goes further.

More than 1 in 4 houses for sale here list no HOA fee. Almost every condo for sale lists one. Our guide to HOA fees when a home sells covers the letter the association sends for the sale.

Why is my mortgage payoff more than my balance?

Your payoff includes the interest due through the day the loan is paid off, and any fees charged and not yet paid. Some loans also charge a prepayment penalty, a fee for paying the loan off early.

On a home loan made for personal use, the servicer must send an accurate payoff statement within seven business days of a written request. A loan made to buy a rental the owner does not use counts as a business loan, and that rule does not apply to it. A beach place the owner planned to use more than 14 days a year still counts as personal.

Closing day: guard the wire

Never follow wiring instructions that arrive in an email. Confirm them by phone with the attorney's office, at a number you agreed on in advance.

Who pays the property taxes when you sell in Horry County?

You pay for the part of the year you owned the home, and the buyer pays for the rest. South Carolina property taxes for a year are due between September 30 and January 15 after they are assessed.

For most of the year, the tax for the months you have owned the home has not been billed yet. At closing you credit the buyer your share. The credit is your own tax for those months, not an extra cost.

Why a second home pays about three times as much

A home its owner lives in is assessed at 4 percent of its value and pays no school operating tax. A second home or rental is assessed at 6 percent and pays the full county levy.

Inside a city or town, such as Myrtle Beach or Conway, the tax and the credit differ from these figures.

If the bill turns out different

The closing attorney figures the tax split from the tax information they judge reliable. If the bill later turns out different, you and the buyer settle the difference between yourselves.

When a home's last deed is old, its tax bill, and the tax credit at closing, is often smaller than one figured on today's price. Our property tax page explains why.

Is the money you keep from the sale taxed?

Often not, if it was your main home. You can exclude up to $250,000 of gain from your income, or $500,000 for a married couple filing jointly.

  • Who can use the exclusionYou owned and lived in the home at least 2 of the last 5 years. You also did not use the exclusion on another home in the 2 years before.
  • Your selling costs lower the gainThe commission, legal fees, and the deed stamps and other fees you pay as the seller count as selling expenses.
  • The sale is reported on Form 1099-SThe closing attorney's office reports most sales to the IRS. A main home that sells for $250,000 or less may be left off if you certify the whole gain is excluded. For a married seller the limit is $500,000.

A rental or a second home is taxed differently. Our capital gains guide works through it.

How do sellers here keep more of the price?

Price it once. On a $350,000 home, each 1 percent off the asking price is $3,500, the same as 1 percent of commission.

We checked every resale house and townhouse for sale in Horry County for a price cut. More than half have cut their asking price at every price: about 63 percent under $300,000, and about 56 percent in each higher band.

  • The commission is yours to agreeAgents' fees are fully negotiable, and no law fixes them. Offers to pay the buyer's agent are no longer made on the MLS, the agents' shared listing service. Offering to pay the buyer's agent is a choice, not a requirement.
  • A credit to the buyer is your moneyThe contract caps it at a dollar amount. If the buyer's costs come in lower, or the lender allows less, the rest stays with you. On an FHA loan, the seller and others may pay up to 6 percent of the price toward the buyer's closing costs. On a VA loan, other concessions are capped at 4 percent of the home's reasonable value.
  • A home warranty is rare on a resale hereOnly 62 of 2,188 resale houses and townhouses for sale advertise one. Nearly all the home warranties offered here come with new homes. The standard contract has no home warranty clause; buyer and seller can add one with a separate addendum.

Common questions

Seller costs FAQ

What is a seller net sheet?

A list of every line on the seller's side of the closing: the price, less the commission, the fees, the tax credit and the payoff. The $350,000 example near the top of this page is one.

Who pays title insurance in South Carolina?

The buyer pays both policies, the owner's and the lender's, on the state REALTORS' standard contract. Buyer and seller can agree to something else in writing.

Does the seller need an attorney in South Carolina?

Every South Carolina home closing must be supervised by a South Carolina attorney, and a lawyer prepares the seller's deed. When the buyer has a loan for a home they will live in, the buyer picks that attorney, who does not advise you. Your own attorney is your cost.

Can I sell my house before the mortgage is paid off?

Yes. The closing pays off the loan out of the sale money, and you get what is left.
