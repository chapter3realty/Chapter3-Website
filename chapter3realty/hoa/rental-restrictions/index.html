Home/HOA Guide/Rental Restrictions

HOA rental rules

Can an HOA stop you from renting your home in Myrtle Beach?
Yes, if its papers say so. We read the rental rules of 15 Grand Strand communities.

By Devin Day, Operations Officer · Reviewed by Tim Nash, Broker-in-Charge · Updated November 14, 2026

An HOA or condo can limit renting in Horry County when its recorded papers say so, and the real rules differ by community.

The short answer

Yes. A South Carolina HOA or condo association can ban or limit renting when the rule is in its recorded papers. South Carolina law has no exemption for owners who bought before a new rule.

On the Grand Strand, North Beach Plantation's papers allow "any form of rental". At Del Webb at Grande Dunes and Robber's Roost, no lease may be shorter than a year.

Can an HOA ban rentals in South Carolina?

Yes, when the rule is in the association's papers and its words are clear. State law and the courts say four things about it:

  • The papers must be recordedAn HOA's governing documents must be recorded at the county to be enforced. Board rules take effect when adopted, and stay enforceable only if recorded by January 10 of the next year (27-30-130).
  • A condo's master deed states itThe master deed is the paper that creates a condo building and its association. South Carolina requires it to state any limits on leasing a unit, including the term. Each owner must follow it strictly (27-31-100 and 27-31-170).
  • No cap and no grandfather ruleNeither South Carolina's HOA Act nor its condo law caps an association's rental rules, or exempts owners who bought before a new one.
  • A clear rule holds; an unclear one is read for the ownerIn a 2015 case, a Richland County condo barred renting to college students not related to the owner. Owners who paid $470,000 in 2007 rented to students anyway, and lost. In a 2017 Hilton Head case, the court read unclear rules the way that "least restricts the use of the property", and the owners won.

In the 2015 case, the court also said the association had not given up its rule, even if it had not watched rentals before. It acted once it got a complaint.

The city's rule comes on top. The city or county decides whether a home may be rented by the night at that address. The association's papers say whether that one home may be. Both must say yes.

What do HOA rental rules say in real Grand Strand communities?

Some allow any rental at all. Others require a lease of a year or more. These 15 come from each community's own posted or recorded papers. The first seven are ordered from the most open to the most limited.

1North Beach Plantation, North Myrtle Beach: rent it any way you like

Its resort towers stand on a 7.5-acre island between the Atlantic Ocean and Whitepoint Swash. Around them are 2.5 acres of pools, spas, a lazy river and a swim-up pool bar.

The master declaration says its residential-use rule "shall not prohibit or restrict any form of rental". The Exchange lets an owner rent by the day, week, month or year.

Rental guests must register. In the Exchange, the single-family homes and the Villas, a guest not staying with the owner registers with the on-site access company. The unit's front door must have that company's lock and fob system.

Send me homes in North Beach Plantation

2Sand Dunes Oceanfront Tower, Myrtle Beach: papers that allow short stays

Sand Dunes Resort has a water park with slides and a large lazy river on the oceanfront. Our oceanfront condo building guide has its fees and prices.

The tower's master deed allows "permanent or transient residential use". It also says nothing in it limits an owner's right to rent or lease a unit.

Transient means short stays. The city's zoning says whether a stay by the night is legal in a unit, so we check it for that unit before you offer.

Another oceanfront building, the Palace Resort, has its own guide, covering how its owners rent by the night.

Get the Sand Dunes condos for sale

3The Farm at Carolina Forest: renting is allowed, and no board rule can ban it

The state land along The Farm's north side holds habitat for the wild Venus flytrap. South Carolina's largest wild population of the plant is centered in the Lewis Ocean Bay Heritage Preserve.

The declaration says no board rule may prohibit leasing, though the board may require a minimum lease of up to six months. Its rules require every lease in writing. The owner sends the manager a copy and the tenants' names and phone numbers within 10 days.

The board's minutes say the builder's documents include no limit on the share of homes that may be rented. More on the neighborhood is in our guide to The Farm.

Have us read The Farm's rules for one home

4Coquina Harbour, Little River: a condo on a boat basin, with 12-month leases

Coquina Harbour's condos line a marina basin off the Intracoastal Waterway in Little River. The county lists 78 boat-slip parcels in the harbor's marina, each its own parcel, about 100 to 500 feet from the condo buildings.

The association's rules require a lease of at least 12 months. A copy of each lease goes to the management company.

It fits a buyer who wants a harbor home and a tenant by the year. It does not fit a plan to rent it by the week.

Have us find a condo in Coquina Harbour

5Del Webb at Grande Dunes, Myrtle Beach: a year's lease, once a year

At Del Webb at Grande Dunes, every owner pays a required fee for the Grande Dunes Ocean Club, the beach club, and the association bills it with its own dues. Pulte said in 2017 it was the only Del Webb in the country to include beach club access in its base HOA dues.

The recorded declaration requires a lease of at least 12 months, one time a year, unless the association's rules say otherwise. The posted rules add that the whole home is leased, in writing, with notice to the manager within 10 days.

This is a 55+ community. An owner may not lease to anyone under 55 who will live in the home without the board's written approval. Life there is in our Del Webb at Grande Dunes guide.

Ask us about homes in Del Webb at Grande Dunes

6Seasons at Prince Creek West, Murrells Inlet area: a one-year lease

The Murrells Inlet MarshWalk is about 10 minutes away by car, and so is the beach in Garden City. At home, the management company lists indoor and outdoor pools, tennis and bocce.

The association's posted copy of its charter says no lease may be shorter than one year. Every lease is in writing and says the homes are for people 55 or older.

Send me Seasons at Prince Creek West homes

7Myrtle Trace, Conway: a tenant 55 or older who lives there full time

Myrtle Trace has fifteen ponds. They sit on 42 acres of common land the HOA owns, with the clubhouse and the pool.

Its owner and tenant agreement says at least one person on the lease must be 55 or older and live in the home full time. If no one on the lease meets that rule, the lease ends and the tenants leave within 30 days.

The rule fits a tenant who lives there, not a vacation guest. Our Myrtle Trace guide covers the rest.

Can the HOA change the rental rules after you buy?

Yes, by the vote its papers require.

The Atrium, a condo building on North Ocean Boulevard

In 1997 the owners of The Atrium, at 7600 North Ocean Boulevard in Myrtle Beach, met with 93.75 percent of owners present. Not less than two-thirds of all owners voted to amend the master deed.

The amendment, filed in 1998, says no unit may be leased for 30 days or less. It says it was meant to end "short term or vacation rentals". The posted papers show no later change.

Its master deed asks for two approvals: owners of at least 66 percent of the units, and the lenders holding first mortgages on 66 percent of them.

How often the papers change

More often than buyers expect. From 2012 through October 2026, the county's index lists 10 amendments to The Farm at Carolina Forest's declaration, its sixth to fifteenth, and 7 to Berkshire Forest's.

Can an HOA ban Airbnb but allow yearly leases?

Yes. Eleven of the 15 communities above do: their papers bar stays of a month or less and allow a longer lease. Waterfall's rules name Airbnb and VRBO.

For the state's lodging tax, a short-term rental is a stay of fewer than 90 days in a row by the same guest (12-36-920). Papers may use their own definition: Surf Estates calls any use under a year short-term.

The same city can give two answers. In North Myrtle Beach, the city asks for no special zoning or permit beyond its license. There, 27 of 119 houses for sale with an HOA fee say nightly rentals are allowed, fewer than 1 in 4.

Of the 135 houses there with no HOA fee, 86 do, about 6 in 10.

In Carolina Forest, every one of the 297 houses for sale has an HOA fee, and 1 says nightly rentals are allowed. Most of them are outside city limits, where the county's zoning has no limit on how long a home may be rented.

Condos lean the other way: about 7 in 10 condos for sale say short-term rentals are allowed. Our guide to Myrtle Beach condos has that count by area.

Does a building full of renters change the loan?

It can. FHA and Fannie Mae both look at how many owners live in a condo building and how it is rented.

  • FHA approval of the buildingAt least half of a condo project's units must be owner-occupied. Under extra conditions, an existing project more than a year old can be approved with 35 percent, and a new one with 30 percent (HUD Handbook 4000.1).
  • What counts as owner-occupiedA unit counts only if the owner lives there part of the year and does not rent it out for most of the year.
  • One unit in an unapproved buildingFHA may insure it only if, among other things, at least half the units are owner-occupied. In a building of 20 or more units, FHA may stop new loans once it insures more than 10 percent of them. In a smaller one, the limit is two.
  • Fannie MaeIt will not buy a loan in a building whose papers require a rental pool or limit when owners may stay. The same goes for papers that make owners share rental profits with the association or a rental company. A building with hotel services, such as daily rentals and daily cleaning, is out too (B4-2.1-03).

Loans on these buildings are on our page on condos most lenders will not lend on and our condotel financing page.

What other HOA rules apply only to renters?

Some papers keep a few rules for renters alone. The ones we read cover pets and parking:

  • PetsAt Teal Lake Village, North Myrtle Beach condos, renters on a lease shorter than half a year may not keep pets. A lease of 6 months or longer on file allows them. At Cypress Bay II and North Beach Towers, renters and guests may not bring pets.
  • The golf cart spaceAt Carolina Keyes, an owner who rents the unit to long-term renters loses the unit's golf cart parking space.

How do you find out before you buy?

We find out for the one home, before you make an offer, in three steps:

  1. We check the address. Whether the city or county allows the rental you plan there. Each town's rule is on our short-term rental rules page.
  2. We pull the papers. The recorded papers, their amendments and the latest rules. The county's index is online, but its pages are not, so the copies come from the Register of Deeds or the association. One North Myrtle Beach manager posts 210 files for the 62 associations it runs.
  3. We read the seller's answer. South Carolina's HOA disclosure addendum asks the seller about any owners association "resale or rental restrictions". The seller may answer "No Representation", so we read the papers either way.

Common questions

HOA rental restrictions FAQ

Am I grandfathered if my HOA bans rentals after I buy?

Not by South Carolina law. Neither its HOA law nor its condo law exempts owners who bought before a new rule. Florida has such a law, and South Carolina does not. At The Atrium in Myrtle Beach, a 1998 amendment ended leases of 30 days or less for every unit.

Does residential use only ban Airbnb?

Not by those words alone in the papers we read. North Beach Plantation's residential-use rule says it does not restrict any form of rental, and Sand Dunes Oceanfront Tower allows transient residential use. A South Carolina court reads unclear words the way that least restricts the owner.

Can an HOA make my renters register?

Yes, if its papers say so. In parts of North Beach Plantation, rental guests not staying with the owner register with the on-site access company. Del Webb at Grande Dunes wants notice of each lease within 10 days, and The Farm at Carolina Forest wants the tenants' names.

What counts as a short-term rental in South Carolina?

For the state's lodging tax, a stay of fewer than 90 days in a row by the same guest. A community's papers can define it differently. Surf Estates treats any use under a year as short-term.

Can I rent out a home in a 55+ community near Myrtle Beach?

Yes, with the age rule. Del Webb at Grande Dunes asks for a 12-month lease and board approval for a tenant under 55. Seasons at Prince Creek West asks for a one-year lease. At Myrtle Trace, a tenant 55 or older must live there full time.
