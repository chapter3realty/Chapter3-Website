HOA fees in 382 Myrtle Beach area neighborhoods
What each one costs a month, and what you get for it.

By Devin Day, Operations Officer · Reviewed by Tim Nash, Broker-in-Charge · Updated November 14, 2026

Waterbridge has a pool complex, pocket parks and over 80 acres of lakes and natural areas. Most of its homes for sale list a $150 monthly HOA fee.

The short answer

In the middle Myrtle Beach area neighborhood of houses, the HOA fee is about $95 a month. About 6 in 10 charge $100 or less.

The fee is higher where it includes more. It is about $300 where the listings say it includes lawn care, and about $250 in the middle townhome neighborhood.

Ask us about every fee on a home

Why are townhome HOA fees higher?

The fee covers more. In the middle townhome neighborhood the HOA fee is about $250 a month, more than twice the fee for houses. Counted home by home instead, the middle townhouse fee is about $290, as our Myrtle Beach HOA fees guide shows.

What a townhome fee includes

Of the townhouse listings that give a fee, 84 in 100 say it includes lawn care, 72 in 100 trash pickup and 37 in 100 insurance. For houses it is 8, 46 and 4 in 100. Pest control, cable and water and sewer also show up in townhouse listings, and almost never in house listings.

How to compare it with a house

A townhome fee buys more items, not the same items at a higher price. A house owner usually does those jobs alone and pays for them apart from the HOA. To compare a house fee with a townhome fee, add what you would pay for the same items on the house.

The listing says the fee includes insurance. It does not say which policy. Ask the HOA what its insurance covers and what yours must cover.

For a condo, each oceanfront building's fee is on our condo buildings page. To add the HOA fee to the tax and insurance for a year, use our cost to own page. How much an HOA may raise its fee is on our dues increases page.

HOA fees by neighborhood: FAQ

What is the HOA fee in Lakeside Crossing in Conway?

Lakeside Crossing has no HOA fee. The homes are on leased land in a 55+ community, and the lot rent is the monthly cost.

Why does a listing's HOA fee differ from the HOA's?

A home's listed fee is what its agent typed for that home. A home in two associations may list one fee or both. We get the HOA's own figure before you offer.

Is the HOA fee part of the monthly cost of a home?

Yes. Add it to the property tax and the insurance. Our cost to own page adds them up for a year.

Why are townhome HOA fees higher?

The fee covers more. Most townhouse listings that give a fee say it includes lawn care and trash pickup, and fewer than 1 in 10 house listings include lawn care. Insurance, pest control and water and sewer also show up far more often for townhouses.
