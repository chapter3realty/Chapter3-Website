The HOA guide

Myrtle Beach HOA fees,
and what each one covers.

  • A houseAbout $95 a month
  • A condoAbout $650 a month
  • Oceanfront towerAbout $940 a month

By Devin Day, Operations Officer · Reviewed by Tim Nash, Broker-in-Charge · Updated November 14, 2026

About 6 in 10 homeowners in Horry County pay an HOA or condo fee. In the rest of South Carolina, about 1 in 3 do.

The short answer

The typical homeowners association (HOA) fee on the Grand Strand is about $95 a month for a house, $290 for a townhouse and $650 for a condo. In an oceanfront tower, a condo's fee is about $940.

A condo fee is higher because the owners share the building's bills. Most condo fees include insurance, water and sewer, trash and internet, and about 6 in 10 oceanfront tower fees include the power bill.

How much are HOA fees in Myrtle Beach?

Myrtle Beach HOA fees depend most on the kind of home, and on whether a condo is on the ocean.

Listings here give the fee by the month. Over a year, that is about $1,140 for a typical house and about $7,800 for a typical condo. For the fee in each community, see our HOA fees by neighborhood guide.

What do HOA fees cover in Myrtle Beach?

A house fee rarely includes anything inside the house. A condo fee includes far more.

The owner of a house buys its insurance apart from the fee. Owners of a one-family house here pay a middle of about $2,000 a year for it. A condo owner's own policy costs about $800 a year, and the condo fee may also pay the insurance on the shared building.

For the fee, the tax and the insurance added up, see the yearly cost to own a home here.

Why are HOA fees so high in Myrtle Beach?

Myrtle Beach's highest HOA fees are in the oceanfront towers. Most of a $940 tower fee goes to the building's insurance, its utilities and its upkeep.

The building's federal flood policy is a small part of the fee. For condo buildings in Horry County with one, the middle cost was about $405 a unit a year, or about $34 a month.

Our oceanfront condo building directory gives the fees building by building. Where the building's insurance stops and yours starts is on our page on the building's policy and your own condo policy.

What does an HOA fee buy in a neighborhood of houses?

A house's fee goes to what the neighborhood shares.

In a gated golf community, about 9 in 10 houses for sale list a security service. In Prestwick, with a main gate off Highway 544, homes for sale list a monthly fee of $185 to $197. In Barefoot Resort's Leatherleaf, the fee is $385 to $398.

Most homes for sale in Waterbridge, a neighborhood of houses with a pool complex and a clubhouse, list a monthly fee of about $150.

Our pages on gated communities and golf communities compare the fees community by community.

What are HOA fees in Myrtle Beach's 55+ communities?

The 55+ communities we have checked charge from $95 a month in Myrtle Trace to about $400 for a house in Del Webb at Grande Dunes.

In between, most listings in Del Webb North Myrtle Beach show $315 a month. The builder lists lawn care and a 175-channel TV package in the fee, and most listings name internet. In Seasons at Prince Creek West, the fee is about $340.

Their public budgets show where each $100 goes at the two ends of that range. Myrtle Trace is a 55+ community of houses in Conway.

Where are HOA fees lowest in Myrtle Beach?

Condo fees are lowest inland, away from the ocean. Among condos for sale, the middle fee is about $375 a month in Socastee, against about $875 at the north end of Myrtle Beach.

For a house, the lowest fee is often none. About 3 in 10 houses for sale list no HOA fee. In North Myrtle Beach and Surfside Beach, more than half of houses for sale list none.

Older houses rarely have a fee. Of houses built before 1980, about 4 in 100 list one. Of houses built in 2010 or later, about 8 in 10 do. Our page on homes with no HOA shows where they are.

Are HOA complaints common in Horry County?

Yes. Of the HOA complaints in South Carolina's latest yearly report, 31 percent were about associations in Horry County, more than any other county. Horry also had the most complaints per person, and Georgetown County was second.

The most common complaint here was that the HOA did not follow or enforce its own rules. Next came maintenance and repairs.

What the state's HOA law requires of an association is on our page on South Carolina HOA laws. How far an association may raise its fee is on our page on how much an HOA can raise dues.

How we check an association before you offer

We check four things, in this order, every time.

  1. Whether lenders will lend in the building. If most will not, your loan choices change before anything else. Our page on condos most lenders will not lend on explains why.
  2. The budget and the savings for repairs. We read the budget, the balance sheet and the reserves, to judge whether the association can pay for the repairs ahead.
  3. The rules, against how you plan to live. Pets, parking, renting, a home business, what you may keep on the porch. We compare the rules with what you told us you want to do.
  4. What we found, good and bad. We tell you all of it, including anything that argues against buying.

Common questions

Myrtle Beach HOA fees FAQ

What is a regime fee?

A regime fee is the condo fee. South Carolina's condo law calls a condo a regime, so its association fee is also called a regime fee.

Can I refuse to join an HOA?

No, not when the home is in one. Under South Carolina law, the recorded declaration requires each owner to pay the association's assessments.

Can HOA fees go up?

Yes. South Carolina's HOA law and condo law set no cap on dues, on a special assessment or on a fine. The limits are in the association's own recorded rules.
