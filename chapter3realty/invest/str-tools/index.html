How much would you keep from a Myrtle Beach Airbnb?
Count the costs before you offer.

  • Oceanfront Myrtle BeachAbout 110 nights to pay the bills
  • Lower-fee condosAbout 40 to 70 nights to pay the bills
  • June to AugustNearly half the year's income

By Devin Day, Operations Officer · Reviewed by Tim Nash, Broker-in-Charge · Updated November 12, 2026

A rough yearly estimate for one Grand Strand nightly rental, from town averages: what is left after the HOA fee, tax and Airbnb's cut.

The short answer

From a few thousand dollars a year over its bills to a few thousand short, before any mortgage payment. It depends most on the HOA fee, the monthly fee every condo owner pays. Run as an Airbnb by its owner, a typical two-bedroom beach condo in Garden City Beach keeps about $5,000 a year. With a manager, who takes a share of each booking, it keeps under $2,000.

A typical oceanfront two-bedroom inside Myrtle Beach has a fee more than twice as high. At the nights the average listing books, counting listings open only part of the year, it falls about $4,000 short. Open all year, it keeps about $4,500, or falls a little short with a manager.

The worked example

The calculator starts on a typical two-bedroom beach condo for sale in Garden City Beach that allows nightly rentals. It asks about $345,000, and its HOA fee is about $575 a month. The average two-bedroom Airbnb there books about 95 nights a year, and this condo pays its bills at about 66. With a manager at 15 percent of the bookings, it needs about 83 nights and keeps under $2,000.

The oceanfront towers in Myrtle Beach are the hard case. A typical two-bedroom there has a fee of about $1,200 a month and needs about 110 booked nights. The average listing books about 87, counting listings open only part of the year, so it falls about $4,000 short. Open all year, it keeps about $4,500, or falls a little short with a manager.

What to tell a guest about Garden City Beach:

  • Parking is free all year at every oceanfront beach access Horry County runs in Garden City.
  • At the inlet end of Waccamaw Drive, people go up to the rooftop deck of Gulfstream Cafe to watch the sun set over the water.
  • From a dock on the marsh, you can tie a chicken neck to a string and pull up blue crabs for dinner, and with three or fewer drop nets, fold-up traps or hand lines you need no license.
  • Every Fourth of July afternoon, decorated golf carts drive the "wet" parade while people along the route soak them with water guns.

Why does the HOA fee matter so much for a Myrtle Beach Airbnb?

In an oceanfront tower the HOA fee is the biggest bill. On the typical Myrtle Beach oceanfront two-bedroom it is nearly three quarters of the bills. It is due every month, in January as well as July. Where the fee is lower, the bills take far fewer booked nights to pay.

The low fees are in two kinds of places: on the beach in Garden City Beach, and inland in Surfside Beach.

  • Garden City Beach, on the beachNearly all are on Waccamaw Drive, the street along the beach. Beach House, Sea Watch and Garden Sands are among those with fees of $600 a month or less.
  • Surfside Beach, mostly inlandMost are at Retreat at Glenns Bay, Golf Colony at Deerfield and Grand Palms Resort, a mile or more from the beach, with fees of $600 or less. On the oceanfront, the units listed at Sea Shadow have fees over $1,000, and the few others listed there have fees of $1,000 or less. An inland condo may book fewer nights than the town's average of 80, which counts the beach condos too. It pays its bills at about 42, about half that average.
  • Myrtle Beach, oceanfrontThe fees of $800 a month or less are at places like Ocean Forest Villas and Beach Colony. The fee pays the electric on every unit listed at Sand Dunes, the Caribbean, Dunes Village, Carolinian Beach Resort and Grande Shores.
  • North Myrtle Beach, oceanfrontThe fee pays the electric on most of the units listed at Bay Watch and Beach Cove.

When the fee pays the electric, tick the box under "Change the numbers" in the calculator, and the utilities cost goes. Most oceanfront units are in the building's own rental program, which takes its own share. Our page on a rental program or Airbnb compares the two.

How do the Grand Strand towns compare for an Airbnb?

For the same size of home, the towns are close. In seven of the eight, the average two-bedroom took in about $21,000 to $28,000 a year. Little River took in far less, and Murrells Inlet and Garden City Beach did best. What Airbnbs take in by town, size and month is on our Airbnb income page.

Counting all sizes, Murrells Inlet's average Airbnb took in nearly twice Myrtle Beach's. Most of Murrells Inlet's listings are houses, and most of its homes sleep groups. Four in five of Myrtle Beach's listings are condos, and Myrtle Beach has far more listings than any other town here.

How slow is the off season for a Myrtle Beach Airbnb?

A Myrtle Beach Airbnb earns about five times as much in July as in January, when the ocean is about 50 degrees. June, July and August bring in nearly half its year. In Murrells Inlet, where most Airbnbs are houses, they bring 43 percent.

Murrells Inlet earns more in every month, with bigger homes. Pawleys Island has the most even year, with 37 percent of its income in June to August.

Summer guests book early. A typical July booking in Myrtle Beach is made in mid-May, and Murrells Inlet's July stays are booked even further ahead.

The quiet months still give a listing something to offer a guest:

  • In Myrtle Beach, after the summer season, from September to May 1, you can walk a leashed dog or ride a bike on the beach and the boardwalk at any hour.
  • In Surfside Beach, from mid-September to mid-May, you can surf anywhere on the town's beach from sunup to sundown, except within 300 feet of the pier.
  • In Murrells Inlet, from the day after Thanksgiving to New Year's Eve, every restaurant along the MarshWalk is outlined in lights that play in time to Christmas music, on the hour and the half hour from 5 to 11 p.m.

South Carolina does not tax a stay of 90 continuous days or more by one guest as lodging. Our page on mid-term rentals covers renting by the month.

What does Airbnb take from a Myrtle Beach rental, and what else comes off?

At AirROI's low-end cost shares, an owner-run Myrtle Beach Airbnb keeps about 63 cents of each dollar guests pay, before insurance, the HOA fee, property tax and any loan.

  • Airbnb's fee15.5%Most hosts pay 15.5 percent of each booking, taken from the payout. Some still pay less on an older plan, which Airbnb is phasing out, so the calculator uses AirROI's average for the town.
  • Cleaningabout $150 a stayThe guest's cleaning fee pays the cleaner, so the owner keeps none of it. It is the typical fee in Myrtle Beach.
  • Utilities, supplies, repairsabout 10%Utilities and internet are about half of it. Supplies, repairs and linens are the rest. Where the HOA fee pays the electric and internet, the owner does not pay most of the utilities.
  • Rental insurance and permit$390 a yearA short-term rental policy on top of Airbnb's free cover, and a permit where one is needed. This is AirROI's cost for Myrtle Beach; it differs by town, and the calculator fills in each town's. The owner's own condo policy is extra.
  • A manager, basic planabout 13 to 17%The basic plans we have seen here cover marketing, guest messages and basic accounting. Full service adds taxes, maintenance and repairs, and is priced as a share of the profit.

The guest pays the lodging taxes on top of the price, about 13 percent inside the City of Myrtle Beach, and Airbnb collects them. Our accommodations tax page has the rates town by town.

The worst short-term rental mistake our team sees is running it yourself with the cheapest cleaner. We have seen theft and damage follow. If you will not be here to check the cleaning, pick the manager in the calculator.

The top short-term rentals here pick one kind of guest and stay filled, such as golfers, workers or people moving here.

Is Myrtle Beach getting too many Airbnbs?

Myrtle Beach has many more Airbnbs than a year ago, and each new one competes with the rest for the same guests. It had 43 percent more active listings than a year earlier. Every other town in the chart grew by about a quarter or more.

Many listings book few nights. More than half of Myrtle Beach listings were booked, or blocked by their owner, 90 days or fewer in the year.

For a buyer, that means a condo should pay its bills at the nights the average listing books now. Do not plan on more.

Can you Airbnb a home in Myrtle Beach?

In an oceanfront condo building, usually yes; in a house inside the city, usually not. Inside the City of Myrtle Beach, a stay under 90 days is short-term, and any rental needs a city business license. The city allows short-term stays in no residential zone except RMV, the zone for apartment and condo buildings for visitors. Fewer than 30 houses in the other residential zones may still rent nightly, because they did before the zoning.

Homes for sale show the same split. Inside Myrtle Beach city limits, about 5 in 6 condos for sale are marked for nightly rentals, and few houses are. In Garden City and North Myrtle Beach, many more houses are.

North Myrtle Beach requires a business license for every short-term rental, renewed every year, and no separate zoning or permit process. Our page on short-term rental rules covers each town.

A listing's mark is the listing agent's word, not the city's. Before you offer, we read the zoning for the address and the building's own rules.

Myrtle Beach Airbnb questions

Do you need a business license for an Airbnb in Myrtle Beach?

Yes. Inside the City of Myrtle Beach, any rental needs a city business license. North Myrtle Beach asks every short-term rental for one, renewed each year.

How much does an Airbnb make in Myrtle Beach?

The average one took in about $26,000 from guests in a year, before any cost. Our Airbnb income page breaks it down by town, size and month.

How many nights does a Myrtle Beach Airbnb condo need to book?

The count depends most on the HOA fee. The four typical condos in the chart above need about 42 to 110 booked nights a year to pay their bills if the owner runs them, and more with a manager.

What percentage does Airbnb take?

For most hosts, 15.5 percent of each booking. The list of costs above shows what else comes off before the HOA fee.

How much is an Airbnb cleaning fee in Myrtle Beach?

About $150 is typical. The guest pays it, and it goes to the cleaner, so the owner keeps none of it.

Who pays the lodging tax on a Myrtle Beach Airbnb?

The guest. Inside the City of Myrtle Beach the lodging taxes add about 13 percent to the price, and Airbnb collects them on stays under 90 nights.

What is the slow season for a Myrtle Beach Airbnb?

November to February are the four slowest months. The season chart above shows each month.

Can you finance an Airbnb condo?

Yes, with limits. A loan officer at our preferred lender tells us that loans approved on the rental's income, called DSCR loans, count only about 75 percent of a short-term rental estimate. A condotel, a condo run as a hotel, needs its own kind of loan.
