Where should you buy a rental near Myrtle Beach?
Rent by the night at the beach, and by the year inland.

For a nightly rental, buy at the beach, where the zoning on this map allows it. For a yearly lease, buy inland.

Eleven Grand Strand areas compared for a rental investor.

The short answer

For a nightly rental, buy in one of the beach areas, where the rules for the address allow it. A rental-data company estimates that the average nightly rental there took in about $25,000 to $48,000 in a year, before costs. The license, the tax and every town's rules are on our short-term rental rules page.

For a yearly lease, buy inland. In Longs, Socastee and Conway, a year's rent is about 7 to 9 percent of a house's price. In North Myrtle Beach, Myrtle Beach, Surfside Beach, Murrells Inlet and Pawleys Island, it is 3 to 4 percent. A higher share means more rent for each dollar of the price.

1. North Myrtle Beach: nightly rentals with a business license

North Myrtle Beach has no special zoning or permit for nightly rentals. It asks for a business license renewed each year and the lodging taxes. It also has rules on trash, parking, noise and how many guests may stay.

Most homes here are condos, and four in five condos for sale are listed as open to nightly guests. In the rental data's North Myrtle Beach area, the average nightly rental took in about $34,000 a year, before costs.

The middle house within the city limits sold for about $565,000 and the middle condo for about $300,000.

Get rentals for sale in North Myrtle Beach

2. Surfside Beach: nightly rentals in two districts

Surfside Beach allows stays of less than 30 days in only two of its zoning districts. A lease of a year or more is allowed wherever homes are. The town's code does not define a lease of one month to a year. For a monthly rental, we get the town's reading before you make an offer.

Houses cost more here than in any of the other ten areas, and almost no new ones are built. In the rental data's Surfside Beach area, the average nightly rental brought in about $43,000 a year, before costs.

The middle house within the town limits sold for about $705,000 and the middle condo for about $370,000.

Get rentals for sale in Surfside Beach

3. Garden City: the most houses for sale that say nightly is allowed

Garden City is outside the towns, in Horry County. A nightly rental here needs a county business license, a state sales tax number and a county hospitality and accommodations tax account.

More than half the houses for sale here say nightly stays are allowed, the highest share of the eleven areas. In the rental data's Garden City Beach area, the average nightly rental took in about $41,000 a year, before costs.

Houses here cost less than in the other five beach areas. FEMA puts 39 percent of homes here in its high-risk flood zone, the highest share of the eleven areas, so we price the flood insurance for each house.

The middle house in the Census area of Garden City sold for about $406,000 and the middle condo for about $221,000.

Get rentals for sale in Garden City

4. Murrells Inlet, Georgetown County side: no county business license, and a 30-day limit

Murrells Inlet sits on both sides of the county line. On this page, Murrells Inlet is the Census area of that name, which lies on the Georgetown County side. Georgetown County requires no business license. Its zoning allows a house to be rented for stays of up to 30 days in the county's resort zone. The lot may hold only that house, with a parking space for each bedroom. We look up the zoning of each address.

About three in four homes here are their owner's main home. In the rental data's Murrells Inlet area, the average nightly rental made about $48,000 a year, the most of the six beach areas.

The middle house in the Census area of Murrells Inlet sold for about $610,000, counting every recorded sale.

Get rentals for sale in Murrells Inlet

5. Pawleys Island and Litchfield: nightly rentals on the island, with its own tax

Nightly rentals are allowed on Pawleys Island, and the town collects its own accommodations tax for its Beach Fund. Off the island, the county's resort zone allows stays of up to 30 days, and a community's HOA can limit rentals.

About 7 percent of homes here are on the oceanfront, in the part of FEMA's high-risk zone where the flood map expects waves. That is the largest share of the eleven areas, and flood insurance costs the most there. Around Pawleys Island, a nightly rental brought in about $31,000 a year, before costs.

The middle house in ZIP code 29585 sold for about $650,000 and the middle condo for about $325,000, counting every recorded sale.

Get rentals for sale in Pawleys Island and Litchfield

6. Myrtle Beach: condos in the zones marked for nightly rentals

Myrtle Beach is a condo market: condos outnumber houses. About three in four condos sit in a zone the city marks for nightly rentals, the dark blue on the map. Most houses do not.

In the city, any stay of less than 90 days counts as short-term, and most neighborhoods of houses are zoned against it. Some houses for sale are still listed as open to nightly stays. What a listing says is not enough, so we check the zone and the HOA's rules for each house. In the rental data's Myrtle Beach area, the average nightly rental took in about $26,000 a year, the least of the six beach areas.

The middle condo within the city limits sold for about $175,000.

Get rentals for sale in Myrtle Beach

7. Longs: the highest rent next to the price

The federal housing agency, HUD, publishes a rent for a standard 3-bedroom in each ZIP code, counting the utilities a tenant pays. In Longs it is $2,270 a month. A year of that rent comes to about 9.1 percent of the middle house price, the highest of the eleven areas.

Builders are still selling here: at least 46 percent of house sales in the past year were a builder's first sale. Each new street adds houses for rent near yours, and rents do not always rise with prices. Eight townhouses in Little River show how.

The middle house in ZIP code 29568, outside North Myrtle Beach, sold for about $300,000.

Get rentals for sale in Longs

8. Socastee: houses priced like Longs, with tenants all year

Socastee is outside the towns, in Horry County, so zoning puts no limit on nightly rentals.

Socastee houses cost about what Longs houses do. About a third of homes here are rented, so the tenants a yearly lease needs already live here.

Socastee has the most homes in the high-risk flood zone of the five inland areas, about 11 percent. We check the zone for the address and price the insurance.

The middle house in the Census area of Socastee sold for about $300,000.

Get rentals for sale in Socastee

9. Conway: the lowest house prices, and nightly rentals downtown

A house costs less in Conway than in any of the other ten areas. Census figures put what tenants here pay at about $870 a month at the middle, for rentals of every size. That is less than in Socastee, Myrtle Beach or Carolina Forest, and we price each house against Conway rents, not beach rents.

Conway allows a nightly rental only in three of its business districts, including downtown and the riverfront. A stay may last up to 90 days, with a city business license. A bed and breakfast, where the host lives on site, is a separate use. Conway's tax on a rental is the highest per dollar of the eleven areas.

The middle house within the city limits sold for about $271,000.

Get rentals for sale in Conway

10. Little River: the lowest condo prices

Little River has the lowest middle condo price of the eleven areas, and a house's rent for a year is 6.6 percent of its sale price.

Nightly stays here depend on the subdivision's recorded rules. About 1 in 7 houses for sale say they are allowed.

New communities here keep adding rentals. Eight townhouses in one of them show why we run today's rent against your price.

The middle house in the Census area of Little River sold for about $380,000 and the middle condo for about $155,000.

Get rentals for sale in Little River

11. Carolina Forest: a yearly-lease area with almost no flood zone

Under 1 percent of Carolina Forest homes sit in FEMA's high-risk flood zone, the lowest share of the eleven areas. Census figures put what tenants pay at about $1,670 a month at the middle, more than in Conway, Socastee or Myrtle Beach.

Carolina Forest is outside the towns, so zoning puts no limit on nightly rentals. Even so, this is a yearly-lease market: none of the houses for sale here is listed for nightly stays. A subdivision's recorded rules can bar them, so we read those rules for each house.

The middle house in the Census area of Carolina Forest sold for about $440,000.

Inland, a year's rent is a bigger share of a house's price. In the six beach areas, houses cost more for the same rent, so a yearly lease earns less there. That is where nightly rentals are allowed.

Why do we run the rent against your price?

We price a rental against the rent it will get now, not against what earlier buyers paid. An agent now on our team learned why in Little River. He helped buyers purchase about eight nearly identical townhouses in a new community there. Each rented for a little over $2,000 a month.

The sale price rose from about $230,000 for the first to about $292,000 for the last, over about a year and a half. The rent stayed the same, so the later buyers lost the cash flow. Before you offer, we put today's rent against your price. The rest of the story, and the rules on renting a new house, are on our page on new construction as a rental.

What does the tax on a rental cost in each area?

South Carolina taxes the home you live in on 4 percent of its value, and a rental or second home on 6 percent. Only the home you live in skips the school operating tax. Outside city limits, a rental's tax bill is about 3.3 times a resident's on the same house; in the towns it differs.

Will the rules change?

Two bills in the state legislature would set a state rule. One would bar cities and counties from banning short-term rentals, and the other would expressly allow them to. Neither has left committee.

North Myrtle Beach's council has held workshops and public sessions on new rules. The city's page lists no new rule.

For a rental of one to three months, each area has its own rule:

  • Myrtle Beach counts any stay under 90 days as short-term, so it needs a marked zone.
  • In Conway, the same rule as for a nightly rental: one of three business districts.
  • Surfside Beach's code leaves it open, so we ask the town first.
  • In Georgetown County's resort zone, a stay may last up to 30 days.
  • Outside the towns in Horry County, zoning puts no limit.

South Carolina's lodging tax applies to stays of less than 90 days in a row, unless the home is rented fewer than 15 days in a year. No state law bans nightly rentals.

What do we check before you offer?

A house works for your plan only if five things line up. We check each one for the address:

  • The nightly rule for the address: the town's zone or district, or in Georgetown County the zoning district.
  • The HOA's recorded rules and papers: many communities here ban renting a house out at all. We filter those out, and we read the papers for any rule that would block your plan.
  • The building's own rental rules, for a condo.
  • The flood zone for the address, and what the flood insurance costs there.
  • The tax at the rental rate on your price, in the tax district the home is in.

Common questions

Where to buy a rental: questions

Where are Airbnbs legal in Myrtle Beach?

In the City of Myrtle Beach, only in the zones the city marks for them. Most neighborhoods of houses are zoned against any stay of less than 90 days. About three in four condos in the city are in a marked zone. North Myrtle Beach has no special zoning step for them, and Surfside Beach allows them in two districts.

Is Myrtle Beach a good place to buy a rental property?

For a yearly lease, the inland areas earn more rent for the price. A year's rent is about 9.1 percent of a house's price in Longs, against about 3.9 percent in Myrtle Beach. For a nightly rental, buy at the beach, where the rules for the address allow it.

North Myrtle Beach or Myrtle Beach for a rental?

For a nightly rental, North Myrtle Beach asks only for a license, the lodging taxes and its everyday rules. Myrtle Beach allows it only in marked zones, where most of its condos are. Nightly rentals took in more a year on average in North Myrtle Beach. The middle condo sold for about $300,000 in North Myrtle Beach and about $175,000 in Myrtle Beach.

Is Murrells Inlet in Horry or Georgetown County?

Both. The county line runs through Murrells Inlet. The Census area of that name, which this page counts, lies in Georgetown County. Georgetown County requires no business license, and its resort district allows a house to be rented for stays of up to 30 days.

What is the property tax on a rental in Horry County?

Outside the towns, most homes pay about $1,206 a year on each $100,000 of price at the rental rate, before fees. Inside the towns it is higher, and highest in Conway.

Where is the cheapest place to buy a rental near Myrtle Beach?

Conway for a house, at about $271,000 at the middle in the past year's recorded sales, and Little River for a condo, at about $155,000.
