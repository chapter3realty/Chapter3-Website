What are the oceanfront condo buildings in Myrtle Beach?

Myrtle Beach's oceanfront buildings run from Myrtle Beach Resort, south of the state park, past the Boardwalk and the SkyWheel, north to SeaWatch Resort near the Tanger Outlets.

Myrtle Beach Resort

Myrtle Beach Resort's Renaissance Tower stands about 236 feet tall, the southernmost tower on this page. More than a mile up the beach is Myrtle Beach State Park, with its seaside forest and fishing pier, one of the last green stretches of beachfront on the Grand Strand.

The resort has more two-bedroom units for sale than any other building here. Every listing gives the same address, so we find which tower a unit is in before you offer. The typical unit asks about $170,000, with a fee near $835 a month.

Get the condos for sale at Myrtle Beach Resort

Compass Cove

Compass Cove has 23 water features, among them three lazy rivers and two waterslides, the Schooner Screamer and the Compass Cruiser. The Crow's Nest Oceanfront Bar & Grill is on site.

It stands under a mile north of Springmaid Pier. Units here change hands often: only three buildings on this page had more sales. Most are studios and one-bedrooms, and they sold for about $149,000, with fees near $960 a month.

Get the condos for sale at Compass Cove

Bluewater Resort

Bluewater has indoor pickleball and racquetball courts, so you can play all year. It stands about halfway between Springmaid Pier and Family Kingdom's rides, a little over a mile from each.

It is mostly one-bedrooms, and most list the same fee, about $905 a month. Its sales in the past year had a typical price of about $94,000. Its name covers more than one kind of unit, so we price a Bluewater unit from sales of units like it.

Get the condos for sale at Bluewater Resort

The Palace

The Palace stands 241 feet tall. It has two beach accesses and a putting green next to its oceanfront pools, and its guests get two free passes a day to the H2Oasis water park at Landmark, in season.

It stands beside Landmark Resort. Its units for sale are studios, one-bedrooms and two-bedrooms in about equal numbers. A typical unit sold for about $142,000, with a typical fee of about $685 a month.

For its fee by unit size and the units that sold, see Palace Resort condos for sale.

Get the condos for sale at The Palace

Landmark Resort

Landmark has 17 water features. Its 300-foot indoor lazy river is heated and open all year, and a second one runs outside in season. Its water park, H2Oasis, has three slides, for guests who stay through the resort's own rental program.

It is the biggest building on this page, with 571 units, and it has more for sale this week than any other. Family Kingdom's rides are under a mile north. Every unit for sale is a studio or a one-bedroom. They sold for about $100,000, with a typical fee near $905 a month.

Get the condos for sale at Landmark Resort

Sea Mist Resort

Sea Mist Resort is where an oceanfront condo costs the least: a typical unit sold for about $53,000, the lowest here.

Family Kingdom's rides are about half a mile north, and the 2nd Avenue Pier at the Boardwalk's south end is under a mile away. Every unit for sale this week is a studio, asking about $70,000, with a fee near $610 a month.

Get the condos for sale at Sea Mist Resort

Coral Beach Resort

Coral Beach Resort stands beside Sea Mist, on its north side, and its units sold for about $86,000, the second lowest here. Only two buildings had more sales.

Most of its 40 units for sale are one-bedrooms, and the typical fee is about $1,345 a month. That is a high fee for the price, so we get the building's budget before you offer, to see what it pays for.

Get the condos for sale at Coral Beach Resort

Bay View Resort

Bay View sits right in front of the Boardwalk, and Starbucks is on the ground floor of the tower. The 2nd Avenue Pier is about a third of a mile south, and the SkyWheel is about half a mile north.

Inside are a heated pool and a heated lazy river. Most units for sale are one-bedrooms. They sold for about $188,000, and the typical fee is about $780 a month.

Get the condos for sale at Bay View Resort

Camelot by the Sea

From Camelot by the Sea, the Boardwalk's north end at Pier 14 is about a ten-minute walk, and the SkyWheel is about half a mile away.

It charges one of the lowest fees on the oceanfront, about $625 a month, and its units, from studios to three bedrooms, sold for about $220,000.

Get the condos for sale at Camelot by the Sea

Caribbean Resort

The Caribbean has a lazy river outside and a second one indoors. Beside it is the restaurant Sea Captain's House, which began as an oceanfront beach cottage built in 1930.

Its units run from one-bedrooms to four bedrooms, the widest spread of fees on this page: a larger unit pays a much larger fee. A Caribbean unit sold for about $215,000, with a typical fee of about $1,395 a month.

Get the condos for sale at Caribbean Resort

Beach Colony

Between Beach Colony's pool deck and the sand is an oceanfront lawn with hammocks. There is a lazy river too, with indoor and outdoor pools.

It stands next to Dunes Village and has one of the lowest fees here, about $595 a month. Almost every unit for sale allows pets. Most are one- and two-bedrooms; they sold for about $225,000, and the typical one asks about $220,000.

Get the condos for sale at Beach Colony

Dunes Village Resort

Dunes Village Resort has room for a big group: most of its units for sale have two bedrooms or more, up to four bedrooms, and the building has an indoor pool. It stands next to Beach Colony.

Only Margate Tower charges more: the typical fee here is about $1,690 a month. Dunes Village units sold for about $300,000.

Get the condos for sale at Dunes Village Resort

Monterey Bay Suites

Monterey Bay Suites has a pool on its roof, with a view of the Myrtle Beach skyline. Downstairs, the indoor pool opens into the outdoor pool, so you can swim out into the sun. There is an indoor lazy river too.

Every unit for sale is a one-bedroom. About one in ten of its units sold in the past year, for about $150,000, and the typical fee is about $940 a month.

Get the condos for sale at Monterey Bay Suites

Grande Cayman Resort

Grande Cayman has 720 feet of beach on the north end of Myrtle Beach, and a waterslide three stories tall, open in season. Its lazy river is under a roof, so you can float it all year.

It was once called Long Bay Resort. Units for sale are mostly studios and one-bedrooms, with a few of three bedrooms. The typical fee is about $750 a month, and units sold for about $170,000.

Get the condos for sale at Grande Cayman Resort

Caravelle Resort

The Caravelle's pool deck is a full block long, from the beach to Ocean Boulevard. In the main Caravelle building, the indoor pool is on the seventh floor, behind floor-to-ceiling windows.

It has been a Myrtle Beach resort since 1960, mostly studios and one-bedrooms. One of its nine buildings, Sea Mark Tower, is on HUD's list of condos approved for FHA loans, and its units are listed under their own name. Only seven buildings had more sales. Units sold for about $140,000, with a typical fee of about $750 a month.

Get the condos for sale at Caravelle Resort

Ocean Reef Resort

Ocean Reef completed a renovation of more than $15 million. Children get the Shipwreck Water Park, with giant tiki men, a sinking ship and a waterslide, and there is an indoor lazy river for a rainy day.

About half its units for sale are studios, and a few have three bedrooms or more, so the fees spread wide. The typical fee is about $940 a month, and a quarter of the units for sale pay about $1,740 or more. Units sold for about $124,000.

Get the condos for sale at Ocean Reef Resort

Sand Dunes Resort

Sand Dunes has a 130,000-gallon water park on its grounds, an oceanfront lazy river and a blacklight indoor mini golf course. The resort's suites, condos and villas fill eight buildings, some on the sand and some across the street.

It has more three-bedroom units for sale than any other building here, and studios too, so its fees range widely; the typical one is about $1,245 a month. It had among the most sales of any building, at a typical price of about $270,000.

Get the condos for sale at Sand Dunes Resort

Sands Ocean Club

Sands Ocean Club sits on Shore Drive, under a quarter mile from the Dunes Golf and Beach Club, a private club since 1948. The club's course hosted the PGA Tour's 2026 Myrtle Beach Classic.

It has the lowest fee on this page, about $425 a month. Most of its units for sale are studios, and a studio's fee is smaller. It is a large resort of at least 491 units, and a unit here sold for about $138,000.

Get the condos for sale at Sands Ocean Club

Kingston Plantation

The next three towers stand in Kingston Plantation. Kingston Resorts counts more than half a mile of beach on its grounds, and 157 acres built around trees, with lakes beside its lodges and villas. Families have stayed here since the 1960s, when it was a campground called Lake Arrowhead, and the Arcadian Shores golf course is across the street. Each tower has its own fee.

Margate Tower

Margate Tower rises about 330 feet, the tallest building in Myrtle Beach. Its units climb to the 29th floor.

It has the largest units on this page too: every unit for sale has three or four bedrooms, and the typical one has about 2,400 square feet. Its fee is the highest here, about $1,820 a month, and so are its sale prices, about $950,000. Every unit for sale allows pets.

Get the condos for sale at Margate Tower

Brighton Tower

Brighton's units climb to the 21st floor, over Kingston's half mile of beach. It has the lowest fee of the three Kingston towers, about $1,075 a month.

Each unit for sale has two or three bedrooms. They sold for about $580,000, and the typical one for sale asks about $650,000.

Get the condos for sale at Brighton Tower

South Hampton

South Hampton stands beside the Embassy Suites, on Kingston's half mile of beach. It has 144 units, fewer than Margate or Brighton, and they sold for the least of the three, about $450,000.

All its units for sale have two or three bedrooms, and the typical fee is about $1,305 a month.

Get the condos for sale at South Hampton

Royale Palms

Royale Palms rises about 255 feet beside the Hilton, with units up to the 24th floor. For guests who rent here, the resort lists the Hilton's sand-bottom pool and Currents Seaside Entertainment.

Most of its units for sale have three bedrooms, and the rest have two. The typical fee is about $1,600 a month, the third highest here, and units sold for about $550,000.

Get the condos for sale at Royale Palms

SeaWatch Resort

SeaWatch has 19 water features: six oceanfront pools, two lazy rivers and ten hot tubs. In season there is a tiki bar and grill on the oceanfront, and a fire pit.

It is a gated resort about a mile from the Tanger Outlets. Only five units are for sale this week, but 23 sold in the past year, for about $235,000. We tell you when a unit like the one you want comes up.

Get the condos for sale at SeaWatch Resort

What are the oceanfront condo buildings in North Myrtle Beach?

North Myrtle Beach's oceanfront buildings run from Ocean Creek Resort, near Barefoot Landing, north to Prince Resort at the Cherry Grove Pier.

Ocean Creek Resort

From Ocean Creek's towers, you watch the sun go down past the dunes and a creek that rises and falls with the tide. Its 57 acres have walking and biking trails, tennis and pickleball courts, and a stretch of beach the resort calls secluded.

The gated grounds reach US 17, across the highway from Barefoot Landing. Units for sale run from studios to three bedrooms. The typical one asks about $225,000, with a fee of about $790 a month.

Get the condos for sale at Ocean Creek Resort

North Beach Towers

North Beach Towers stand on a 7.5-acre island between the ocean and White Point Swash, the resort says. Around them are 2.5 acres of pools, with a lazy river, a swim-up bar and private cabanas.

Units run from one-bedrooms to four bedrooms and have the second-highest sale prices on this page, about $850,000. The typical fee is about $1,295 a month.

Get the condos for sale at North Beach Towers

Beach Cove Resort

Every suite and condo at Beach Cove faces the ocean, the resort says. A 350-foot lazy river winds past three outdoor pools, heated in season.

Every unit for sale is a one- or two-bedroom. Units here sell often: about one in nine sold in the past year, for about $171,000. The typical fee is about $785 a month.

Get the condos for sale at Beach Cove Resort

Bay Watch Resort

Bay Watch is three towers on the sand at Crescent Beach: North, Center and South. The grounds have lazy rivers, indoor and outdoor pools, and Fishtails, a bar by the pool.

With 532 units it is one of the largest buildings here, about two and a half miles south of Main Street. Its typical fee, about $610 a month, is one of the lowest, and its units, from studios to three bedrooms, sold for about $265,000.

Get the condos for sale at Bay Watch Resort

Avista Resort

Avista stands about a quarter mile from the ocean end of Main Street. The state Senate calls North Myrtle Beach the birthplace of the shag; in the 1940s, visitors came to dance at the beach music clubs on Main Street and Ocean Drive.

Its pools, lazy river and hot tubs are outdoors and indoors, so a rainy day still has a swim. Most units for sale are one-bedrooms. With a typical fee of about $840 a month, Avista units sold for about $260,000.

Get the condos for sale at Avista Resort

Prince Resort

Prince Resort has a pool and a lazy river on its roof, beside the Cherry Grove Pier. Some condos look out over the inlet instead of the ocean. It is the northernmost building on this page.

Six units sold on their own in the past year, for about $365,000. The typical fee is about $1,035 a month.

Get the condos for sale at Prince Resort

What does an oceanfront condo HOA fee pay for?

An oceanfront fee pays the building's shared bills. Of every 100 condos for sale in oceanfront buildings, this many listings say the fee includes:

  • 97 in 100Water and sewer
  • 88 in 100Internet
  • 81 in 100Insurance
  • 57 in 100The power bill

The power bill changes the most from building to building. At Landmark, 80 of 93 listings say the fee pays it. In 10 buildings on this page, none of the listings does.

Three more things come with the fee:

  • InsuranceWhich policy the fee pays, and what your own unit policy covers, is on our page on the building's policy and yours.
  • SavingsPart of each fee goes to savings for future repairs. When those run short, owners pay a special assessment on top of the fee.
  • At closingBuying into one of these buildings usually means a transfer fee to the HOA, and often a few months of dues held at closing, as in any HOA. Our page on HOA fees due at closing explains them.

Which oceanfront buildings sell the most units?

In the past year, Sea Mist and Landmark sold the most units. Sea Mist had 64 sales. Two units sold twice, so 62 of its 271 units changed hands, more than 1 in 5. At the typical building on this page, about 1 in 17 did.

Condo, condotel or timeshare: what are you buying on the oceanfront?

Most units in these buildings are condos. Three kinds of ownership share the oceanfront:

  • CondoYou own the unit, and the owners together own the building through the HOA.
  • CondotelA condo in a building run like a hotel, which rents units to guests by the night.
  • TimeshareThe use of a unit for some weeks a year, not the whole unit.

About 94 in 100 condos for sale in oceanfront buildings say short-term rentals are allowed, and we read each building's own rules on renting before you offer. The rules town by town are on our short-term rental rules page, and the choice of a building's rental program or Airbnb has its own page.

A condotel is harder to buy with a loan: Fannie Mae will not buy a loan on a unit in a building run like a hotel. Which loans close on these buildings is on our condotel financing page, and a lawsuit against the association is on our page on buying a condo in litigation.

Whether an oceanfront condo is a good investment is in our condo investing guide.

Common questions

Oceanfront condo buildings FAQ

Which Myrtle Beach oceanfront condos have the lowest HOA fees?

Sands Ocean Club, at a typical $425 a month, where most units for sale are studios. Then Towers on the Grove, about $500 from 3 listings, Anderson Ocean Club, about $570, and Beach Colony, about $595.

Why are oceanfront condo HOA fees so high?

The fee pays the building's shared bills. Most listings in oceanfront buildings say it includes water and sewer, internet and insurance, and more than half say it pays the power bill.

Can you have a dog in a Myrtle Beach oceanfront condo?

In some buildings. At Beach Colony, Margate Tower, Royale Palms and Ocean Creek, most units for sale allow pets. At Coral Beach, none of the 40 do. We read the building's pet rule before you offer.

What is the tallest condo building in Myrtle Beach?

Margate Tower, in Kingston Plantation. It rises about 330 feet, and it also has the largest units and the highest fee of the oceanfront buildings on this page.

Which Myrtle Beach oceanfront condo buildings have a lazy river?

Fifteen of the buildings on this page have one. You can float all year at Landmark, Bay View, the Caribbean, Monterey Bay, Grande Cayman, Ocean Reef and Avista, where it is indoors or under a roof. Prince Resort's is on its roof.

What is the difference between a condotel and a timeshare?

A condotel unit is owned whole, like any condo. A timeshare sells the use of a unit for some weeks a year.
