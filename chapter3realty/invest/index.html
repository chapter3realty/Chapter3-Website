Why invest with Chapter 3

We underwrite the deal before you do

Before you make an offer, we build the real numbers on the exact property: projected long-term and short-term rent, average daily rate and occupancy by the month, and every figure we can calculate from them, net operating income, cap rate, cash-on-cash return, and expected appreciation. We pull the HOA bylaws and financials, check the short-term-rental zoning, and flag the litigation and permit activity a public listing will never show you.

And because our lender, BrickWood Mortgage (NMLS #189497), is in-house, the person running your analysis and the person structuring your loan are on the same team, which matters a lot on DSCR, condotel, and non-warrantable deals.
