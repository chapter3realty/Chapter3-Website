DSCR loans in Myrtle Beach: which rentals here pass the ratio?
A house inland passes with room to spare. A nightly oceanfront condo can fail before the loan.

  • 46 to 49%of the rent left for the loan on an inland house
  • 75 to 80%of nightly income that lenders count
  • 1.25the ratio we aim for

By Devin Day, Operations Officer · Reviewed by Tim Nash, Broker-in-Charge · Updated November 12, 2026

The 6 percent rental tax and the HOA fee sit inside a DSCR loan's ratio. Houses inland often pass, while a beach condo's dues can use the rent first.

The short answer

A DSCR loan qualifies on the rent. The lender divides the rent it counts by the monthly bill: principal and interest, property tax, insurance and HOA dues. The three program sheets we read ask for a ratio of 1.00 or more.

On the Grand Strand, the middle 3-bedroom house in Longs or Carolina Forest leaves 46 to 49 percent of the rent for the loan at our 1.25 target. On the middle oceanfront two-bedroom in Myrtle Beach rented by the night, the dues, tax and insurance alone come to more than the income a lender counts.

What is a DSCR loan, and does it work in South Carolina?

DSCR is short for debt service coverage ratio. It is the rent a home collects, divided by the monthly bill it must cover. The lender qualifies the loan on the rent, not on your income. You sign that the home is for business and that you will not live in it.

DSCR loans work on South Carolina rentals, and the formula does not change at the state line. The local numbers do. A rental here is taxed on 6 percent of its value, condo dues can be high, and towns and HOAs set their own rules on nightly rentals. Each of those sits inside the ratio.

The rent the lender counts is the appraiser's. The appraiser fills in a rent schedule with the appraisal, and with a lease in place the lender may use the lease, within limits. HUD's rents on this page are a guide; the appraiser's figure is the one that counts.

How do you calculate DSCR on a Grand Strand rental?

Add up the monthly bill, then divide the rent by it. The bill lenders divide by holds principal and interest, the property tax, the insurance and the HOA dues.

The formula

DSCR = the monthly rent the lender counts, divided by the monthly bill: principal and interest, property tax, insurance and HOA dues.

What is a good DSCR for a rental, and what if it is below 1.00?

A ratio of 1.00 means the rent just covers the bill. Our target is 1.25.

Below 1.00, the rent does not cover the bill, and you pay the rest each month. At 1.25, the cushion is there for an empty month or a repair.

Which Grand Strand rentals pass a DSCR loan?

We worked out the bill on six kinds of home for sale here, at the middle asking price of each. The chart shows how much of the rent the tax, the insurance and the HOA fee use before the loan.

  • A city condo on a yearly lease is tightThe middle 2-bedroom condo inside Myrtle Beach leaves about 14 percent. Its dues alone, about $550 a month, take 39 percent of the rent.
  • An oceanfront condo rented by the night fails before the loanTake a typical oceanfront two-bedroom in Myrtle Beach, booked the nights the average listing books. That average counts listings open only part of the year. Its HOA fee is about $1,200 a month. With the tax and insurance, that is about 109 percent of the nightly income a lender counts at 75 percent. The ratio is about 0.91 with no loan at all.

Taxes stop more rental deals here than insurance does. A home that is not its owner's legal residence, such as a house rented out year-round or a second home, is taxed on 6 percent. Our property tax guide works out the bill.

Longs: the Waccamaw River, and the most rent left for the loan

The Waccamaw River runs past Longs, and it is the only river that starts in a Carolina bay. From the landing on SC 9, you can paddle a state preserve along 28 miles of it, past bald cypress and water tupelo. Otters and black bears live there, and you may camp on the riverbank on preserve land.

Of the six homes we worked, the Longs house leaves the most of the rent for the loan, about 49 percent. The middle 3-bedroom house for sale asks about $320,000, and HUD's rent for a 3-bedroom in its ZIP code is $2,270 a month.

Much of Longs is new. About half the houses sold there in a year of county deeds were sales by builders, 280 of 607. About 2 in 5 homes in its ZIP code were built in 2010 or later. Only 3 in 100 of its houses are in FEMA's high-risk flood zone. Builders here rarely offer incentives to a buyer using a DSCR loan, so plan on none.

Carolina Forest: trails in the woods, and almost no houses in the flood zone

Off River Oaks Drive, the county's Bike and Run Park, which riders call "The Hulk", has 7 miles of mountain bike and running trails on about 70 acres. It sits between Carolina Forest and the Intracoastal Waterway.

Almost no houses in Carolina Forest are in FEMA's high-risk flood zone, and half its homes were built in 2007 or later. The middle 3-bedroom house for sale asks about $390,000 and leaves about 46 percent of the rent for the loan.

A condo there does almost as well. At HUD's 2-bedroom figure for the ZIP code, the middle 2-bedroom condo, about $165,000 with dues of about $425 a month, leaves about 44 percent.

Can you use Airbnb income for a DSCR loan in Myrtle Beach?

Yes, at some lenders. Lenders count 75 to 80 percent of the nightly-rental estimate, to allow for advertising, furnishing and cleaning. A loan officer at our preferred lender says only 75 percent counts. Two of the three program sheets we read count at most 80 percent of nightly-rental income; the third asks for a 1.15 ratio instead.

The cut can make a nightly rental count for less than a yearly lease. The middle two-bedroom nightly rental in the Myrtle Beach area took in about $20,100 in a year, counting listings open only part of the year. Counted at 75 percent, that is about $1,256 a month. HUD's figure for a 2-bedroom on a yearly lease in ZIP code 29577 is $1,420.

  • Some programs take it only on a refinanceTwo of the three sheets we read take nightly income only when you refinance. On a purchase, the home may have to qualify on the yearly-lease rent.
  • The rules must allow nightly rentalsThe lender's appraiser must show no city, county or HOA rule against them. In the City of Myrtle Beach, a stay under 90 days is short-term, and most of the city's residential zones bar it. Our short-term rental rules guide goes town by town.
  • Most condos allow them; few houses with an HOA doAbout 7 in 10 condos for sale in Horry County say short-term rentals are allowed. Among houses for sale with an HOA fee, about 4 in 100 say so.

Garden City Beach: the pier, and a beach condo that still passes

Garden City Beach has a 668-foot pier. In summer, bands play every night at both ends of it, weather permitting, and you can fish from it around the clock. A pier pass covers fishing, so no state license is needed.

It is also where a condo by the sea can still pass the ratio. About two dozen condos for sale in Garden City Beach are marked for nightly rentals. The middle 2-bedroom among them has dues of about $575 a month, under half the oceanfront tower's. Counted at 75 percent, its nightly income leaves about 20 percent for the loan at 1.25.

Can you get a DSCR loan on a condotel or non-warrantable condo here?

A non-warrantable condo is in a building that fails one of Fannie Mae's project rules, so the usual conventional loan will not work there.

Often not. About 8 in 10 condos for sale in oceanfront buildings in Horry County say the unit is in a rental program. All three DSCR program sheets we read exclude non-warrantable condos, and two of them also name condotels. Fannie Mae will not buy a loan in a building run like a hotel or with a rental pool. It is one of the two companies Congress created to buy mortgages from lenders.

How much down, what credit score and what else does a DSCR loan need?

A DSCR loan asks you for money down, a credit score, money in reserve and a plan for the first few years.

  • Money down15 to 25 percent on most purchases, and more on a condotel. Figures from our affiliated lender, BrickWood Mortgage, a separate company.
  • Credit scoreThe program sheets we read set credit score floors from 660 to 720.
  • ReservesMoney in the bank after closing, counted in months of the home's bill. The programs we read ask for 3 to 6 months, and one asks a first-time investor for 12.
  • A fee to pay off earlyMany DSCR loans charge a prepayment fee if you sell or refinance in the first few years. Ask your lender before a quick resale or a BRRRR refinance.
  • The price of the loanAccording to a loan officer at our preferred lender, with good credit and a 1.25 to 1.5 ratio, a DSCR loan is priced about like a conventional loan.

To see what the loan would cost on a home here, call us. We will work with our affiliated lender to give you solid numbers.

Who can get a DSCR loan: an LLC, a first-time investor, a Canadian?

Each of them can get a DSCR loan at some programs, with limits.

  • An LLCA DSCR loan can close in your name or in an LLC; the investor clients we work with usually close in an LLC. About 9 in 10 of them form a new LLC for each house. Our LLC guide covers the steps.
  • A first-time investorOne program we read takes a first-time investor with a 700 score, 12 months of reserves and a home they own and live in. Another does not take them, and a third asks them for a 1.00 ratio. All three refuse a first-time home buyer.
  • A CanadianCanadians usually need a DSCR loan unless they have a Social Security number and U.S. credit, or a U.S. work card. They need a Social Security number or a U.S. taxpayer ID number from the IRS, called a TIN. Some programs take only U.S. citizens and permanent residents. Our guide for Canadian buyers has the rest.
  • Someone who will use it tooPlan to stay in the home more than 14 days in the coming year? Then federal rules no longer treat it as a rental its owner does not live in. The business-loan exemption may not apply. Tell your lender early.

Common questions

DSCR loan FAQ

What is a good DSCR for a rental property?

Our target is 1.25, which leaves a fifth of the rent after the whole monthly bill. The three program sheets we read ask for 1.00 or more.

Can I get a DSCR loan in South Carolina without tax returns?

Yes. A DSCR loan is judged on the home's rent and its monthly bill. You sign that the home is a rental for business and that you will not live in it.

Can I get a DSCR loan if the rent does not cover the bill?

Sometimes. Our affiliated lender, BrickWood Mortgage, a separate company, starts writing DSCR loans at a ratio of 0.75. Below 1.00, you pay the rest of the bill each month.

Can I use Airbnb income for a DSCR loan in Myrtle Beach?

At some lenders. They count 75 to 80 percent of the nightly-rental estimate, and two of the three sheets we read take it only on a refinance. The city and the HOA must allow nightly rentals.

Does a DSCR loan need an appraisal?

Yes. The rent the lender counts is the appraiser's market rent, written on a rent schedule with the appraisal.

Can I get a DSCR loan in an LLC?

Yes. A DSCR loan can close in your name or in an LLC. The investor clients we work with usually close in an LLC.

Can a first-time home buyer get a DSCR loan?

Not at the three programs we read. A first-time investor who already owns a home can qualify at some programs.

Can Canadians get a DSCR loan?

A Canadian without a Social Security number and U.S. credit usually needs a DSCR loan, and needs a Social Security number or a U.S. taxpayer ID number (TIN). Some programs take only U.S. citizens and permanent residents.
