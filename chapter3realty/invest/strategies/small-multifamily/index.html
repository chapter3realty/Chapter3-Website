How to buy a duplex or fourplex in Myrtle Beach
Nearly 3 in 4 of Horry County's duplexes, triplexes and fourplexes stand within a mile of the ocean.

By Devin Day, Operations Officer · Reviewed by Tim Nash, Broker-in-Charge · Updated November 12, 2026

About 1,270 buildings of 2 to 4 homes stand in Horry County, most of them near the ocean, and few come up for sale.

The short answer

On November 10, 2026, 55 buildings listed as 2 to 4 units were for sale in Horry County. Their middle asking price was about $680,000.

In the past year, 58 sold at a middle price of about $520,000. Those within a quarter mile of the ocean sold for about $842,000.

How do you buy a duplex or fourplex in Myrtle Beach?

A duplex, triplex or fourplex is two, three or four homes in one building, bought in one purchase with one loan. You can live in one unit and rent the others, or rent them all. Buying one takes five steps:

  1. Pick the kind of building and the placeBuildings by the ocean cost more. Buildings away from the old beach blocks cost less. We send you the buildings for sale on the Grand Strand in the place you pick.
  2. Add up the rent rollThe rent roll is the rent from every unit added together. For one building, our agents run the number for each unit from current local leases and listings, at no cost, before you write the offer.
  3. Look at the whole buildingWe go through the expenses and any association documents with you before you write an offer.
  4. Choose how you will payIf you will live in one unit, you can buy it with an FHA, VA or conventional loan. If you will not, you can use an investor loan such as a DSCR loan. We introduce our affiliated lender, BrickWood Mortgage, a separate company, for the numbers.
  5. Offer and closeWe write the offer with you and work with you up to closing. The seller gives you the state's disclosure form before you both sign the contract, the same form a house comes with.

Where are the duplexes and fourplexes on the Grand Strand?

By the ocean. Horry County's tax records put about 1,270 parcels with a building under the land-use codes we read as two-, three- and four-family buildings, about 3,000 homes in all.

Nearly 3 in 4 of them stand within a mile of the ocean, in a straight line, and more than a third within a quarter mile. Of the 439 with a North Myrtle Beach mailing address, 354 stand within a quarter mile.

The beach towns

North Myrtle Beach's old beach towns

North Myrtle Beach was formed in 1968 from four beach towns: Cherry Grove, Ocean Drive, Crescent Beach and Windy Hill. About 340 of the city's 439 duplexes, triplexes and fourplexes stand on the old streets of these towns and of Ingram Beach beside them. The middle building in each stands about 160 to 340 yards from the ocean.

The shag is South Carolina's official state dance, by law since 1984, and the state Senate calls North Myrtle Beach its home. Each November, the Carolina Beach Music Awards weekend brings beach music bands to the stages of the shag clubs on Main Street.

Within a quarter mile of the ocean, buildings like these sold for about $842,000 at the middle in the past year.

Surfside Beach: Ocean Terrace and Ocean Pines

The Surfside Beach Fishing Pier is 814 feet long, all concrete, owned by the town and opened in 2024. Walking on it is free.

Ocean Terrace has 54 of these buildings and Ocean Pines 32. The middle building in each stands about 0.4 to 0.5 mile from the ocean. In Ocean Terrace, 20 of the 54 are taxed at the owner's 4 percent rate on all or part.

Garden City

The Garden City Pier was rebuilt after Hurricane Hugo destroyed the old one in 1989. At its far end, the rain shelter has a bandstand and a dance floor bar. People learn line dances there, out over the ocean.

Garden City, where the mail goes to Murrells Inlet, has 42 of these buildings, about 0.6 mile from the ocean at the middle. A quarter mile to a mile from the ocean, as here and in Surfside Beach, buildings sold for about $500,000 at the middle.

Away from the old beach blocks

Three large groups away from the old beach blocks cost less. The county's middle value for tax is about $230,000 to $255,000 in these groups, and about $447,000 to $685,000 in the largest beach groups. It is a figure for the tax bill, not a sale price.

A Place at the River, Little River

The old Little River waterfront is about a mile from A Place at the River, about 4 minutes by car. Each October, more than 200 vendors and two stages of live music set up there for Little River ShrimpFest.

All 22 buildings in A Place at the River are duplexes, about 2.7 miles from the ocean.

Tarpon Bay II, near Forestbrook

All 75 buildings in Tarpon Bay II are duplexes, the largest group of them away from the beach. More than 3 miles from the ocean, as here, buildings sold for about $245,000 at the middle, the least of any distance.

The neighborhood is near Forestbrook, outside any town limits, about 5 miles from the ocean in a straight line. The nearest beach is about 12 minutes by car.

Windtree Estates, Myrtle Beach

Windtree Estates is inside the City of Myrtle Beach, around 62nd to 67th Avenue North. From the middle of it, the beach is about a 15-minute walk. Grand Strand Medical Center, a hospital with a heart surgery program, is about 6 minutes by car.

All 49 buildings in Windtree Estates are fourplexes. 23 of the 49 are taxed at the owner's rate on all or part, the highest share of the large groups.

What does a duplex unit rent for in Myrtle Beach?

In most of Horry County, a 2‑bedroom unit in a building of 2 to 4 units rents for about $1,000 a month. With the utilities the tenant pays, the total is about $1,135.

  • Any unit in these buildingsAbout $950 a month, or about $1,100 with utilities.
  • A house, for comparisonAbout $1,400 a month in rent.
  • BedroomsAbout 6 in 10 rented units in these buildings have 2 bedrooms, and about 2 in 10 have one.

These are the rents tenants pay now, on leases of every age. Our rent prices guide has what landlords ask for a new lease, by ZIP code. South Carolina does not let a city or county limit rents on privately owned homes.

Is a duplex a good investment in Myrtle Beach?

Yes, if you will live in one unit. You can buy it with an FHA or VA home loan. On FHA, the other units' rent can count toward your income. Your own unit can be taxed at the owner's 4 percent rate.

If you will rent every unit, the whole building is taxed at 6 percent, and you borrow a smaller share of the price. Then two things count most: what each home costs, and where the building stands.

  • What each home costsPer home, the middle duplex sold for about $256,000. In the same 12 months, a resold house or other home sold for about $350,000 and a resold condo for about $215,000.
  • Where you buyAt the middle, a building within a quarter mile of the ocean sold for more than 3 times as much as one over 3 miles away.
  • How often one comes upAbout 4 in every 1,000 home sales in the county are one of these buildings. We send you each new one in your price range as it lists.

Can you live in one unit and rent the others? FHA and VA in Horry County

Yes. Living in one unit and renting the others is called house hacking. FHA insures loans on buildings of one to four units that the buyer will live in as their main home.

At least one buyer must move in within 60 days of signing and plan to live there for at least a year.

Loans that fit a building you live in

  • FHARent from the other units can count toward your income. With no rent history, the lender counts 75 percent of the lower of the appraiser's market rent and the lease.
  • VAA veteran can buy a home of up to 4 units with a VA loan, if the veteran will live in it. Our VA loan guide covers who qualifies.
  • ConventionalOn Freddie Mac's standard loans, the top loan on a 2 to 4 unit building you live in matches its top loan on a one-unit home. Freddie Mac's system must accept the loan. Some of its low down payment programs allow more on a one-unit home only.

When every buyer on the loan will live there, FHA asks the same smallest down payment on a duplex or fourplex as on a one-unit home. If a co-borrower, even a family member, will not live in a 2 to 4 unit building, FHA asks for a much larger down payment.

Our first-time buyer guide has FHA's other rules.

If you will not live there

On Freddie Mac's loans, an investor who lives elsewhere borrows a smaller share of the price. A DSCR loan is one choice: the lender qualifies the loan on the rent, not on your income.

The tax on a building you live in

In South Carolina, the home you live in is taxed on 4 percent of its value, once you apply. The rented units on the same property are taxed on 6 percent. Our property tax guide covers the application.

About 1 in 6 of these buildings is taxed at the owner's 4 percent rate on all or part of it.

What is the FHA self-sufficiency test for a triplex or fourplex?

For an FHA loan on a triplex or fourplex, the rents must cover the loan. FHA takes the rent from every unit, including yours, and subtracts 25 percent, or the appraiser's figure for empty months and upkeep if that is larger. The monthly loan payment, taxes and insurance must not be more than what is left.

Worked example: FHA's rent test on a fourplex

  1. Four 2-bedroom units at about $1,000 a month each$4,000
  2. Less 25 percent for empty months and upkeep−$1,000
  3. The rent FHA counts$3,000

FHA uses the appraiser's rent estimates, so your building's figures can differ.

The lender must also see savings equal to three months of the full monthly payment, taxes and insurance, left after closing. A duplex does not take the test.

Can you Airbnb a duplex in Myrtle Beach?

Not on an FHA loan. On an FHA loan for a 2 to 4 unit building, the buyer signs HUD's form 92561. On it, the buyer promises not to rent any part of the building for under 30 days, or with hotel services, while the FHA loan is in place.

Each town on the Grand Strand has its own rules for nightly rentals. Our short-term rental rules guide covers them. Renting to summer workers by the season is in our J-1 rentals guide.

Common questions

Duplex and fourplex FAQ

What is the FHA loan limit for a duplex in Horry County?

$693,050 for 2026. The limit is the largest loan FHA insures on a duplex here, not the price you pay. It is higher for a triplex or a fourplex.

Can I use a VA loan on a duplex?

Yes. A veteran can buy a building of up to four units with a VA loan if the veteran will live in one of them.

How much down payment does a duplex need if I live in it?

On an FHA loan, the same smallest down payment as a one-unit home, when every buyer on the loan will live there. Our affiliated lender, BrickWood Mortgage, a separate company, gives the numbers for one building.

Is a fourplex a commercial property?

Not for an FHA home loan. FHA's home loan programs cover buildings of one to four homes that the buyer lives in.

Does the seller of a duplex have to give a disclosure form?

Yes. The seller of a home of one to four units must give the buyer the state's disclosure form. A duplex or fourplex comes with the same form as a house.

Can a South Carolina city cap the rent on my units?

No. South Carolina does not let a city or county limit rents on privately owned homes.
