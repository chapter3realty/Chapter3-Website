Moving to Myrtle Beach

Relocating to Myrtle Beach? You can buy before you move.
How to buy from home, and every moving guide by question.

By Devin Day, Operations Officer · Reviewed by Tim Nash, Broker-in-Charge · Updated November 12, 2026

When relocating to Myrtle Beach from another state, you can sign, inspect and close on a home from where you live now.

The short answer

If you are relocating to Myrtle Beach, you can buy a home before you move. The offer can be signed electronically, and a South Carolina attorney may close by mail, so you do not have to be at the closing.

In the latest IRS count, about 15,000 households moved into Horry County in one year, and about 9,100 moved out. In Myrtle Beach, a January day averages a high of about 56 degrees. An owner who lives in the home here typically pays about $944 a year in property tax. Our guide to why so many people move here gives the four reasons, with numbers.

Can I buy a house in Myrtle Beach before I move?

We do most of the work here while you stay where you are. These are the seven steps, in order.

  1. Pick the towns first.Tell us how you want to live, and we narrow the search to the towns that fit. Start with our 13 towns near Myrtle Beach, compared.
  2. See the houses.We tour the homes you like for you, and we tell you what the listing photos do not show. If you want to see a house yourself, we plan the showings around your trip.
  3. Know the insurance before you offer.When you buy with us, we get an insurance quote for the address before you make an offer.
  4. Offer from home.Under South Carolina law, an electronic signature on a contract has the same effect as one in ink. You can sign the offer from anywhere.
  5. Inspect.A home inspection is the buyer's job in South Carolina. We line up the inspection.
  6. Choose the closing attorney.Every South Carolina home closing must be supervised by a South Carolina attorney. We work with several closing attorneys and can recommend them once we are working with you.
  7. Sign the closing papers at home.In a closing by mail, the papers are sent to you to sign and return. The attorney must be reachable by phone or email to explain them. You sign the deed or mortgage before a notary where you live, with two witnesses, and it is filed here. A lender may set its own signing rules.

You will not be the only buyer doing this from afar. About 3 in 10 homes sold in Horry County in the latest year went to a buyer whose tax bill goes to another state or country. For condos it was more than half.

The market and the steps to buy a house in South Carolina are in our buying guide. Still own a home where you live now? Sell first or buy first shows the three ways to time the two sales.

Common questions

Questions about relocating

Can I sign my closing papers on a video call?

Not with a South Carolina notary. South Carolina law has no remote (video) notarization. Ask your closing attorney how you will sign.

Who picks the closing attorney?

You do, when you buy with a home loan for personal or family use. The lender must ask which attorney you want for the closing and follow your choice.

Which state do most out-of-state buyers come from?

North Carolina, by far. Our count of Horry County deeds over one year found 916 buyers with a tax bill in North Carolina. New York was next, with 438.

Can I move to Myrtle Beach before I have a job here?

Yes. Most of our buyers who move here find work within a month or two. That is our clients' experience, not a promise.
