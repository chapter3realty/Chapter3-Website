Zones, choice programs, and registration

Schools, for buyers moving
to the Grand Strand.

By Devin Day, Operations Officer & licensed MLO, NMLS 2721275 · Reviewed by Timmy Fredrick Nash, Broker-in-Charge · Updated September 2, 2026

How Horry County school attendance zones are set and changed, the choice and charter options, the application-based academies, and when you qualify for in-state tuition, for buyers moving here.

The direct answer

How school zones work here

Horry County Schools serves all of Horry County: Myrtle Beach, North Myrtle Beach, Conway, Carolina Forest, Socastee, Surfside Beach, Little River, Aynor, Loris, and the Horry side of Murrells Inlet. Georgetown County School District serves Pawleys Island, Litchfield, and the Georgetown side of Murrells Inlet. Every address is assigned one elementary, one middle, and one high school.

Horry County publishes an address lookup tool. Georgetown County does not, so you call the district. Both change zones when schools open or fill, so confirm the assignment for the exact address before you write an offer that depends on it.

How we handle this

Why we do not rate schools

Fair housing law prohibits steering, which means directing a buyer toward or away from an area based on a protected characteristic. Courts have treated an agent's opinions about schools as evidence of that, because phrases like “good schools” and “poor schools” can be used as code for who lives somewhere. That is why we do not rank schools, repeat ratings, or tell you which zone to want.

That is not a dodge. South Carolina publishes a report card for every public school, both districts publish their own data, and the people who can confirm a zone will answer the phone. Everything below is where that information lives and what to ask for. You reach your own conclusion, which is the only one that matters anyway.

Step one

Check the exact address, not the neighborhood

Horry County

The district runs an Attendance Zone Lookup that takes a street address and returns the assigned schools. The district's own instruction is to use it if you are new to the area and unsure, and to call the technology helpdesk at 843-488-6825 if questions remain. The tool is address-based, so a house under construction may not return a result until the lot has a posted address.

The district is organized into nine attendance areas: Aynor, Carolina Forest, Conway, Green Sea Floyds, Loris, Myrtle Beach, North Myrtle Beach, Socastee, and St. James. It publishes a feeder chart showing which elementary schools lead to which middle and high schools, on its Our Schools page.

Georgetown County

There is no public address lookup. Call the district office at 843-436-7000, or the school serving the area, and ask for the zoned school for that street address. Ask for it by email so you have it in writing. The district groups its schools into four areas, and the one covering Pawleys Island, Litchfield, and the Waccamaw Neck is the Waccamaw group.

One practical note for both districts: buses. Horry County does not transport students across attendance boundaries, and Georgetown County provides bus service to students living more than a mile and a half from their zoned school. If a child is close to a boundary, ask the district's transportation office what that means for your street before you commit.

This comes up in real transactions. One family moved here because their oldest child was starting at Coastal Carolina, and their younger child still needed a school. They knew which zone they wanted, and the houses they liked sat near its edge, some inside it and some not. We checked each address against the district lookup, told them which was which, and gave no opinion on any school. They picked with the full picture, and the younger child enrolled where the family intended. That is the service: not an opinion, a confirmed address.

What to check

Zones change, and this area is building schools

Horry County is one of the faster-growing counties in the country and the district builds to keep up. When two elementary schools opened in Carolina Forest, the board redrew the lines for three existing elementary schools and published the list of affected subdivisions by name. That process took roughly four months from first draft to final vote, and the new lines took effect the following August. Another replacement school is in the district's capital plan.

Two things follow. First, the school names in a home's advertisement are not a district record. Agents and websites copy them forward for years without checking. Second, a zone you confirm in March can change for the school year after next. If a specific school assignment is the reason you are buying a particular house, confirm it with the district in writing, and understand you are confirming today's line.

Capacity affects transfers, not enrollment

Each year Horry County publishes a list of schools at or above 95 percent of designed capacity, which the district calls Category 1 or Red Zone. A student zoned for one of those schools always enrolls there. The designation closes that school to elective transfers from outside its zone, with no exceptions for siblings or hardship. If your plan depends on transferring in, check the current list first. The list is republished every spring on the district's student transfer page.

The official data

Read the state's report card yourself

South Carolina publishes an annual report card for every public school and district at screportcards.com. Each school's page carries an overall rating on a five-step scale, along with the underlying measures, and the site lets you compare schools and download the raw data files. It is a state publication, its methodology is public, and it is the source both districts point to.

1Type the school's name in the Search for Your School box, or choose View Report Cards and browse by district.
2The school's page shows the overall rating on the state's five-step scale, with the measures behind it: academics, student progress, graduation rate, college and career readiness, teacher qualifications, and the school climate surveys.
3Use Compare Schools to put schools side by side, or open a district to see every school in Horry or Georgetown at once.
4Report cards refresh each fall, so check the school year printed on the page before you rely on it.

We deliberately do not reprint any school's rating here, including the state's. Repeating a rating in our voice adopts it, and that is the line between giving you information and steering you. Look the schools up, decide what matters to you, and ask the district about anything the report card does not answer.

If you are not sure what to look at, the useful move is to decide first what you need. The district can answer directly on class sizes, a specific special education service, a language, a music or arts program, a sport, distance from the house, bus eligibility, and start times. None of those are on a rating scale. Both districts list every school with contact numbers: Horry County and Georgetown County.

Beyond the zoned school

Choice programs, transfers, and application windows

Horry County optional secondary programs

The district runs a set of application-based programs alongside the ten base high schools. They include the Academy for Arts, Science and Technology, the Academy for Technology and Academics, Scholars Academy, Early College High School, and a Middle College at St. James. There is also International Baccalaureate at three high schools, dual-credit programs with Horry-Georgetown Technical College, and technical scholars tracks in fields such as nursing, welding, and cybersecurity. Each publishes its own program guide and admission rubric.

Two mechanics matter to a family moving in. The application runs through the district's student portal in a short window in early May, so a mid-year arrival may be looking at the following year. A student accepted into one of these programs is not classed as a transfer, which means transportation is provided; an ordinary elective transfer gets none. Program details and the application link are on the district's high school programs page.

Transfers

Horry County accepts in-district transfer applications through June for the following year, approves them one year at a time, provides no transportation, and refuses transfers into schools at or above capacity. Students living in South Carolina but outside Horry County can apply to attend, with tuition set annually from the average per-pupil tax revenue. The figure for the most recent published year was $6,713, handled through the office of student affairs at 843-488-6770.

Georgetown County is different in kind, not degree. Its attendance zones were set under a 1970 federal court order and a later consent decree with the Department of Justice, and the district describes them as inflexible. Transfers are limited to the narrow categories written into district policy, and the application window closes in the spring. Ask the district rather than assuming.

Georgetown County magnet programs

The district operates five magnet programs from kindergarten through twelfth grade, all in the Carvers Bay area in the western part of the county rather than on the Waccamaw Neck. Any student living in the district may apply, there is no cost, transportation is provided on request, and seats are assigned by a random computerized process among timely applications. The window has typically run in April; confirm the current year with the district.

Other options

Charter, private, virtual, and home school

Charter schools are tuition-free public schools that operate outside a district's attendance zones, with their own applications and, when demand exceeds seats, a lottery. Several operate in Horry County and more have been approved, so the current roster is worth checking directly rather than trusting a list. South Carolina authorizes charters through statewide authorizers as well as districts; the state Department of Education keeps the charter schools program page with the accredited list.

Private schools are outside both districts and set their own admission timelines, which often close earlier in the year than public registration. South Carolina also runs a scholarship program that puts state funds in an account families can spend on approved education expenses. It has income limits, a participant cap, and a per-student cap, and its rules have changed more than once, so read the current terms on the state's school choice page before counting on it.

Home schooling is legal statewide under three different oversight options, and the state runs a free online public school program for full-time and supplemental courses. Both are described on the same state pages. We have no view on which of these is right for your family; we are listing them so the choice is visible.

Enrolling

What to bring, and the dates that bind

Horry County enrollment starts with an online pre-enrollment form for any student who was not in a district school at the end of the previous year. It asks you to upload four documents: a parent or guardian identification card, proof of residency, the student's birth certificate, and the student's immunization records. The school then emails you a code to finish the second stage. Registration for the coming year opens well before the school year starts.

Age cutoffs are firm: a child must be five years old by September 1 to enter kindergarten and six by September 1 for first grade. The four-year-old child development program has its own application, requires Horry County residence, and adds a Medicaid card or proof of income to the document list. It assigns seats by priority points and a developmental screening rather than first come first served. Applications completed after mid-July go to a waiting list.

Georgetown County requires online registration for every student and asks for two forms of residency verification for a new student, plus the birth certificate and current immunization record. Returning students need one residency document.

Immunization is worth handling early. South Carolina requires a state certificate, and an out-of-state record usually has to be converted by a South Carolina provider or the health department before a school will accept it. That conversion takes an appointment, so it is a thing to start before the truck arrives rather than the week school opens.

If you have a college student

In-state tuition takes twelve months

South Carolina charges in-state tuition to students whose family has been domiciled in the state for at least twelve continuous months before the first day of class, and the burden of proving it sits with the student. Moving in August does not make the fall semester in-state. There are exceptions, including one for people employed full time by a South Carolina employer, and each institution applies the state regulation itself. Coastal Carolina University and Horry-Georgetown Technical College both publish their residency pages, and the state Commission on Higher Education publishes the regulation. If a child is close to college age, ask the school's residency officer how your move date lands against their deadline.

Common questions

Myrtle Beach schools FAQ

How do I find out which school an address is zoned for in Horry County?

Use the district's Attendance Zone Lookup, which takes a street address and returns the assigned elementary, middle, and high school. If the address is new construction it may not return a result until the lot has a posted address. The district's helpdesk at 843-488-6825 answers zone questions, and the school's front office can confirm before you write an offer.

Do school attendance zones change in Horry County?

Yes, and recently. When two elementary schools opened in Carolina Forest, the district moved the lines for three existing elementary schools and published the list of subdivisions that changed. The rezoning took about four months from first draft to board vote, and the new lines took effect the following August. Never rely on the school names in a home's advertisement, and never assume this year's zone is next year's.

Which school district serves Pawleys Island and Murrells Inlet?

It depends on the county line. Horry County Schools serves all of Horry County, which includes Myrtle Beach, North Myrtle Beach, Conway, Carolina Forest, Socastee, Surfside Beach, and the Horry side of Murrells Inlet. Georgetown County School District serves Pawleys Island, Litchfield, and the Georgetown side of Murrells Inlet.

What documents do I need to register a child for school in Horry County?

Pre-enrollment is online and asks you to upload four things: a parent or guardian identification card, proof of residency, the student's birth certificate, and the student's immunization records. The school then sends a code to finish registration. A child must be five by September 1 for kindergarten and six by September 1 for first grade.

Can my child attend a school we are not zoned for?

Sometimes. Horry County accepts in-district transfer applications through June, does not provide transportation for elective transfers, and denies transfers into schools at or above capacity. Its optional secondary programs are separate: students accepted there are not transfer students and do get transportation. Georgetown County's zones are set under a federal court order, so its transfers are limited to the narrow categories in district policy.
