The short answer

Where are the homes for sale with a pool in Myrtle Beach?

A house near the ocean is the most likely to have its own pool. About 1 in 3 houses for sale with a pool is east of US 17, the highway along the coast. Of all houses for sale, about 1 in 6 is.

Surfside Beach and the Garden City area have the highest share. In each, 1 house in 4 or more for sale has its own pool.

How much does a house with a pool cost in Myrtle Beach?

A private pool comes mostly with the top of the market. Over $1,000,000, more than half of houses for sale have one.

At the same size, a house with a pool still asks far more. Houses of 1,500 to 1,999 square feet ask about $515,000 with a pool and $350,000 without. At 3,000 to 3,999 square feet it is about $1,120,000 against $775,000.

That gap is not the price of a pool. Houses with a pool are more often on the water, about 1 in 5 against 1 in 7.

Should you buy a house with a pool or use a community pool?

A house with its own pool asks more than twice as much in the middle as one in a neighborhood with a shared pool.

  • Your own poolSwim when you like, and keep it up yourself

    You pay for the chemicals, the repairs, the fence and the power for the pump. A pool pump can use as much as $310 of electricity a year.

    About 1 in 4 of these houses also lists a hot tub.

  • A neighborhood poolSwim down the street, and the HOA keeps it up

    The middle homeowners association (HOA) fee in these neighborhoods is about $105 a month. The middle asking price of their houses is about $435,000.

    The state requires a yearly permit for the pool, and a certified pool operator checks it at least three times a week while it is open.

What does a pool cost to keep up in Horry County?

A pool adds power, care and insurance to your bills. A pool you add later also raises your property tax.

  • Power for the pumpThe pump can be a home's second largest user of energy. An ENERGY STAR certified in-ground pump uses about 20 percent less and saves about $50 a year.
  • Chemicals and careEach pool service company charges its own price for weekly care and chemicals. We get a quote for the house you want before you make an offer.
  • InsuranceTell your insurer about the pool before you buy. A pool can raise the premium, and the insurer can ask whether it is covered or locked when no one is using it, and what fence you have. An umbrella policy adds liability coverage above your homeowners policy.
  • Property taxA pool that is already there is part of the home's value for tax. If you add a pool after you buy, the county adds its full value to your home's value. There is a 15 percent limit on how much the county can raise a home's value at a countywide revaluation. It does not cover the new pool in its first taxed year. Our property tax page has the rates.

Do you need a fence around a pool in Horry County?

Yes. Outside city limits, Horry County requires a fence or other barrier with gates around a pool. The county uses the 2021 International Swimming Pool and Spa Code.

Inside a city such as Myrtle Beach or North Myrtle Beach, the city's own building official issues the pool permit. South Carolina has no statewide pool code, so each city decides its own rule. We ask the city's building office about the house you want.

We read the county's pool fence form, which quotes the code, and drew its rule:

  • Covers instead of a fenceA hot tub with a safety cover that locks needs no fence. Neither does a pool with a powered safety cover that meets the safety standard.
  • Instead of door alarmsThe code also allows a safety cover on the pool, tested and labeled to the safety standard. The county can approve another protection, such as doors that close and latch by themselves.
  • A new poolIt needs a county permit and inspections, and no one may swim in it until the county approves them.

The rule is there for small children. Most children who drown in a pool or spa drown at a home, the Consumer Product Safety Commission reports.

Do home inspectors check pools in South Carolina?

Not unless you ask. The standard South Carolina home inspectors follow comes from the American Society of Home Inspectors. It does not require them to inspect a pool, a hot tub or a fence.

You and the inspector can agree to add the pool. The inspector must tell you before the inspection what it will not cover.

Common questions

Pool homes FAQ

Are there homes with a pool under $300,000 in Myrtle Beach?

Few. On the day we counted, 5 of the 575 houses for sale in Horry County at $300,000 or less had their own pool. 168 houses at that price were in a neighborhood with a shared pool.

Does a pool add value to a home in Myrtle Beach?

On asking prices, yes. At every size we checked, houses with a pool ask more than houses without one. Part of that gap comes from where they are: nearer the beach and more often on the water.

Do pool homes in Myrtle Beach allow short-term rentals?

More often than other houses. About 28 percent of houses for sale with their own pool say short-term rentals are allowed, against about 12 percent of all houses.

Are pool homes near Myrtle Beach in gated communities?

About 1 in 5 houses for sale with their own pool is in a gated community, against about 1 in 9 of all houses.

Do townhouses near Myrtle Beach have a pool?

Many share one. Half of the 298 townhouses for sale in Horry County, 149, are in a community with a shared pool. Their middle asking price is about $270,000.
