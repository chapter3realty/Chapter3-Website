New home builders in Myrtle Beach, ranked by homes sold
The 15 that sold 50 or more homes in Horry County in a year, where they build and what their homes cost.

Every builder that sold 50 or more new homes in a year, the towns it builds in, what its homes are like and what they cost.

The short answer

D.R. Horton and Lennar build the most new homes around Myrtle Beach. Together they sold about 45 percent of the 5,277 new homes sold to their first owners in Horry County in a year.

Ryan Homes, Pulte Homes and Meritage Homes come next, and 15 builders sold 50 or more. About 3 in 4 of the new homes are inland, with Conway, Longs and Myrtle Beach addresses.

Which builders build the most new homes around Myrtle Beach?

D.R. Horton sold the most, about 1 in 4 of the new homes. Each bar is one builder, with the middle price its buyers paid:

The five largest sold about two-thirds of the new homes. A builder's new home sold for about $335,000 in the middle, against about $306,000 for a home resold by its owner.

Five more sellers are companies that each sell new homes in one community, such as Waterbridge SC LLC in Waterbridge. Their deeds name no builder, so we count them apart. Together they sold 239 homes, mostly with Myrtle Beach and Little River addresses.

This page counts builders that sell new homes in their own communities. To build on a lot you own, you hire a builder under a construction contract. To buy the lot first, see our guide to buying land in Horry County. The license check in the last section applies to that builder too, and our guide gives how long each kind of build takes.

Who are the other large builders, and who owns them?

Several builders here sell under one name and sign deeds under another, and some have been bought by other companies. The name on the deed is the company that sold you the house, and it stands behind the warranty:

  • Mungo HomesIts busiest community is Arcadia in Myrtle Beach, and about half its homes have Conway addresses. It is part of Clayton Properties Group, a Berkshire Hathaway company, and the deeds name Clayton Properties Group.
  • Great Southern Homes, now Stanley MartinMostly Conway addresses. This South Carolina builder became part of Stanley Martin Homes in 2026, and Stanley Martin now lists its homes for sale. Before the sale, it gave 1 year on materials and workmanship, 2 years on plumbing, wiring and heating and air, and 10 years on the structure.
  • Starlight HomesSpring Grove in Aynor, Andalusia in Loris and Bayview in Conway, at some of the lowest prices on the chart. It is an Ashton Woods company. Ashton Woods also sells homes here under its own name, mostly with Little River addresses.
  • Dream Finders HomesMostly Conway addresses. It came here by buying the homebuilding business of H&H Constructors of Fayetteville, and its count includes those homes. It gives 1 year on workmanship and up to 10 years on the structure, and owns a mortgage company, Jet HomeLoans.
  • Beazer HomesIt became part of Dream Finders Homes in 2026. Before that, its annual report said it owned no mortgage company and named several lenders for each community. It gave 1 to 2 years on workmanship and up to 10 years on certain structural failures.
  • Toll BrothersThe highest prices on the chart. Its homes are around North Myrtle Beach, Murrells Inlet and Myrtle Beach, including SayeBrook. Many of its buyers get a limited 10-year warranty on the structure, and it owns a mortgage company.

Why some builders are hard to find in the listings

Many builders' homes never reach the MLS, the database brokers share to list homes for sale. Ryan Homes sold 477 homes here and had 1 listed that day. Lennar sold 967 and had 60. We ask those builders for their homes for sale directly.

What should you ask a builder before you sign?

Seven things. We check each one with you before you sign a builder's contract:

  1. The licenseSouth Carolina requires a state license for anyone who builds a home for someone else on a job over $5,000. It can be a residential builder license or a general contractor license. Building without one is a misdemeanor. We check the builder's license with the state.
  2. The warranty, in writingWe get the builder's warranty booklet. It says what each part of the house is covered for, for how long, and who you call.
  3. The HOAWe read the homeowners association (HOA) papers with you. They give the monthly fee, what it pays for, and when the builder hands the HOA to the owners.
  4. The lot premium and the upgradesA lot premium is an extra charge for a particular lot. Upgrades are the features you add, which builders call options. We compare both with the base price. Our guide lists what else the price leaves out, including the first full tax bill.
  5. The incentive and its lenderSome incentives come only with the builder's own lender. We ask the builder in writing what it offers, and our guide covers whether to use the builder's lender.
  6. Your own inspectionA new house can still have mistakes in it. We set up an inspection before you close, and our guide covers when to inspect a new home.
  7. Your own agent, from the first visitTell us before your first visit or online sign-up. We register with the builder as your agent. Our guide explains why the first visit matters.

Common questions

New home builders FAQ

Which home builder in Myrtle Beach is best?

We rank builders by homes sold, not by quality. D.R. Horton sold the most new homes in Horry County. To judge one builder, check the seven things in the last section of this page, starting with its license and its written warranty.

Is D.R. Horton or Lennar bigger in Myrtle Beach?

D.R. Horton. It sold 1,397 new homes in Horry County in those 12 months, against 967 for Lennar. Lennar's homes sold for less in the middle, about $258,000 against about $325,000.

Is Del Webb part of Pulte?

Yes. Del Webb is PulteGroup's brand for active adults. Pulte's count on this page includes the homes it sold at Del Webb North Myrtle Beach.

Why can't I find Ryan Homes in the listings?

Few of its homes are listed in the MLS, the database brokers share. Ryan Homes sold 477 homes here in those 12 months and had 1 listed on the day we counted. We ask the builder for its homes for sale directly.

Who is Clayton Properties Group?

The Berkshire Hathaway company that owns Mungo Homes. It is the seller on the deeds for Mungo's homes in Horry County.

Does a home builder need a license in South Carolina?

Yes, for a home job over $5,000. Building without one is a misdemeanor.
