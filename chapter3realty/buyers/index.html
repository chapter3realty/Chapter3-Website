How We Help

An agent and a lender, under one roof

Most buyers hire a real estate agent, then go find a lender somewhere else, and spend the whole deal hoping the two talk to each other. At Chapter3 Realty they already do. We are a brokerage with our own mortgage team, so the person showing you homes and the person handling your loan share an office.

That means fewer surprises, faster answers, and a team of experts that are on the same page. Below are the guides we point buyers to most. Find the one that fits you and start there.
