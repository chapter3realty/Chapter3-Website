Del Webb North Myrtle Beach is a newer 55+ neighborhood of mostly one-story houses on short streets around ponds.

The short answer

Del Webb North Myrtle Beach has about 400 homes built so far, and its builder is still selling new ones. At least one person in each household must be 55 or older.

Owners share a clubhouse with an indoor lap pool, an outdoor pool and a fitness center. There are also courts for pickleball and bocce, and walking trails.

What are the homes like in Del Webb North Myrtle Beach?

Most homes in Del Webb North Myrtle Beach are one story, so there are no stairs. They have two or three bedrooms and a garage, and most are about 1,400 to 2,700 square feet.

The homes are on short streets around ponds, with the clubhouse, pools and courts in the middle of the neighborhood.

New homes come with:

  • Fabric hurricane covers for the windows
  • A warranty on the structure for 10 years, which a later owner keeps
  • Sprinklers in every yard

What is there to do in Del Webb North Myrtle Beach?

The clubhouse in Del Webb North Myrtle Beach, also called the amenity center, has a gathering room, an arts and crafts room and a fitness center.

  • Indoor lap poolSwim laps all year
  • Outdoor poolWith a beach-style walk-in entry
  • PickleballCourts beside the clubhouse
  • Walking trailsPaths through the neighborhood

The city's community center is about a minute away by car. On weekdays you can play indoor pickleball there for a few dollars a day. Behind it are outdoor pickleball courts that are free and always open.

The center also has fitness classes, such as gentle yoga, that you pay for by the class. The city's recreation department runs trips for adults too, such as a monthly day trip around the Carolinas and bus tours.

Where is Del Webb North Myrtle Beach?

Del Webb North Myrtle Beach is inside the city of North Myrtle Beach, about a mile from the ocean. The beach is on the other side of US 17, the main highway.

In our experience, rush hour adds 5 to 10 minutes to the drive times on the map.

Open Del Webb North Myrtle Beach in Google Maps

For an emergency, the North Strand emergency room is closer than the hospital, about 5 minutes by car. It is open day and night.

What is North Myrtle Beach like?

North Myrtle Beach has a wide sand beach lined with beach houses. The city keeps hundreds of paths to the beach and dozens of public parking lots, so you can find a place near the sand.

  • Beach parkingFree early in the morning, in the evening and all winter.
  • Golf cartsAllowed with a state permit sticker, on roads of 35 mph or less, from sunrise to sunset.
  • Dogs on the beachAlways on a leash. From mid-May to Labor Day, no dogs on the beach from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m.

To reach the nearest city beach parking lot, you drive a short way along US 17. Golf carts may only cross US 17, so plan to take your car to the beach.

These places in town are close by car.

  • Central ParkA city park with a paved walking path and tennis courts. About 2 minutes.
  • Ingram DunesA city trail through an old seaside forest with tall dunes. About 4 minutes.
  • Main StreetShops, places to eat and free concerts on summer Thursdays. About 7 minutes.
  • Barefoot LandingDozens of shops and restaurants by the Intracoastal Waterway. About 10 minutes.
  • Cherry Grove PierWalk out over the ocean or fish from it. About 15 minutes.
  • Heritage ShoresCity boardwalks and docks over the salt marsh. About 20 minutes.

What does a home in Del Webb North Myrtle Beach cost?

In the last year, new homes from the builder in Del Webb North Myrtle Beach usually sold for about $635,000. Homes resold by their owners sold for about $535,000.

The homeowners association (HOA) fee is about $315 a month, and it includes lawn care, internet and a TV package.

If you live in a new home at that price, the property tax is a little under $3,000 a year, city tax included. See how a home you live in is taxed in North Myrtle Beach.

Do you need flood insurance in Del Webb North Myrtle Beach?

You probably will not need flood insurance in Del Webb North Myrtle Beach, because every lot is outside the high-risk flood zone on the government's flood map. If you want a policy, it usually costs about $600 a year.

What are the pros and cons of living in Del Webb North Myrtle Beach?

Del Webb North Myrtle Beach has new homes about a mile from the ocean, and the builder is still selling them.

Pros

  • The beach is about 4 minutes away by car.
  • The homes are new, built since 2022.
  • If you sell, the next owner keeps the builder's warranty on the structure.
  • The city's community center, with indoor pickleball, is about a minute away by car.

Cons

  • The builder is still building here. Ask which streets are finished before you choose a lot.
  • Golf carts may cross US 17 but not drive along it.
  • The airport is about 35 minutes away by car.

Can you rent out a home in Del Webb North Myrtle Beach?

Not by the week. Renters in Del Webb North Myrtle Beach must stay at least 30 days.

Common questions

Del Webb North Myrtle Beach FAQ

How much is the HOA fee in Del Webb North Myrtle Beach?

The HOA fee is about $315 a month. It includes lawn care, internet and a TV package.

Is Del Webb North Myrtle Beach finished?

Not yet. The builder is still building and selling new houses in the neighborhood today.

Can someone under 55 live in Del Webb North Myrtle Beach?

Yes, a younger husband or wife can live in the home, as long as one person in the household is 55 or older.

Is Del Webb North Myrtle Beach inside the city limits?

Yes. It is inside North Myrtle Beach, so owners pay a city tax on top of the county and school tax.

Who builds the homes in Del Webb North Myrtle Beach?

Pulte builds them and sells them under its Del Webb name. Most of its homes here are one story, with a garage.
