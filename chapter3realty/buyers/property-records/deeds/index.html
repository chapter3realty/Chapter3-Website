Horry County property records

Horry County land records: how to search deeds, liens and plats
Deeds, mortgages and liens back to late 1979, free to search by name.

By Devin Day, Operations Officer · Reviewed by Tim Nash, Broker-in-Charge · Updated November 14, 2026

The Horry County land records search is free, needs no account and takes three steps. Reading the deed itself takes a trip to Conway or a copy.

The short answer

Horry County land records are free to search online at the Register of Deeds' search, with no account. Choose Name and type the last name first, like SMITH JOHN. The list shows the deeds, mortgages, liens and plats recorded under that name.

The list is online, but the papers are not. You read them in Conway, order a copy by mail from $8, or send us the address and we pull them free.

How do I search Horry County land records?

Open the Horry County deed search

The search belongs to the Horry County Register of Deeds. Chapter3 is a real estate brokerage, not the county.

  1. Open the search and accept the county's terms.

    Click "I accept the conditions above." You then search as a guest, with no account and no fee.

  2. Choose Name, and type the last name first.

    Type SMITH JOHN for John Smith. Use at least 3 letters, and click Search.

  3. Read the list, then click a row.

    Each row is one paper under that name. Click it for the date, the kind of paper, both names, the book and page, and the price. It also gives a short description of the land.

The deed itself is not online.

Send us the address. We pull the deed, the plat, any liens and the HOA papers for you, free.

Are Horry County land records free?

Yes. Searching the Horry County deed index costs nothing and needs no account. The county charges only for copies and for recording a paper.

The index is online, but the documents are not. Horry County does not show images of recorded documents on the internet. You view and print them at the public viewing area at 1301 Second Avenue in Conway.

The free search still tells you a lot. It shows that a paper exists, when it was recorded, who signed it, where it is filed and what land it covers.

The county also has an app called "Land Records". It is a parcel map, not the deed search, and it asks you to sign in with a county account. Our GIS map guide shows how to use it.

How do I get a copy of my deed in Horry County?

You can get a copy of a Horry County deed three ways: at the office in Conway, by mail, or through us.

  • At the office. View and print the deed in the public viewing area.
  • By mail. Send the county's copy request form with a check or money order to the Register of Deeds, PO Box 470, Conway, SC 29528. A deed is $8 for the first 4 pages.
  • Through us. Free, by email, from the address alone.

How do I search Horry County deeds by name?

Choose Name in the Horry County deed search, type the last name first, then the first name, and click Search. These are the four boxes that matter.

  1. 1

    Name: the last name first, then the first name.

    Search every spelling too, with and without a middle initial.

  2. 2

    Show Names: leave it on All.

    All finds the name on either side of a paper. On a deed, the grantor is the seller and the grantee is the buyer.

  3. 3

    From and To Record Date: narrow a long list.

    The form fills in a From date of January 1, 1984, but names reach back to late 1979. For an older deed, set the From date earlier. A shorter range cuts a common name down to a few rows.

  4. 4

    Document Type: pick one kind of paper.

    The four groups are deeds, mortgages, liens and plats. The table below names the exact types to pick.

A company is searched the same way, by the name on the paper. The index also searches by book and page, by price range, by recording date and by the legal description or memo.

Can I search Horry County deeds by address?

Go from the address to the owner's name or the deed's book and page first. A deed's index entry describes the land by its lot and subdivision, so two steps are quicker.

  1. 1

    Find the lot on the county's parcel map.

    On the Horry County GIS map, search the address and click the lot. Its card shows the owner and the Deed Book and Deed Page. The map asks you to sign in with a county account.

  2. 2

    Open the deed.

    Type the book and page into the box below, or search the owner's name in the deed search.

To see every lot in a subdivision, use the Legal Description/Memo search and type the subdivision's name. Almost every deed, 999 in 1,000, carries that short description of its lot.

How do I look up a deed by book and page in Horry County?

Type the deed book and page below, and the county's page for that deed opens. A book and page is printed on the county map's parcel card and in the index entry of every paper.

Opens the county's page for that deed book and page in a new tab, with no terms screen.

For a plat, mortgage or lien book, use the county's Book/Page search. Pick the book type there: D for deeds, MTG for mortgages, LN for liens and P for plats.

Search the owner's name, then pick the document type. The Horry County index has more than 140 types. These are the ones a buyer or an owner needs.

What the deed index does not hold

  • Court judgments. The Clerk of Court keeps them. A judgment entered there is a lien on the debtor's land in the county for ten years. The courts' Public Index searches Horry County's cases.
  • Property tax. Whether a home's tax is paid shows on its county tax bill. Our Horry County property tax guide explains the rates.
  • HOA dues owed. An association's claim shows only once it is recorded as a lien. Our HOA liens guide explains what follows.

How do I read a Horry County deed?

Start with the deed's entry in the index. It holds the facts most readers need, and you can see it free, without a trip to Conway.

Deeds that show $0

We read every one of the 30,339 deeds Horry County recorded in the 12 months to September 2026. 11,075 of them show $0 or no price.

You cannot tell from a blank price what was paid, and the index does not say why it is blank. State law exempts some deeds from the deed fee, such as an estate's deed of distribution. The full list is under recording fees.

For what homes sold for, see what homes sold for by area, worked out from the county's deeds that show a price.

What the deed itself adds

  • The kind of deed. In a warranty deed, the seller promises to defend the title against anyone's lawful claim. A tax deed goes to the buyer at a sale for unpaid property tax, once the owner's time to redeem it runs out.
  • The owner and the previous owners.
  • A full description of the land.
  • The affidavit filed with it. A deed is normally filed with a sworn affidavit. It gives the tax map number and either the value the fee is figured on or the reason the deed is exempt.

How far back do Horry County land records go online?

The Horry County index lists names and dates from late 1979, starting at Deed Book 660. Older deed books, back to Book 1, show in the index only by book and page, marked non-indexed.

We checked the oldest books in the index. Book 1 still opens by its book and page, with no names to search.

For a deed from before late 1979, call the office before you drive to Conway.

The online list runs a few days behind the recording desk. A paper recorded this week may not show yet. About 2,500 deeds are recorded in Horry County each month, besides the mortgages, liens and plats.

Does Horry County have a property fraud alert?

Yes. Horry County's Recording Notification Service is free for property owners. It sends you an email when a paper is recorded under a name you list.

  1. 1

    Open the county's sign-up page.

    It is part of the Register of Deeds' search site, and the county's service page describes it.

  2. 2

    List every version of your name.

    The name must match the recorded name exactly. DOE JOHN, DOE JOHN J and DOE JOHN JR are three different names, so add each one.

  3. 3

    Click the link in the county's email.

    The alert starts once you activate it.

Sign up for the Horry County alert

An alert comes after a paper is recorded. It cannot stop the recording, but it lets you act quickly if a paper is filed that you did not sign.

How do I search Georgetown County deeds for Pawleys Island and Litchfield?

Pawleys Island and Litchfield Beach are in Georgetown County, so their deeds are in Georgetown County's own search, not Horry's. Unlike Horry, Georgetown shows a deed on its website when the image is available.

Open the Georgetown County deed search

  • The name: last name, then first name, or a company name. The search ignores spaces and punctuation, and it can search both sides at once.
  • New book codes: papers recorded after May 14, 2021 use RB for Record Book, PLS for Plat Slide and MLB for Miscellaneous Book.
  • Old records: Georgetown County's property transfers and mortgages go back to the late 1860s.

Horry County recording fees

Most papers cost one flat fee to record, whatever their length.

Horry County recording fees
To recordFee
Deed$15
Mortgage$25
Mortgage satisfaction (a paid-off loan)$10
Plat$25
Easement$25
Mechanics lien$25
Restrictions (a neighborhood's rules)$25

The deed fee, often called deed stamps, is separate. It is $1.85 for each $500 of value, or part of $500, which is $3.70 per $1,000.

By law the seller owes the deed fee, except on a master-in-equity or government deed, where the buyer owes it. Our seller's net proceeds calculator and closing costs guide show it at your price.

State law exempts some deeds from the deed fee:

  • A deed of distribution from an estate.
  • A deed to a government.
  • A transfer into a company or a trust in return only for an interest in it.
  • A corrective or quitclaim deed that only confirms title the grantee already holds.
  • A deed in lieu of foreclosure.

Common questions

Deed search FAQ

Are Horry County deed records public?

Yes. Anyone can search the Register of Deeds index online as a guest, free, after accepting the county's terms.

Can I see my deed online in Horry County?

No. Horry County puts the index online, not the documents. You view them in Conway, order a copy by mail, or have us pull it for you.

How much does a copy of a deed cost in Horry County?

By mail, $8 for the first 4 pages and $1 for each page after. At the office, 50 cents a page on the copy machine. A certified copy is $2 more.

What is a plat, and how do I find one in Horry County?

A plat is a scale drawing of lots, usually made by a surveyor. It shows each lot's size and boundaries. Search the name on the plat and pick a plat type, or open it by book and page with book type P.
