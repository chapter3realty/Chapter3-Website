Horry County property records

Horry County property records: look up any address
The parcel, the flood zone, the deed and the tax bill, in one box.

    Public records from Horry County and FEMA, read live for each address. Chapter3 is not the county. , the county courthouse.

    By Devin Day, Operations Officer · Reviewed by Tim Nash, Broker-in-Charge · Updated November 14, 2026

    Type an address. The card shows Horry County's and FEMA's public records for it, line by line, with no sign-in.

    The short answer

    Horry County keeps property records in three places: deeds at the Register of Deeds, tax bills at the Treasurer, and lot lines on the county's online map (GIS).

    Our free lookup reads the county's and FEMA's public maps for one address. Its card opens that parcel's deed record and tax bill.

    Your mail says Myrtle Beach. The city line may not.

    About 3 in 4 street addresses that say "Myrtle Beach" are outside the City of Myrtle Beach. The city in a mailing address comes from the post office, not from the town line. Across Horry County, about 7 in 10 street addresses are outside every city and town. We checked all 246,621 points on the county's address map against the town limits.

    The line matters when you buy. Inside a town, the town's zoning applies. Outside, the county's does: see what the county's zoning codes mean.

    Not sure which side an address is on? Look it up: the card's Town line says.

    Where are Horry County property records kept?

    Each county office keeps its own records, and each has its own search.

    Let us make it simple

    Can you search Horry County property records by name?

    Yes, in two county searches. Both ask for the last name first.

    • The deed index at the Register of Deeds. It is free and needs no account. Our deed search guide shows the steps.
    • The Treasurer's tax search. It also finds a bill by parcel number or notice number. Our tax bill guide shows the steps.

    The county's map searches by owner too, after you sign in with a county account. Our lookup works by address only and shows no names.

    What do Horry County property records not show?

    Four things a buyer needs to know are not in the deed index, the tax bill or the county map.

    • Unpaid HOA charges. A condo buyer and the seller can both owe the seller's unpaid HOA charges, up to the amount in the statement the association must give on request. Our agents check the balance before closing, and an unpaid fee is usually paid from the seller's proceeds. See HOA statements and transfer fees.
    • Rental rules. Whether a home can be rented by the night depends on the town and the HOA, not on the deed or the tax bill. See the Myrtle Beach short-term rental rules and HOA rental restrictions.
    • The cost of insurance. It is not a county record. See what coastal home insurance costs.
    • Your own tax bill. Last year's bill can be the seller's, figured at the 4% ratio. Yours is figured at 4% if it is your legal residence, which you apply for, and at 6% if not. See Horry County property taxes.

    Pawleys Island and Litchfield: Georgetown County records

    Pawleys Island and Litchfield Beach are in Georgetown County, so their records are Georgetown's. Our lookup reads Horry County's maps only.

    • The map: Georgetown's online map opens with no sign-in. It searches by tax map number, address or road, not by owner.
    • Deeds: georgetowndeeds.com shows document images online when it has them.
    • Tax bills: georgetowncountysctax.com.

    Pawleys Island itself is less than 4 miles long and mostly one house wide, with a salt marsh between it and the mainland. The island and most of the land around it share ZIP code 29585, so a Pawleys Island address does not always mean a house on the island.

    Common questions

    Horry County property records questions

    How do I find the owner of a property in Horry County?

    Look up the address, then tap Deed record on the card. The county's deed record names the parties to that deed. The Treasurer's tax search, behind the Tax bill button, also shows the owner's name.

    Why can't the lookup find my address?

    Type the house number and the street, then pick a suggestion. Without a house number, the county's address finder can match only the street, so the card cannot name a parcel.

    Why does a condo building show many parcel numbers?

    Each unit in the building has its own parcel number, drawn on the building's outline. Send us the unit number and we find the right one.

    What is my hurricane evacuation zone?

    Type the address in the lookup. The card's Hurricane line gives zone A, B or C, or none. About 1 in 3 street addresses in Horry County is in a zone.

    Who answers flood map questions in Horry County?

    Horry County Code Enforcement has 12 certified floodplain managers on staff. To reach one, call its flood office at (843) 915-5090.
