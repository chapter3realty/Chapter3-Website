Home/Buyers/SC Down Payment Help

Buying a home

Down payment help in Horry County:
you do not have to be a first-time buyer.

By Devin Day, Operations Officer · Reviewed by Tim Nash, Broker-in-Charge · Updated November 12, 2026

SC Housing gives an eligible buyer $10,000. Most houses for sale in Conway, Loris, Aynor and Longs are under its price limit. Across the beach towns, about a third are.

The short answer

The $10,000 is a second mortgage from SC Housing, the state's housing agency. A second mortgage is a second loan on the home. It pays toward the down payment and closing costs. After you have lived in the home 15 years, SC Housing forgives it, and you owe nothing on the $10,000. If you sell, refinance or move out sooner, you repay all of it, from the sale or the new loan.

To qualify, a household of one or two can earn up to $114,960, or $134,120 for three or more. The home can cost up to $450,000, you need a credit score of 640, and you must own no other property when the loan closes.

Do you qualify for SC Housing's down payment help in Horry County?

You qualify for SC Housing's Homebuyer Program if you pass the seven tests below.

  • Household incomeUp to $114,960 for 1 or 2 people, $134,120 for 3 or moreThe income of everyone on the loan or the deed counts together.
  • Home priceUp to $450,000The lot can be 5 acres at most.
  • What you own nowNo other property when the loan closesOwning bare land is allowed.
  • Credit and debtsA credit score of 640 or moreIf the FHA, VA, USDA or conventional loan's own rules ask for a higher score, the higher score applies. All your monthly debt payments together can be at most 45 percent of your income.
  • CitizenshipA U.S. citizen or a permanent residentA permanent resident needs a green card. A visa of any kind does not qualify.
  • Who is on the loanOnly buyers who will live in the homeThe Homebuyer Program allows no cosigner. A gift toward the purchase can come from a relative, a domestic partner or a fiancé.
  • The homebuyer courseOne buyer takes it before loan approvalOnline or in a classroom, within 12 months of closing, even if you have owned a home before. The lender arranges it.

Do you earn too much?

Probably not. SC Housing's limit for one or two people is about 1.7 times the median, or middle, household income in Horry County.

About four in five renter households in the county earn under $100,000, below the limit for any household size.

How much of your own money do you still need?

With a VA or USDA loan, the $10,000 can cover most or all of the cash you bring to closing. Those loans need no down payment, so all of it goes to closing costs and prepaid items. Prepaid items are the first property taxes, homeowners insurance and loan interest paid at closing.

With an FHA or conventional loan, you also need a down payment, and the $10,000 goes toward both.

The $10,000 comes only with SC Housing's own first mortgage, which can be any of these four loans:

  • FHA: insured by the Federal Housing Administration, part of HUD.
  • VA: backed by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs, for veterans and service members.
  • USDA: backed by the U.S. Department of Agriculture, for homes inside its area on our map below.
  • Conventional: a loan no government agency backs.

Do you have to pay back SC Housing's $10,000?

Only if you sell, refinance or stop living in the home before 15 years are up. It does not shrink a little each year: until year 15, you owe all of it.

Is there a tax if you sell early?

Only if three things are all true: you sell within nine years, you sell at a gain, and your income has risen above the levels in the recapture notice from your lender. Then you may owe a federal recapture tax, an increase in your federal income tax for the year you sell. SC Housing pays it back if you ask in writing by June 30 of the next year; the refund is reported to the IRS as income.

Which homes count in Horry County, and where are they under $450,000?

Houses, townhomes, condos in an approved building, and modular homes count, up to $450,000. A modular home is built in sections in a factory and set on a foundation, not on a steel frame.

Conway has more houses for sale at or under $450,000 than any other town, Myrtle Beach included.

Condos: under the price limit, but the building must be approved

  • 89%of condos for sale in the same towns are listed at or under $450,000.
  • 9condo projects in all of Horry County are approved for FHA loans. HUD, the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development, keeps the list.

Several of those nine are approved only for some phases or buildings. An FHA loan on a condo in any other building needs a separate approval for that one unit.

In Chapter3's experience, a building with a rental program usually fails the rules for a conventional loan, because so many owners are investors. Our guide to condos most lenders will not lend on explains why. We check the building's approval before you make an offer on a condo.

Manufactured homes

A manufactured home, once called a mobile home, is built on a permanent steel frame to HUD's code. The Homebuyer Program does not take it. Palmetto Home Advantage takes doublewide or larger manufactured homes on a permanent foundation, usually with a credit score of at least 660. A doublewide is built in two sections; a single-wide does not qualify.

Can you buy with no money down in Horry County?

Yes, with a USDA or VA loan. SC Housing offers both with no down payment. A USDA loan needs a home inside USDA's area, and that area leaves out the coast.

The part left out is the coastal strip from Little River through Myrtle Beach to Murrells Inlet, and inland past Carolina Forest and Socastee. It is less than a fifth of the county's land.

We checked every house for sale at or under $450,000 in Horry County against USDA's map. About six in ten were inside USDA's area.

Which SC Housing program fits you in Horry County?

Four SC Housing programs are open to Horry County buyers now. A fifth, Palmetto Heroes, is closed until its next round. The voucher program works only where the local housing authority takes part. Find the line that matches you.

  • You own no other property, your household is under the income limit, and the home costs $450,000 or less.The Homebuyer ProgramThe $10,000 on this page
  • You earn more, the home costs more than $450,000, you already own a home, or you want a manufactured home.Palmetto Home AdvantageUp to 4 percent of the loan, forgiven after 10 years
  • You are a teacher, nurse, police officer, firefighter, EMT, veteran, or in the military or National Guard.Palmetto Heroes$10,000, closed for 2026
  • You, or anyone in your household, has a permanent disability shown by Social Security disability income (SSI or SSDI) or long-term disability income.Families with DisabilitiesThe same 15-year forgivable help, homes up to $450,000
  • You have had a Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8) for at least a year in the county where you buy.Voucher homeownershipWhere the local housing authority takes part

Palmetto Home Advantage

Palmetto Home Advantage counts only the borrowers' own income, not the whole household: up to $140,000, for any household size. SC Housing has no price limit for it, but the FHA, VA, USDA or conventional loan still has its own. A repeat buyer may hold at most two financed homes, the new one included. If you sell, refinance or move out within the 10 years, you repay all of the help.

Palmetto Heroes and Families with Disabilities

Palmetto Heroes' 2026 round filled in about four weeks, after 272 loans. SC Housing has not published a date for the next round. A buyer needs a signed sales contract to get the help, so we start the home search before it opens.

Families with Disabilities is open in every county, has no first-time buyer rule, and uses FHA, VA or USDA loans.

Is there down payment help from Horry County or Myrtle Beach?

Not from the county or the cities directly. Horry County runs HUD's HOME program for Horry, Georgetown and Williamsburg counties, and that money goes to developers and nonprofits, not to buyers. Two other routes help buyers themselves.

  • Habitat for Humanity of Horry CountyBuilds and sells homesFor households earning 35 to 80 percent of the county's median income: for three people in 2026, about $23,000 to $62,000 a year. You must have lived in Horry County for the last 12 months. Buyers put in at least 180 hours of work. You start with a short interest form on its homeownership page, and Habitat pairs you with a volunteer Financial Mentor.
  • Federal Home Loan Bank of AtlantaGrants through member banksEarning under 80 percent of the area's median income: up to $17,500 for a first-time buyer, and up to $20,000 for police, teachers, firefighters, health care workers and veterans. Earning 80 to 120 percent: up to $15,000, from its Workforce Housing Plus+ grants.

The bank grants go through banks that applied for them, and we find out which nearby banks still have money.

Can you use a bank grant and SC Housing's $10,000 together?

Yes, if the grant's own rules allow it. SC Housing's checklist for the lender has a line for a grant from anyone else, and the lender puts the grant's approval letter in the file.

How do you apply for SC Housing's down payment help?

You apply through an SC Housing approved lender, in the five steps below. The lender handles the application and the reservation.

Is the $10,000 open now?

Yes. SC Housing's program page offers the $10,000. The only program it lists as closed is Palmetto Heroes.

The money is limited, and SC Housing pays it out while money lasts. Once the lender reserves your loan and the $10,000, SC Housing holds them for 60 days on an existing home or 120 days on a new one, and the lender can ask for more time.

The five steps

  1. Pick an SC Housing approved lender.SC Housing's Find a Lender list for Horry County has about 90 loan officers at about 40 companies. The lender takes your application and checks that you qualify.
  2. Take the homebuyer course.The lender arranges it. One buyer finishes it, online or in a classroom, before the loan is approved.
  3. Find a home under the limit with us.We search houses, townhomes and condos at or under $450,000 in the towns you pick.
  4. Reserve the money.Once you qualify and decide to go ahead, the lender reserves your loan and the $10,000.
  5. Close.The $10,000 goes toward your down payment, closing costs and prepaid items at closing. It cannot pay a real estate commission or an appraisal gap, which is the difference when a home appraises for less than its price.

Our preferred lender is not on SC Housing's lender list for Horry County, so when one of these programs fits you, we introduce you to an approved lender.

Common questions

SC Housing questions

Do I have to be a first-time buyer in Horry County?

No. Horry County is one of SC Housing's "targeted" counties, where the first-time buyer rule does not apply. A buyer who has owned a home before can use the $10,000 once that home is sold.

Can I use SC Housing's help on a new home from a builder?

Yes, if the price is $450,000 or less. SC Housing has a form for the builder who sells the home. Its lender list for Horry County includes DHI Mortgage, Lennar Mortgage and NVR Mortgage Finance, the mortgage companies of the builders D.R. Horton, Lennar and NVR.

Can the seller pay part of my closing costs with SC Housing's help?

Yes, within the limits of your FHA, VA, USDA or conventional loan. SC Housing's guide adds no limit of its own on help from the seller.

Who decides the interest rate on an SC Housing loan?

SC Housing decides the rate on its own first mortgage. It is a fixed rate, posted every day for its approved lenders, and your lender locks it for you when it reserves the loan. SC Housing makes the loan with or without the $10,000, so we have the lender quote it both ways before you choose.

Can I get SC Housing's Made It Home! help or its tax credit in Horry County?

No. SC Housing's Made It Home! help is only for new homes in five other cities, and its Mortgage Credit Certificate, a federal tax credit for buyers, has ended.
