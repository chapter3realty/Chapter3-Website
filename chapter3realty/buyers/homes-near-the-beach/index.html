Where the houses a walk from the sand are near Myrtle Beach, what each beach town is like, what houses cost, and the flood zone.

The short answer

On November 8, 2026, 186 houses for sale near the beach were a 5-minute walk or less from a public path to the sand. They are spread from Cherry Grove to Pawleys Island. About half the houses under $400,000 a 10-minute walk or less from the sand are in Crescent Beach, in North Myrtle Beach.

In the last year, a Horry County house within half a mile of the ocean sold for about $675,000 at the middle. That means half sold for more and half for less. Two to 5 miles inland, the middle was about $390,000.

How far is the walk to the beach from a house near the beach?

We checked the walk to the sand from the houses for sale within 1.5 miles of the ocean. Each walk follows mapped streets and paths to the nearest public beach access on the state's map. A quarter mile takes about 5 minutes on foot, and half a mile about 10.

In the beach towns, the state's map shows a public path to the sand about every 250 to 400 feet. North Myrtle Beach manages over 240 beach accesses, and Myrtle Beach counts 143.

Houses for sale, by the walk to the nearest public path to the sand

  • 186 for salea 5-minute walk or less. Middle asking price about $950,000.
  • 109 for salea 5- to 10-minute walk. Middle asking price about $680,000.
  • 104 for salea 10- to 20-minute walk. Middle asking price about $650,000.

These are asking prices for houses for sale today. The sale prices on this page are what houses sold for in the last year, from the county's deed records.

A house that looks close on a map can still be a long walk. About 1 in 13 houses for sale within half a mile of the ocean is more than a mile on foot from a public path. Two in 3 of those are on the Litchfield and Pawleys Island coast, where the paths are far apart.

Which beach town near Myrtle Beach fits you?

The coast from Cherry Grove to Pawleys Island is four stretches of beach towns, one after another. Here they are from north to south.

North Myrtle Beach and Cherry Grove

Four old beach towns, marsh channels

North Myrtle Beach is four old beach towns that voted to join in 1968: Cherry Grove, Ocean Drive, Crescent Beach and Windy Hill.

  • Cherry GroveThe quieter beach, at the north end. About 680 houses here are on long channels dug in the marsh, often with a dock. The city's boat ramp reaches the ocean through Hog Inlet. Heritage Shores Nature Preserve has about 7 acres of paths on an island in the marsh.
  • Ocean DriveMain Street, and the Ingram Dunes Nature Preserve, with dunes 30 feet tall and a half-mile trail. In Ocean Drive and Crescent Beach, a pavilion drew crowds to dance the shag, now South Carolina's state dance.
  • Crescent BeachSouth of Ocean Drive, with the 18th and Edge pocket park.
  • Windy HillPast the separate town of Atlantic Beach. Investors from Conway built it up after World War II. It has Yow Park and the 41st Avenue South pocket park.

Houses near the ocean here sold for about $625,000 at the middle in the last year. Our North Myrtle Beach page covers each part of the city.

Myrtle Beach, east of Kings Highway

The boardwalk, the busy beach, and the fewest houses in the flood zone

In Myrtle Beach, the main road near the ocean is Kings Highway, also called US 17 Business. We measured its distance from the ocean along its whole length in the city. For half of it, the road is within about half a mile of the ocean.

Listings say "east of 17" for the streets between Kings Highway and the sand. Houses marked east of 17 in our home search are a little under half a mile from the ocean at the middle.

Downtown is the busy beach, with the boardwalk and volleyball. The Boardwalk is about 1.3 miles long, beside the ocean, with a raised wooden walk and a paved walk along the beach.

This stretch runs from Briarcliffe Acres south to Surfside Beach, and includes Arcadian Shores and Myrtle Beach State Park. Within half a mile of the ocean on this stretch, the middle sale price of a house was about $670,000.

Our Myrtle Beach page covers the city's neighborhoods and its rental rules.

Surfside Beach, Garden City and Murrells Inlet

A town pier, free county parking, and the marsh at Murrells Inlet

This stretch is Surfside Beach, Garden City and Murrells Inlet, from north to south.

  • Surfside BeachA town of about 1.9 square miles. Every home in it is within about 0.8 mile of Ocean Boulevard, the beach road. The concrete town pier is free to walk on, with two restaurants and an ice cream shop.
  • Life in Surfside BeachOn summer Sunday evenings, the town holds a free concert in Memorial Park. On Memorial Day, the Fourth of July and in December, golf cart parades go down Ocean Boulevard.
  • Garden CityNot a town: Horry County runs it. Its beach road is Waccamaw Drive, and houses on the marsh line Dogwood Drive. Horry County's oceanfront beach accesses here have free parking all year.
  • Murrells InletThe MarshWalk is a half-mile wooden boardwalk along the marsh, with restaurants and free live music.

This stretch has the highest prices of the three in Horry County: about $775,000 at the middle for a house within half a mile of the ocean. Our pages on Surfside Beach, Garden City and Murrells Inlet cover each town.

Litchfield and Pawleys Island

A barrier island of old beach houses, and the highest prices

  • Pawleys IslandA barrier island less than 4 miles long, mostly one house wide, with salt marsh behind it. The town has a 25 mph speed limit and no lifeguards on the beach.
  • Life on the islandPeople crab and fish from the two marsh bridges, and the town still calls itself by its old nickname, "arrogantly shabby". On about 6 in 10 island houses, the last deed was not a sale, the way a house passes to children or an estate.
  • LitchfieldThe beach between the island and Huntington Beach State Park. A bike path goes along US 17 from behind the island through Litchfield to Murrells Inlet.

There are fewer public paths here. Pawleys Island has the largest free beach parking lot in Georgetown County, at the south end, and 8 other public accesses. The state's map shows one about every 2,400 feet on the island.

Houses for sale within half a mile of the ocean here ask about $2.1 million at the middle. This coast is in Georgetown County, outside the Horry County deed records we used, so we give asking prices here. Our Pawleys Island page has the island's own sales and flood zones.

How much does a house near the beach cost in Myrtle Beach?

The nearer the ocean, the more a house sold for. Houses within a quarter mile of the ocean sold for about $805,000 at the middle in the last year.

The price is highest in the first tenth of a mile, about 500 feet. Houses there sold for about $1.33 million. From half a mile to 2 miles back, the price stays near $500,000. Our waterfront homes page covers houses on the ocean itself.

Where can you buy a house near the beach for under $400,000?

About 1 in 6 house sales within half a mile of the ocean was under $400,000 in the last year. Most of those were in North Myrtle Beach and Cherry Grove.

In our count of houses for sale, 20 under $400,000 were a 10-minute walk or less from a beach path. Of those, 14 were in North Myrtle Beach, most of them in Crescent Beach.

Will you need flood insurance for a house near the beach?

Often, if the house is close to the ocean and you buy with a mortgage. About half of Horry County houses within a quarter mile of the ocean are in FEMA's high-risk flood zone. A quarter to half a mile back, about 1 in 4 are.

If the house is in the high-risk flood zone and you buy with a mortgage, the lender requires flood insurance.

Past about three quarters of a mile from the ocean, fewer than 1 in 20 houses are in the high-risk zone.

Near the ocean, it depends on the town. Of the three Horry County stretches, Myrtle Beach has the fewest houses in the high-risk zone:

Houses within half a mile of the ocean in the high-risk flood zone

  • About 4 in 10in North Myrtle Beach and Cherry Grove
  • About halffrom Surfside Beach to Murrells Inlet

What are the pros and cons of living near the beach?

With a house near the beach, you can walk to the sand, and you usually pay no HOA fee. You pay more for the house, and flood insurance is more likely.

The good

  • You walk to the beachFrom a house near the beach, the sand can be a 5-minute walk. From a house inland in Carolina Forest or Conway, the beach is a 20- to 30-minute drive with no traffic.
  • Usually no HOA feeAbout 3 in 4 houses for sale in the first quarter mile report no homeowners association (HOA) fee. Two to 5 miles inland, about 1 in 10 report none. Our homes with no HOA page covers what that allows.
  • A few streets back costs much lessPast the first quarter mile, houses sold for about a third less at the middle. Half a mile from the ocean is still about a 10-minute walk.

The hard

  • Fewer houses, higher pricesOnly about 1 in 16 house sales in Horry County was within half a mile of the ocean. Those houses sold for about 1.7 times as much as houses 2 to 5 miles inland.
  • Flood insurance is more likelyNear the ocean, many houses are in the high-risk flood zone. We look up the zone and the policy cost for the house before you make an offer.
  • Many neighbors are part-timeAt the 2020 Census, close to half the homes in North Myrtle Beach were held for seasonal or vacation use.

Common questions

Houses near the beach FAQ

Is Myrtle Beach in a flood zone?

Parts of it are. Inside the City of Myrtle Beach, only about 1 in 23 houses is in FEMA's high-risk flood zone. About 1 in 4 condos in the city is.

Can you rent out a house near the beach in Myrtle Beach?

The rules differ by city. In the City of Myrtle Beach, a rental of less than 90 days is legal only where the zoning allows it. About 96 in 100 houses there are not in those zones. North Myrtle Beach allows short-term rentals across the city, with a city business license renewed each year.

Is a condo near the beach cheaper than a house?

In North Myrtle Beach, yes. In its beach sections, a condo sold for about $310,000 to $360,000 at the middle in the last year. This page covers houses; our condos page covers condos.

What is a second-row house at the beach?

A second-row house is in the row just behind the oceanfront houses. Our waterfront homes page covers the oceanfront row itself.
