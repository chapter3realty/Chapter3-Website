Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Chapter 3 Realty LLC (“Chapter 3,” “we,” or “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect on chapter3realty.com, why, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us, when you submit a contact or property-analysis form, we collect your name, email, phone number, the property or interest you tell us about, and any message you send.
  • Automatic information, like most websites, we use analytics (Google Analytics) to understand traffic. This includes things like pages visited, general location, device, and referring site. It does not identify you by name.

How we use it

To respond to your request, provide the property information or analysis you asked for, follow up about buying, selling, or investing, improve our website, and meet legal and recordkeeping obligations. We only send marketing messages if you ask us to, and you can opt out at any time.

How your form is processed

Form submissions are delivered to us through a third-party form service (Web3Forms) that transmits your message to our email. We also work with an affiliated mortgage lender; if your request involves financing, we may share your information with them so they can help, see our Affiliated Business Arrangement disclosure in the site footer.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us operate (such as our form and analytics providers and our affiliated lender when relevant), or when required by law.

Cookies and analytics

We use cookies and similar tools for analytics. You can control cookies in your browser settings, and you can opt out of Google Analytics using Google’s browser add-on.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the information you’ve given us, and you can unsubscribe from our messages, by emailing hello@chapter3realty.com.

Changes

We may update this policy; the “last updated” date above reflects the current version.

Questions about this policy? Email hello@chapter3realty.com or call 854.333.2135.
