Wild Wing Plantation, Conway

By Devin Day, Operations Officer · Reviewed by Tim Nash, Broker-in-Charge · Updated November 17, 2026

Listing sites name Avocet as the 18-hole golf course at Wild Wing Plantation, and a company owns its land.

The short answer

Wild Wing Plantation is a golf neighborhood of 756 lots inside the City of Conway. In the past year its houses sold for about $510,000, from about $340,000 to about $975,000.

Listing sheets show an association fee of $135 a month for most homes for sale. No part of the neighborhood is in FEMA's high-risk flood zone. Next door, the Fairways townhomes sold for about $215,000.

What is it like to live in Wild Wing Plantation?

Of the 756 lots, 189 touch a golf-course parcel or sit within 50 feet of one. Buying on the course does not make you an owner of it. Our golf communities guide compares the area's golf neighborhoods.

Lakes sit between the street loops. The owners' association owns a 33-acre lake and wetland, and 46 lots touch it. See the Wild Wing homes tagged waterfront.

A typical lot is about a third of an acre, 16,400 square feet. The typical house is about 3,400 square feet under roof, garage included. Streets are named for birds: Wood Stork Drive, Whooping Crane Drive, Wigeon Drive and Spoonbill Drive.

Most of these are full-time homes: the county taxes 86 in 100 houses here as their owner's primary residence.

Which part of Wild Wing Plantation is which?

The listings use three names here. Wild Wing Plantation is the main neighborhood. The Sanctuary at Wild Wing Plantation is a part of it with smaller lots. The Fairways at Wild Wing is a separate group of townhomes next door, and our counts for Wild Wing leave it out.

Searching "Wild Wing" also finds Kiskadee Parke At Wild Wing, which the listings keep apart from both. Its homes are not in our counts.

What do homes in Wild Wing Plantation sell for?

In the past year, about 40 houses here sold with a price on the deed. The middle price was about $510,000. They ranged from about $340,000 to about $975,000. We read the county's deed index to get them.

On November 14, 2026, 25 homes were for sale here, asking about $300,000 to $1.75 million.

Sale prices: Horry County deeds recorded in the past 12 months, houses only. Deeds with no price stated are not counted. Asking prices: CCMLS listings, counted in aggregate.

Besides the mortgage and insurance, tax on a house bought at about $510,000 is about $3,300 a year as your primary residence. As a second home or rental it is about $8,300 a year. Both assume the county values the house at the price you pay, and the lower rate needs a legal-residence application.

If you own here and want to sell, our home value page explains how we price a home from recorded sales.

Is there an HOA at Wild Wing Plantation?

Yes. Wild Wing Plantation has a property owners' association, the POA. The listing sheets for most homes for sale here show a fee of $135 a month. The Sanctuary's sheets show $66 and $74.

  • The fee shown for a home for saleThe listing agent enters it, so it is not the association's budget. We have not found what it covers. We confirm both with the association before you offer.

Is Wild Wing Plantation in the City of Conway?

Yes. The county's map puts every lot inside the City of Conway, and the mail says Conway, SC 29526. Two things follow for an owner:

  • Property taxThe bill includes the City of Conway's tax. The yearly figures above already count it.
  • TrashThe city collects garbage, recycling and yard waste at the curb once a week.

Our Conway guide covers the town itself.

Is Wild Wing Plantation in a flood zone?

No part of the neighborhood is in FEMA's high-risk zone, the zone where a lender requires flood insurance. One FEMA map area covers it, and FEMA calls it an "area of minimal flood hazard".

  • What a zone does not showA zone does not show whether a house has flooded. We check each house's flood history before you offer. Our page on flooding a seller may not mention explains how.
  • InsuranceOur coastal insurance guide covers insurance for homes near the coast.

How far is Wild Wing Plantation from the beach?

About 22 minutes by car, 11.6 miles to the nearest public beach access, with no traffic. The Myrtle Beach airport is about 23 minutes away, and Broadway at the Beach about 22.

None of the 12 public beach accesses closest to Wild Wing has parking, says the state's beach access list. The nearest one with parking is about 23 minutes away.

Other neighborhoods we cover, nearest first: River Oaks in Carolina Forest, Riverwalk in Myrtle Beach and Plantation Point in Myrtle Beach. Then Oak Forest in Myrtle Beach, Grande Dunes in Myrtle Beach and Kingston Plantation in Myrtle Beach. Then the town of Briarcliffe Acres and Willbrook Plantation in Pawleys Island.

Common questions

Wild Wing Plantation FAQ

Which golf course is at Wild Wing Plantation?

Listing sites name Avocet as the 18-hole course. A company owns the golf-course land, not the homeowners. Call the club for what play and membership cost.

Is Kiskadee Parke part of Wild Wing Plantation?

Not in the listings. Kiskadee Parke At Wild Wing is its own name there, and our counts leave it out. Its homes are on our search.

Is Wild Wing Plantation in Conway or Myrtle Beach?

Conway. The neighborhood is inside the City of Conway limits, and the mailing address is Conway, SC 29526.

Are there empty lots for sale in Wild Wing Plantation?

The county map shows 74 empty lots, and about a dozen sold in the past year. Ask us which are for sale now.

Can I rent out a house in Wild Wing Plantation?

Renting can depend on the City of Conway and the association's rules, so we check both for the house you pick.
