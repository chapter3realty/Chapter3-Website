The short answer

In the past year 1,260 houses sold under a Longs address, at a middle of $319,000. The houses for sale ask a middle of $320,000.

Longs is not a city. Part of its land lies inside the city of North Myrtle Beach, and that changes a house's tax bill.

Which Longs houses were listed this week?

About 20 townhouses and about 22 condos are for sale in Longs too.

Longs is an unincorporated community in Horry County, zip code 29568. Its mailing address covers new neighborhoods and open land, and part of it lies inside the city of North Myrtle Beach.

What do houses in Longs cost?

Longs houses for sale ask a middle of $320,000. The middle half ask about $290,000 to $395,000. The lowest asks about $134,000 and the highest about $2.1 million.

The largest group, about 140 houses, asks $300,000 to $399,999.

Another 71 ask $400,000 or more.

Is Longs part of North Myrtle Beach?

Mostly no, and in part yes. Longs has no city limits of its own. The land with a Longs address is mostly in Horry County, outside any city. The city of North Myrtle Beach also reaches inland over part of it.

About 1 house in 4 sold under a Longs address is in the North Myrtle Beach tax district. Those 322 houses sold for about $388,000. The other 938 sold for about $300,000.

The tax district changes the tax bill. On a house at the middle sale price, the yearly bill is about $170 higher in the North Myrtle Beach tax district if you live there.

On a second home or rental it is about $300 higher. The yearly cost section has the dollars.

The North Myrtle Beach guide covers the city itself. Before you offer on a Longs house, we tell you which side of the line it is on. A house is in a different tax district, with a different bill, on each side.

Are there new homes for sale in Longs?

Yes. About half of the Longs houses for sale are new construction, 165 of 323 on 2026-11-17. Some are finished and ready to move into now.

Builders sold most of what changed hands. A business, mostly a builder, sold 950 of the 1,260 houses in the past year, about 3 in 4.

What have Longs houses sold for?

In the past year 1,260 houses sold with a Longs address. The middle price was $319,000. The middle half sold for $270,000 to $380,000. We counted them one deed at a time in the county's recorded deeds.

That is a little under the $320,000 the houses for sale ask in the middle. Sale prices are from Horry County deeds. A new house counts when its builder sold it.

What does a year cost to own a house in Longs?

Two bills come every year besides your loan and insurance: the county's property tax and, in a neighborhood with an association, its fee.

South Carolina taxes a home you live in on 4 percent of its value. It taxes a second home or a rental on 6 percent. You must apply for the 4 percent rate.

Take a house that sold for $319,000, the middle sale price. The county tax on it is below. Each figure assumes the county values the house at its price, before any small watershed levy.

The fee shown for a Longs house for sale is a middle of about $81 a month, and the middle half show $70 to $103. About 22 houses show no fee. The agent who listed the house enters that fee, so we confirm it with the association.

For a house you live in, outside any city, tax plus twelve months at that middle fee comes to about $2,230 a year. Our property tax guide explains how the county works out the bill.

Is Longs in a flood zone?

For most houses, no. The flood map and the storm record each tell part of it.

  • The map: about 2 in 100 of the houses that sold under a Longs address sit in FEMA's high-risk flood zone, 28 of 1,230. None of the houses in the North Myrtle Beach tax district do. We checked each house's lot against FEMA's map.
  • The storm: after Hurricane Florence in 2018, the National Weather Service reported five feet of water in homes in Polo Farms, off SC 905. It reported up to three feet in Aberdeen Country Club, in Longs.

We do not rate a place. We check the house you like against FEMA's map and its flood history. See how to check a house's flood zone.

How far is Longs from the beach?

The beach is 17 to 19 minutes by car from the middle of Longs, at Ocean Drive and at Cherry Grove Pier. Both are in North Myrtle Beach. Myrtle Beach itself is farther.

Barefoot Landing is about 24 minutes away and Grand Strand Regional hospital about 29. Coastal Grand Mall is about 38 minutes and the airport about 43.

Is Longs a good place to live?

That depends on what you want to drive to. About half of the houses for sale are new, and the hospital is 12 minutes away.

We do not rate a place as safe or unsafe. For public records on any address, see the property records guide. For a town with its own center and downtown, compare Little River and Conway.

Common questions

Longs, SC FAQ

What county is Longs, SC in?

Horry County. Longs is an unincorporated community, so it has no city government. Its zip code is 29568.

How far is Longs from Myrtle Beach?

The beaches in North Myrtle Beach are 17 to 19 minutes by car from the middle of Longs. Coastal Grand Mall in Myrtle Beach is about 38 minutes and the airport about 43, without traffic.

Does Longs have an HOA?

Longs is not one association. Each neighborhood has its own, and about 22 houses for sale show no fee. The rest show a middle of about $81 a month. That is the entry of the agent who listed the house, not the association's bill.

Is Longs in North Myrtle Beach?

Part of it is. Longs has no city limits, but about 1 house in 4 sold under a Longs address is inside North Myrtle Beach, which changes its tax bill.
