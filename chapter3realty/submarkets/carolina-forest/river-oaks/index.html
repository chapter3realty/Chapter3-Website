River Oaks, Carolina Forest

River Oaks condos in Carolina Forest:
what they sell for, the HOA fee and the tax.

  • Condos1,203
  • Typical sale$155,000
  • HOA fee a month$330 to $455
  • Beach by carAbout 9 minutes

By Devin Day, Operations Officer · Reviewed by Tim Nash, Broker-in-Charge · Updated November 15, 2026

A typical River Oaks condo sold for $155,000 this past year. The county tax on one is about $570 a year as your full-time home.

The short answer

River Oaks is a community of 1,203 condos and 118 houses on the Intracoastal Waterway in Carolina Forest. Mail goes to Myrtle Beach, but the land is outside every town, in Horry County.

The monthly HOA fee on the 37 condos for sale is $330 to $455. River Oaks Golf Club, the 27-hole course that sat among the condos, closed on November 30, 2025.

What is River Oaks in Myrtle Beach?

River Oaks is several groups of homes under one name. It has seven sections in the county's records, and the listings use five names. They put Waterway Village and the resort condos together as "River Oaks Condos".

The land is in Carolina Forest, not inside Myrtle Beach. None of River Oaks' lots is in a town's limits. Mail goes to Myrtle Beach, so listings say Myrtle Beach.

What do River Oaks condos sell for?

In the past year 88 River Oaks condos sold, at a middle price of $155,000. The middle half went for $125,000 to $180,000.

In our latest count, 37 River Oaks condos were for sale, asking $110,000 to $190,000. Most were two-bedrooms, and most were built in the 1990s, with the newest in 2004. The River Oaks Condos listings are about 890 square feet at the middle.

How much is the HOA fee at River Oaks, and what does it cover?

The monthly HOA fee on River Oaks condos for sale is $330 to $455. In the Fairways it is $368 to $425.

The listings do not say what the fee pays for, and the county does not keep it. Before you offer, we ask the association for its budget and its rules.

What the houses pay

The houses pay less. The Landing's fee is $80 to $81 a month, with trash pickup. The one cottage for sale shows $34 a month, and the one Oakmont house $42. Our HOA fees by neighborhood page has the rest of Carolina Forest.

Renting, and the association's rules

On renting: the county's zoning has no limit on how long a home outside the towns may be rented. The association's recorded rules can have one. Our short-term rental rules guide covers renting by the night.

Six sets of rules and regulations were recorded for Waterway Village between 2019 and 2025. We read the current ones before you offer.

How much is the property tax on a River Oaks condo?

On a typical condo, the county tax is about $570 a year as your full-time home. As a second home or rental it is about $1,870. Most bills add a flat $89.40 county stormwater fee.

A typical condo, $155,000About $570About $1,870

The arithmetic: the price, times 4 percent for a home you live in or 6 percent otherwise, times the county's current tax rate. Your own full-time home bill depends on filing for the lower rate.

The county taxes about 8 in 10 River Oaks condos at the second-home and rental rate, and the rest at the full-time rate.

The county's current values work out to about $1,700 for a condo taxed as a second home. For one taxed as a full-time home, they work out to about $570. Both are before any stormwater fee. A sale resets the value to about the price, so a buyer's bill follows the price paid.

Is the River Oaks golf course still open?

No. River Oaks Golf Club closed on November 30, 2025, after 38 years. It had 27 holes, in three nines called Fox, Otter and Bear.

The Bear nine closed first, in September 2021. It became 220 home lots, and On The Green described that project as well underway. For the other 18 holes, the owner bought the land in February 2024 and plans 370 home lots on 172 acres.

Five parcels named FAIRWAYS, about 170 acres, belong to a builder's company. They are land, not the Fairways condos. About 480 homes sit within 50 feet of that land.

A condo that once looked onto a fairway now looks onto land a builder owns. What goes there is the owner's plan, not a promise.

Waterway Village: the condos on the Intracoastal

Waterway Village has 279 condos, and 54 of them are within about 80 feet of the county's outline of the Intracoastal Waterway. No other part of River Oaks comes close.

16 sold in the past year, at a middle price of $205,000, the highest middle price in River Oaks. For the ones on the water, our waterfront homes guide explains what the water adds and costs.

The Fairways at River Oaks

The Fairways is the other set of condos, 252 of them on the east side of River Oaks. The middle sale in the past year was about $150,000, from 24 sales. The 7 for sale were built from 1999 to 2004, and 4 of them have three bedrooms.

The houses: cottages, Oakmont and The Landing

River Oaks has 118 older houses in the cottages, Oakmont and Par 4. Their lots are about 9,100 square feet at the middle. 3 cottages sold in the past year, for $280,000 to $330,000. No Oakmont house sold.

The Landing is the new part: 101 lots. In our latest count, 9 Landing houses were for sale, asking $420,000 to $548,000. No Landing house sale is recorded yet.

The listings mark 6 of the 9 as waterfront. The county's outline puts the Intracoastal 320 meters from the nearest Landing lot, so we ask which water a house faces. The Landing's fee covers trash pickup, a community pool and tennis.

Is River Oaks in a flood zone?

None of River Oaks' home and lot parcels touches FEMA's high-risk zone or its 0.2 percent zone on the county's map. That includes the Waterway Village lots next to the water.

A map does not show whether a building has flooded, and it does not set an insurance bill. We check a condo's flood history before you offer. Our page on flooding a seller may not mention explains how, and coastal insurance covers wind and flood cover.

How far is River Oaks from the beach?

The nearest public beach access is about 9 minutes by car from the condos, cottages and Oakmont, and about 14 from the Fairways and The Landing. It is about 6.3 miles from the east side. Walking from the west side takes about two hours.

Common questions

River Oaks FAQ

Is River Oaks in Myrtle Beach or Carolina Forest?

Both, in a sense. Mail goes to Myrtle Beach, but the land is in Carolina Forest, outside every town's limits, in Horry County.

Is the River Oaks golf course open?

No. River Oaks Golf Club has closed, and a builder's company owns the course land now.

Can I rent out a River Oaks condo?

The county's zoning has no limit on how long a home outside the towns may be rented. The association's recorded rules can have one, so we read them before you offer. Our short-term rental rules guide covers renting by the night.

Does River Oaks have a pool?

The Landing has a community pool, on all 9 of its listings, and tennis on 5 of them. For the condos, the listings do not say, so we ask the association.

Why is the HOA fee not in the county's records?

The county does not keep an association's fee. It comes from the listing, and we confirm it with the association.

Is this River Oaks Estates in Pawleys Island?

No. River Oaks Estates is a different place, in Pawleys Island. This page is about River Oaks in Carolina Forest, near Myrtle Beach.

How many condos are in River Oaks?

The county counts 1,203 condos: 279 in Waterway Village, 672 resort condos and 252 in the Fairways.
