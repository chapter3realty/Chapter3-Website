Community guide

Carolina Forest, SC real estate:
is it Myrtle Beach, which neighborhood, what the HOA costs.

By Devin Day, Operations Officer · Reviewed by Tim Nash, Broker-in-Charge · Published July 20, 2026

Carolina Forest is a large master-planned community in unincorporated Horry County, with a Myrtle Beach mailing address (ZIP 29579) and no city tax.

A house's HOA fee is about $110 a month at the middle, and each neighborhood has its own. About four houses sold here every day for the past year: 1,340 recorded sales.

The direct answer

Where is Carolina Forest?

Carolina Forest is a large master-planned community in unincorporated Horry County, planned in the late 1990s. It is still being built.

Of the homes for sale in it today, about 300 are houses, 117 are condos and 28 are townhouses. Many are new construction.

We pull the flood map and the HOA papers on every home we show here. Horry County Schools decides the attendance zones: see the district's current maps and our guide to schools for relocating buyers.

See every Carolina Forest house for sale. Condos and townhouses are in the search too.

Myrtle Beach or not

Is Carolina Forest part of Myrtle Beach?

No. Carolina Forest is a census-designated place in Horry County, not a city. It has a Myrtle Beach mailing address and ZIP 29579. On the homes for sale we read, 248 of 297 houses show 29579.

Wild Wing Plantation and its sections carry a Conway ZIP, 29526, so the mailing city can differ inside the area. See Wild Wing Plantation.

Not being a city means no city property tax. The costs section below has the rate.

The HOA

What does the HOA cost in Carolina Forest?

It depends on the neighborhood, because each one runs its own association. We found no single HOA for all of Carolina Forest.

The fee shown on a house for sale is about $110 a month at the middle, and half of the houses sit between $92 and $135. It runs from about $50 in North Cove to about $155 in The Bluffs on the Waterway and Waterway Palms Plantation. A condo's fee is about $405 a month at the middle.

The fee shown on a home for sale is the agent's entry, not the association's bill, so we get the association's papers before you offer. See HOA fees by neighborhood and how to get the HOA documents.

The market

What do houses cost in Carolina Forest?

Every Carolina Forest home for sale now, by price and size, is on our Carolina Forest homes for sale page.

A house sold for a middle price of about $400,000 in the past year, and half sold between $303,000 and $540,000. These are county deed records, counted by us. They include builders' first sales.

Nine companies each sold 30 or more of the 1,340 houses, 370 in all. The five largest were Lennar Carolinas, D.R. Horton, Banbury SC, NVR and Clayton Properties Group.

The houses for sale now ask more than the ones that sold: the middle ask is about $486,000. About 90 ask under $400,000, about 105 ask $400,000 to $600,000, and about 105 ask more. About 1 in 6 is new construction. See the latest market report for the region.

Condos are a different market. The condos for sale ask about $170,000 at the middle, and their HOA is about $405 a month.

Costs and risk

What does a year cost to own in Carolina Forest?

Taxes

The 2026 county rate is 201.0 per $1,000 of the value the county taxes, with no city rate, plus a fee of $89.40 on the bills we read. City of Myrtle Beach residents pay 83.4 more per $1,000.

How much is property tax on a house in Carolina Forest?

A lived-in house, taxed at the 4 percent rate, skips 109.1 of that, the school operating tax. At a $400,000 sale price, if the county values it at its price, that is about $1,560 a year. A second home or rental, taxed at 6 percent, is about $4,900. Exact numbers for any price: our Horry County property tax calculator.

Flood

About 2 in 100 of the houses that sold sit in a FEMA high-risk flood zone: 26 of 1,307. They cluster in one tax district, so the parcel matters more than the neighborhood. Check any address with our flood zone check and read the flood history guide.

Wind

The state's backup insurer for wind covers a strip near the coast. Whether an address is inside it is a map question: check the Department of Insurance map, and read our coastal insurance guide.

History and roads

How Carolina Forest grew

In 1997 the Horry County council voted to freeze zoning rules in an 11 square mile area for 20 years, in exchange for land for parks and roads. Most of the development follows the Carolina Forest Master Plan, made by International Paper with Horry County. Source: Wikipedia, citing news reports.
