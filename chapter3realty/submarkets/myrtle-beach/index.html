Myrtle Beach real estate: where should you buy?
Where people live, and where you can rent by the night.

  • Boardwalk1.3 miles
  • Airport50+ nonstop cities
  • HousesMostly main homes
  • Nightly rentalsMost condos

By Devin Day, Operations Officer · Reviewed by Tim Nash, Broker-in-Charge · Updated November 12, 2026

A 1.3-mile Boardwalk, golf, a large hospital and the airport are all inside the city. Where you buy depends on whether you will live here or rent by the night.

The short answer

Buy a house if you will live here. About 3 in 4 houses in the city are their owners' main homes, and almost none may be rented by the night. Buy an oceanfront condo if you want to rent it to visitors: about 3 in 4 condos are in zones where the city allows nightly rentals.

Houses in the city resold for about $540,000 in the middle, and their prices are steady. Condos resold for about $175,000, and their prices are falling.

What is it like to live in Myrtle Beach?

Myrtle Beach is a city of about 40,900 people on the ocean. About 15 percent more people live here than in 2020.

  • The Boardwalk. Downtown, the Boardwalk is about 1.3 miles long, beside the ocean. It has a raised wooden boardwalk, a wide section for entertainment, and a winding walk along the ocean.
  • A large hospital. Grand Strand Medical Center is inside the city, about a mile from the ocean. It has a heart surgery program and a trauma center for the most serious injuries.
  • The airport. Myrtle Beach International Airport is inside the city too, less than 2 miles from the ocean. It has nonstop flights to more than 50 cities.
  • Golf and shops. Two golf clubs, Pine Lakes and The Dunes, are in the older neighborhoods described below. The Market Common, on the south end, has shops, restaurants, a movie theater and two parks.

What is the off season like?

Quieter, not closed. In the off season, nothing closes except tourist attractions and water parks.

Hotels, restaurants and other leisure businesses in the county had about 30 percent more workers in July than in January last year.

Where should you live in Myrtle Beach?

In one of three parts of the city where 7 in 10 or more of the houses are their owners' main homes: Grande Dunes, The Market Common or the older neighborhoods near the golf clubs. Or in a condo, on the oceanfront or off it. For a house near $300,000, look at the two smaller neighborhoods after part 3. To pick a neighborhood by what you want close to home, see the best neighborhoods in Myrtle Beach.

The numbers on the map show where each part is. The orange areas are where the city allows nightly rentals.

1Grande Dunes: on the Waterway, with a beach club

Grande Dunes is a planned community on both sides of the Intracoastal Waterway, the inland water route behind the beach. Every homeowner gets a free household membership to the Grande Dunes Ocean Club. It has a large pool, a dining room, classes and clubs, and its own way onto the beach.

The community also has a public marina, a public golf course with holes along the water, a private members' golf course and a tennis club.

About 4 in 5 houses here are their owners' main homes. Houses, villas and townhouses resold for about $880,000 in the middle, the highest of the parts in this guide.

2The Market Common: shops, a lake and parks

The Market Common is a town center with shops, restaurants and a movie theater, and apartments above the stores. It was built on land that was the Myrtle Beach Air Force Base. Our Market Common guide covers its neighborhoods, what homes there cost and the airport next door.

Grand Park and Valor Park are next to the shops. They have a lake with a 1.1-mile path around it, sports fields, picnic areas, fishing spots and Savannah's Playground, built for children of all abilities.

About 2,500 houses are in the neighborhoods around it, among them Emmens Preserve, The Reserve, Sanctuary at Withers Preserve and Belle Harbor. About 6 in 7 are their owners' main homes, the highest share in this guide. Houses, villas and townhouses here resold for about $500,000 in the middle.

The city allows nightly rentals in the town center's zone, which includes a few hundred of these houses. It does not allow them in the other neighborhoods around it. Each neighborhood's HOA can still ban them, and we read its rules for you.

3The older neighborhoods: golf and streets by the ocean

These neighborhoods are built around two golf clubs. Pine Lakes Country Club opened in 1927 as Myrtle Beach's first golf course. The Dunes Golf and Beach Club, on the ocean, hosts a PGA Tour event, the Myrtle Beach Classic.

Myrtle Heights, Oak Park, Forest Dunes and Northwood are here too. On some streets, the houses face the beach across wide lawns.

Nightly rentals are not allowed here, and about 7 in 10 houses are their owners' main homes. Houses resold for about $550,000 in the middle, with a wide range from street to street.

Where are the houses near $300,000?

In a few smaller neighborhoods. Houses in the city resold for about $540,000 in the middle in the past year, and only about 1 in 6 sold for $350,000 or less. Of the 869 homes for sale in the city at $300,000 or less, only 8 are houses. The rest are condos and townhouses.

4A condo on the oceanfront

You can live in a condo too. Nearly every oceanfront condo building is where the city allows nightly rentals, so most of your neighbors there will be guests. Only about 1 in 18 condos there is someone's main home.

Off the oceanfront, where nightly rentals are not allowed, about 1 in 3 condos is its owner's main home. Those condos resold for about $270,000 in the middle, more than oceanfront condos, but there were only a few dozen sales. The oceanfront buildings are next.

Can you rent a home by the night in Myrtle Beach?

Most condos, yes. Almost every house, no. Inside the City of Myrtle Beach, nightly rentals are legal only in the zones where the city allows them, and any stay shorter than 90 days counts.

Those zones include most of the oceanfront hotel and condo strip, the Broadway at the Beach area, stretches of the US 17 Bypass and US 501, and The Market Common's town center. They are the orange areas on the map.

The city bans nightly rentals in every zone for homes, except one zone set aside for visitor stays. A house outside the allowed zones cannot be switched over to nightly rentals. Breaking the rule is a misdemeanor, with a fine, jail time or both for each violation.

Fewer than 30 houses in the zones for homes may still be rented by the night, because they were rented that way before the current zoning. No new ones can be added.

If a listing says short-term rentals are allowed, can you rent it nightly?

Not always. 35 houses and townhouses for sale in the city say so in the listing. We checked each of them against our map, and 3 are not in an allowed zone. Many HOAs also ban rentals of any length. We check the zone and the HOA's rules for any home you like, and we leave out the homes where your plan is not allowed.

What does a nightly rental need?

A city business license. Every rental in the city needs one, short or long, and its fee is based on the rent you collect.

Guests pay about 13 percent in lodging taxes on top of each stay under 90 days. When a guest books through Airbnb, Airbnb collects these taxes for the state, the county and the city. On a booking you take yourself, you collect and pay them.

What are oceanfront condos like in Myrtle Beach?

About 460 condos in oceanfront buildings inside the city resold in the past year. They range from hotel-style studios to three-bedroom condos. For a one-bedroom on the oceanfront, plan on about $160,000, the middle asking price today.

Building by building, the fees, sale prices and units for sale are on our oceanfront condo buildings page.

The lowest prices are for studios of about 400 square feet at Tides, Driftwood and Oasis. Many of these buildings have a rental program for their owners.

Most buyers here live out of state. About 3 in 4 buyers of these oceanfront condos have their tax bill mailed outside South Carolina.

Can you get a loan on an oceanfront condo?

Yes, but in many of these buildings not the most common kind. Fannie Mae will not back a loan in a building managed like a hotel: with a front desk, daily cleaning, or a rule that owners put their unit in the rental program.

Our preferred lender can tell you which loans work in the building you like. At closing, buyers usually also pay an HOA transfer fee and a few months of HOA dues in advance.

Does an oceanfront condo in Myrtle Beach pay for itself?

At typical figures, it covers its running costs and property tax, with about $8,100 a year left. That money must pay the HOA fee, a policy on the unit itself and any loan.

Are Myrtle Beach home prices dropping?

For condos, yes. The typical condo value in Myrtle Beach is about 14 percent below its high in 2024, and it is still falling. For houses, no: the typical house value has stayed about the same since 2022.

Condos also sell far more slowly than houses. At the past year's pace, it would take about a year and a half to sell every condo for sale now.

About 18 monthsto sell every condo for sale in the city, at the past year's pace
About 4 monthsto sell every house and townhouse for sale in the city

That gives a condo buyer room to negotiate. On the house side, there are fewer homes for sale than buyers in a year.

In the past year, condos in the city resold for about $175,000 in the middle: half sold for more, half for less. Houses resold for about $540,000.

Is a Myrtle Beach address in the City of Myrtle Beach?

Usually not. About 3 in 4 houses and buildings with a Myrtle Beach mailing address are outside the city limits. Outside the city, the county's rental rules apply, not the city's.

Carolina Forest and Forestbrook are almost all outside the city, and Socastee is entirely outside it. Our Carolina Forest guide covers that area.

North Myrtle Beach is a separate city. A short-term rental there needs a business license, and for now there is no special zoning or permit process beyond it. Our North Myrtle Beach guide covers it.

What do property tax and flood insurance cost in Myrtle Beach?

Inside the city, a second home or rental pays almost four times the property tax of a home you live in. You apply for the lower rate on the home you live in, and our property tax guide shows how.

Will you need flood insurance?

For most houses, no. Only about 1 in 23 houses in the city is in the high-risk flood zone of FEMA, the Federal Emergency Management Agency. About 1 in 4 condos is, many of them at the ocean's edge.

In the high-risk zone, your lender will require flood insurance. On a house there, it usually costs about $800 a year. Outside that zone, federal rules do not require it, and a policy usually costs about $600.

A condo's HOA insures the building itself and adds the cost to the HOA fee.

What are the pros and cons of Myrtle Beach?

The good points are the house neighborhoods, the beach and the price of a condo. The hard ones are the condo market, the season and the flood zone at the ocean.

Pros

  • The house neighborhoods are mostly owners' main homes.
  • The beach, the Boardwalk, golf, a large hospital and the airport are all inside the city.
  • Condos cost far less than houses, a way to own at the beach for less.
  • More people live in the city every year.

Cons

  • Condo prices are falling, and condos take far longer to sell than houses. We help you pick the building and negotiate the price.
  • Rental income comes mostly in summer. We plan the year with you before you buy.
  • Many oceanfront condos are in the high-risk flood zone. We check the flood zone and the building's insurance for you.
  • Most houses cannot be rented by the night. We check the zone of any address you send us.

Common questions

Myrtle Beach real estate FAQ

Can you do short-term rentals in Myrtle Beach?

Inside the city, only in the zones where the city allows them, which hold most condos and almost no houses. The city bans rentals under 90 days in every zone for homes, except one zone set aside for visitor stays.

Is Myrtle Beach a buyer's market?

For condos, yes: there are about 18 months of condos for sale, and prices are falling. For houses, no: there are about 4 months of houses for sale.

Is Carolina Forest in Myrtle Beach?

No. Carolina Forest has a Myrtle Beach address, but only about 1 in 75 houses and buildings in its ZIP code, 29579, is inside the city.

What is the property tax on a second home in Myrtle Beach?

About $1,528 a year for every $100,000 of the county's value inside the city. A home you live in pays about $404.

What is the difference between Myrtle Beach and North Myrtle Beach?

Myrtle Beach and North Myrtle Beach are two separate cities with different rental rules. Myrtle Beach allows nightly rentals only in some zones. North Myrtle Beach, for now, asks only for a business license, with no special zoning or permit process beyond it.
