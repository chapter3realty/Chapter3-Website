The short answer

Riverwalk is a community of 321 condos on Riverwalk Drive in Myrtle Beach, beside Arrowhead Country Club and the Intracoastal Waterway. The land is outside the city limits. In the past year 22 condos sold, and the middle price was $208,500.

Homes for sale list a monthly HOA fee of about $315 to $495, by the size of the condo. On most listings the fee covers trash, lawn care and pest control. The original buildings sold for less than Riverwalk II.

What is it like to own a condo at Riverwalk?

Riverwalk sits next to Arrowhead Country Club, a public golf course with 27 holes, designed by Raymond Floyd and Tom Jackson. The county's map puts the course's land about 600 feet from the middle of the condos.

The other edge is water. We measured every condo's distance to the waterway on the county's map. Of the 321 condos, 66, one in five, sit within about 80 feet of the Intracoastal Waterway's mapped edge.

The map shows distance, not a view or a dock. Use it to pick the condos to ask about first. Whether a condo has a water view is in its listing, and we confirm it with you. On November 14, 3 Riverwalk condos listed as waterfront.

Of the 13 Riverwalk homes listed on November 14, 11 name a community pool and 9 a clubhouse.

A normal week here means a round at Arrowhead, a boat ride on the Intracoastal and Publix for groceries. The beach is not a walk: it is about 17 minutes by car to the closest access with parking.

The three parts of Riverwalk

Riverwalk was built in parts. The first buildings went up from 1997 to 1999. Riverwalk II came later, and the two Riverwalk II condos for sale are three-bedrooms. The townhomes were built in 2022 and list their own, lower fee.

How much are condos at Riverwalk Myrtle Beach?

In the past year 22 Riverwalk condos sold, from about $140,000 to $325,000. The middle price was $208,500, and half the sales fell between about $175,000 and $250,000.

What is the HOA fee at Riverwalk?

The HOA fee at Riverwalk goes by the size of the condo. This is what the homes for sale list, with a year of fees beside each.

  • One-bedroomAbout $315 a month, $3,800 a year
  • Two-bedroomAbout $400 a month, $4,800 a year
  • Three-bedroomAbout $495 a month, $5,900 a year

Every one of the 13 listings says the fee covers trash and lawn care. Most also name pest control, water and sewer, and the building's insurance. Fewer name cable or internet, and none names the power bill.

The yearly totals are before the power bill, your own condo insurance and property tax. Our property tax page explains the two tax rates.

The listing agent types the fee and what it covers, so the association's own budget is the real source. We get it first.

Was Riverwalk in a lawsuit?

Yes. The first buildings were built from 1997 to 1999. In 2003 the condo association sued the builder, and the builder had closed by then. A trial began in January 2009, and in 2011 the Court of Appeals affirmed.

We read both court opinions. In 2017 the state Supreme Court decided what the builder's insurer owed, in its last ruling. The buildings still sell: 22 condos sold in the past year. Our condos in litigation page explains what a suit in a building's past means for a buyer.

Is Riverwalk inside the City of Myrtle Beach?

No. The mail says Myrtle Beach, but the county's map shows no city at Riverwalk. The land is outside the city limits, in Horry County, as Plantation Point is.

The county does not collect trash at the curb outside the towns. That makes the fee's trash line matter: it is on all 13 listings. Our Myrtle Beach guide covers what changes inside the city.

Is Riverwalk in a flood zone?

We checked every condo's land against FEMA's map. It shows a high-risk strip along the water, the zone where a lender requires flood insurance. The strip touches the land of 12 of the 321 condos. A lower-risk zone, the 0.2 percent chance zone, touches 69 more. The rest touch neither.

A zone touching the land does not put the building in it, and the map does not show a condo's own floor. We check each condo's zone first. Our pages on coastal insurance and checking a flood zone show what to ask.

Can you rent a condo at Riverwalk?

12 of the 13 listings say rentals are allowed, and 9 say short-term rentals are allowed. The association's own rules and any minimum stay are the real source.

The county taxes 209 of the 321 condos at the 6 percent rate, the rate for a home the owner does not live in full time. It taxes 112 at the 4 percent rate for a home you live in. Our short-term rental rules page and Airbnb income page cover renting by the night.

Common questions

Riverwalk at Arrowhead FAQ

Where is Riverwalk in Myrtle Beach?

Riverwalk is on Riverwalk Drive, zip 29579, beside Arrowhead Country Club and the Intracoastal Waterway. It is about 17 minutes by car from the closest beach access with parking.

Is this the Riverwalk in Conway?

No. The Riverwalk in Conway follows the Waccamaw River in that city. This page is about the Riverwalk condos on Riverwalk Drive in Myrtle Beach.

Are the Riverwalk townhomes the same community?

The townhomes sit next to the condos but are a separate part, built in 2022. The townhomes for sale list a fee of about $185 a month, lower than the condos'.

Do Riverwalk condos accept FHA loans?

Of the 13 Riverwalk homes listed on November 14, 7 say FHA is accepted, 5 say VA and 12 say conventional. Each condo is its own case, and our condos in litigation page explains the FHA rule for a building with a suit in its past.

Do I need flood insurance at Riverwalk?

A lender requires it only in FEMA's high-risk zone, which touches the land of 12 of the 321 condos. We check the unit's own zone before you offer.
