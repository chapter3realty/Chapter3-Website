Oak Forest, Myrtle Beach

Oak Forest homes in Myrtle Beach:
what a house costs to own, and which houses touch the flood zone.

By Devin Day, Operations Officer · Reviewed by Tim Nash, Broker-in-Charge · Updated November 15, 2026

Oak Forest is 185 detached houses on small lots beside the Highway 17 Bypass, with one street where no house touches FEMA's high-risk flood zone.

The short answer

Oak Forest is a neighborhood of 185 detached houses on small lots on the south side of Myrtle Beach, close to the US 17 Bypass and Socastee Boulevard. The land is outside the city limits, in Horry County.

The homes for sale come first below. After them: what houses sold for, the yearly tax, the flood map, the HOA and the drives.

What is Oak Forest, and where is it?

The mail says Myrtle Beach and the ZIP code is 29577. OpenStreetMap calls the area Back Gate.

Three streets, Temperance, Morlynn and Triumph Drive, hold nearly every lot.

A typical lot is about 6,300 square feet. A typical house is about 1,900 square feet under roof, and that counts the garage.

Oak Forest Lane, where Costco and Best Buy stand, is a different place with the same name. It is a shopping area a few miles away, not part of this neighborhood.

What do homes in Oak Forest sell for?

In the past year the county recorded 14 sales of Oak Forest houses with a stated price. The middle price was about $258,000, and half of them sold for $254,000 to $282,000.

On November 14, 2026, 3 houses were for sale here, asking $242,500 to $275,000. All three are detached, with 3 or 4 bedrooms.

Sale prices: Horry County deeds recorded in the past 12 months. Asking prices: CCMLS listings, counted in aggregate.

If you own here and want to sell, our home value page explains how we price a home from recorded sales.

What does it cost to own a house in Oak Forest?

Take a house bought at the typical price, $258,000. Besides the mortgage and insurance, the property tax is about $1,040 a year if it is your full-time home. As a second home or a rental it is about $3,200 a year.

The county counts 4% of the price for an owner's primary residence and 6% for any other home. It then applies its 2026 tax rate and adds a flat fee of about $90. Our property taxes guide explains each step.

We checked this arithmetic against 13 real 2026 tax bills for Oak Forest houses. It matched 11 to the cent. The other two were lower, so some owners pay less. Owners who bought years ago often do: the full-time-home bills we read ran from about $370 to about $1,110 a year.

The HOA fee on the three homes for sale is $30 a month. The listings do not say what it covers, so before you offer, we ask the association.

Is Oak Forest in a flood zone?

Part of it is. FEMA's map puts a strip of its high-risk zone across the neighborhood, the zone where a lender requires flood insurance. The roof outlines of 29 of the 185 houses touch it: 17 on Temperance Drive and 12 on Morlynn Drive. On Triumph Drive, no house touches it.

FEMA also shades a wider zone with a 1 in 500 chance of flooding each year. Our flood zone check guide explains both.

A zone does not show whether a house has flooded. We check each house's flood history before you offer. Our page on flooding a seller may not mention explains how.

Does Oak Forest have an HOA, and what are its rules?

Yes. The association is Oak Forest Homeowners Association Inc, and the Oak Forest homes for sale each list a fee of $30 a month. We do not yet know what that fee covers.

Is Oak Forest inside the City of Myrtle Beach?

No. The mail says Myrtle Beach, but the land is outside the city limits, in Horry County. Three things change for an owner:

  • Property taxThe bills we read show county and school tax and no city tax. The yearly figures above already count that.
  • TrashThe county does not collect trash at the curb. Owners hire a private hauler or use a county recycling and convenience center. The county's 25 centers are open only to residents outside the towns.
  • City parking decalThe city's resident parking decal is only for cars kept inside the city limits that pay city vehicle tax. Oak Forest owners do not qualify.

Our Myrtle Beach guide covers the city's own neighborhoods and what changes inside its limits.

How far is Oak Forest from the beach, Costco and the airport?

Everything on this list is a drive. The beach is too far to walk, about an hour and a half on foot. The Market Common, Coastal Grand Mall and Costco are the closest of the places below.

Other neighborhoods we cover, nearest first: Riverwalk in Myrtle Beach, Windsor Plantation near Myrtle Beach and River Oaks in Carolina Forest. Then Myrtle Beach Golf and Yacht Club, Plantation Point in Myrtle Beach and The Preserve at Pine Lakes in Myrtle Beach. Then Wild Wing Plantation in Conway and Grande Dunes in Myrtle Beach. Farther south: Willbrook Plantation in Pawleys Island.

Common questions

Oak Forest FAQ

Is Oak Forest the same as Oak Forest Lane?

No. Oak Forest Lane is the Costco and Best Buy shopping area, a few miles from these houses. The Oak Forest neighborhood has three streets: Temperance, Morlynn and Triumph Drive.

Does Oak Forest have a pool or a clubhouse?

Few houses have a pool: the county's building map shows an in-ground pool at 3 of the 185. The shared parcels the county lists carry names like Tract C and Open Space. None is named for a clubhouse or a pool.

How old are the houses in Oak Forest?

The three houses for sale were built in 1998, 1999 and 2014. The county's public map has no year built, so we cannot give the age of the rest.

Can I rent out a house in Oak Forest?

County zoning has no limit on how long a home here may be rented. The recorded rules can have one, so we pull them for the house you pick.

Do I need flood insurance in Oak Forest?

A lender requires it on a house in FEMA's high-risk zone. In Oak Forest, 29 houses have a roof outline that touches the zone. We check the zone and the flood history of the house you like before you offer.
