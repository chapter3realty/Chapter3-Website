The short answer

Kingston Plantation is a resort-style community of about 1,360 condos in 11 buildings, on the ocean in Myrtle Beach. Listing agents describe it as gated.

In the past year 83 condos sold here, at a middle price of $435,000. The building matters most, and the table below shows each one.

What is Kingston Plantation?

Kingston Plantation is a community of condo buildings behind one name, on the beach at the north end of Myrtle Beach. Listing agents put it at about 145 acres, with half a mile of beach.

In the photo, Margate Tower, Brighton Towers, South Hampton and North Hampton stand along the sand. The other buildings sit behind them among lakes and trees.

The resort's own site lists tennis and pickleball courts, a spa, a golf simulator, waterslides and a free shuttle across the property. It also lists a Hilton and an Embassy Suites hotel. Some of those amenities are for hotel guests or cost owners extra, so ask before you count on one.

Five buildings sold enough condos in the past year to show a middle price. The chart shows what each one's condos went for.

Margate Tower

Margate Tower has the highest middle price, $990,000. Seven are for sale now, from $640,000 to $1.69 million, all with three or four bedrooms. Margate is the newest of the 11 buildings, by the years on the listings.

Brighton Towers

Brighton Towers condos sold at a middle of about $580,000. Ten are for sale, from $519,000 to $719,000. It is in the county's moderate flood zone, which the flood section explains.

South Hampton

South Hampton condos sold at a middle of about $430,000. Thirteen are for sale, from about $410,000 to $629,000. It is the one building in a high-risk flood zone.

Richmond Park

Richmond Park had the most sales, at a middle of $245,000. That is the lowest middle price of the five buildings in the chart. Twelve are for sale, from $225,000 to about $480,000.

West Hyde Park

West Hyde Park condos sold at a middle of $420,000, and its listings start at $210,000. The six smaller buildings sold too few for a middle price, and the table has their counts.

The county also lists a group of condo parcels called Kingston Lakes, all under one owner name. None sold in the past year and none is for sale, so they are left out of every count here. The resort's website lists Royale Palms, with one- to three-bedroom condos, as a separate property, and it is not counted either.

From 3 to 11 condos sold in a month, by the county deed index.

On November 14, 2026, 63 condos were for sale, asking $210,000 to $1.69 million. They have one to four bedrooms, and most have two or three.

What is the HOA fee at Kingston Plantation?

Monthly fees on current listings are about $500 to $2,100. The fee follows the building: Richmond Park fees are about $700 to $1,300, and Margate Tower fees about $1,500 to $2,100.

  • Two layersThe resort's owner manual describes an association for each building, called a regime, and a Kingston Plantation Master Association above it. The manual is an older document, so we confirm the current setup.
  • What the fee pays forThe listing agent enters the fee. Each association decides what it covers, so we get that in writing for the building you pick.
  • The resort amenitiesListing agents write that some are only through the hotel rental program. They write that others cost owners extra, such as the fitness center, indoor pool, water park and courts. We find out which apply to your condo.

Can you rent out a Kingston Plantation condo?

The resort runs a rental program for owners. We pull the agreement with the association papers.

County zoning has no limit on how long a home outside the towns may be rented. A building's rules or the master association's rules can have one. Our short-term rental rules guide covers renting by the night.

Is Kingston Plantation in a flood zone?

It depends on the building. On the county's FEMA map, South Hampton's 143 condos are in the high-risk zone, the zone where a lender requires flood insurance. Brighton Towers and North Hampton, and one Richmond Park condo, are in the moderate zone. The rest are outside the mapped zones. We checked each building against that map.

  • Check the buildingWe place each building by the middle of its parcel, and a building can sit across a zone line. A lender and an insurer decide by the building. Our coastal insurance guide explains what they look at.
  • Flood historyA zone does not show whether a condo has flooded. Our page on flooding a seller may not mention explains how we check.

Is Kingston Plantation in Myrtle Beach or North Myrtle Beach?

Myrtle Beach. Every listing here has a Myrtle Beach mailing address and the zip code 29572. The land is outside every town's limits, in Horry County. We checked the county's town map at the middle of the community, and no town is there. Three things change for an owner:

  • Property taxThe bill has county and school tax and no city tax. The yearly figures below already count that.
  • TrashThe county does not collect trash at the curb.
  • City parking decalThe city's resident parking decal needs a car registered in the city, so Kingston Plantation owners do not qualify.

The drive from the middle of the community is short to Barefoot Landing and the hospital, and longer to the mall and the airport.

Our Myrtle Beach guide covers the city's own neighborhoods and what changes inside its limits.

What does a Kingston Plantation condo cost a year to own?

Property tax on a condo at the middle price of $435,000 is about $1,700 a year as your primary residence. As a second home or rental it is about $5,300 a year. Most bills also include a flat county fee of about $90. That is on top of the mortgage, insurance and the HOA fee.

The county uses the lower rate only for the home an owner lives in full time. It does that for 158 of the 1,364 condos here, or 12%.

Common questions

Kingston Plantation FAQ

Is Kingston Plantation in North Myrtle Beach?

No. Its mailing address is Myrtle Beach, and the land is outside every town's limits, in Horry County.

Is Kingston Plantation gated?

Listings describe it as a gated community. The association papers we pull for a building say how the gate works for owners and guests.

Is there a rental program at Kingston Plantation?

Yes. The resort runs a rental program for owners, and we pull its current agreement with the association papers.

Can you walk to the beach from Kingston Plantation?

Margate Tower, Brighton Towers, South Hampton and North Hampton stand along the beach in the photo above. The other buildings are farther back, and we measure the walk from the one you like.

How far is Kingston Plantation from the airport?

About 25 minutes by car, 14 miles, with no traffic. The hospital is about 9 minutes away and Broadway at the Beach about 15.
