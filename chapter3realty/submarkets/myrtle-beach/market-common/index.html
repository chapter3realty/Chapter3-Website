The Market Common, Myrtle Beach

Market Common homes in Myrtle Beach:
a town center on the old air base.

By Devin Day, Operations Officer · Reviewed by Tim Nash, Broker-in-Charge · Updated November 8, 2026

The Market Common: shops, a lake and a movie theater on the old air base, with about 3,000 homes in the neighborhoods around them.

The short answer

The Market Common is a town center on the old Myrtle Beach Air Force Base. It has about 17 places to eat and drink, shops, a movie theater and a lake. About 3,000 homes stand on its streets and in the neighborhoods around it. The beach is about 5 minutes away by car.

In the past year a typical house there resold for about $525,000, and a townhome for about $375,000. Most houses list an HOA fee of $150 a month or less. Just 62 of about 3,000 homes are in FEMA's high-risk flood zone. You will hear planes, but no home is in the area the airport's map marks as too loud for homes.

What is it like to live in The Market Common?

The town center has shops and restaurants along its streets, with a lake and two parks beside them. Its own directory lists about 17 places to eat and drink, among them P.F. Chang's, Tupelo Honey, Gordon Biersch, CO Sushi, Travinia and Dirty Don's Oyster Bar.

It also has a movie theater, a Barnes & Noble, a bowling and billiards hall, a children's museum, a gym and a yoga studio. Shops include Anthropologie, Pottery Barn, Chico's and J.Jill.

Grand Park, with its lake and the path around it, and Valor Park sit beside the shops. They have sports fields, picnic areas, fishing spots and Savannah's Playground, built for children of all abilities.

A normal week at the town center

The town center's calendar has something on most days. In a normal week it lists:

  • MondaysA mystery movie night at the theater.
  • Tuesdays and ThursdaysFree trivia nights at the bowling hall, and on Tuesdays at a restaurant too.
  • Wednesdays and SaturdaysMorning tours on electric bikes from the bike shop.
  • SaturdaysA farmers market at Valor Park, from April into December.
  • Some eveningsLive music at restaurants, such as jazz at Gio's and a band at Gordon Biersch each second Tuesday.
  • Twice a yearArt in the Park is held at Valor Park, in April and in November.

Which homes are a walk from the shops?

The homes on the town center's own streets are about an 8-minute walk from the shops, measured from the middle of those streets to Valor Park. From the middle of the larger neighborhoods it is a 20- to 35-minute walk, and about 50 minutes from Belle Harbor.

  • A city gym and a collegeFrom Valor Park, the Crabtree Gymnasium is about 11 minutes on foot, and Horry-Georgetown Technical College's Grand Strand campus about 18.
  • GroceriesThe Food Lion on South Kings Highway is about a 25-minute walk, just over a mile, or about 5 minutes by car.

What do homes cost in The Market Common?

In the past year a typical house in The Market Common resold for about $525,000: half sold for less and half for more. The middle half sold between about $475,000 and $567,000. A condo resold for about $310,000.

A townhome resold for about $375,000 across the whole area. In the town center it was about $400,000, and in Emmens Preserve about $380,000. The neighborhood list above gives each one's house price.

Resales recorded in Horry County deeds in the past year. A neighborhood's price is given when it had 10 or more sales.

New homes cost more. The only builder sales in the past year were 9 townhomes on the town center's streets. They sold for about $580,000, about $180,000 more than a resale townhome on the same streets.

On November 8, 2026, 66 homes were for sale in the area: 43 houses, 20 townhomes, 2 condos and 1 lot.

What does a Market Common home cost to own each year?

Besides the mortgage and insurance, a typical house here that you live in costs up to about $3,900 a year in property tax and HOA fee. A town center townhome costs about $6,400 to $7,600 a year, mostly because its HOA fee is higher.

About, as your main home: a house bought for about $525,000 and a town center townhome bought for about $400,000.

The fees are what the homes for sale list: most houses $150 a month or less, and town center townhomes about $400 to $500. Before you offer, we get the HOA's budget for the home you pick, so you know what the fee covers.

Inside the City of Myrtle Beach, a home you live in pays about $404 a year in property tax for every $100,000 of the county's value. A second home or rental pays about $1,528, about 3.8 times as much.

When a home sells, its county value becomes about the price a buyer pays, so your tax starts from your price. The lower rate needs an application to the county. Our property tax guide explains both rates.

Is The Market Common in a flood zone?

Almost none of it is. Only 62 of about 3,000 homes in The Market Common sit in FEMA's high-risk flood zone, all of them houses:

  • Cresswind38 houses.
  • Balmoral14 houses.
  • The town center10 houses, on its own streets.

No other neighborhood has a home in the zone. At all 62, a big flood can bring rising water, but not ocean waves. A mortgage from a federally backed or regulated lender on a home in that zone requires flood insurance.

Another 30 homes are in the moderate zone, where a lender does not require flood insurance. Our page on flooding a seller may not mention explains how we look into a home's flood history.

How loud is the airport next to The Market Common?

You will hear planes here, because the airport's runway runs along the town center's east side. Even so, no home in The Market Common is inside the area the airport's own map marks as too loud for homes. The FAA treats homes outside that area as suitable next to an airport, with no limits.

We placed every home's lot on the airport's map, for today and for its forecast in 2038. The closest home is about a quarter mile outside the loud area, on the town center's east edge.

The loud area runs along the runway and out past both ends, where planes take off and land. A small loud spot also sits around a helicopter pad near US 17 Business, at the runway's south end, used for oceanfront helicopter flights.

The airport's map uses a yearly average. A single takeoff is louder than the average, and planes are heard across the area.

  • The town centerThe closest part. Its nearest home is about a quarter mile from the loud area, and a little over half a mile from the runway's center line.
  • The neighborhoods to the westThe nearest home in them is about 0.9 mile from the loud area. In Belle Harbor and the Sanctuary section of Withers Preserve, at the west end, the nearest is about 1.8 miles.

How far is The Market Common from the beach?

The beach is about 5 minutes by car with no traffic, at the city's beach access at 29th Avenue South, which is marked fully accessible. On foot it is about 45 minutes. Summer traffic on US 17 adds time to every drive.

What was The Market Common before?

The Market Common stands on the old Myrtle Beach Air Force Base, which closed in 1993. The town center opened on part of it in 2008.

The Air Force has closed 229 cleanup sites on the old base since the 1990s. In 2019 it said 15 were still open, among them old landfills and groundwater sites. It said every site is being cleaned up to the standard for homes, and that no limit on the soil or groundwater rules out homes there.

Three Air Force jets still stand in Warbird Park on Farrow Parkway: an F-100 Super Sabre, an A-7D Corsair and an A-10 Warthog. They are on permanent loan to the city from the Air Force.

Common questions

The Market Common FAQ

Is The Market Common part of Myrtle Beach?

Yes. Every home in it is inside the City of Myrtle Beach, so the city's zoning and tax rate apply. Nearly every home has a Myrtle Beach mailing address in ZIP code 29577.

Can I rent out a Market Common home by the night?

Only in the town center. The city's zones for rentals under 90 days include the town center's zoning district, but not the neighborhoods around it. Each HOA's rules also decide, and we read them for the home you pick.

Is Cresswind a 55-plus community?

Kolter Homes built Cresswind as what it called an active adult community, aimed at older buyers. Its rules on who may buy or live there are not published online. We read them for any Cresswind home you are thinking about.

Can I buy one of the apartments over the shops?

No. The apartments above the town center's shops are rented by the year. The area's 37 condos are in High Market, south of Valor Park.

Do the neighborhoods have pools?

On the 2023 aerial photo, seven of them have one. Cresswind has a clubhouse with a pool and courts. Emmens Preserve, Belle Harbor, Balmoral, Park Place and The Reserve each have a pool with a pool house or small clubhouse. Withers Preserve has a pool with a pool house in its Sanctuary section.
