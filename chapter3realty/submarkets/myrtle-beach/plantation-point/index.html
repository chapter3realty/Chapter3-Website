Plantation Point, Myrtle Beach

Plantation Point homes in Myrtle Beach:
half-acre lots, and half the houses have a pool.

By Devin Day, Operations Officer · Reviewed by Tim Nash, Broker-in-Charge · Updated November 14, 2026

Myrtlewood Golf Club, next to Plantation Point, serves food and drink to people who do not play, and it has a patio by the Intracoastal Waterway.

The short answer

Plantation Point is a neighborhood of 352 houses in Myrtle Beach, between the US 17 Bypass and the Intracoastal Waterway, near 38th Avenue North. The nearest public beach access is about 2.7 miles away.

In the past year a typical house here sold for about $785,000. No home for sale lists a monthly HOA fee. Across the golf course, The Landing at Plantation Point has 139 smaller houses that sold for about $280,000.

What is it like to live in Plantation Point?

The county's map shows an in-ground pool at 176 of the 352 houses here. Across Horry County, the same map shows about 1 in-ground pool for every 16 residential buildings. Our pool homes guide covers what a pool costs to keep.

Next door is Myrtlewood Golf Club, with two courses, PineHills and Palmetto.

"PineHills is widely regarded as the soul of Myrtle Beach golf, and its Myrtlewood sister, the Palmetto Course, as the heart."Play Golf Myrtle Beach

The clubhouse is across the course, a 10-minute drive. Our golf communities guide compares the area's golf neighborhoods.

A typical lot is about half an acre, and the smallest is about a third of an acre. The typical house is about 3,900 square feet under roof, garage included. The houses for sale this fall were built from 1987 to 2021.

Most are full-time homes: the county taxes about 85 in 100 houses here as their owner's primary residence.

Where a normal week takes you

The nearest groceries are at the Food Lion in Plantation Point Plaza, across the bypass at 38th Avenue North.

A little history

Myrtle Beach Farms, formed in 1912 and later Burroughs & Chapin, played a central role in the growth of Myrtle Beach. It recorded Plantation Point's restrictions in phases, starting in 1990.

Several streets share their names with old rice plantations in Georgetown County, such as Annandale, Hobcaw and Hopsewee.

Which Plantation Point lots are on the waterway and the golf course?

Every waterway lot is in Plantation Point itself, and the map shows where they are. Two of the waterway houses sold in the past year, for $1.17 million and $1.9 million.

Lots in both parts border the Myrtlewood courses. Empty lots are rare: the county map shows 14, and one on the course sold for $560,000 in the past year. The county zones these streets for single-family houses on lots of at least 10,000 square feet.

Before you offer on a waterway house, we find out what dock it has and what the rules allow. Our waterfront homes guide explains the dock rules on the Intracoastal.

What do homes in Plantation Point sell for?

In the past year 13 houses in Plantation Point sold, at a middle price of about $785,000. They ranged from about $375,000 to $1.9 million.

On November 13, 2026, 6 houses were for sale here, asking about $750,000 to $2.1 million.

Sale prices: Horry County deeds recorded in the past 12 months, houses only. Asking prices: CCMLS listings, counted in aggregate.

Besides the mortgage and insurance, property tax on a typical house here is about $2,900 a year as your primary residence. As a second home or rental it is about $9,500 a year. Most bills also include a flat county fee of about $90.

If you own here and want to sell, our home value page explains how we price a home from recorded sales.

Does Plantation Point have an HOA fee?

Not one of the 6 Plantation Point homes for sale lists a monthly fee, and 3 of them say there is no HOA at all. Before you offer, we find out what the association collects, if anything, for the house you pick.

  • The associationThe only parcel the county lists for Plantation Point HOA is a small common area by the US 17 Bypass.
  • The fee shown for a home for saleThe listing agent enters that fee, so we confirm it with the association.
  • The recorded rulesEach lot comes with recorded restrictions, the neighborhood's rules. The county's index of them is online, but the documents can only be read at the Register of Deeds in Conway. We pull them for the lot you like.
  • RentingCounty zoning has no limit on how long a home outside the towns may be rented. A subdivision's recorded rules can have one. Our short-term rental rules guide covers renting by the night.

Is Plantation Point in the City of Myrtle Beach?

No. Every house here has a Myrtle Beach mailing address. The land is outside the city limits, in Horry County. Three things change for an owner:

  • Property taxThe bill has county and school tax and no city tax. The yearly figures above already count that.
  • TrashThe county does not collect trash at the curb. Owners hire a private hauler or use a county recycling and convenience center. The county's 25 centers are open only to residents outside the towns.
  • City parking decalThe city's resident parking decal is only for cars kept inside the city limits that pay city vehicle tax. Plantation Point owners do not qualify.

Our Myrtle Beach guide covers the city's own neighborhoods and what changes inside its limits.

Is Plantation Point in a flood zone?

No house in Plantation Point or The Landing is in FEMA's high-risk zone, the zone where a lender requires flood insurance. We checked every house outline here against FEMA's map.

  • Along the waterA high-risk strip runs along the Intracoastal. It touches the waterway edge of 35 lots and stops short of the houses.
  • Flood historyA zone does not show whether a house has flooded. We check each house's flood history before you offer. Our page on flooding a seller may not mention explains how.

The Landing at Plantation Point: the same location for less

The Landing at Plantation Point is the older, smaller part of the neighborhood, near the US 17 Bypass. Its houses sold for about $280,000 in the past year, about a third of Plantation Point's middle price.

It is the part closer to the beach, and the Food Lion is about 3 minutes away by car.

Common questions

Plantation Point FAQ

Is there a Plantation Point golf course?

The course beside the lots is Myrtlewood Golf Club, with two courses, PineHills and Palmetto. Lots in both Plantation Point and The Landing border it.

Where is Plantation Point Plaza?

Plantation Point Plaza is the shopping center at the US 17 Bypass and 38th Avenue North, with a Food Lion. It is about 8 minutes by car from the middle of Plantation Point and about 3 from The Landing.

Can you walk to the beach from Plantation Point?

Not easily. The nearest public beach access is about 2.7 miles away, about an hour on foot or about 9 minutes by car.

Can I rent out a house in Plantation Point?

County zoning has no limit on how long a home here may be rented. The recorded restrictions can have one, so we pull them for the house you pick.

Do I need flood insurance in Plantation Point?

No house here is in FEMA's high-risk zone, the zone where a lender requires flood insurance. Where the zone touches 35 lots along the water, it stops short of the houses, so we check each house's flood history before you offer.
