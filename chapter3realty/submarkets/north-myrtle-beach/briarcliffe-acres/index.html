Briarcliffe Acres, South Carolina

Briarcliffe Acres:
a small town of acre lots, two lakes and a dune beach.

  • HousesAbout 250
  • LotsAbout an acre
  • LakesTwo
  • BeachNo cars

By Devin Day, Operations Officer · Reviewed by Tim Nash, Broker-in-Charge · Updated November 17, 2026

Briarcliffe Acres allows no car or golf cart on its beach. The nearest house is about 650 feet from the surf, past dunes, a tidal creek and salt marsh.

The short answer

Briarcliffe Acres is its own small town in Horry County, next to North Myrtle Beach. It has two lakes, a dune beach and a town hall of its own.

Below, you find what houses sold for, the tax, the rules for renting, the flood map and the beach.

What is it like to live in Briarcliffe Acres?

The lot is the point here. The middle lot is about an acre, and 102 of the 250 houses sit on an acre or more. The town's zoning asks for at least 40,000 square feet a lot, so the houses stand well apart.

Two lakes sit inside the town, about 10 acres in all. The town's plan calls them ponds and says residents fish and canoe on them. 76 of the houses have an in-ground pool.

Past the lots, the town's beach has no cars at all. People walk or ride a bicycle to the sand.

Where a normal week takes you

Barefoot Landing is about 5 minutes by car, and the medical center about 11.

Is Briarcliffe Acres oceanfront?

No. No house touches the beach. The middle house is a little over half a mile from the ocean, with the dunes, a tidal creek and marsh between. Our homes near the beach guide compares the places where a house is closer to the sand.

Is Briarcliffe Acres in North Myrtle Beach or Myrtle Beach?

Neither. Briarcliffe Acres is its own town in Horry County, with its own town hall and municipal court. The mail says Myrtle Beach, and North Myrtle Beach borders the town to the north.

  • Town Hall121 North Gate Road, ZIP code 29572, 843.272.8863.
  • Water and sewerThe City of Myrtle Beach lays and keeps the water lines. Most houses use a septic system.
  • Trash and recyclingThese are town services, run by a hauler.
  • The streetsThree entrances open to US 17: North, Middle and South Gate Road.

The Briarcliffe West condos on North Kings Highway are outside the town. Our North Myrtle Beach guide covers the city next door, and our Myrtle Beach guide covers the city its mail says.

What do homes in Briarcliffe Acres sell for?

In the past year 9 houses in Briarcliffe Acres sold, at a middle price of about $1,175,000. They ranged from about $815,000 to $4.65 million. One empty lot sold for $800,000.

On November 14, 2026, 2 houses were for sale in the town, asking about $875,000 and $1.05 million. See the houses for sale in the town.

Sale prices: Horry County deeds recorded in the past 12 months, houses only. Asking prices: CCMLS listings, counted in aggregate.

Besides insurance, property tax on a house at the typical sale price is about $5,300 a year as your full-time home. As a second home or rental it is about $15,500 a year, before any credit. About 4 in 5 houses here are taxed as the owner's full-time home. Read how the property tax rates work.

The bill has a town levy on top of the county and school tax, and a flat county fee of about $90.

Can you rent a house in Briarcliffe Acres?

Not for less than six months. The town's zoning code, as we read it, calls the rental of a single-family house for under six consecutive months a commercial activity and does not allow it. A nightly or weekly rental is out. A lease of six months or more is not covered by that rule.

Our short-term rental rules guide covers the towns around it, and our long-term rental page covers a lease. The town's code is posted on Municode.

  • Work at homeThe code allows no professional or business activity in a house in the single-family district.
  • The lotBuildings, garages and sheds included, may cover no more than 15 percent of the lot.
  • BoatsFor a boat kept more than 72 hours: under 25 feet is fine, one item in the front yard. A boat of 25 to 40 feet goes in the back or side yard, screened. A boat over 40 feet is not allowed in the town.

Is there an HOA in Briarcliffe Acres, and what about the cabana?

The town's plan names the Briarcliffe Acres Association as the owner of the town's recreation property and its old walking trails.

  • The trailsThe old walking trails are being closed.
  • The cabanaA beachfront cabana serves the residents.
  • The duesNeither of the 2 houses for sale lists an association fee. We have not found what the association charges or who may use the cabana.

Is Briarcliffe Acres in a flood zone?

Mostly not. FEMA's map puts the center of 221 of the 250 house lots outside every mapped flood zone.

24 lots have their center in FEMA's high-risk zone, the zone where a lender requires flood insurance, and 23 house outlines touch it. No lot is in the coastal wave zone.

A zone does not show whether a house has flooded. We check each house's flood history before you offer. Our page on flooding a seller may not mention explains how, and our flood zone check shows how to look up one address.

Where is the beach in Briarcliffe Acres, and can you drive on it?

No. The town's beach lies between Ocean Creek Resort to the north and the Meher Spiritual Center to the south. The town bans motor vehicles on it, golf carts included, all year. It is open to people on foot and on bicycles.

The nearest public beach access is North Myrtle Beach access no. 1, about 2.1 miles by road. That is about 6 minutes by car, or about 45 minutes on foot. We confirm who may use the town's own stretch of sand for the house you pick.

Other neighborhoods we cover, nearest first: Kingston Plantation in Myrtle Beach, Grande Dunes in Myrtle Beach and The Preserve at Pine Lakes in Myrtle Beach. Then Cherry Grove in North Myrtle Beach, Plantation Point in Myrtle Beach and River Oaks in Carolina Forest. Then Shaftesbury Glen near Conway and Wild Wing Plantation in Conway. Farther south: Willbrook Plantation in Pawleys Island.

Common questions

Briarcliffe Acres FAQ

Can you drive a golf cart on the beach in Briarcliffe Acres?

No. The town bans motor vehicles on its beach, golf carts included, all year. We have not confirmed the town's rules for golf carts on its streets.

Does Briarcliffe Acres have its own police?

The town has one full-time police chief, helped by Horry County Police and the South Carolina Highway Patrol.

What is Briarcliffe West?

Condos on North Kings Highway just outside the town. They are not part of Briarcliffe Acres.
