Willbrook Plantation, Pawleys Island

Willbrook Plantation homes in Pawleys Island:
seven in ten lots touch the golf course.

By Devin Day, Operations Officer · Reviewed by Tim Nash, Broker-in-Charge · Updated November 14, 2026

The golf course at Willbrook Plantation has water on 15 of its 18 holes, and the nearest public beach access is about 9 minutes away by car.

The short answer

Willbrook Plantation is a neighborhood in Pawleys Island, inside the gated community of Litchfield by the Sea, built around Willbrook Plantation Golf Club. Its main part has 189 houses. A typical lot is a little over half an acre, and 131 of the houses have lots that touch the golf course land. Tee times are booked on the club's website.

In the past year a typical house here sold for about $1.02 million. About one in three has part of its roof outline in FEMA's high-risk flood zone.

What is it like to live in Willbrook Plantation?

The lots are roomy, and so are the houses. A typical house has a roof outline of about 4,200 square feet, garage included. The aerial below shows houses spaced along a lake and a fairway.

The course next door is Willbrook Plantation Golf Club, a Dan Maples design, and the club sells memberships. Our golf communities guide compares the area's golf neighborhoods.

Willbrook is gated, and it sits inside Litchfield by the Sea. The association's page lists Willbrook as one of its sub-associations, with a clubhouse and an outdoor pool. Our gated communities guide covers the gate and the beach club.

Where a normal week takes you

The entrance is on Willbrook Boulevard at US 17, about 7 minutes from the middle of the neighborhood, with Litchfield Market Village beside it. Brookgreen Gardens is 15 minutes away, and the Murrells Inlet MarshWalk is 20.

A little history

A compiled history of local plantations says the land was one plantation of 2,400 acres in 1941. The Hunter heirs sold it to Litchfield-by-the-Sea in 1984.

The association's page says the Oatland slave cemetery, with 38 graves, remains a sacred and protected site within the community.

What is inside the name Willbrook Plantation?

The name covers three neighborhoods that differ in lot size, price and flood zone. The county's records call them Willbrook, Tradition Club at Willbrook and Mingo at Willbrook. Willbrook includes the Oak Point and Allston Point sections.

What do homes in Willbrook Plantation sell for?

In the past year at least 8 houses in Willbrook sold, from $775,000 to $1,375,000. Over three years, 18 sold at a middle price of about $995,000.

Tradition Club houses sold for about $570,000 in the past year, on lots of about a quarter acre. Mingo sold for about $490,000.

On a house at that middle sale price, the county's own calculator puts property tax at about $4,740 a year as your full-time home. As a second home or rental it is about $14,600 a year. Both include $96 in storm water and household fees.

Sale prices: Georgetown County's last recorded deeds, houses only; a few more show a nominal deed that can hide a sale, so the counts are a floor. Tax: an estimate on a sale price, not a bill.

Who runs the Willbrook Plantation HOA, and what does it cost?

Willbrook sits under the Litchfield by the Sea Community Association, which lists it as a sub-association. The county's records name five associations that own the roads, lakes and common areas inside the Willbrook name.

They are the Willbrook Plantation Community Association, the Willbrook Plantation Property Owners Association, Tradition at Willbrook, Mingo, and a road maintenance association.

Every home for sale here shows a monthly fee of $350 or less, and the lowest shows $250 or less. We confirm the fee with the association for the house you pick. See every Willbrook Plantation home for sale, or compare HOA fees across neighborhoods.

Is Willbrook Plantation in a flood zone?

About one in three Willbrook houses, 63 of 189, has part of its roof outline in FEMA's high-risk flood zone, the zone where a lender requires flood insurance. In Oak Point it is 6 of 7. No Tradition Club or Mingo house touches it.

  • House by houseA lender decides by the house, not the lot. A lot can touch the zone while the house stays out of it.
  • Flood historyA zone does not show whether a house has flooded. We check each house's flood history before you offer. Our page on flooding a seller may not mention explains how.

Is Willbrook Plantation part of the Town of Pawleys Island?

No. Every Willbrook address reads Pawleys Island, but the land is in unincorporated Georgetown County. None of the neighborhood's lots touches the limits of the Town of Pawleys Island, Andrews or Georgetown.

The tax bill uses the county's tax district 4, so the yearly figures above use the county's own rates. Our Pawleys Island guide covers the town and the mainland around it.

Common questions

Willbrook Plantation FAQ

Is Willbrook Plantation gated?

Yes. Litchfield by the Sea's page describes Willbrook as a private, gated community, and Willbrook sits inside Litchfield by the Sea.

Can I play the Willbrook Plantation golf course?

Tee times are booked on the club's website, and the club sells memberships. The club is part of Founders Group International, and its clubhouse is about 6 minutes by car from the middle of the neighborhood.

How far is Willbrook Plantation from the beach?

About 9 minutes by car. The nearest public beach access is about 4 miles away, about 80 minutes on foot.

Is Willbrook Plantation the same as Litchfield by the Sea?

No. Willbrook is one of the sub-associations inside Litchfield by the Sea, with its own neighborhoods, golf course and rules.

Are there empty lots in Willbrook Plantation?

The county's map shows 12 empty lots in the main part of Willbrook. We pull the recorded rules for any lot you like.

Where is the entrance to Willbrook Plantation?

On Willbrook Boulevard at US 17. Litchfield Market Village is beside it, and the middle of the neighborhood is about 7 minutes from the entrance by car.
