Surfside Beach, SC

Surfside Beach real estate:
life in a two-mile beach town, and where you can rent by the night.

  • BeachAbout 2 miles long
  • Concrete pier814 feet
  • Nightly rentalsNear the ocean
  • Airport15 min drive

By Devin Day, Operations Officer · Reviewed by Tim Nash, Broker-in-Charge · Updated November 14, 2026

Surfside Beach is a town of about 4,200 people with a free concrete pier, summer concerts in the park and a clear line where nightly rentals stop.

The short answer

Surfside Beach is its own town of about 4,200 people, on about 2 miles of beach south of Myrtle Beach. Every home is within about 0.8 mile of the beach road.

The town has a new concrete pier, free summer concerts and golf cart parades down Ocean Boulevard. Nightly rentals are allowed only on the blocks near the ocean. The inland streets, with bigger lots under the trees, are for living in or renting by the year.

What is it like to live in Surfside Beach?

The town calls itself the Family Beach. Its center is the pier, at the ocean end of Surfside Drive.

The pier

The town's fishing pier is all concrete and reaches 814 feet into the ocean. It opened on the town's 60th birthday, and walking on it is free.

The pier has two restaurants, The Whistling Duck and Surf Diner, plus an ice cream shop and a bait shop. A day of fishing is $14. From April through October it stays open until 1 a.m.

A year of town events

The town has its own events department, and many of its events are free. Many take place at the pier, on Ocean Boulevard or in Memorial Park.

  • SpringA two-day barbecue festival with a cook-off in March. A kite festival at the pier in April. The Memorial Day golf cart parade down Ocean Boulevard.
  • SummerA free concert in Memorial Park, next to the library, on Sundays from 4 to 7 p.m. The Fourth of July golf cart parade, free to join.
  • FallAn end-of-summer fireworks festival at the pier in September, with free shuttles from the town's parking lots.
  • All seasonAn arts and crafts market every Thursday from March to November, at Surfside Drive and Willow Drive. The Yuletide golf cart parade in December.

Golf carts

Most trips here need a car or a golf cart. To drive a cart on the street, South Carolina law requires a DMV permit decal, liability insurance and a licensed driver 16 or older.

Carts may use only roads posted at 35 mph or less, and never the sand. A cart driven at night must have working headlights and taillights. The town passed its own golf cart ordinance in 2025.

The beach

The town has 36 public beach accesses and 12 beach parking lots. Five accesses have wheelchair access, and beach wheelchairs are available through the town police department.

An owner who lives elsewhere gets two free parking passes per household for the town's lots. The town's lots charge by the hour from March through October. Dogs stay off the beach from 10 a.m. to 5 p.m., May through Labor Day.

Is Surfside Beach part of Myrtle Beach?

No. Surfside Beach is its own town in Horry County, incorporated in 1964, with its own council, police department and beach rules. Its mailing ZIP code is 29575.

A "Surfside Beach" address does not prove a home is in the town. Of the homes for sale with that address when we counted, about two in three were outside the town limits.

Those homes are on county land, where the town's rental rules do not apply. Horry County's zoning has no limit on how long a home there may be rented, but a subdivision's recorded rules can. We read those rules for the home you like.

How the town began. Much of its land was once the Ark Plantation. The beach was later called Roach's Beach, then Floral Beach, and took the name Surfside Beach in 1952. Hurricane Hazel in 1954 destroyed 18 of the 65 houses then at the beach.

What do you own at Ocean Lakes?

Ocean Lakes Family Campground is next to the town's north end, about 7 minutes from the pier by car, and it is not in the town. It has about 2,600 sites leased by the year.

At Ocean Lakes you buy the house, mobile home or camper on the site, not the land. The site comes with a yearly lease from the campground, and we go through it with you before you offer.

Where can you rent by the night in Surfside Beach?

Nightly rentals are allowed only on the blocks near the ocean, the orange band on the map. The town calls them its R3 and C3 zones, and its own zoning sheet names R3 "High Density/Weekly Rental".

North of the pier, the line follows Dogwood Drive North, and houses on that street fall on both sides. South of the pier, it follows Willow Drive South for about 0.3 mile, to about 6th Avenue South. From there it follows Lakeside Drive: houses on the ocean side of Lakeside are on the rental side, and houses on its inland side are not.

At the pier, the town's entertainment district breaks the band. Inland, the green streets reach back to the commercial strip on US 17 Business. On those streets the town allows rentals of a year or more, and none under 30 days.

The gray areas are planned developments and commercial land. Each planned development has its own approved uses, so we read the approval for the parcel.

What the line looks like from the air

The two sides look different. Near the ocean, tall beach houses stand close together on small lots. Inland, houses are farther apart, under the trees.

What do homes cost in Surfside Beach?

A house near the ocean costs about twice an inland house. In the past year, houses near the ocean sold for about $1 million at the middle, and inland houses for about $485,000 to $540,000.

The two sides are built to different rules. Near the ocean, the town allows taller buildings on smaller lots:

What does it cost to own a home in Surfside Beach each year?

Besides the mortgage and home insurance, the yearly cost here is mostly property tax and flood insurance. Both depend on where the house is and how you use it.

A buyer's tax starts from the county's value, which resets toward the price at the sale. Owners who bought years ago often pay less because of the state's 15 percent cap on reassessment.

Why a main home pays about a third as much

South Carolina does not tax the whole value of a home. For your main home, the tax rate applies to 4 percent of the county's value, and the school operating tax is skipped.

For a second home or rental, the rate applies to 6 percent of the value, with every tax. On the same home, that makes the tax about 3 times as much. Our property tax calculator has every town's rate.

Flood insurance near the ocean and inland

From the pier to the south end of town, most homes are on the rental side. There, a typical flood policy on a house costs about $1,250 a year. On the inland streets it is about $700.

FEMA takes 15 percent off flood insurance in the town, for the town's flood rules and programs.

Does Surfside Beach flood?

Inland, few homes are in a high-risk flood zone: 2 percent on the smaller-lot streets and 15 percent on the largest lots. Near the ocean it is most homes, and every home on the ocean side of Ocean Boulevard. To check one address, use our flood map check.

A federally backed or regulated mortgage on a home in a high-risk zone requires flood insurance.

The 48 percent rule

This town rule matters most for an older, low house. It covers a building whose lowest floor is below the 100-year flood level, the flood height on FEMA's map.

If repairs or improvements cost 48 percent or more of the building's value, the whole building must be raised. It goes up to that flood level plus 3 feet.

The town uses the county assessor's building value, land excluded, unless the owner submits a licensed appraisal. Half the town's homes were built before about 1985.

Before you offer on an older house near the ocean, we pull its flood zone and the county's building value. You then know how much work the rule allows before the house must be raised.

What the storms did

The pier was first built in 1953. Hurricanes Hazel and Hugo each destroyed it, and after Hugo it was not rebuilt until 1993. Hurricane Matthew in 2016 took more than half of its length.

Can you Airbnb a house in Surfside Beach, and what does it earn?

Yes, on the blocks near the ocean, with a town business license for each rental property. The owner also files the town's hospitality fee and local accommodations tax every month, even when nothing is due.

Guests pay about 12 percent in lodging taxes on top of the rent, to the state, the county and the town. You collect it and send it on.

What a Surfside Beach area rental takes in

AirROI, a rental data company, counts about 550 active Airbnb listings in its Surfside Beach market, which reaches past the town limits. The average one took in about $43,000 in gross bookings over the past year, before costs.

That was up about 7 percent on the year before. Most of the money comes in summer: the average listing takes in about five times as much in July as in January.

What are the pros and cons of living in Surfside Beach?

Pros

  • About 2 miles of beach with 36 public accesses.
  • A new concrete pier, free to walk, with two restaurants.
  • Free summer concerts in Memorial Park and golf cart parades on Ocean Boulevard.
  • Owners who live elsewhere park free in town lots with two passes.

Cons

  • Near the ocean, only about 1 home in 8 is taxed as its owner's main home. On the inland streets with the largest lots, about 7 in 10 are.
  • Flood insurance costs more near the ocean, and big work on a low house there can mean raising it. We check the zone and the building value first.
  • Two in three "Surfside Beach" listings are outside the town, under county rules. We check the town limits and the subdivision's rules for every listing.

Common questions

Surfside Beach, SC FAQ

Is Surfside Beach part of Myrtle Beach?

No. Surfside Beach is its own town in Horry County, incorporated in 1964, with the ZIP code 29575.

Can you Airbnb in Surfside Beach?

Only on the blocks near the ocean, in the town's R3 and C3 zoning districts. Rentals under 30 days are not allowed in the inland R1 and R2 districts.

Can you drive a golf cart in Surfside Beach?

Yes, on town streets posted at 35 mph or less, with a state permit decal, insurance and a licensed driver 16 or older. Never on the beach.

Are dogs allowed on Surfside Beach?

Not from 10 a.m. to 5 p.m., May 1 through Labor Day. At other times, leashed dogs are welcome.

Is parking free in Surfside Beach?

Town lots charge from March 1 to October 31, 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. An owner who lives elsewhere gets two free passes per household.

What is the property tax rate in Surfside Beach?

On a home the county values at $500,000, the town and county tax is about $2,100 a year as your main home. As a second home or rental, it is about $6,400.

What schools serve Surfside Beach?

Horry County Schools, which assigns schools by address. We look up the assigned schools for any home you like.

What about crime in Surfside Beach?

We do not rate places for crime. The town has its own police department, and the FBI publishes the crime each police agency reports.
