Murrells Inlet, SC

Murrells Inlet real estate:
room for patient buyers.

By Devin Day, Operations Officer · Reviewed by Tim Nash, Broker-in-Charge · Updated November 12, 2026

Murrells Inlet is the seafood village on a salt marsh south of Myrtle Beach, with the MarshWalk on the Georgetown side and golf neighborhoods on the Horry side.

The short answer

Murrells Inlet has two sides, split by the line between Horry and Georgetown counties. The Horry side has the golf neighborhoods, the new houses from builders and 7 in 10 of the homes. Its middle house price in the past year was about $450,000. The Georgetown side has the MarshWalk, the river landing and the houses on the creek. West of the US 17 bypass, its middle house price was about $570,000.

If you want a house on the creek, plan to wait: only about 7 sell in a year. Your tax bill, your schools and the paperwork to rent a house out depend on the county.

Is Murrells Inlet in Horry County or Georgetown County?

Both. Murrells Inlet is not a town, and its ZIP code, 29576, crosses the county line. Each county runs its own side. The address on the mail does not tell you the county, so we look it up for every home before you see it.

US 17 runs through Murrells Inlet as two roads: the US 17 bypass to the west, and Business 17 nearer the marsh. The map shows both, and the four parts of Murrells Inlet that the two roads and the county line make. Garden City, on the beach to the east, shares the ZIP code and has its own guide.

Where should you buy in Murrells Inlet?

Each of the four parts is a different way to live here. The numbers match the map above.

1The Horry side: golf neighborhoods and most of the homes

Most Murrells Inlet homes are here, north of the county line and west of the US 17 bypass. The neighborhoods are laid out around golf courses and ponds: the International Club, Blackmoor, the TPC of Myrtle Beach and Indigo Creek's course are all on this side.

The largest house neighborhoods are Indigo Creek, with about 560 houses, Seasons at Prince Creek West (a 55+ community), The Pines of St. James and Longwood Bluffs. Clients of ours sold their house in Blackmoor, a golf community with no age restriction, to buy in Seasons at Prince Creek West, an age-restricted community a few minutes away.

Publix and Lowes Foods are on SC 707, about 4 minutes from the middle of this side. The MarshWalk is about 9 minutes away.

Almost every house for sale here is in an HOA (homeowners association), and the middle fee was about $110 a month. Most of the houses with no HOA are on the Georgetown side. About 1 in 9 houses sold here in the past year was new from a builder. Townhouses and condos cost less, about $320,000 and $275,000.

2Georgetown, west of the bypass: Wachesaw, golf and the river

South of the county line and west of the bypass, the land runs down to the Waccamaw River. Boats go in at Wacca Wache Landing, off Wachesaw Road. On the river, the tide rises and falls about half as much as in the inlet.

The largest neighborhood is Wachesaw Plantation, with about 650 houses, then Linksbrook and Collins Creek. The Wachesaw Plantation Club and Wachesaw East golf courses are on this side. Prince Creek crosses the county line: Palm Bay is in Georgetown County, Seasons at Prince Creek West is in Horry, and Highwood is split between them.

The middle HOA fee on houses for sale here was about $150 a month.

3Between the bypass and Business 17: streets in the middle of the village

This narrow band is on the Georgetown side, between the US 17 bypass and Business 17. It has about 800 homes on streets of houses like the ones in the photo. Tidelands Waccamaw Community Hospital and a Food Lion are just west of the bypass. The MarshWalk, the restaurants and the creek are just east of Business 17.

A house here sold for about $530,000 at the middle in the past year, and a condo or villa for about $280,000. Some streets here are in FEMA's high-risk flood zone and some are not, so we look up the zone for each house before you offer.

4The creek side: houses on the salt marsh

East of Business 17, on the Georgetown side, houses line the edge of the inlet's salt marsh, many with a dock out to the creek. This is where the village began, and it is a National Register historic district. The county's named areas here include Sunnyside, the Hermitage and Vaux Hall. The MarshWalk's restaurants are on this side.

The creek side has about 370 homes. About 220 of them are houses on the edge of the marsh, and few of those come up for sale: 21 sold in the past three years. The middle one sold for about $930,000, about 1.7 times the middle house west of the bypass over the same years.

What is it like to live in Murrells Inlet?

The MarshWalk is a half-mile wooden boardwalk along the salt marsh, lined with restaurants such as Drunken Jack's, Dead Dog Saloon, Creek Ratz and The Wicked Tuna. Its live music is free to hear. Crazy Sister Marina and the county's Murrells Inlet boat landing are at its south end.

About 85 in 100 houses outside Garden City are taxed as their owner's main home.

Oysters in season

From October 1 into May, you can gather oysters and clams on the inlet's three public shellfish grounds with a South Carolina saltwater fishing license. They are the state grounds named Murrells Inlet and Drunken Jack Island, and a public ground at the old Lachicotte oyster factory. A resident's license costs $15 a year, and no license is needed under 16. There is a daily limit, on no more than two days in any seven, and parts of each ground are closed by the state.

The beach, groceries and the hospital

The creek side has no ocean beach. The nearest are Huntington Beach State Park, about 9 minutes from the MarshWalk, with 3 miles of beach and a freshwater lake known for alligators, and Garden City Beach, about 9 minutes away.

On the Horry side, Publix and Lowes Foods are on SC 707 and a Walmart Neighborhood Market is on the US 17 bypass. On the Georgetown side, a Food Lion is on the bypass near the hospital. Two of our own team chose to live in Murrells Inlet. One of them drives from here to Market Common and to a gym in Carolina Forest.

Can a creek-front house in Murrells Inlet have a dock?

Usually. South Carolina allows one dock per lot on the salt marsh, if the lot has enough marsh frontage and the creek in front of it is wide enough. Before you offer on a creek-front house, we pull the dock's permit and check the frontage and the creek's width.

Few houses for sale have a dock. Of the 465 listings with a Murrells Inlet address, Garden City included, listing agents marked a private dock on 9. Only 2 of those were on the creek side. A creek house with a dock does come up, but you may wait for one.

How much water is at the dock?

The tide in the inlet rises and falls about 4 feet on an average day, at NOAA's prediction station at Captain Alex's Marina. About 3 low tides in 10 drop below the low-water mark on the creek's charts. At a dock with little water, a boat can sit on the mud at those tides. We look at the dock at low tide before you offer. Our waterfront homes guide covers what else to look at.

Where can you launch a boat?

Georgetown County lists four boat landings in the Murrells Inlet ZIP code. The Murrells Inlet Landing is at the MarshWalk, Marina Colony is on Business 17 and Morse Landing is at the inlet's south end. Wacca Wache Landing is on the Waccamaw River.

Does Murrells Inlet flood?

Few homes west of the US 17 bypass are in FEMA's high-risk flood zone. Most homes on the creek side are. In that zone, a lender requires flood insurance.

On the creek side, about 1 in 9 of the houses on the marsh are in FEMA's V zone, where waves are expected in a big storm. A flood policy costs the most there.

What flood insurance costs here

The middle federal flood policy on a house cost about $590 a year on the Georgetown side and about $580 on the Horry side. On the Georgetown side, a house in FEMA's A zone, the high-risk zone away from the waves, paid about $1,080. A house in the V zone, where FEMA expects waves, paid about $2,160.

Most of the flood policies on Georgetown-side houses are on homes outside the high-risk zone. Before you offer, we pull the flood zone and an insurance quote for the house. Our pages on coastal insurance and finding out whether a house has flooded show what we look at.

Can you rent out a house in Murrells Inlet?

It depends on the HOA papers and the county zoning for that lot. An HOA can forbid or limit rentals in its papers, and almost every Horry-side house for sale is in one. We read the HOA papers and check the zoning before you offer.

On the Horry side, the county asks a nightly-rental owner for a business license and tax filings, and names no rental permit. Guests pay about 12 percent in lodging taxes. On the Georgetown side, the county issues no business license, and guests pay about 11 percent. The details are on our short-term rental rules page and our accommodations tax page.

What a Murrells Inlet Airbnb earns

AirROI, a company that tracks Airbnb listings, counts about 480 active listings in Murrells Inlet. Its count may take in Garden City Beach homes with a Murrells Inlet address. The average listing took in about $47,600 in gross bookings in the past 12 months, before costs. Most of that comes in summer.

How did Murrells Inlet become a seafood village?

Murrells Inlet began with the homes of two rice planters. Around 1850, Sunnyside was built for Jacob Motte Alston, and the Hermitage for Dr. Allard Belin Flagg, the master of Wachesaw plantation, on the edge of the marsh. A small settlement grew up along the marsh on their old lands.

Visitors first came for oyster roasts and fried shore dinners put on by the inlet's founding families, the Morses, Nances, Vereens, Olivers, Chandlers and Lees. That grew into the restaurants that earned the nickname "Seafood Capital." Hurricane Hugo struck the creek front in 1989, and the village rebuilt.

Georgetown County plans to dredge the inlet's creeks. In a report to the state, it estimated the cost at about $30 million.

Common questions

Murrells Inlet FAQ

What ZIP code is Murrells Inlet?

29576. It covers the Horry side, the Georgetown side and Garden City, so the ZIP code does not tell you the county.

Is Murrells Inlet part of Myrtle Beach?

No. No part of the Murrells Inlet ZIP code is inside Myrtle Beach or any other town.

Does Murrells Inlet have a beach?

Not on the creek side. Huntington Beach State Park and Garden City Beach are the nearest beaches.

What schools serve Murrells Inlet?

Horry-side homes are zoned for the St. James schools of Horry County Schools. Georgetown-side homes are zoned for the Waccamaw schools of the Georgetown County School District. We look up the assigned schools for any home you like.
