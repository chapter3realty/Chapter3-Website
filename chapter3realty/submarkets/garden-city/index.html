Garden City, SC

Garden City, SC: beach rentals with no city ordinance.
One beach road between the ocean and the salt marsh.

  • Beach roadAbout 5 miles
  • Fishing pier668 feet
  • MarshWalk8 min drive
  • Airport20 min drive

By Devin Day, Operations Officer · Reviewed by Tim Nash, Broker-in-Charge · Updated November 14, 2026

South of Myrtle Beach, people line dance at the end of the Garden City Pier, out over the ocean, and pull up blue crabs from docks on the marsh.

The short answer

Garden City, South Carolina is a beach community between Surfside Beach and Murrells Inlet. It is not a town: the county runs it, and most of its beach road is in Georgetown County, not Horry.

For a condo on the sand, look at the ocean side by the pier. For a house with a dock, look at the marsh and the Georgetown end. For a house lived in all year, out of the high-risk flood zone, look west of Business 17.

Where is Garden City, SC, and which county is it in?

Garden City sits on a strip of land between the Atlantic Ocean and a salt marsh, just south of Surfside Beach. Its beach road, Waccamaw Drive, runs down the strip to Garden City Point at the inlet. Business 17 runs inland of the marsh.

The Horry and Georgetown county line crosses the beach about a third of a mile south of the Garden City Pier. Most of Waccamaw Drive is south of the line, in Georgetown County. The county decides your property tax, your rental license and the beach rules.

Most addresses use Murrells Inlet and its ZIP code, 29576. A band at the north edge uses Surfside Beach.

Where to buy in Garden City, SC

Garden City has five parts, and each is a different way to live at the beach. The numbers match the map above.

1The ocean side of Waccamaw Drive: the beach and the pier

Here you walk out of your building onto the sand. Condo buildings and a few beach houses stand between Waccamaw Drive and the ocean, from the Surfside Beach line south past the pier. The largest buildings include Royal Garden Resort, Waters Edge Resort, Surfmaster and Atalaya Towers.

The pier is the center of this side. At the far end of the Garden City Pier, rebuilt after Hurricane Hugo took the old pier in 1989, the rain shelter holds a bandstand and a dance floor bar, and people learn line dances out over the ocean.

Its café serves breakfast. In summer, its deck bar has karaoke in the afternoons, and bands play every night at both ends of the pier, weather permitting. The arcade is there for a rainy day. A pier pass covers fishing, so no state fishing license is needed, and in summer you can fish around the clock.

Almost every home on this side is a condo. Condos here sold for about $320,000 at the middle in the past year.

2On the marsh: docks and tidal creeks

Behind the beach strip is salt marsh, cut by tidal creeks that reach the inlet at Garden City Point. The houses along its edges look out over the marsh, and many have a long private dock. On the beach strip they line Dogwood Drive. On the mainland side they are on streets like Creekside Drive and Rum Gully Road.

From a dock on the marsh, you can tie a chicken neck to a string and pull up blue crabs for dinner. In South Carolina you need no license to crab with three or fewer drop nets, fold-up traps or hand lines.

These are the most costly houses east of Business 17. The ones that sold in the past year went for about $1.24 million at the middle, about twice the other houses east of Business 17. Few come up for sale.

Get the marsh-front homes for sale in Garden City

3Between the marsh and Business 17: the most kinds of homes

The rest of the land between the marsh and Business 17 is streets of houses, small condo buildings and manufactured homes. Its neighborhoods include Salters Cove, Mount Gilead, Myrtle Dunes and Woodland Terrace. It also takes in the streets north of where Waccamaw Drive ends, up to the Surfside Beach line.

Every Fourth of July afternoon, Garden City holds a "wet" golf cart parade: decorated carts drive the route while people on the side soak them with water guns. It raises money for local first responders.

This part has the widest range of prices. Away from the marsh, a house sold for about $575,000 at the middle in the past year. Condos and manufactured homes cost less. Where a home is on rented land, the lot rent is not in the price, and we find it out for each home.

Get the homes for sale in Garden City east of Business 17

4West of Business 17: subdivisions lived in all year

West of Business 17 are Garden City's subdivisions, with houses on curving streets around ponds. The largest are Woodlake Village, Mallard Landing, Harbour Village and Jamestowne. Condo communities include Sweetwater at Indian Wells, South Bay Lakes, Southbridge and Tupelo Bay Golf Villas.

On the Fourth of July, decorated boats parade along the inlet in the afternoon while the crowd watches from the MarshWalk, and fireworks go up at night. In 2025 the boat parade was at 2 p.m. and the fireworks at 10 p.m. From the subdivisions west of Business 17, it is a short drive.

More people live here all year: about 3 in 4 houses are taxed as the owner's main home, the most of any part. A house sold for about $340,000 at the middle in the past year and a condo for about $173,000, the lowest prices in Garden City.

Get the homes for sale in Garden City west of Business 17

5The Georgetown end: canals, docks and the quietest beach

South of the county line, Waccamaw Drive runs on to Garden City Point, where the marsh meets the inlet. Houses line the beach, and behind them many sit on canals with private docks.

At the inlet end of Waccamaw Drive, Gulfstream Cafe has looked over Murrells Inlet for nearly 40 years. People go up to its rooftop deck to watch the sun set over the water, and come back for its oyster roasts in season.

This is the quietest stretch of the beach road. Georgetown County's rules apply here. It requires no business license for a rental, and its lodging tax is lower.

Prices here are the highest in Garden City. The listings here asked about $1.45 million at the middle, more than any other part.

Can you Airbnb in Garden City, SC?

Yes, unless the building's own rules say no. There is no town, so no town rental ordinance applies. Horry County's list of what a nightly-rental owner must file names a business license and three tax accounts, and no rental permit. We go over the filings with you before you close.

A condo association or an HOA (homeowners association) can forbid or limit rentals in its own papers. We read the building's documents and rules before you offer. Our page on HOA rental restrictions explains how those rules work.

Guests pay lodging taxes on top of the rent: about 12 percent on the Horry side and about 11 percent on the Georgetown end. You collect that money and send it on, so it is not part of your income.

AirROI, a rental data company, tracks the Airbnb listings here under the name Garden City Beach. Over the past 12 months:

What is there to do in Garden City, SC?

A summer day here starts on the beach and ends on the pier. For dinner, the MarshWalk in Murrells Inlet is about 8 minutes away by car. The Murrells Inlet guide has its restaurants and the boat landing at its south end.

For a day out, Huntington Beach State Park is 15 to 20 minutes south. It has 3 miles of beach, a freshwater lake where you are almost sure to see alligators, and what the state calls some of the top bird-watching on the East Coast. The Pawleys Island guide has the park's history and Brookgreen Gardens across the road.

For groceries, Kroger on Business 17 is about 4 minutes from the pier by car, and the Walmart Supercenter on the Garden City Connector about 5.

The beach rules

Horry County manages the beach from the Surfside Beach town line to the Georgetown County line. Its rules:

  • DogsOff the beach from 10 a.m. to 5 p.m., May 1 through Labor Day. Otherwise on a hand-held leash.
  • ShadeRound umbrellas up to 7 feet 6 inches across, and small pop-up cabanas for infants. No tents or canopies, all year.
  • OvernightNothing may be left on the beach overnight.
  • AlcoholNone on the beach or the beach accesses.

Is Garden City, SC busy in winter?

Summer is busy and winter is quiet, most of all on the beach road.

  • On the ocean side, more than 9 in 10 homes are second homes or rentals. The county mails most of their tax bills to another state, North Carolina, New York, Pennsylvania and Ohio most often.
  • At the last Census, taken in April, about 4 in 5 homes along Waccamaw Drive had no one living in them.
  • The pier stays open in winter, with shorter hours for fishing and the deck bar.
  • From November 1 to February 28, you may drive a golf cart onto the Horry County beach, with a state permit and a licensed driver.
Example. In the winter cart season, a golf cart with a cooler and buckets drives down onto the sand at Garden City. The beach is empty. There is time for sandcastles, lunch and an hour of watching the waves.

Does Garden City flood?

East of Business 17, much of Garden City is in FEMA's high-risk flood zone. In that zone a lender requires flood insurance:

Part of Garden City, Horry sideHomes in FEMA's high-risk flood zone
The ocean side of Waccamaw DriveNearly all
Between the marsh and Business 17About half
West of Business 17None

Flood insurance costs about five times as much on the beach strip. Along Waccamaw Drive, the middle federal flood policy on a house cost about $3,260 a year. Elsewhere in Garden City's ZIP code it was about $620. To check one address, use our Horry County flood zone check.

A condo building's own flood policy on the strip cost about $17,000 a year at the middle, shared by the owners through their dues.

What the big storms did

In September 1989, Hurricane Hugo's storm surge wiped out up to 90 percent of Garden City's homes. The pier was destroyed, and sand covered roads three blocks inland.

In 2022, Hurricane Ian's surge broke through the dunes in Garden City Beach and flooded parking lots and streets. It left up to three feet of sand on South Waccamaw Drive.

High tides are rising

High tides that reach flood level come more often than they used to. At the nearest working tide gauge, in Myrtle Beach, they came about 8 days a year in the last ten years, against 1 or 2 days a year twenty years before.

Our pages on finding out whether a house has flooded and coastal insurance show what we look at.

What does it cost to own a home in Garden City each year?

South Carolina figures property tax on a share of a home's value: 4 percent of it for the home you live in, and 6 percent for a second home or rental. The home you live in also skips the school operating tax. As a rental, the same condo pays about 3 times the tax it pays as your main home.

Garden City, Surfside Beach or Murrells Inlet?

The three places sit side by side, and each suits a different buyer.

PlaceWho makes the local rulesVacation homes, whole Census place
Garden CityThe countyAbout 4 in 10

Vacation-use shares are from the 2020 Census. Garden City's share counts the subdivisions west of Business 17 too, so it is lower than along the beach road. Surfside Beach's pier is about 6 minutes north of the Garden City Pier by car, and the MarshWalk about 8 minutes south.

Surfside Beach is a town, so its own rules on renting apply on top of the county's. Murrells Inlet is lived in all year far more than the two beach places, and the MarshWalk's restaurants are there. Garden City has the pier, the marsh and the Georgetown end's canals.

Common questions

Garden City, SC FAQ

Is Garden City, SC in Horry County or Georgetown County?

Both. The pier is in Horry County, and most of the beach road is in Georgetown County.

Is Garden City part of Myrtle Beach?

No. Garden City is not part of any city. Most addresses use Murrells Inlet, ZIP code 29576.

Can you drive a golf cart on Garden City Beach?

On the Horry County beach, from November 1 to February 28, with a state permit and a licensed driver.

Are dogs allowed on Garden City Beach?

Not from 10 a.m. to 5 p.m., May 1 through Labor Day, on the Horry County beach. At other times, on a hand-held leash no longer than 7 feet.

Is Garden City, SC safe?

We do not rate any place as safe or unsafe. On the Horry side, the Horry County Police Department, the county's main law enforcement agency, answers calls. On the Georgetown end, the Georgetown County Sheriff's Office does.

What schools serve Garden City, SC?

Schools are assigned by address. Homes on the Horry side are in Horry County Schools, and homes on the Georgetown end are in the Georgetown County School District. We look up the assigned schools for any home you like.
