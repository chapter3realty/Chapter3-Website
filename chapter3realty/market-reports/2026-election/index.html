By Devin Day, Chief Operating Officer, and Abdulla Hijazi, Chief Marketing Officer · Reviewed by Tim Nash, Broker-in-Charge · Updated November 5, 2026

In the 2026 election's unofficial count, Annie Andrews (D) won the U.S. Senate race and Alan Wilson (R) won for governor of South Carolina.

The short answer

Two dates after the election matter for a Myrtle Beach home this winter. On Dec. 11, federal flood insurance can lapse and stall closings in flood zones, and on Jan. 7, companies with 350 or more houses may no longer buy more.

The bigger changes are proposals that still need votes. Wilson wants a lower property tax on rentals and second homes, and Andrews backs a $50,000 tax credit for first-time buyers.

Ask about your house

How much would a Myrtle Beach rental save if Wilson cuts the rental tax?

About a third of its yearly property tax, if rentals were taxed at 4 percent instead of 6. A home you live in is taxed on 4 percent of its value, and skips the school operating tax. A rental or second home is taxed on 6 percent.

What has to happen first

The 6 percent is in the state constitution. Changing it takes two-thirds of each chamber, so it needs some Democratic votes.

4

More House votes needed beyond every Republican. The change takes 83 of 124 House votes and 31 of 46 Senate votes. Republicans won 79 House seats in the unofficial count and hold 34 in the Senate.

Wilson has published no bill. The other path is a constitutional amendment, which voters could approve in November 2028 at the earliest.

Where this was tried before, and what it points to

Act 388, a 2006 state law, took the school operating tax off homes people live in and added a 1 percent sales tax. A 2020 Lincoln Institute study found the state's system shifted the tax burden onto rentals, second homes and businesses. The South Carolina Chamber Foundation and South Carolina Realtors chose its case-study counties. The 2006 law has defenders too, such as nohometax.org.

  • Next 1 to 2 years

    If a cut passes, the bill on a rental here falls by about a third.

    No plan names who makes up the lost tax: other owners, businesses or the state.

  • 5 years and more

    A cut would move part of the burden the 2006 law shifted back off rentals.

    No study we found shows whether the saving would reach tenants or show up in prices.

More: the rental tax cut for investors and what changing the 2006 school tax law would mean.

Would a $50,000 first-time buyer credit raise Myrtle Beach home prices?

The last credit raised prices a little and sales a lot. A $50,000 credit has never been tried, so how much more is unknown.

From 2008 to 2010, a federal credit paid first-time buyers up to $8,000. A study in the Journal of Finance found it raised home sales by about 10 percent and prices by about 1 percent. Many buyers simply bought a few years earlier than they would have.

Here it did not stop a fall. Myrtle Beach-area prices dropped about 10 percent in 2009 and kept falling until 2012.

  • Next 1 to 2 years

    If it passes, the same pattern points to more sales and slightly higher prices, about 1 percent the last time.

    Some buyers would move a purchase earlier to get the credit.

  • 5 years and more

    Unknown. No study measured a credit this large, or what happens once buyers who bought early are gone.

First it needs a vote in the House and the Senate and the president's signature. It has had no vote. More: the first-time buyer tax credit.

What dates matter for a Myrtle Beach home sale after the election?

Dec. 11 matters most. It is the deadline for federal flood insurance, and it comes before the new senator takes office.

  1. Dec. 11, 2026Federal flood insurance can lapse

    Unless Congress extends it, the program cannot write new policies. A loan on a house in a high-risk flood zone can then wait for the program to return, or for a private policy that meets federal rules.

    It has happened before. In June 2010, over 1,400 closings a day were canceled or delayed across the country, by one estimate. The longest recent lapse ran from Oct. 1 to Nov. 12, 2025. Every lapse so far ended with Congress restoring the program. Flood insurance after the election

  2. Jan. 3, 2027Annie Andrews takes her Senate seat

    Her housing plans need a vote of the House and the Senate and the president's signature. The flood deadline comes before she takes office.

  3. Jan. 7, 2027The limit on big investors starts

    A company that controls 350 or more single-family houses may not buy more, with some exceptions. It became law in July. Andrews wants the number cut to 50.

  4. November 2028The earliest voters could change the rental tax in the constitution

    The other path to a lower rental tax is an amendment to the state constitution. It needs two-thirds of each chamber, then a yes from voters, then a second vote of the legislature.

  • Next 1 to 2 years

    If the program lapses, closings in high-risk flood zones can wait from a few hours to several weeks, based on past lapses.

  • 5 years and more

    Congress has kept the program going with 36 short extensions since 2017. Most premiums can rise up to 18 percent a year by law, whoever is in the Senate.

Will Myrtle Beach home prices go down after the election?

Prices here rose in the year after each of the last five elections, by 3 to 22 percent. They fell only after 2008, in the housing slump.

The record gives no basis for a forecast after this election. The changes swing sevenfold from one election to the next, and we found nothing that ties them to who won. Our home value page shows what homes like yours sold for this year.

Her other plans, with no direct tie to a home

  • A two-year national pause on AI data centers, with no data center costs passed on to power bills.
  • Three income tax brackets of 10, 20 and 35 percent, and no tax rise for families under $500,000.
  • Health care: reverse federal Medicaid cuts, expand Medicaid here, restore marketplace premium help, and add a public option.
  • Drug prices: imports from Canada, a $35 insulin cap for every insured patient, and more prices that Medicare negotiates.
  • Families: child care apprenticeships, a larger child tax credit, higher wages and paid family leave.
  • Taxes: end breaks for the highest earners and for moving jobs overseas, keep no tax on tips and Social Security, and restore free IRS filing.
  • Energy: suspend the federal gas tax and restore clean energy tax cuts.
  • Congress: term limits, a balanced budget amendment, a stock trading ban and no pay in shutdowns.
  • Other: universal background checks, a path to citizenship with border security, a digital bill of rights, phones out of schools, Roe v. Wade protections, and a vote of Congress before military force.

Common questions

Election FAQ

Will Myrtle Beach home prices go down after the election?

Past elections do not say. In the year after each election from 2016 to 2024, Myrtle Beach-area prices rose 3 to 22 percent. In the latest year they were about flat, up 0.8 percent.

Can Alan Wilson cut the property tax on rentals by himself?

No. Changing the 6 percent rate needs 83 House votes and 31 Senate votes. Republicans won 79 House seats in the unofficial count, so it needs Democratic votes too.

Is there a $50,000 first-time buyer tax credit now?

No. Annie Andrews backs one, but it has had no vote in Congress. Plan a purchase this winter without it.

What happens to a closing if flood insurance lapses on Dec. 11?

A loan on a house in a high-risk flood zone can wait until Congress restores the program or the lender accepts a private policy that meets federal rules. Houses outside those zones are not held up.
