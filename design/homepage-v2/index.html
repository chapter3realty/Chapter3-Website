Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Where does Chapter3 Realty work?

Across the Grand Strand, including Myrtle Beach, North Myrtle Beach, Surfside Beach, Garden City, Murrells Inlet, Pawleys Island, Little River, and Conway, plus the wider Horry County, South Carolina.

Does Chapter3 work with investors as well as buyers and sellers?

Yes. Alongside traditional buying and selling, the brokerage specializes in investment analysis, including long-term and short-term rental analysis, DSCR math, and submarket research.

What makes Chapter3 different from other Myrtle Beach brokerages?

A data-first approach. Instead of generic listing search, you get property-level analysis: rent estimates, cap rate and cash-flow math, nearby permit activity, and submarket trends.

Ready to make your next move on the Grand Strand?

Whether you are buying, selling, or investing, talk to a licensed local agent who knows the Grand Strand. No pressure, just straight answers.
