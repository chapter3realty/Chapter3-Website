VOICE
- Literal and direct. Short sentences: never over 40 words, average under 20.
- No metaphors, no personification, no aphorisms, no teaser headers, no em dashes, no hedges, no "in plain terms", no "here is".
- The buyer test on every sentence: would someone standing in the property do anything differently because of it? If not, cut it.
- Never name the body that wrote a rule, when it took effect, or a statute number in body copy. Say "your lender will require" and move on. Sources go in a sources line under 90 words, links only.
- Every article page: one H1, an answer block of 40 to 120 words under it, a hero sub-header, two calls to action inside the article, a sources line, at least two primary sources linked in the body, one attributed sentence from a named person.
- Stories are told as "an agent at Chapter3" or by the named person. Never "Chapter3 did".
- American spelling.
