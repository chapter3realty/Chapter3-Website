### A3. Specialty care that typically means a drive to Charleston (MUSC) -- facts only

- Fact: Adult Level I trauma is available locally (Grand Strand Medical Center is Adult Level I). Pediatric Level I trauma is NOT: MUSC Shawn Jenkins Children's Hospital in Charleston is the only Level I pediatric trauma center in South Carolina, with a burn program. MUSC also runs solid-organ transplant programs (heart, lung, kidney, liver, pancreas). Grand Strand's pediatric trauma level is II.
- Source: https://children.muschealth.org/medical-services/trauma ; https://muschealth.org/medical-services/transplant
- Quote: "MUSC Shawn Jenkins Children's Hospital (SJCH) is the first and only Level 1 Pediatric Trauma Center in the state of South Carolina." ... "As an ACS-verified Level I pediatric trauma center with a comprehensive burn program, we are uniquely qualified to provide emergency care for all injured patients, from infants to adolescents." Transplant page: MUSC describes itself as "The leading provider of transplants in SC" (heart, lung, kidney, liver, pancreas listed). It does NOT say "only" -- do not write "only transplant center".
- Date: opened 2026-08-16 (no page dates).
- Distance: Myrtle Beach to MUSC (171 Ashley Ave, Charleston) -- see C13 for the measured drive time; roughly 95-100 miles via US-17 South, about 2 hours typical.
- Confidence: HIGH for the pediatric Level I claim; HIGH that adult Level I exists locally (this corrects the assumption in the brief).
