# Setting up a short-term rental after closing, Horry County SC: verified facts

Every fact below was read at the primary source on 2026-09-06 with WebFetch, or downloaded with curl and the text
extracted, unless the entry says otherwise. Quotes are verbatim. Facts only; no page copy. Anything a source did not
literally say is under "Not verified". Re-open every source before reuse (non-negotiable 8). Nothing here is a
conclusion about a named building, HOA, or builder.

## 1. SCDOR Retail License

Source A: https://dor.sc.gov/tax/accommodations, "Accommodations". Read 2026-09-06 (WebFetch, curl).

- 1.1 Needed only for direct bookings. Quote: "If you are directly booking short-term rentals, you must have a Retail
  License to file and pay Accommodations Tax."
- 1.2 Not needed if a platform or manager takes every booking. Quote: "if you rent your property exclusively through a
  property management or online travel company that is reserving rooms and accepting payment for booking, they are
  responsible for remitting the tax for the full amount of the booking." Next sentence: "Therefore, you are not required
  to have a Retail License with South Carolina."
- 1.3 One per property. Quote: "If you are a property owner with more than one location that provides short term
  accommodations, you must have a Retail License for each location."
- 1.4 Light direct use. Quote: "A Retail License is not required if you rent a room or space for no more than one week
  each calendar quarter—but you are required to file and pay the tax annually."
- 1.5 A licence creates a filing duty even with no rentals. Quote: "you are required to file a return even if you had no
  rentals. You would report zeros on the return."

Source B: https://dor.sc.gov/businesses/apply-business-tax-account/licensing-retail-license, "Licensing (Retail
License)". Read 2026-09-06 (WebFetch, curl).

- 1.6 Fee and life. Quote: "You must pay a non-refundable fee for each license listed below. These licenses do not
  expire, but you must update your license if your business location changes." Retail License line: "($50 non-refundable
  fee)".
- 1.7 The same page lists an "Accommodations Tax License ($50 non-refundable fee)". Quote: "If you rent out rooms or
  spaces at hotels, campgrounds, boarding houses, mobile home parks, etc., you are required to obtain an Accommodations
  Tax License." Source A calls it a Retail License.
- 1.8 Quote: "You are not required to obtain an Accommodations Tax License if you only rent out rooms for more than 90
  consecutive days. Overnight rentals become tax-exempt on day 90."
- 1.9 Apply. Quote: "To apply, use the Business Tax Application on MyDORWAY". Source C,
  https://dor.sc.gov/tax/registration: "Use the MyDORWAY Business Tax Application to apply online for a Retail License".
  Read 2026-09-06.

## 2. State accommodations tax

Source: https://dor.sc.gov/tax/accommodations (Source A above). Read 2026-09-06.

- 2.1 Scope. Quote: "Accommodations Tax applies to sleeping accommodations of any kind that are rented to guests for
  less than 90 consecutive days."
- 2.2 Rates. Quote: "Accommodations are subject to: 5% Sales Tax 2% Accommodations Tax Any local Sales & Use Tax that is
  collected on behalf of counties by the SCDOR." The page never writes "7%"; do not attribute a 7% figure to it.
- 2.3 Filing. Quote: "Accommodations taxpayers must file and pay all Accommodations taxes electronically." Due date:
  "Returns must be filed by the 20th of the month following the end of the filing period." Also: "Seasonal filers should
  file returns for the months they had sales." The page states no monthly/quarterly/annual assignment rule beyond 1.4.
- 2.4 Owner-occupied exemption. Quote: "A facility with six or fewer bedrooms, provided that: the facility is on the
  same premises where the owner or operator lives at the time the other rooms are rented". Second condition: "the owner
  or operator does not use a rental agency or online travel company to rent rooms."
- 2.5 Who remits. Quote: "The person or business who books the accommodations is responsible for collecting and paying
  Accommodations Tax. That may be the property owner or a real estate agent, listing service, broker, Online Travel
  Company (VRBO, Expedia, etc.), or similar entity."

## 11. Federal

- 11.1 Schedule E. Source https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc414. Quote: "You can generally use Schedule E (Form 1040),
  Supplemental Income and Loss to report income and expenses related to real estate rentals." Income includes "Advance
  rent" and "Security deposits". Depreciation: "You begin to depreciate your rental property when you place it in
  service." Read 2026-09-06.
- 11.2 Personal use. Source https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc415. A unit is a residence if personal-use days are "more
  than the greater of: 1. 14 days, or 2. 10% of the total days you rent it to others at a fair rental price." Under 15
  rental days: "don't report any of the rental income and don't deduct any expenses as rental expenses." Read
  2026-09-06.
- 11.3 1099-K. Source https://www.irs.gov/businesses/understanding-your-form-1099-k. Quote: "required to report payments
  on Form 1099-K when the total amount of payments you receive for goods or services through the platform exceeds
