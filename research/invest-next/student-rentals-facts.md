- Quote: "The Fair Housing Act prohibits discrimination in housing because of: Race Color National

Source: https://www.hud.gov/stat/fheo/rights-obligations, "Fair Housing: Rights and Obligations". Read 2026-09-06.
- Quote: "The Fair Housing Act prohibits this discrimination because of race, color, national
  origin, religion, sex, familial status, and disability."

Source: Federal Register, Vol. 63, No. 243, December 18, 1998, pages 70256-70257, FR Doc 98-33568,
"Fair Housing Enforcement--Occupancy Standards; Notice of Statement of Policy",
https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-1998-12-18/html/98-33568.htm. Read 2026-09-06.
- Scope, quote: "discriminatory conduct under the Fair Housing Act on the basis of familial status
  (the presence of children in a family)".
- Adoption, quote: "adopting as its policy on occupancy standards, for purposes of enforcement
  actions under the Fair Housing Act, the standards provided in the Memorandum of General Counsel
  Frank Keating to Regional Counsel dated March 20, 1991, attached as Appendix A."
- Keating memo (Appendix A), quote: "the Department believes that an occupancy policy of two
  persons in a bedroom, as a general rule, is reasonable under the Fair Housing Act."
- Quote: "However, the reasonableness of any occupancy policy is rebuttable".
- Note: this notice is about families with children. It says nothing about caps on unrelated adults
  such as the Conway and Horry County overlays in sections 4 and 5.

## 7. Leases and cosigners: SC Code Title 27, Chapter 40

Source: https://www.scstatehouse.gov/code/t27c040.php (Residential Landlord and Tenant Act).
Downloaded with curl; whole chapter searched. Read 2026-09-06.
- The chapter never uses "joint and several", "cosigner", "co-signer", "guarantor" or "surety". The
  only "jointly or severally" is in the definition of "owner" (27-40-210(8)). The statute is silent
  on who may sign a lease and on guarantors.
- 27-40-210(15), quote: ""tenant" means a person entitled under a rental agreement to occupy a
  dwelling unit to the exclusion of others".
- 27-40-210(12), quote: ""rental agreement" means all agreements, written or oral, and valid rules
  and regulations adopted under SECTION 27-40-520 embodying the terms and conditions concerning the
  use and occupancy of a dwelling unit and premises".
- 27-40-320(b), quote: "If the tenant does not sign and deliver a written rental agreement which
  has been signed and delivered to the tenant by the landlord, acceptance of possession and payment
  of rent without reservation gives the rental agreement the same effect". (c), quote: "If a rental
  agreement given effect by the operation of this section provides for a term longer than one year,
  it is effective for only one year."
- 27-40-410(a), quote: "Any deduction from the security/rental deposit must be itemized by the
  landlord in a written notice to the tenant together with the amount due, if any, within thirty
  days after termination of the tenancy" (the clock runs from the later of possession being
  delivered and the tenant's demand). (b), quote: "the tenant may recover the property and money in
  an amount equal to three times the amount wrongfully withheld and reasonable attorney's fees."
