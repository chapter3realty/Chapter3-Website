## 1. The 90-day thresholds

### State statute

Source: https://www.scstatehouse.gov/code/t12c036.php, SECTION 12-36-920 "Tax on accommodations
for transients; reporting". Downloaded with curl, read 2026-09-06.

- The statute states one rate, 7%. Quote: "A sales tax equal to seven percent is imposed on the
  gross proceeds derived from the rental or charges for any rooms, campground spaces, lodgings,
  or sleeping accommodations furnished to transients".
- The 90-day line. Quote: "The gross proceeds derived from the lease or rental of sleeping
  accommodations supplied to the same person for a period of ninety continuous days are not
  considered proceeds from transients."

### SC Department of Revenue

Source: https://dor.sc.gov/tax/accommodations, page title "Accommodations | SCDOR". Undated.
Read 2026-09-06.

- Quote: "Accommodations Tax applies to sleeping accommodations of any kind that are rented to
  guests for less than 90 consecutive days."
- Quote: "Accommodations Tax does not apply to: The lease or rental of accommodations supplied to
  the same person for a period of 90 continuous days".
- The 7% shown as two lines. Quote: "Accommodations are subject to: 5% Sales Tax 2%
  Accommodations Tax Any local Sales & Use Tax that is collected on behalf of counties by the
  SCDOR."

### Horry County hospitality fee

Source: https://www.horrycountysc.gov/departments/treasurer/hospitality-fee/, page title
"Hospitality Fee - Horry County SC.Gov". Read 2026-09-06.

- Quote: "Long-term accommodation rentals 90 consecutive days at the same location to the same
  patron does not require collection and remittance of the Hospitality Fee."
- Outside city limits. Quote: "3% of gross proceeds from Rental of Transient Accommodations".
  Inside. Quote: "1.5% of gross proceeds from the Sale of Food/Beverages, Rental of Transient

### City of Myrtle Beach

Source: https://www.cityofmyrtlebeach.com/careers/business/business_faq.php, page title
"Business License FAQs". Read 2026-09-06.

- Quote: "Any stay of ninety (90) or more consecutive days in the same place is not considered a

## 2. Zoning: what stay length makes a rental legal where

### City of Myrtle Beach

Source: Code of Ordinances, Appendix A Zoning, Section 203 "Definitions". Browser URL:
https://library.municode.com/sc/myrtle_beach/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=COOR_APXAZO_ART2DE_S203DE

- Quote: "Visitor Accommodation: A visitor accommodation or transient accommodation, aka "short
  term rental" means a living unit or other accommodation used as a place of human habitation
  with sleeping accommodations". The stay test, same definition. Quote: "which is exchanged,
  furnished to another, used, rented, leased or sub-leased for any time period less than ninety
  (90) continuous days".

Source: same code, Section 1808 "Short-Term Rental Conversion (STRC) Overlay Zone", history
"( Ord. No. 2024-69 , 12-10-24)". Browser URL:
https://library.municode.com/sc/myrtle_beach/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=COOR_APXAZO_ART18OVZO_S1808SHRMRECOSTOVZO
Read 2026-09-06. This cuts the other way: oceanfront short-term buildings may not go mid-term.

- Quote: "No building or structure consisting of more than two (2) units, including each unit
  within such building or structure, that was constructed or used as a Visitor Accommodation".
  Same sentence continues. Quote: "may be used for rental or lease for ninety (90) continuous
  days or more."
- Quote: "It shall be a violation of this prohibition to enter into successive leases or rental
  agreements, each for less than ninety (90) days, for the purpose of allowing an occupant to
  remain for more than ninety (90) continuous days."
- Where. Quote: "located between Kings Highway and the Atlantic Ocean within the municipal
  limits." Units "legally operating with a business license for long term rental at the time of
  adoption" may continue.

Source: https://www.cityofmyrtlebeach.com/news_detail_T6_R1468.php, city news item headed "Ever
wonder if your home or neighborhood in the City of Myrtle Beach is zoned for short-term
rentals?" Undated. Read 2026-09-06.

- Quote: ""Short-term" means any rental of less than 90 days."
- Quote: "Any zoning district that begins with R (for residential) does not allow short-term
  rentals, with the exception of the RMV zone (Residential Multifamily Visitor)."
- Reading of the three sources (mine, not a quote): in an R district other than RMV a furnished
  rental is a residential use only when each stay is 90 continuous days or more; in the STRC
  overlay a short-term building of more than two units may not do that at all.

## 3. When a furnished stay is a tenancy under the Residential Landlord and Tenant Act

Source: https://www.scstatehouse.gov/code/t27c040.php, SECTION 27-40-120 "Exclusions from
application of chapter" and SECTION 27-40-210 "General definitions". Downloaded, read 2026-09-06.

- The exclusion that matters. Quote: "(4) transient occupancy in a hotel, motel, or other
  accommodations subject to the sales tax on accommodations as provided by SECTION 12-36-920;"
- Quote: ""dwelling unit" means a structure or the part of a structure that is used as a home,
  residence, or sleeping place by one person who maintains a household or by two or more persons
  who maintain a common household".
- Reading (mine, not a quote): exclusion (4) hangs on the accommodations tax, and 12-36-920 says
  90 continuous days to the same person are not transient proceeds, so a 90-day-plus stay by one
  person is outside exclusion (4). Neither statute says whether a 30-to-89-day furnished stay is
  "transient occupancy"; no SC page I opened answers it. A question for a lawyer.
