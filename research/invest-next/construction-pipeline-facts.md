# What is being built on the Grand Strand right now: verified facts

Researcher notes for the monthly construction-pipeline page, not page copy. Every
fact gives the claim, the source opened, a verbatim quote, and the read date.
Everything was opened in one session on 2026-09-07 (evening, US Eastern; some
server timestamps show 2026-09-08 UTC). Anything a source did not literally say
is in section 7, "Could not verify". No conclusion about any named builder,
community, HOA or building; observable dated facts only.

The owner's summary was "a new interstate, 3 hospitals and 7k houses". What the
sources support, in one line each: the interstate has no construction contract
and no construction funding (SCDOT); one hospital opens this fall, one is under
construction for late 2027, one is in site work for 2028 (the third only on
news and a 2025 MUSC release, since Tidelands' own site blocks fetches); the
federal permit count was 7,152 in 2024, 6,898 in 2025 and 3,873 for January to
July 2026, 13 percent below the same months of 2025.

## 1. Interstate 73 in South Carolina

Primary: SCDOT's project portal page, https://projectportal.scdot.gov/i-73-project
(opened with curl), and SCDOT's I-73 site, http://www.i73insc.com/default.aspx
and http://www.i73insc.com/southern-corridor.aspx (footer "©2024 South Carolina
Department of Transportation"). All read 2026-09-07. The portal page and the
default.aspx page carry the same status text.

- What the South Carolina project is. "The Southern Project continues from Interstate 95 and runs to State Route 22 (Conway Bypass, or Veteran's Highway) in the Myrtle Beach/Conway area." "The Northern Project ties to Interstate 73/74 in the Rockingham/Hamlet, North Carolina region and runs south across the South Carolina/North Carolina state line through Marlboro and Dillon Counties to a connection at Interstate 95." projectportal.scdot.gov/i-73-project, read 2026-09-07.
- Origin. "Interstate 73, designated a high-priority route from Michigan to South Carolina, was established under the 1991 Intermodal Surface Transportation Equity Act." SCDOT portal card summary, https://projectportal.scdot.org/ (project list), read 2026-09-07.
- Federal wetlands permit. "A Clean Water Act Section 401/404 permit application for impacts from the I-73 project on wetlands, streams, and other jurisdictional waters, was approved by the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers (USACE) and the South Carolina Department of Health and Environmental Control (SCDHEC) on June 19, 2017." Same page, read 2026-09-07.
- Phase 1, I-95 to US 501. "Construction plans for Phase 1 of I-73 South, which extends from I-95 to US Route 501, are currently being finalized. Right of way acquisitions are complete for this segment, and this phase of the project will be shovel ready by the end of 2024." Same page, read 2026-09-07. Southern corridor page adds: "The final design for the section of I-73 from I-95 to US Route 501 began in July 2011 and plans are currently being updated. This design includes 10 bridges" and "Depending on available funding construction could begin in 2025. This will be the first section of I-73 to be designed and constructed in South Carolina". http://www.i73insc.com/southern-corridor.aspx, read 2026-09-07.
- Phase 2, US 501 to the Marion/Horry line. "Right of way plans are complete for Phase 2 of I-73 South, which extends from US 501 to the Marion/Horry County line. Right of way acquisitions are nearly complete for this phase." Portal page, read 2026-09-07.
- Phase 3, Marion/Horry line to SC 22. "Right of way plans are complete for Phase 3 of I-73 South, which extends from the Marion/Horry County line to SC 22. Right of way acquisitions were initiated on the northern section of this phase in early 2024 and will continue throughout the corridor through 2026." Portal page and default.aspx, read 2026-09-07. CONTRADICTION: the southern-corridor.aspx page says the same sentence with "through 2025". Use the portal page's "through 2026".
- Where acquisition stands by county. "Right of Way acquisition is complete for the section of I-73 in Dillon County with acquisition still ongoing for sections of I-73 located in Marion and Horry County." southern-corridor.aspx, read 2026-09-07.
- No construction funding. "Funding has not been identified to advance construction on any of the three phases of I-73 South." Portal page, southern-corridor.aspx and default.aspx, read 2026-09-07.
- Northern project on hold. "Conceptual plans are complete for I-73 North. Advancement of this phase of work is temporarily on hold pending the allocation of additional funding." Portal page, read 2026-09-07.
- Nothing is under construction. No SCDOT page opened this session describes a construction contract, a construction start or an opening date for any segment. The county GIS server carries the corridor as map layer 39 "Interstate 73" in https://www.horrycounty.org/parcelapp/rest/services/HorryCountyGISApp/MapServer (read 2026-09-07); that is a planned line, not a road.
