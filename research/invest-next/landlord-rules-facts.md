## 1. Security deposits: SC Code 27-40-410

Source: https://www.scstatehouse.gov/code/t27c040.php, "SECTION 27-40-410. Security deposits; prepaid rent."
HISTORY: "1986 Act No. 336, SECTION 1; 1994 Act No. 498, SECTION 1." curl, read 2026-09-06.

- Itemized notice within 30 days. Quote (a): "Any deduction from the security/rental deposit must be itemized by
  the landlord in a written notice to the tenant together with the amount due, if any, within thirty days". The
  clock starts "after termination of the tenancy and delivery of possession and demand by the tenant, whichever
  is later."
- Quote (a): "The tenant shall provide the landlord in writing with a forwarding address or new address to which
  the written notice and amount due from the landlord may be sent."
- Penalty. Quote (b): "the tenant may recover the property and money in an amount equal to three times the
  amount wrongfully withheld and reasonable attorney's fees."
- Posting rule, (c): a landlord who "(1) rents more than four adjoining dwelling units on the premises, and (2)
  imposes different standards for calculating security/rental deposits required of different tenants on the
  premises" must "post in a conspicuous place on the premises, or at the place at which rental is paid a
  statement clearly indicating the standards by which such security/rental deposits are calculated" or hand each
  applicant the statement. Penalty: the excess over the lowest comparable deposit "is not subject to deductions
  for damages".
- Quote (e): "the holder of the landlord's interest in the premises at the time of the termination of the
  tenancy is bound by this section." The section sets no maximum deposit and says nothing about interest or a
  separate account (absence, after reading the whole section).

## 2. Required disclosure: SC Code 27-40-420

Source: same page, "SECTION 27-40-420. Disclosure." Read 2026-09-06.

- Quote (a): the landlord or whoever signs for him "shall disclose to the tenant in writing at or before the
  commencement of the tenancy the name and address of an owner of the premises or a person authorized to act on
  behalf of the owner as agent".
- Quote (b): "The information required to be furnished by this section must be kept current and this section
  extends to and is enforceable against any successor landlord, owner, or manager."
- A manager who fails to disclose becomes the landlord's agent for "service of process and receiving and
  receipting for notices and demands" (c)(1). 27-40-210(6): "landlord" "also means a manager of the premises who
  fails to disclose as required by SECTION 27-40-420".

## 3. Landlord duties: SC Code 27-40-440

Source: same page, "SECTION 27-40-440. Landlord to maintain premises." Read 2026-09-06.

- Quote (a): "A landlord shall:" (1) "comply with the requirements of applicable building and housing codes
  materially affecting health and safety;" (2) "make all repairs and do whatever is reasonably necessary to put
  and keep the premises in a fit and habitable condition;" (3) "keep all common areas of the premises in a
  reasonably safe condition, and, for premises containing more than four dwelling units, keep in a reasonably
  clean condition;" (4) "make available running water and reasonable amounts of hot water at all times and
  reasonable heat except where the building that includes the dwelling unit is not required by law to be
  equipped for that purpose" (the exception continues for tenant-controlled heat on a direct utility
  connection); (5) "maintain in reasonably good and safe working order and condition all electrical, gas,
  plumbing, sanitary, heating, ventilating, air conditioning, and other facilities and appliances, including
  elevators, supplied or required to be supplied by him."
- Quote (a)(5): "Appliances present in the dwelling unit are presumed to be supplied by the landlord unless
  specifically excluded by the rental agreement. No appliances or facilities necessary to the provision of
  essential services may be excluded."

## 4. Tenant duties: SC Code 27-40-510

Source: same page, "SECTION 27-40-510. Tenant to maintain dwelling unit." Read 2026-09-06.

- Quote: "A tenant shall:" (2) "keep the dwelling unit and that part of the premises that he uses reasonably
  safe and reasonably clean;" (6) "not deliberately or negligently destroy, deface, damage, impair, or remove
  any part of the premises"; (7) behave and make guests behave "in a manner that will not disturb other tenant's
  peaceful enjoyment of the premises;" (8) "comply with the lease and rules and regulations which are
  enforceable pursuant to SECTION 27-40-520."
- 27-40-530(e): "A tenant shall not change locks on the dwelling unit without the permission of the landlord."

## 5. Landlord access: SC Code 27-40-530

Source: same page, "SECTION 27-40-530. Access." Read 2026-09-06.

- Quote (c): "Except in cases under item (b) above, the landlord shall give the tenant at least twenty-four
  hours notice of his intent to enter and may enter only at reasonable times." Also: "A landlord shall not abuse
  the right of access or use it to harass the tenant."
- Purposes, (a): "inspect the premises, make necessary or agreed repairs, decorations, alterations, or
  improvements, supply necessary or agreed services, or exhibit the dwelling unit to prospective or actual
  purchasers, mortgagees, tenants, workmen, or contractors."
- No consent needed, (b)(1): "At any time in case of emergency—prospective changes in weather conditions which
  pose a likelihood of danger to the property may be considered an emergency". (b)(2): "Between the hours of
  9:00 a.m. and 6:00 p.m. for the purpose of providing regularly scheduled periodic services such as changing
  furnace and air-conditioning filters, providing termite, insect, or pest treatment, and the like", only if
  that right "is conspicuously set forth in writing in the rental agreement". (b)(3): "Between the hours of 8:00
  a.m. and 8:00 p.m. for the purpose of providing services requested by the tenant", again announced first.

## 6. Nonpayment: SC Code 27-40-710(B) and 27-37-10(B)

Source: same page, "SECTION 27-40-710. Noncompliance with rental agreement; failure to pay rent; removal of
evicted tenant's personal property." HISTORY ends "1999 Act No. 59, SECTION 1." Read 2026-09-06.

- Quote (B): "If rent is unpaid when due and the tenant fails to pay rent within five days from the date due or
  the tenant is in violation of Section 27-40-540, the landlord may terminate the rental agreement". Condition:
  "provided the landlord has given the tenant written notice of nonpayment and his intention to terminate the
  rental agreement if the rent is not paid within that period."
- Once per lease, or in the lease. Quote (B): "The landlord's obligation to provide notice under this section is
  satisfied for any lease term after the landlord has given one such notice to the tenant or if the notice is
  contained in conspicuous language in a written rental agreement."
- The clause. Quote (B): "The written notice requirement upon the landlord under this subsection shall be
  considered to have been complied with if the rental agreement contains the following or a substantially
  equivalent provision:". The provision, three consecutive pieces: "IF YOU DO NOT PAY YOUR RENT ON TIME" / "This
  is your notice. If you do not pay your rent within five days of the due date, the landlord can start to have
  you evicted." / "You will get no other notice as long as you live in this rental unit."
- Carries into month-to-month. Quote (B): the provision "applies to a month-to-month tenancy following the
  specified lease term in the original rental agreement."
- Typeface rule. Source: https://www.scstatehouse.gov/code/t27c037.php, 27-37-10(B), read 2026-09-06. Nonpayment
  within five days is notice "if a written rental agreement specifies in bold conspicuous type that nonpayment
  of rent constitutes such notice. This requirement is satisfied if the written rental agreement contains the
  notice specified in Section 27-40-710(B)."
- Brokers may file, 27-40-710(C): a licensed broker-in-charge or property manager "may, either in person or
  through one or more regular employees, complete a form writ of eviction and present facts to judicial officers
  on behalf of his landlord/principal" where "no separate charge is made for this service."

## 7. Other lease breaches: SC Code 27-40-710(A); ending a periodic tenancy

Source: same section, read 2026-09-06.

- Quote (A): a written notice "specifying the acts and omissions constituting the breach and that the rental
  agreement will terminate upon a date not less than fourteen days after receipt of the notice, if the breach is
  not remedied in fourteen days."
- Cure, (A)(1)-(2): no termination "if the breach is remediable by repairs or otherwise and the tenant
  adequately remedies the breach before the date specified in the notice", or the remedy "is commenced within
  the fourteen-day period and is pursued in good faith to completion within a reasonable time". (A) excludes
  nonpayment and breaches "materially affecting health and safety or the physical condition of the property"
  (those sit in 27-40-720; heading read, text not).
- 27-40-770(b): "The landlord or the tenant may terminate a month-to-month tenancy by a written notice given to
  the other at least thirty days before the termination date specified in the notice." Holdover, (c): "the
  landlord may bring an action for possession."

## 8. Abandonment: SC Code 27-40-730

Source: same page, "SECTION 27-40-730. Remedies for absence, nonuse, and abandonment." Read 2026-09-06.

- Quote (a): "The unexplained absence of a tenant from a dwelling unit for a period of fifteen days after
  default in the payment of rent must be construed as abandonment of the dwelling unit."
- Quote (b): "If the tenant has voluntarily terminated the utilities and there is an unexplained absence of a
  tenant after default in payment of rent, abandonment is considered immediate and the fifteen day rule as
  described in (a) does not apply."
- Quote (c): "If the tenant abandons the dwelling unit, the landlord shall make reasonable efforts to rent it at
  a fair rental."
- Property left behind, (d): if worth "five hundred dollars or less, the landlord may enter the dwelling unit,
  using forcible entry if required, and dispose of the property." Otherwise, (e): "the landlord may have the
  property removed only pursuant to the provisions of Sections 27-37-10 to 27-37-150." (f): "not liable unless
  the landlord was grossly negligent."

## 9. The ejectment process: SC Code 27-37-10 to 27-37-160

Source: https://www.scstatehouse.gov/code/t27c037.php, "CHAPTER 37 Ejectment of Tenants". curl, read 2026-09-06.

- Grounds, 27-37-10(A): "(1) the tenant fails or refuses to pay the rent when due or when demanded, (2) the term
  of tenancy or occupancy has ended, or (3) the terms or conditions of the lease have been violated."
- Application and rule, 27-37-20: "Upon application by the landlord or his agent or attorney any magistrate
  having jurisdiction" "shall issue a written rule requiring the tenant forthwith to vacate the premises
  occupied by him or to show cause why he should not be ejected before the magistrate within ten days after
  service".
- Service, 27-37-30: (A) as a summons; or (C) post after two failed attempts, which "must be separated by a
  minimum of forty-eight hours and must occur at times of day separated by a minimum of eight hours", then mail
  "in the presence of the clerk of the magistrates court". (C)(3): "Mailing of the rule constitutes service when
  the requirements of items (1) and (2) have been met and ten days have elapsed from the time of mailing." The
  answer period "begins to run on the eleventh day after mailing."
- No answer, 27-37-40: "then the magistrate shall issue a warrant of ejectment and the tenant shall be ejected
  by his regular or special constable or by the sheriff of the county."
- Hearing, 27-37-60: "If the tenant appear and contest ejectment the magistrate shall forthwith hear and
  determine the case as any other civil case, allowing trial by jury if demanded by either party." 27-37-80:
  "Either landlord or tenant may demand trial by jury."
- Landlord wins, 27-37-100: "If the verdict be for the plaintiff the magistrate shall within five days issue a
  writ of ejectment and the tenant shall be ejected by the constable or special constable or the sheriff of the
  county."
- Rent runs on, 27-37-150: rent "shall continue to accrue so long as the tenant remains in possession of the
  premises, at the same rate as prevailed immediately prior to the issuance of such rule". Accepting it "shall
  not operate as a waiver of the landlord's right to insist upon ejectment, nor as a renewal or extension of the
  tenancy".
- The 24 hours, 27-37-160: the officer "shall proceed to the premises, present to the occupants a copy of the
  writ and give the occupants twenty-four hours to vacate voluntarily." Then: "If necessary, the deputy sheriff,
  but not a constable, may then enter the premises by force, using the least destructive means possible, in
  order to effectuate the ejectment." If nobody answers, the writ is left "taped or stapled at each corner and
  attached at the top of either the front or back door". Quote: "Discretion may be exercised by the constable or
  deputy sheriff in granting a delay in the dispossession of ill or elderly tenants."
- Self-help is barred. 27-40-660 (t27c040.php): a landlord who "unlawfully removes or excludes the tenant from
  the premises, or wilfully diminishes services" owes "an amount equal to three months' periodic rent or twice
  the actual damages sustained by him, whichever is greater, and reasonable attorney's fees."
- Money limit. Source: https://www.sccourts.org/courts/trial-courts/magistrate-court/, "Magistrate Court", read
  2026-09-06. Quote: "Magistrates have civil jurisdiction when the amount in controversy does not exceed
  $7,500."

## 12. Late fees

Source: https://www.scstatehouse.gov/code/t27c040.php, whole chapter searched for "late", "fee", "penalt". Read
2026-09-06.

- No section of Chapter 40 caps or regulates a late fee. The only mention is 27-40-210(11). Quote: ""rent" means
  the consideration payable for use of the premises including late charges whether payable in lump sum or
  periodic payments, excluding security deposits or other charges;".
- 27-40-310(a): "A landlord and a tenant may include in a rental agreement terms and conditions not prohibited
  by this chapter or other rule of law, including rent, term of the agreement".
- Limits that exist: 27-40-230 "Unconscionability" (heading only) and 27-40-330(a), which bars a clause where
  the tenant "agrees to waive or forego rights or remedies under this chapter".

## 13. Retaliation: SC Code 27-40-910

Source: same page, "SECTION 27-40-910. Retaliatory conduct prohibited." Read 2026-09-06.

- Quote (a): "a landlord shall not retaliate by increasing rent to an amount in excess of fair-market value or
  decreasing essential services or by bringing an action for possession after:" (1) "the tenant has complained
  to a governmental agency charged with responsibility for enforcement of a building or housing code of a
  violation applicable to the premises materially affecting health and safety"; or (2) "the tenant has
  complained to the landlord of a violation of this chapter."
- Exceptions, (c): the violation "was caused primarily by lack of reasonable care by the tenant"; "material
  noncompliance by the tenant under SECTION 27-40-710 or SECTION 27-40-720"; or repairs that "would effectively
  deprive the tenant of use of the dwelling unit."
- (f): the tenant "must notify the landlord in writing within ten days after service of the Rule to Vacate or
  Show Cause". (g): retaliatory non-renewal with rent current: "may not recover possession of the dwelling unit
  for seventy-five days". (h): "liable for damages up to three month's rent or treble the actual damages
  sustained by the tenant, whichever is greater, and reasonable attorney's fees."
