Researcher notes, not page copy. Every fact gives the claim, the primary URL
opened, a verbatim quote under 40 words, and the read date. Anything a source
did not literally say is under "Not verified". No conclusions about any named
building, HOA or builder.

## 0. State law that applies in every row

- State accommodations tax is 7% of gross rental proceeds. "A sales tax equal to seven percent is imposed on the gross proceeds derived from the rental or charges for any rooms, campground spaces, lodgings, or sleeping accommodations furnished to transients". SC Code 12-36-920(A), https://www.scstatehouse.gov/code/t12c036.php, read 2026-09-06.
- State transient threshold is 90 days. "The gross proceeds derived from the lease or rental of sleeping accommodations supplied to the same person for a period of ninety continuous days are not considered proceeds from transients." Same URL, read 2026-09-06.
- A local accommodations tax is capped at 3%. "A local governing body may impose, by ordinance, a local accommodations tax, not to exceed three percent." SC Code 6-1-520, https://www.scstatehouse.gov/code/t06c001.php, read 2026-09-06.
- County plus city local accommodations tax cannot exceed 3% in any one place. "The cumulative rate of county and municipal local accommodations taxes for any portion of the county area may not exceed three percent". SC Code 6-1-540, same URL, read 2026-09-06.
- A local hospitality tax is capped at 2%, cumulative 2%. "A local governing body may impose, by ordinance, a local hospitality tax not to exceed two percent." (6-1-720); "The cumulative rate of county and municipal hospitality taxes for any portion of the county area may not exceed two percent" (6-1-740). Same URL, read 2026-09-06.
- Legal residence is assessed at 4%, everything else at 6%. "are taxed on an assessment equal to four percent of the fair market value of the property" (12-43-220(c)); "All other real property not herein provided for shall be taxed on an assessment equal to six percent of the fair market value" (12-43-220(e)). https://www.scstatehouse.gov/code/t12c043.php, read 2026-09-06.
- Two 2025-2026 bills would change local STR power; the pages show both still in committee. H.3861: "a governing body of a municipality, county, or other political subdivision of the State may not enact or enforce an ordinance, resolution, or regulation that prohibits the rental of a residential dwelling to a short-term guest." Status line "Referred to Committee on Medical, Military, Public and Municipal Affairs" (Jan 30, 2025). https://www.scstatehouse.gov/sess126_2025-2026/bills/3861.htm, read 2026-09-06. S.442: "The governing body of a municipality or county in this State may enact and enforce an ordinance regulating short-term rentals." including "(4) prohibit the rental of residential dwellings to short-term guests within the jurisdiction"; status "Referred to Committee on Judiciary" (Mar 11, 2025). https://www.scstatehouse.gov/sess126_2025-2026/bills/442.htm, read 2026-09-06.

## 2. City of Myrtle Beach (Horry County)

STR zoning
- Residential districts prohibit STR except RMV. "Any zoning district that begins with R (for residential) does not allow short-term rentals, with the exception of the RMV zone (Residential Multifamily Visitor)." https://www.cityofmyrtlebeach.com/news_detail_T6_R1468.php, read 2026-09-06.
- Short-term means under 90 days. "'Short-term' is anything less than 90 days, according to both city and state law." and "Unless it is zoned for short-term rentals (less than 90 days), you cannot do it." https://www.cityofmyrtlebeach.com/departments/planning_and_zoning_department.php, read 2026-09-06.
- Only the pink zones on the city map allow STR. "The areas shaded in pink on the zoning map below are the only zones that allow short-term rentals in the city." Same URL; map link on that page: https://www.cityofmyrtlebeach.com/ShortTermRentalZoneMap.pdf (not opened). Read 2026-09-06.
- Grandfathered houses: the two city pages give two counts. News page: "City-wide, fewer than 30 houses in traditional residential zones are grandfathered for short-term rentals". Planning page: "24 houses in traditional residential zones are grandfathered for short-term rentals". URLs above, read 2026-09-06.
- Penalty. "Violations of the zoning code and the business license ordinance are misdemeanors, with fines of up to $500 and/or 30 days upon conviction." R1468 URL, read 2026-09-06.

2024-2025 conversion overlay and moratorium (item 6)
- Ordinance 2024-69, first reading 12-3-2024, second reading 12-10-2024, listed on the city's own agendas: "2024-69 Conversion Overlay Short-Term Rentals (2nd Reading)". https://www.cityofmyrtlebeach.com/agenda_details_T15_R388.php (12/10/24) and https://www.cityofmyrtlebeach.com/agenda_details_T15_R387.php (12/03/24), read 2026-09-06.
- Ordinance text (scanned PDF, OCR'd; quotes carry OCR risk on punctuation). Title: "TO AMEND THE CODE OF ORDINANCES, CITY OF MYRTLE BEACH, APPENDIX A ZONING ARTICLE 18 OVERLAY ZONES TO ADD NEW SECTION 1808 SHORT TERM RENTAL CONVERSION OVERLAY ZONE". https://cms6.revize.com/revize/myrtlebeachsc/07.%202024-069%20Conversion%20Overlay_Short%20Term%20Rentals%20(2nd).pdf, read 2026-09-06.
- Rule. 1808.B: buildings of more than two units "that was constructed or used as a Visitor Accommodation, commonly known as short term rental, may be used for rental or lease for ninety (90) continuous days or more" is prohibited ("No building or structure consisting of more than two (2) units"). Same PDF.
- Back-to-back leases are a violation. "It shall be a violation of this prohibition to enter into successive leases or rental agreements, each for less than ninety (90) days, for the purpose of allowing an occupant to remain for more than ninety (90) continuous days." Same PDF.
- Where. 1808.C: "This section applies to all commercial uses listed in City of Myrtle Beach Code of Ordinances, Appendix A Zoning, Article 14, Section 1407.C located between Kings Highway and the Atlantic Ocean within the municipal limits." Same PDF. "This ordinance will take effect upon second reading." "1st Reading: 12-3-2024" "2nd Reading: 12-10-2024".
- The 2024 moratorium that preceded it. Cover memo: "Ordinance 2024-27, passed on 4.9.24, approved a moratorium on short-term rental properties being converted to long-term rentals in areas east of Kings Hwy." Same PDF, page 1.

Business licence
- Every rental needs a city licence. "The ordinance requires a business license for ALL rental properties which include short-term, long-term, residential and commercial." https://www.cityofmyrtlebeach.com/careers/business/business_faq.php, read 2026-09-06. Also "Please note that all owners of rental property are required to have a business license." https://www.cityofmyrtlebeach.com/departments/business_license_division.php, read 2026-09-06.
- Licence year: the two city pages disagree. FAQ: "The Business License year starts June 1 and ends on May 31." Division page: "New Business License Year...  Business license renewals are due by April 30." URLs above, read 2026-09-06.
- City tax definition of short-term. "Any stay of ninety (90) or more consecutive days in the same place is not considered a short-term accommodation." FAQ URL, read 2026-09-06.

Local taxes
- 0.5% local accommodations tax plus 1% hospitality fee. "Accommodations & Short Term Rental Providers Collect 0.5% Local Accommodations Tax & 1% Hospitality Fee" "Effective July 1, 2021". https://www.cityofmyrtlebeach.com/news_detail_T6_R1323.php, read 2026-09-06. ## 3. City of North Myrtle Beach (Horry County)

STR zoning and rules
- No STR zoning restriction or permit today. "There is currently no special zoning or permit process required beyond standard licensing." https://nmb.us/833/Short-term-Rentals, read 2026-09-06.
- Licence and tax are required. "All short-term rentals located within the City of North Myrtle Beach are required to obtain a business license." "STR's are required to collect and remit rental accommodations tax to the city, county and state." Same URL.
- City definition of STR: under 90 days, in the 2021 transient-parking ordinance. Sec. 21-45: "Short-term Rental means a dwelling unit offered for overnight accommodations that is rented for a period of less than ninety (90) days, for financial or any other consideration." https://www.nmb.us/AgendaCenter/ViewFile/Agenda/_05212021-192?packet=true (May 21, 2021 packet, ordinance 21-17), read 2026-09-06. The city's rules sheet confirms adoption: "Ordinance 21-17, Effective May 21, 2021". https://www.nmb.us/DocumentCenter/View/5580/Short-term-Rental-Rules, read 2026-09-06.
- Parking spaces must be declared at licence time. Sec. 21-47: "Short-term rental property owners shall report to the City the total number of parking spaces on-site that comply with the regulations of this section." Same packet. Trash: "the City now collects trash from short-term rentals on their regular pick up day, plus Friday, Saturday, and Sunday." Rules sheet.

2024-2026 actions (item 6)
- Failed and unadopted attempts, on the city's own timeline: "July 30, 2018 ... 4-month moratorium for residential units with 8+ sleeping areas intended for use as a Short Term Rental (Ordinance Failed)"; "August 19, 2020 ... possible short-term rentals ordinance (Ordinance not adopted)". STR page, read 2026-09-06.
- Current process. "November 20, 2024, City Council Workshop: City Council began preliminary discussion about short-term rentals, licensing, enforcement, and public concerns." "June 10, 2025, STR Information Gathering Event (Resident Perspective): Over 200 residents attended"; "September 30, 2025, STR Information Gathering Event (Owner/Operator Perspective)". STR page.
- The January 2024 draft (not adopted) would require a local agent. "Proposed Draft"; "All short-term rentals within city limits would be required to designate a responsible local agent"; "Be available 24/7 by phone and to be physically present at the short-term rental, if necessary, within 1 hour". https://www.nmb.us/AgendaCenter/ViewFile/Item/1160?fileID=3607, read 2026-09-06.
- No STR ordinance appears in the 2026 council results I opened. July 20, 2026 results list only: "ORDINANCE/SECOND READING: Amendments to Chapter 23, Zoning ... removing the maximum area/count for bedrooms/sleeping areas in residential districts (APPROVED)". https://www.nmb.us/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/1562, read 2026-09-06. August 17, 2026 results: no STR item. https://www.nmb.us/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/1572, read 2026-09-06.
- That July 2026 change removed a bedroom cap. Staff memo: the code had been "restricting dwellings to five bedrooms or sleeping areas of no more than 300 square feet each in certain residential districts"; "A resident from Windy Hill spoke in favor of maintaining limits on bedrooms or sleeping areas, citing the increasing impact of short-term rentals". https://www.nmb.us/AgendaCenter/ViewFile/Item/1431?fileID=4664, read 2026-09-06.

Business licence
- Year is May 1 to April 30. "Business licenses are valid from May 1 to April 30 each year"; "Any business physically located within the City of North Myrtle Beach (in-town business) is required to have a Business License." https://www.nmb.us/m/faq?cat=18, read 2026-09-06.

Local taxes
- 1.5% local accommodations fee; the 1% hospitality fee is on food, beverage and admissions, not lodging. Sec. 7-126: "A uniform fee equal to one and one-half (1 1/2) percent is hereby imposed on gross proceeds derived from: The rental or charges for any rooms". Sec. 7-125 (1%): "(a) The sale of all food and beverages" and "(b) Paid admissions to places of amusement". 90-day rule: "supplied to the same person for a period of ninety (90) continuous days are not considered proceeds from transients." Memo: effective date "corrected to July 1, 2021". May 21, 2021 packet URL above, read 2026-09-06.
- Horry County adds 1.5% inside the city: "inside: North Myrtle Beach $ . x 1.5% (.015)". County form URL above.
