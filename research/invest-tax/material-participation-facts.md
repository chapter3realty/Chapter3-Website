# Short-term rental tax treatment — verified facts

All quoted from IRS Publication 925, read 2026-09-04 at
https://www.irs.gov/publications/p925. Re-open before reuse.

**Owner decision 2026-09-03: teach the test, never apply it.** No self-check
tool. The page never tells a reader they qualify. This is the highest-liability
page in the cluster.

## Why a short stay changes the category

An activity is **not** a rental activity when either applies:

1. "The average period of customer use of the property is 7 days or less."
2. "The average period of customer use of the property... is 30 days or less
   and you provide significant personal services with the rentals."

Significant personal services are those "performed by individuals", judged on
"the frequency of the services, the type and amount of labor required" and
"the value of the services relative to the amount charged".

This matters because the passive loss rules treat rental activities as passive
by default. Falling outside the rental-activity definition is only step one.
Material participation is a separate question and both have to land.

## The seven material participation tests, quoted

1. "You participated in the activity for more than 500 hours."
2. "Your participation was substantially all the participation in the activity
   of all individuals for the tax year, including the participation of
   individuals who didn't own any interest in the activity."
3. More than 100 hours, and at least as much as any other individual.
4. A significant participation activity: over 100 hours in it, and over 500
   hours across all such activities combined.
5. "You materially participated in the activity (other than by meeting this
   fifth test) for any 5 (whether or not consecutive) of the 10 immediately
   preceding tax years."
6. "The activity is a personal service activity in which you materially
   participated for any 3 (whether or not consecutive) preceding tax years."
7. Facts and circumstances: regular, continuous and substantial participation.
   Disqualified at "100 hours or less".

## Participation and records

- "Any work you do in connection with an activity in which you own an interest
  is treated as participation in the activity."
- Records: "an appointment book, calendar, or narrative summary" is acceptable.
  Daily contemporaneous logs are not required. Say this plainly, because the
  fear of it stops people keeping anything at all.

## The local angle, and it is a real one

Most Grand Strand short-term rentals are run through a management company, and
condotel units are usually in a rental pool. Both arrangements move the work to
someone else, which cuts directly against tests 1, 2 and 3. An owner who hands
everything to a manager is in a materially weaker position than one who does
the work.

**State this as an observation about the tests, never as a verdict on a
reader.** Phrase it as what the tests measure, not as who passes.

## Hard limits on this page

- Never tell a reader they qualify, or that a strategy will work for them.
- Never state or imply a tax saving figure.
- Never write that Chapter3 or Devin can determine participation status.
- The page teaches what is measured and what to keep. The CPA decides.
