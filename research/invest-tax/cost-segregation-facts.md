# Cost segregation on a beach condo — verified facts

Read at the sources on 2026-09-04. Re-open before reuse.

## What a study does — IRS Cost Segregation Audit Techniques Guide (Pub 5653)
- The guide exists to help examiners review studies. Its own framing: "The
  allocation of building components to §1245 property is often a contentious
  issue."
- Bright-line tests do not exist: "Complex and often conflicting... line
  tests, have impacted the ease of distinguishing §1245 property from §1250
  property." And: "the determination of §1245 property is factually intensive
  and must be supported."
- "taxpayers still must substantiate their depreciation deductions and
  classifications."
- "A quality cost segregation study is a study that is both accurate and
  well-documented."
- "The 13 principal elements of a quality study are: Preparation by an
  Individual with Expertise and Experience, Detailed Description of the
  Methodology, Use of Appropriate Documentation, Interviews Conducted with
  Appropriate Parties, Use of a Common Nomenclature, ..."
- "A quality study includes a site visit", for acquired and used properties as
  well as new construction.

## Recovery periods — Publication 946
- Residential rental property: 27.5 years, mid-month, straight line (GDS
  table confirms "GDS/27.5 Mid-Month Residential Rental").
- Nonresidential real property: 39 years.
- 5-, 7-, 15-year classes exist under GDS; carpets and appliances appear in
  the 5-year asset class tables (Table B-2 references). Office furniture and
  fixtures are 7-year. Land improvements are 15-year.
- Land: "You cannot depreciate the cost of land because land does not wear
  out, become obsolete, or get used up."
- Bonus: for property acquired after January 19, 2025, "100% special
  depreciation allowance." A 40 percent election exists as an alternative.
  **Do not write "permanent"** — that word comes from CPA blogs, not from the
  publication. Write the rate and the date.

## Recapture — Publication 544 and Topic 409
- §1245 property (the reclassified personal property): gain up to the
  depreciation allowed or allowable is treated as ordinary income. This is the
  cost of the acceleration, paid at sale.
- §1250 property (the building): "The portion of any unrecaptured section 1250
  gain from selling section 1250 real property is taxed at a maximum 25%
  rate." (Topic 409, quoted.)
- Net capital gain otherwise "no higher than 15% for most individuals" for
  2025 (Topic 409).
- A 1031 exchange defers the gain; link the 1031 page rather than repeat it.

## The beach-condo angle, and its limits
- A furnished condotel or rental-pool unit carries furniture, appliances,
  carpet, window treatments and decorative lighting in its basis, which is
  where a study finds short-life property. That is why the topic matters more
  here than on an unfurnished long-term rental.
- A condo owner holds an undivided share of the common elements, including
  the land. **How the land share is allocated on a condo is a CPA question.
  State it as a question, not an answer.**
- Whether the accelerated loss can be used at all is decided by the passive
  loss rules and the material participation tests on
  `/invest/str-tax-treatment/`. National cost-seg blogs skip this. It is the
  most important sentence on the page.

## Hard limits on this page
- No study-cost dollar figure. Not sourced.
- No savings figure, no percentage of basis reclassified. Not sourced, and
  varies by property.
- Never write that a reader should order a study. Write what decides whether
  one is worth ordering, and stop.
- No "permanent" on bonus depreciation.
