# Accommodations tax and business licence — verified facts

Every line here was read at the primary source on the date shown. Re-open
before reuse (non-negotiable 8; 20 of 163 facts were wrong in the last batch).

## State level — verified 2026-09-03 at SCDOR

| Component | Rate | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Sales tax on accommodations | 5% | dor.sc.gov/sales-use-tax-index/accommodations |
| State accommodations tax | 2% | same |
| **State total** | **7%** | same |
| Local add-on ceiling | up to 3% | same |

**Not the general 6% sales rate.** Accommodations are taxed at 5% sales plus
the 2% accommodations tax. Getting this wrong is the single easiest error on
the page; the site already states it correctly on /invest/str-rules/.

**90-day rule.** SCDOR: the tax "does not apply to the lease or rental of
accommodations supplied to the same person for a period of 90 continuous days."

**Six-bedroom exemption.** Properties with six or fewer bedrooms are exempt
where the owner or operator lives on site AND does not use a rental agency or
an online travel company. The second condition kills it for most investors.

**Who remits when a platform is involved.** SCDOR: "the person or business who
books the accommodations is responsible for collecting and paying." Where an
online travel company handles the reservation and the payment, that company is
responsible for the full booking amount, and the owner does not need a retail
licence for those bookings. This is the fact most owners get wrong in both
directions.

## Horry County — verified 2026-09-03 at horrycountysc.gov treasurer

| Where | Hospitality fee on transient accommodations |
|---|---|
| Outside city limits | 3% of gross proceeds |
| Inside city limits | 1.5% of gross proceeds |

Filing is monthly, due the 20th of the month after the reporting period. The

## Business licence — verified 2026-09-03

The pattern across the Grand Strand: a rental is a business, the fee is
measured on **gross rental income and not on profit**, the year runs to
**April 30**, and late costs **5 percent per month**. No national CPA blog
carries this. It is the strongest single fact on the page.

### City of Myrtle Beach — cityofmyrtlebeach.com business license division
- "All owners of rental property are required to have a business license."
  Applies to long-term and short-term alike; the page draws no distinction.
- Renewal due **April 30** each year.
- Late penalty: **5 percent of the licence fee for each month or fraction of a
  month** until fee and penalty are paid in full.
- Base fee calculation not stated on that page. **Still to confirm.**

### North Myrtle Beach — nmb.us business licence FAQ
- Licence period runs **May 1 to April 30**. Renewals mailed mid-March, due
  back by **April 30**.
- Late penalty: **5 percent** if postmarked May 1 or later, **increasing 5
  percent each month**.
- Fee is "calculated based on gross income"; classification is assigned by
  their staff.
- **The FAQ does not state that a rental property is a business requiring a
  licence.** It says any business physically located in the city needs one.
  Do NOT write that North Myrtle Beach requires one for rentals until someone
  confirms it with the business licence office, 843-280-5585.

### Surfside Beach — surfsidebeach.org business licensing
- "All real estate rentals are considered a business requiring a business
  license."
- Renewal due **April 30**.

## The three jurisdictions he asked me to verify — 2026-09-03

### The headline finding: the transient threshold is NOT the same everywhere

| Jurisdiction | A stay stops being taxable at | Source |
|---|---|---|
| South Carolina (state) | **90** continuous days | SCDOR |
| Horry County hospitality fee | **90** consecutive days | horrycountysc.gov |
| City of Conway | **90** continuous days | Conway code of ordinances, Art. D |
| **Georgetown County** | **30** continuous days | gtcountysc.gov/351 |

An owner in Murrells Inlet or Pawleys Island who learned the 90-day rule from a
Horry County source will get this wrong. A 45-day stay is exempt from the
Georgetown County accommodations tax and still owes the state tax. This is the
best fact on the page and no national source carries it.

### Georgetown County — gtcountysc.gov/351, read 2026-09-03
- Local accommodations tax **3%** on accommodations furnished to transients.
- Local hospitality tax **2%**, but that is on prepared food and beverages, not
  on lodging. Do not add it to a lodging total.
- Transient threshold **30 or more continuous days**.
- Remit by the **20th** of each month for the prior month.
- Filing frequency scales: monthly over $50/month estimated, quarterly $25 to
  $50, annually under $25. Small owners file once a year.
- The business establishment collects and remits.
- Separately: an owner renting a **furnished** residence in Georgetown County
  files a **PT100** with the state each year. Business personal property, and
  widely missed. Verify at SCDOR before publishing.
- Contact: Beth Elliott, 843-545-3002.

### City of Conway — Conway code of ordinances, Article D
- Hospitality fee **1%** of gross proceeds on transient accommodations.
- Same 1% also applies to prepared food and beverages and to paid admissions.
- **90 continuous day** exemption.
- Monthly remittance when estimated average fee is over $50 a month.

### Unincorporated Horry County — horrycountysc.gov business licence
- A county business licence "is required of every person engaged in any
  calling, business, occupation or profession within the unincorporated areas
  (outside the city limits) of Horry County."
- Hospitality fee 3% outside city limits, as recorded above.
- If a property manager arranges the booking, confirm the manager is actually
  collecting and remitting. The obligation does not disappear.
- Business Licence Department: 843-915-5620.

## CORRECTION 2026-09-05: Georgetown County requires NO business license

The owner challenged the sentence "Georgetown County still requires one for
stays under 30 days." He was right. The county's own FAQ
(gtcounty.org/Faq.aspx?QID=116): Georgetown County does not issue or require
business licenses in the unincorporated areas. The City of Georgetown, Andrews
and the Town of Pawleys Island may have their own; Pawleys Island has none.

The wrong sentence came from a search summary that cited a third-party
listing site, not the county. It was published on `/invest/accommodations-tax/`
and used to "correct" a sentence on `/invest/str-rules/` that had been right
all along. Both are now fixed. MISTAKES 67.

What unincorporated Georgetown County does require of a furnished rental: the
yearly PT-100 business personal property return to the state, a tax on the
furniture. That is now the chart row.
