The short answer

North Myrtle Beach Airbnb Market at a Glance

Bookings in North Myrtle Beach trail the South Carolina average, at prices about 6% higher. Listings averaged $375 a night and were booked on 35% of available nights, against $355 and 39% for the South Carolina average.

Timing matters: in July, usually the best month, the average listing earns about five times as much as in January, usually the slowest.

With 4,564 active listings, North Myrtle Beach is the third-largest of 116 South Carolina markets AirROI tracks. From a year earlier, average revenue rose 12% while the number of active listings grew 31%.

Revenue

How Much Do Airbnb Hosts Make in North Myrtle Beach?

Gross bookings per North Myrtle Beach listing averaged $34,401 over the 12 months to September 2026 (South Carolina average: $37,525), before cleaning, platform and management costs and taxes.

In a typical year, the average North Myrtle Beach listing brings in $9,640 in July, its best month, and $1,890 in January, its slowest. The three strongest months bring in 47% of a full year's revenue, where an even calendar would give any three months 25%.

In a typical month, a top-quarter listing earns at least 3.6 times as much as a bottom-quarter one.

  • Best-in-class properties (top 10%) earn $9,850+ a month, about three times the typical property.
  • Strong performers (top 25%) earn $5,760 or more, roughly $2,500 a month above the median.
  • Typical properties (median) earn about $3,220 a month.
  • Entry-level properties (bottom 25%) earn $1,580 or less, about half of the median.

These are monthly figures for listings active in each month. The annual average above also counts listings that operated for only part of the year, so it runs below twelve times these figures.

Profit

Is an Airbnb in North Myrtle Beach Profitable?

Yes, when it's run well. The average North Myrtle Beach Airbnb took in $34,401 in the 12 months to September 2026, and run by its owner at everyday costs, about $1,700 a month of that is left after running costs, so a home that costs less than that to own or rent makes money. A listing in the top quarter leaves about $3,080 a month.

Cleaning it yourself keeps the $5,190 a year guests pay in cleaning fees, for about $2,130 a month. Handing the listing to a co-host at about 10% of nightly bookings still leaves about $1,460 a month.

These amounts come before housing costs. Where the mortgage or rent is less each month, the difference is profit; a home owned outright keeps it all after property tax, home insurance and any HOA or condo fees.

Occupancy

What Is the Airbnb Occupancy Rate in North Myrtle Beach?

North Myrtle Beach listings were booked on 35% of the nights they were open over the past year, 4 percentage points below the South Carolina average (39%) and 6 points below the U.S. average (41%). In a typical year, occupancy swings from 22% in January to 58% in July.

The busiest tenth of listings are booked about 4.3 times as often as the bottom quarter.

  • Best-in-class properties (top 10%) are booked 71%+ of open nights, 39 points above the median.
  • Strong performers (top 25%) fill 53% or more, roughly four nights a week.
  • Typical properties (median) fill about 32% of open nights.
  • Entry-level properties (bottom 25%) fill 16% or less, under two nights a week.

Nightly rates

The average nightly rate in North Myrtle Beach, known as ADR (average daily rate), was $375 over the past year, about 6% above the South Carolina average of $355 and about 17% above the U.S. average of $320.   • Typical properties (median) charge about $290 a night.

What Is the RevPAR for Airbnb in North Myrtle Beach?

RevPAR, or revenue per available night, divides a listing's income by every night it was open to book, so it rises with both price and bookings. In North Myrtle Beach it averaged $145 over the past year, from about $61 in a typical January to about $311 in a typical July.

Per open night, by tier:

  • Best-in-class properties (top 10%) earn $290+ per open night, about 2.7 times the median.
  • Strong performers (top 25%) earn $181 or more, about $75 a night above the median.
  • Typical properties (median) earn about $108 per open night.
  • Entry-level properties (bottom 25%) earn $64 or less, about 60% of the median.

About This Data

Figures on this page cover 4,564 active Airbnb listings in North Myrtle Beach from October 2025 to September 2026 (trailing twelve months). AirROI refreshes them monthly from listing-level booking and calendar data covering 20+ million properties worldwide. Money figures are in U.S. dollars. Annual revenue averages every listing active during the period, including those listed for only part of it; monthly figures average the listings active in each month; the tier lists show the spread. Neighborhood descriptions are AI-assisted. See the methodology for definitions.
