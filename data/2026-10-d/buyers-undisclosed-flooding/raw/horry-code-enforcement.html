Code Enforcement

Code Enforcement

The major function of code enforcement is to issue building permits and inspect new construction and improvements to all buildings located within the unincorporated area of Horry County. The building inspection branch of the Code Enforcement Department also manages the enforcement of the National Flood Insurance Program and performs fire inspections on commercial buildings located in the unincorporated areas.

All complaints need to be submitted through a 311 request.

Online Portal Inspection Scheduling & Invoice Payment

The Online Portal provides access for inspection scheduling for listed permit types, online invoice payments and displays information for office submitted permit cases.

Building Permits

Building permits are a type of authorization that must be granted by a government or other regulatory body before the construction of a new or existing building can legally occur.
