Gross bookings per Murrells Inlet listing averaged $47,603 over the 12 months to September 2026 (South Carolina average: $37,525), before cleaning, platform and management costs and taxes.

Profit

Is an Airbnb in Murrells Inlet Profitable?

Yes, when it's run well. The average Murrells Inlet Airbnb took in $47,603 in the 12 months to September 2026, and run by its owner at everyday costs, about $2,390 a month of that is left after running costs, so a home that costs less than that to own or rent makes money. A listing in the top quarter leaves about $4,290 a month.

Cleaning it yourself keeps the $6,890 a year guests pay in cleaning fees, for about $2,960 a month. Handing the listing to a co-host at about 10% of nightly bookings still leaves about $2,050 a month.

These amounts come before housing costs. Where the mortgage or rent is less each month, the difference is profit; a home owned outright keeps it all after property tax, home insurance and any HOA or condo fees.
