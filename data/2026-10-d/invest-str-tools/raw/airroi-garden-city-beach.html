Revenue

How Much Do Airbnb Hosts Make in Garden City Beach?

Gross bookings per Garden City Beach listing averaged $41,223 over the 12 months to September 2026 (South Carolina average: $37,525), before cleaning, platform and management costs and taxes.

In a typical year, the average Garden City Beach listing brings in $10,200 in July, its best month, and $2,090 in January, its slowest. The three strongest months bring in 45% of a full year's revenue, where an even calendar would give any three months 25%.

In a typical month, a top-quarter listing earns at least three times as much as a bottom-quarter one.

  • Best-in-class properties (top 10%) earn $10,700+ a month, about three times the typical property.
  • Strong performers (top 25%) earn $5,870 or more, roughly $2,400 a month above the median.
  • Typical properties (median) earn about $3,470 a month.
  • Entry-level properties (bottom 25%) earn $1,930 or less, about 56% of the median.

These are monthly figures for listings active in each month. The annual average above also counts listings that operated for only part of the year, so it runs below twelve times these figures.

Profit

Is an Airbnb in Garden City Beach Profitable?

Yes, when it's run well. The average Garden City Beach Airbnb took in $41,223 in the 12 months to September 2026, and run by its owner at everyday costs, about $2,110 a month of that is left after running costs, so a home that costs less than that to own or rent makes money. A listing in the top quarter leaves about $3,610 a month.

Cleaning it yourself keeps the $5,720 a year guests pay in cleaning fees, for about $2,590 a month. Handing the listing to a co-host at about 10% of nightly bookings still leaves about $1,820 a month.

These amounts come before housing costs. Where the mortgage or rent is less each month, the difference is profit; a home owned outright keeps it all after property tax, home insurance and any HOA or condo fees.

How Garden City Beach's Top Earners Get There

  • More nights booked. The busiest quarter of Garden City Beach listings are booked on 61% or more of their nights, against 39% for the median listing. Ten more booked nights a year at the median rate of $280 adds about $2,800 in bookings.
  • Prices that follow demand. Ranked by price, the top quarter charge $420 a night or more, against a median of $280. That is a separate ranking from the busiest quarter, so the two marks don't multiply into one listing's takings. For the average listing, a 10% higher rate on the nights it already books adds about $3,550 a year.
  • Open for the busy months. In a typical year, July brings the average listing about $10,200, against $2,090 in January, so a calendar that is open and full in the busy months carries the year.
  • Reviews that stand out. Garden City Beach listings average 4.80 stars, and 43% carry Airbnb's Guest Favorite badge, which guests can filter for.

What a listing keeps comes down to the figures this page measures: nightly rate, nights booked and the months it's open. A listing at the top-quarter mark takes in about $5,810 a month, against $3,440 for the average one.

Occupancy

What Is the Airbnb Occupancy Rate in Garden City Beach?

Garden City Beach listings were booked on 40% of the nights they were open over the past year, in line with the South Carolina average and in line with the U.S. average. In a typical year, occupancy swings from 25% in January to 65% in July.

The busiest tenth of listings are booked about four times as often as the bottom quarter.

  • Best-in-class properties (top 10%) are booked 79%+ of open nights, 40 points above the median.
  • Strong performers (top 25%) fill 61% or more, roughly four nights a week.
  • Typical properties (median) fill about 39% of open nights.
  • Entry-level properties (bottom 25%) fill 20% or less, under two nights a week.

Nightly rates

How Much Does an Airbnb Cost per Night in Garden City Beach?

The average nightly rate in Garden City Beach, known as ADR (average daily rate), was $371 over the past year, about the same as the South Carolina average ($355) and about 16% above the U.S. average of $320. In a typical year, rates swing with the seasons, from about $285 in November to about $460 in June and July.

Prices span a wide range: the top 10% charge about 3.4 times the rate of the bottom quarter.

  • Best-in-class properties (top 10%) charge $705+ a night, about 2.5 times the median.
  • Strong performers (top 25%) charge $420 or more, about $140 above the median.
  • Typical properties (median) charge about $280 a night.
  • Entry-level properties (bottom 25%) charge $205 or less, about three-quarters of the median.

What Is the RevPAR for Airbnb in Garden City Beach?

RevPAR, or revenue per available night, divides a listing's income by every night it was open to book, so it rises with both price and bookings. In Garden City Beach it averaged $159 over the past year, from about $67 in a typical January to about $328 in a typical July.

Per open night, by tier:

  • Best-in-class properties (top 10%) earn $331+ per open night, about 2.8 times the median.
  • Strong performers (top 25%) earn $188 or more, about $70 a night above the median.
  • Typical properties (median) earn about $120 per open night.
  • Entry-level properties (bottom 25%) earn $74 or less, about 62% of the median.
