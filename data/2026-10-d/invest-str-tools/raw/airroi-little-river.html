The short answer

Little River Airbnb Market at a Glance

Rates and bookings both trail the South Carolina average. Listings averaged $200 a night and were booked on 34% of available nights, against $355 and 39% for the South Carolina average.

Timing matters: in July, usually the best month, the average listing earns about 2.8 times as much as in November, usually the slowest.

With 169 active listings, Little River is the 30th-largest of 116 South Carolina markets AirROI tracks. From a year earlier, average revenue rose 19% while the number of active listings grew 56%.

Revenue

How Much Do Airbnb Hosts Make in Little River?

Gross bookings per Little River listing averaged $14,963 over the 12 months to September 2026 (South Carolina average: $37,525), before cleaning, platform and management costs and taxes.

In a typical year, the average Little River listing brings in $3,650 in July, its best month, and $1,300 in November, its slowest. January runs within 3% of November, so either can be the slowest in a given year. The three strongest months bring in 40% of a full year's revenue, where an even calendar would give any three months 25%.

In a typical month, a top-quarter listing earns at least 3.5 times as much as a bottom-quarter one.

  • Best-in-class properties (top 10%) earn $4,600+ a month, about 2.7 times the typical property.
  • Strong performers (top 25%) earn $3,000 or more, roughly $1,300 a month above the median.
  • Typical properties (median) earn about $1,700 a month.
  • Entry-level properties (bottom 25%) earn $854 or less, about half of the median.

These are monthly figures for listings active in each month. The annual average above also counts listings that operated for only part of the year, so it runs below twelve times these figures.

Profit

Is an Airbnb in Little River Profitable?

Yes, when it's run well. The average Little River Airbnb took in $14,963 in the 12 months to September 2026, and run by its owner at everyday costs, about $730 a month of that is left after running costs, so a home that costs less than that to own or rent makes money. A listing in the top quarter leaves about $1,320 a month.

Cleaning it yourself keeps the $2,370 a year guests pay in cleaning fees, for about $930 a month. Handing the listing to a co-host at about 10% of nightly bookings still leaves about $630 a month.

These amounts come before housing costs. Where the mortgage or rent is less each month, the difference is profit; a home owned outright keeps it all after property tax, home insurance and any HOA or condo fees.

How Little River's Top Earners Get There

  • More nights booked. The busiest quarter of Little River listings are booked on 48% or more of their nights, against 29% for the median listing. Ten more booked nights a year at the median rate of $180 adds about $1,780 in bookings.
  • Prices that follow demand. Ranked by price, the top quarter charge $245 a night or more, against a median of $180. That is a separate ranking from the busiest quarter, so the two marks don't multiply into one listing's takings. For the average listing, a 10% higher rate on the nights it already books adds about $1,260 a year.
  • Open for the busy months. In a typical year, July brings the average listing about $3,650, against $1,300 in November, so a calendar that is open and full in the busy months carries the year.
  • Reviews that stand out. Little River listings average 4.73 stars, and 40% carry Airbnb's Guest Favorite badge, which guests can filter for.

What a listing keeps comes down to the figures this page measures: nightly rate, nights booked and the months it's open. A listing at the top-quarter mark takes in about $2,210 a month, against $1,250 for the average one.

Occupancy

What Is the Airbnb Occupancy Rate in Little River?

Little River listings were booked on 34% of the nights they were open over the past year, 5 percentage points below the South Carolina average (39%) and 7 points below the U.S. average (41%). In a typical year, occupancy climbs to 49% in June and falls to 25% in January.

The busiest tenth of listings are booked about 4.8 times as often as the bottom quarter.

  • Best-in-class properties (top 10%) are booked 70%+ of open nights, 41 points above the median.
  • Strong performers (top 25%) fill 48% or more, roughly three nights a week.
  • Typical properties (median) fill about 29% of open nights.
  • Entry-level properties (bottom 25%) fill 15% or less, under two nights a week.

Nightly rates

How Much Does an Airbnb Cost per Night in Little River?

The average nightly rate in Little River, known as ADR (average daily rate), was $200 over the past year, about 44% below the South Carolina average of $355 and about 38% below the U.S. average of $320. In a typical year, rates peak in July at about $225 and bottom out in October and November at about $155.

The top 10% charge about 2.4 times the rate of the bottom quarter.

  • Best-in-class properties (top 10%) charge $310+ a night, about 1.7 times the median.
  • Strong performers (top 25%) charge $245 or more, about $65 above the median.
  • Typical properties (median) charge about $180 a night.
  • Entry-level properties (bottom 25%) charge $130 or less, about three-quarters of the median.

What Is the RevPAR for Airbnb in Little River?

RevPAR, or revenue per available night, divides a listing's income by every night it was open to book, so it rises with both price and bookings. In Little River it averaged $71 over the past year, from about $43 in a typical January to about $118 in a typical July.

Per open night, by tier:

  • Best-in-class properties (top 10%) earn $135+ per open night, about 2.2 times the median.
  • Strong performers (top 25%) earn $88 or more, about $25 a night above the median.
  • Typical properties (median) earn about $61 per open night.
  • Entry-level properties (bottom 25%) earn $41 or less, about two-thirds of the median.

Permits

Airbnb Permits and Registration in Little River

None of the 169 active listings in Little River display a registration, permit or license number. That doesn't mean no rules apply: some places don't ask hosts to show a number on the listing, and listing data doesn't capture every scheme.

Registration, permit, zoning and occupancy-tax rules vary by address and change often, so confirm them with the city or county before buying or listing in Little River.

Guests

Who Books Airbnbs in Little River?

Little River draws mostly domestic guests: 98% of those who left reviews live in the United States. No single city dominates: Charlotte is the largest source, at about 2% of reviews. English is spoken by 79% of guests, and Spanish by 14%.

What Are Airbnb Guest Ratings in Little River?

Little River Airbnbs average 4.73 stars, and 40% of listings hold Airbnb's Guest Favorite badge. The category scores are tightly bunched, and cleanliness is the lowest, at 4.69.

Booking patterns

How Guests Book in Little River: Stays, Rules and Fees

Nearly all Little River listings are open to guests more than 180 days a year, but only 3% are booked or blocked that many days. The most common booked-or-blocked total is 31 to 90 days a year, covering 39% of listings; blocked nights include any the host closes off.

What Is the Average Length of Stay for Airbnb in Little River?

The average Little River booking lasts 5.8 nights, longest in January and shortest in August. Listings took about 14 bookings each over the year, some 2,330 in all. About three-quarters of listings that give a check-in time set it at 4 p.m., with check-out usually at 10 a.m.

Little River Airbnb Minimum Stay Requirements

More than six in ten listings in Little River accept stays of one or two nights.

What Cancellation Policies Do Airbnb Hosts Use in Little River?

Firm is the most common cancellation policy in Little River, used by 54% of listings; it refunds guests in full only if they cancel at least 30 days before check-in. Hosts here lean strict: nearly seven in ten listings use Firm or stricter terms, against 24% on Flexible or Moderate.

How Much Are Airbnb Cleaning Fees in Little River?
