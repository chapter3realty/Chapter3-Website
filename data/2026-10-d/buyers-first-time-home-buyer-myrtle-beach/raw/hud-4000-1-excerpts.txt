== interested party contributions (15125-15142)
                Interested Parties refer to sellers, real estate agents, builders, developers,
                Mortgagees, Third-Party Originators (TPO), or other parties with an interest in the
                transaction.

                Interested Party Contribution refers to a payment by an Interested Party, or
                combination of parties, toward the Borrower’s origination fees, other closing costs
                including any items POC, prepaid items, and Discount Points.

                (2) Standard

                Interested Parties may contribute up to 6 percent of the sales price toward the
                Borrower’s origination fees, other closing costs, prepaid items, and Discount
                Points. The 6 percent limit also includes:
                    • Interested Party payment for permanent and temporary interest rate
                        buydowns, and other payment supplements;
                    • payments of mortgage interest for fixed rate Mortgages;
                    • Mortgage Payment protection insurance; and
                    • payment of the UFMIP.
== glossary, First-Time Homebuyer
First-Time Homebuyer
A First-Time Homebuyer refers to an individual who has not held an ownership interest in
another property in the three years prior to the case number assignment. First-Time Homebuyer
includes an individual who is divorced or legally separated and who has had no ownership
interest in a Principal Residence (other than joint ownership interest with a spouse) during the
three years prior to case number assignment.
