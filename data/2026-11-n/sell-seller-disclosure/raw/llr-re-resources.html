Inspections Information

In accordance with S.C. Code of Law Section §40-57-60(C), the Commission shall conduct periodic inspections of offices of licensees to assist with and ensure compliance with SC Law.

LLR inspectors are legitimate and perform inspections of all real estate and property management offices supervised by a licensee to assist with and ensure compliance with the laws governing real estate in South Carolina. If you are contacted by an LLR Inspector, you are required to respond promptly and follow all instructions provided.
