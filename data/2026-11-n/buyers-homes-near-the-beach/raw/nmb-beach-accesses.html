Beach Accesses & Amenities

The City of North Myrtle Beach Parks and Grounds division manages over 240 beach accesses in North Myrtle Beach. The City strives to provide access to all visitors and residents.

Parking Locations


The City of North Myrtle Beach has over 50 locations for public parking for beach access. Please use the interactive map to see the location of each parking lot and any special amenities available (bathroom and handicap accessibility.)

Park North Myrtle Beach is responsible for the operation and maintenance of the parking pay stations. If you need more information on parking in North Myrtle Beach, use the following link Beach Access Parking Information. For further questions or need to report a problem, please contact us at parking@nmb.us or (843) 480-3711.
