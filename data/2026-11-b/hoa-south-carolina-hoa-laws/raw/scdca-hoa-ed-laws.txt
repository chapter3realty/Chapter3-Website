 Homeowners Association (HOA) Education: SC Laws 
 While the Homeowners Association Act , Nonprofit Corporations Act and Horizontal Property Act are the most commonly known laws that may apply to a homeowners association (HOA), other statutes may apply as well. While SCDCA makes its best effort to include all relevant laws, the below listing may not be an exclusive one. Any suggestions for content may be submitted to us by e-mail or snail mail .
