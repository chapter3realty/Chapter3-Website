Intro
The Clerk of Court's (Circuit and Family Court) purpose is to provide all the services of the judicial system to the general public. Our goal is to ensure that our services are rendered in a timely and efficient manner.

. This change ONLY affects support payments.
If you appeared before a judge due to failure to pay your child support and were ordered to pay a specific amount (sometimes referred to as a purge payment) to be in compliance with the judge’s ruling, you must come to the payment window in the Clerks of Court office and pay cash. There is no ATM machine on premises.
Other types of county payments (for example, property taxes) can still be paid by credit card from the county’s website.
