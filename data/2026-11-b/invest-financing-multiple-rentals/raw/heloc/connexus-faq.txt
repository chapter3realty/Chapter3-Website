get a HELOC for my second home or one of my investment properties? We do not offer HELOCs for investment properties; however, you can get a HELOC for a second home if the property meets our eligibility standards. Eligible property types include primary residences and second homes (located at least 50 miles from the primary residence) owned for at least 6 months. Properties must be one of the following: single-family homes, 2-4-unit condominiums, owner-occupied duplexes, or townhouses.
