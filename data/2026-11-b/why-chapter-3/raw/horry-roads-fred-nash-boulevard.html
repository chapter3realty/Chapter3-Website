Fred Nash Boulevard

Fred Nash Boulevard

Project Number
S100016
Type
Major Projects
Program Source
RIDE 3

Description

Construct new 3-lane road, including a center turn-lane, to extend Fred Nash Boulevard around the end of the airport runway (MYR) to provide a direct connection to Harrelson Boulevard. The project includes bicycle facilities.

Features and Benefits

  • New 3-Lane Road
    Constructs a 3-lane road with a center turn lane to improve traffic flow and access.

  • Direct Connection to Harrelson Boulevard
    Provides a more efficient route around the airport, enhancing regional connectivity.

  • Bicycle Facilities
    Includes dedicated bike lanes to support safe, multimodal transportation options.

  • Improved Access for Airport & Surrounding Areas
    Strengthens infrastructure around the Myrtle Beach International Airport (MYR).
