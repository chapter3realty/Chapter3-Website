 OVERVIEW 
 The Parks, Recreation & Sports Tourism Department   offers daily, monthly, three-month and annual memberships and/or payment options are available for Crabtree Memorial Gymnasium, Mary C. Canty Recreation Center and Pepper Geddings Recreation Center.  Also, multi-use punch cards are available.  
 Fee totals are divided into two groups:  city residents and non-city residents.  The city's recreation facilities are funded by property taxes paid by city residents, therefore city residents receive a discounted fee.   US military veterans can join at the in-city rate, whether they live inside the city or not.   Additional fees may be applicable when participating in specific activities and programs like adult sports or youth sports.
