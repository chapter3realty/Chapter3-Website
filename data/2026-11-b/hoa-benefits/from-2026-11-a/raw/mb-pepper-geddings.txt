 OVERVIEW 
 The Parks, Recreation & Sports Tourism Department manages Pepper Geddings Recreation Center, one of the city's four recreation centers.   The facility is well-equipped with a variety of exercise options, including... 
 Aquatic Center 
  25-Yard Swimming Pool (Six Lanes) 
  Shallow Pool 
 Cardio Room (Treadmills, Ellipticals, Bikes, Stair Climbers & More) 
 Exercise Classes 
 Exercise Program 
 Indoor Track 
 Weight Room (Free Weights & Machines) 
 Two Gyms 
 Eight Basketball Court Goals 
 Pickleball Equipment 
 Volleyball Equipment
