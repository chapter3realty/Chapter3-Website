On behalf of the staff, we welcome your membership and hope you will consider making the Aquatic & Fitness Center a regular part of your life. Types of Memberships We Have to Offer We offer a daily rate of $15 per person ages 5 and above. A valid driver’s license must be provided in order to purchase a daily pass. For ages 4 years old and under a daily pass is $5 per person. We offer weekly passes of $58 per person. Weekly passes are for one person only. A valid driver’s license must be provided in order to purchase a weekly pass.
