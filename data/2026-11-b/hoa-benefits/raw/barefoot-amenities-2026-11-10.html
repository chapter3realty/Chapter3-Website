Beach Cabana

Barefoot Resort offers owners in the Single-family home communities and Mulit-family communities who are part of The Barefoot Resort Residential Association (BRRA) private access to the oceanfront Cabana.  The Beach Cabana is located at 4611 South Ocean Boulevard, just south of 46th Avenue, in North Myrtle Beach. The Beach Cabana provides residents who are part of the BRRA an area to get out of the sun or sunbathe on the top-deck with a spectacular view of the Atlantic Ocean.

Cabana conveniences include:

  • Private Owner Parking with Key Fob entry
  • Key Fob entry with private access to the beach
  • Fully equipped kitchen with refrigerator and microwave
  • Ice machines located on the first and second floors
  • Showers on the ground floor
  • Pet washing station
  • Elevator allows for easy accessibility to multiple floors
  • Bathroom facilities
  • Staff attendants
  • Surveillance cameras
  • Shuttle service drop off at Public Access parking lot on Ocean
    Boulevard

North Tower Pool

This beautiful ionized amenity is one of, if not the, largest pools in the area, holding approximately 356,000 gallons of water. The pool is available for the recreation pleasure for the Barefoot Resort Residential Association (BRRA) and Non-Residential Association (NRA) owners, their families and guests. With its variety of water depths and abundant chaise lounges, the pool is a favorite sunning and swimming spot for Barefoot. Located within view of the beautiful Intracoastal Waterway next to the North Tower Condominiums, the pool is tucked away from the main thoroughfares and close to restaurant service.

Residents’ Club

This beautiful 5,000 square foot facility is located in the center of Barefoot Resort and one of the main focal points for select single-family communities. The first-class amenity features a spacious pool, weight room, library, craft/multi-purpose room, open main room with fireplace, card tables and pool table, tennis/pickleball courts, basketball court, picnic pavilion, and playground. Under the direction of the Residents’ Club Lifestyle Director, you will enjoy activities and special events that will make you feel like you are on vacation every day… right here at home.
