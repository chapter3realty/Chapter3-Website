Prince Resort Amenities

Given its breathtaking oceanfront location only steps from the bustling Cherry Grove Pier, it’s little wonder that Prince Resort is known as one of the best resorts in North Myrtle Beach. Whether seeking a little family fun or a quiet romantic escape, our guests enjoy the full range of luxury services and amenities for North Myrtle Beach getaways. We invite you to relax, leave your watch and phone behind, and let the rhythms of the South Carolina coast guide you through your day. 

Enjoy a soak in the rooftop pool or grab an inner tube and meander along the lazy river. Tone up with a workout in the fitness center or slow things down by arranging a spa treatment through the front desk. Ask about booking a helicopter tour in the skies above Myrtle Beach or tee times at some of the top golf courses in South Carolina. Or you could just drop a towel on our sandy beach and do nothing at all. At Prince Resort, the choice is entirely yours.

Your Oceanfront Escape Awaits at Prince Resort

From the rooftop lazy river to fresh oceanfront dining, everything you need for the perfect Cherry Grove getaway is right here. Book direct for our best available rate.
