Your Myrtle Beach Vacation Made Easy

Sand Dunes Resort is oceanfront in Myrtle Beach’s Dunes neighborhood, with a wide range of accommodations and plenty to do on property, beach time, water fun, family activities, and places to grab a bite without overplanning your days. Choose from suites, condos, and villas across eight distinct buildings then dive into free waterpark access, pools, dining, and family fun. Vacation at a resort so packed with fun and relaxation you can truly settle in.

ABOUT US

Sand Dunes is part of Sands Resorts and our resort’s beach location is considered one of the best along the Myrtle Beach coastline, each of the Sands Resorts are nestled within the quaint communities of the Grand Strand. Our oceanfront Myrtle Beach resort, long sandy beaches, is considered by many to be among the most beautiful in Myrtle Beach.
