Myrtle Beach Resort Amenities

Experience Sand Dunes Resort’s Family-Fun Amenities

Sand Dunes oceanfront resort is located along the best coastline of the Grand Strand. Walk from your accommodation to wide sandy beaches great for a fun filled or relaxing time.  Splash about in the waves. Pull out a sand bucket or ball to enjoy on the beach or simply sit back and soak in the rays. Myrtle Beach provides seasonal lifeguards along with beach umbrella and chair rentals. During your stay, discover the many ways you can relax, rejuvenate, and make lasting memories in what most vacationers consider the best shoreline section of Myrtle Beach.

Your visit to Sand Dunes Resort offers the perfect balance of fun and relaxation. Enjoy the sights and sounds of our beautiful oceanfront surroundings, a variety of water excitement, oceanfront dining, the Family FunZone packed with challenging games along with an opportunity to pamper yourself in the Atlantis salon.  Our amenities add up to a great vacation beach trip as Sand Dunes Resort provides fun and relaxation for an awesome vacation!

Waterpark & Pool Activities

Experience 130,000 gallons of splashing fun at our onsite waterpark! Admission to the Sands Resorts Waterpark is included when you book through Sands Resorts — directly or through a third-party site. It features dozens of water attractions for all ages and comfort levels-no long lines and ridiculously high prices. To truly experience what Sand Dunes Resort has to offer you need to take advantage of all of the resort’s water activities. Please contact the resort for the 2026 seasonal hours.

Family FunZone

Kid Approved and guaranteed to provide the entire family excitement throughout the day and night during your beach vacation. Enjoy the newest attraction Neptune Blacklight Indoor Mini Golf, 9 holes of glow-in-the-dark mini golf surrounded by a virtual canvas, as our walls are alive with witty, comical, and relatable artwork of beach fun and sea creatures belonging to our area. During the winter season, we will continue to offer mini-golf and arcade services depending on occupancy. Please contact our reservation center for details.

Oceanfront Dining

Fill your vacation appetites and thirst for relaxation and enjoyment. The Sand Dunes Resort offers a casual oceanfront dining atmosphere with indoor/outdoor seating available. Oceanside restaurant and lounge provide a distinctive personality, that cohesively matches the different themes of our beachfront resort.

Atlantis Nail Salon

Pamper and indulge yourself with the Atlantis Nail Salon’s professional nail care service and supplies. The salon is located next door in the North Shore Hotel and offers relaxing body treatments and skin services from experienced professionals.

Guest Laundry

The guest laundry facility features coin operated washers and dryers where guests can freshen up their clothes, wash beach towels or do a full load of laundry. Coin dispensers are available in the laundry room located on the first floor of the Sand Dunes Resort main building.

Convenience Store

Stop by our convenience store to pick up some souvenirs and all the extra supplies you may have forgotten when packing for your vacation. The convenience store is located on the first floor of the Sand Dunes Resort main building. Seasonal hours of operation.

Exercise Room

Keep up with your fitness routine during your stay in our on-site exercise room.

Free Wi-Fi

Free Wi-Fi is offered throughout the resort and in its guest rooms, dining facilities, pool areas and lobby for travelers to keep in touch while on the go.

Free Parking

Free parking at the resort’s numerous parking garages and convenient parking lots is available so our guests can park up to two vehicles free during their stay. Motorcycles are not permitted at Sand Dunes Resort.
