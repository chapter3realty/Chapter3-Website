Making PGA TOUR History in "The Golf Capital of the World."

The Myrtle Beach Classic, a PGA TOUR event sponsored by Visit Myrtle Beach, will return May 13-16, 2027. The Myrtle Beach Classic is the first PGA TOUR event in Myrtle Beach history, and was the first major professional golf tournament to the Grand Strand in 24 years. Myrtle Beach has long been known as the “Golf Capital of the World”. The Myrtle Beach Classic solidifies this claim, combining the world-renowned accolades and success as American’s favorite traveling golf destination with the prestige of hosting the world’s top professional golfers.

Volunteer with a Cause

Volunteerism is a core principle of the PGA TOUR, vital to the success of the Myrtle Beach Classic, and directly impacts the dollars raised for charitable organizations in our community. To date, tournaments across all Tours have generated more than $3.64 billion. Proceeds from the Myrtle Beach Classic will be designated to benefit nonprofit organizations in the areas of STEM (science, technology, engineering, and math), diversity and inclusion, reskilling programs, and local Myrtle Beach nonprofits.
