Myrtle Beach Royale Palms Condominiums

Beachside Family Fun

The Royale Palms oceanfront tower, located adjacent to Hilton Myrtle Beach Resort and within the 157-acre Kingston Resorts.  Enjoy all of the amenities throughout the property from the Wet Whistle oceanfront bar at Hilton to Currents Seaside Entertainment and Black Drum restaurant at Embassy Suites along with 78Fitness and Spa33 at The Landing.

Suites & Accommodations

Royale Palms offers versatile condo options. Our 1-bedroom units feature a bedroom and bathroom, ideal for couples or solo travelers. Meanwhile, our 2 and 3-bedroom units are more spacious, including a living room, dining area, fully-equipped kitchen, and washer-dryer for added convenience. Whether for a cozy retreat or a family getaway, we have the perfect oceanfront condo for you!

Spacious Oceanfront Condominiums for Every Vacation

Whether you’re planning a family beach vacation, golf getaway, group trip, or an extended stay along the Grand Strand, Royale Palms Condominiums offers the space and flexibility to make yourself at home. Choose from one, two, three, and four-bedroom oceanfront condominiums, each thoughtfully designed with full kitchens, spacious living and dining areas, private balconies, and the comforts of home.

Wake up to ocean or resort views, prepare meals at your own pace, and unwind with plenty of room for everyone to relax after a day exploring Myrtle Beach. With more space than a traditional hotel room and direct access to the amenities of Kingston Resorts, Royale Palms combines the privacy of a vacation rental with the convenience of a full-service beachfront resort.

Close to the Best of Myrtle Beach

Royale Palms Condominiums places you within easy reach of many of Myrtle Beach’s most popular attractions while offering a peaceful beachfront retreat away from the busiest areas. Nearby attractions include:

  • Broadway at the Beach
  • Barefoot Landing
  • Tanger Outlets
  • The Myrtle Beach Boardwalk
  • Championship golf courses
  • Live entertainment theaters
  • Fresh seafood restaurants and waterfront dining
  • Family attractions, miniature golf, and amusement parks

Royale Palms provides the ideal base for exploring the Grand Strand before returning to the comfort of your spacious oceanfront condominium.

Experience Kingston Resorts from Royale Palms

A stay at Royale Palms gives you more than spacious condominium accommodations, it provides access to everything Kingston Resorts has to offer.

Spend your days relaxing on the beach, swimming in resort pools, enjoying waterfront dining, or unwinding with a treatment at Spa33.

Stay active with the fitness center, walking trails, pickleball courts, nearby championship golf courses, and seasonal family activities designed for guests of all ages.

Whether you prefer laid-back days by the ocean or filling your itinerary with recreation and entertainment, Royale Palms makes it easy to experience the best of a Myrtle Beach resort while enjoying the comfort, privacy, and flexibility of your own condominium.
