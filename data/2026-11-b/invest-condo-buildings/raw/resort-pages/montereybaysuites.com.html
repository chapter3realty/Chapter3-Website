Monterey Bay Suites

Myrtle Beach’s Premier Oceanfront Hotel

Welcome to Monterey Bay Suites, a spacious oceanfront hotel right on Myrtle Beach, offering guests comfort and the best ways to enjoy the Grand Strand. Located on the north end of Myrtle Beach, Monterey Bay is just a stone’s throw away from one of the most picturesque stretches of sand on the East Coast. Wake up to sweeping Atlantic views from the comfort of your condo-style suite, complete with a private balcony and an in-suite kitchen.

Start each morning with a complimentary hot breakfast, then spend the day between the beach, indoor and outdoor pools, the lazy river, hot tubs, and our rooftop pool deck. Dry off with a friendly game of cornhole or ping pong, then refuel at the Surf Shack Bar & Grill.

In addition to the fun games and kiddie pool, Monterey Bay Suites’ location also makes it a good choice for families, thanks to nearby restaurants, shopping centers, and kid-friendly attractions.

Discover All The Fun at Monterey Bay Suites

At Monterey Bay Suites, your vacation comes with more than just an oceanfront view. As one of the top Myrtle Beach hotels with suites, Monterey Bay offers the spacious accommodations and resort-style amenities that keep guests coming back year after year. Our amenities help you make the most of every beach day, from water features to convenient dining options and relaxing spaces to unwind.

Guests start the day with a complimentary hot breakfast before heading out to explore the Grand Strand or spending the afternoon poolside. When hunger strikes, stop by The Surf Shack Oceanfront Snack & Smoothie Bar for casual bites and refreshing drinks, or walk a few blocks north to Starbucks at Grande Cayman Resort.. Families, couples, and groups alike appreciate the laid-back atmosphere and convenient amenities throughout the property.
