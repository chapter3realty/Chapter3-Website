Welcome To

Compass Cove Oceanfront Resort

We’ve been busy since your last visit! Our Our $25+ million renovation is complete and we can’t wait to welcome you back to an elevated level of luxury, style, comfort, and relaxation.

Conveniently located near the heart of Myrtle Beach, Compass Cove has been a family favorite for generations. The resort offers more than 530 rooms, efficiencies, suites and one- to three-bedroom condos across three buildings. Guests can also enjoy 23 water features, including six indoor and outdoor pools, three lazy rivers, five kiddie pools, two waterslides and seven hot tubs At Compass Cove Resort, you will find a variety of suites and amenities that range from cozy and comfortable to fun-filled and exciting.
