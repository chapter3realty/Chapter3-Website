Sand Dunes Resort – Your Ultimate Oceanfront Getaway in Myrtle Beach

Get ready for an unforgettable beach escape at Sand Dunes Resort, one of the top oceanfront resorts in Myrtle Beach. From spacious accommodations to exciting on-site amenities, we offer everything you need for a fun-filled family vacation or a relaxing coastal retreat.

Located in the upscale Dunes neighborhood of Myrtle Beach, our resort is a favorite among families, couples, and groups looking to enjoy both relaxation and adventure by the sea.

Comfortable Oceanfront Accommodations for Every Group

Sand Dunes Resort features a wide variety of Myrtle Beach vacation rentals, including fully furnished and tastefully decorated one-, two-, and three-bedroom condos, suites, and villas. With eight unique buildings—each offering different layouts—you’ll find the perfect space to fit your style and group size.

Wake up to breathtaking ocean views from your private balcony, and enjoy the convenience of home-away-from-home amenities during your stay.

On-Site Water Park, Family Fun & Relaxation

Whether you’re traveling with kids or just young at heart, Sand Dunes Resort offers nonstop entertainment right on property:

  • Sands Water Park – a splash-filled experience with slides, fountains, and fun for all ages
  • Access to North Shore Family FunZone with arcade games and entertainment
  • Neptune Blacklight Indoor Mini Golf – a glowing adventure the whole family will enjoy
  • A large oceanfront lazy river, beachfront pools, sundecks, and lawn areas for total relaxation

It’s the ideal place to stay active or simply unwind under the Myrtle Beach sun.

Oceanfront Dining & Refreshing Drinks with a View

After a day in the sun, indulge in delicious food and drink at our oceanfront dining venues. Whether you’re craving casual bites, fresh seafood, or tropical beverages, our panoramic waterfront bar and resort restaurants have something for every taste.

Sip on wine, craft beers, or frozen cocktails as you enjoy a sunset view over the Atlantic—no shoes required.

Endless Activities Along the Myrtle Beach Grand Strand

No matter the season, Myrtle Beach offers year-round fun for all ages. From golf and live entertainment to shopping, dining, and thrilling attractions, the Grand Strand is packed with things to do.

Located minutes from top destinations like Broadway at the Beach, Carolina Opry, and Barefoot Landing, Sand Dunes Resort places you in the heart of it all—while still providing a peaceful coastal escape.

Book your stay today at Sand Dunes Resort and discover why families return year after year. With our unbeatable location, exciting amenities, and spacious oceanfront accommodations, this is where the best Myrtle Beach vacations begin.
