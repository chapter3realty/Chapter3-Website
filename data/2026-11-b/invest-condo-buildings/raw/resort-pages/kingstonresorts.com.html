Oceanfront Resorts & Hotels in Myrtle Beach

Discover the pristine property of Kingston Resorts, where oceanfront living is a lifestyle. With more than a half-mile of beach, spectacular waterfront views and sun-soaked activities are never more than a few steps away and now, both the Hilton and Embassy Suites are welcoming guests to fully updated rooms and suites plus a new pool and two waterparks. Come see why generations of families and friends keep coming back to our resort located on the Grand Strand of Myrtle Beach, South Carolina.

Discover an unparalleled pool experience. Guests staying at Embassy Suites and Hilton Myrtle Beach Resorts enjoy exclusive access to Currents Seaside Entertainment and Hilton’s Sand Bottom Pool.
