Resort highlights

Guests love Bay Watch Resort for its on-the-water location and variety of amenities that make every stay easy and breezy:

  • Direct beach access to North Myrtle Beach’s quiet shoreline
  • Indoor and outdoor pools
  • Lazy rivers, hot tubs, and kiddie pools
  • On-site dining and bars
  • Fitness center and gift shop
  • Event space with ocean views

Dine without leaving the resort

Start your morning at the Bay Watch Café with big breakfasts, beach views, and Southern staples. Spend the afternoon poolside with tropical drinks from Fishtails Beach Bar, then spend the evening at Sandtrap Sports Bar & Grill for pub fare and game-time fun—all without straying far from your rental.

Event-friendly and group ready

Bay Watch Resort is a top choice for gatherings of all kinds. With over 6,500 square feet of flexible oceanfront event space, it’s easy to host:

  • Beach weddings and receptions
  • Family reunions and group trips
  • Corporate retreats and meetings

Guests can stay on-site and take full advantage of everything the resort offers, from the beach to built-in dining and recreation.

Your coastal retreat starts here

Whether you’re gathered with friends or looking to relax with loved ones, Bay Watch Resort puts all the best of North Myrtle Beach at your fingertips. From sunrises over ocean waves to peaceful afternoon floats in the lazy river, your worry-free beach vacation is ready to begin.
