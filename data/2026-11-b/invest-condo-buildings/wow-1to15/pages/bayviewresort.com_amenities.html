Amenities at Bay View Resort

Myrtle Beach is unique for its wide array of diverse resorts, giving any visitor to the Grand Strand the luxury of picking the perfect hotel for their vacation. At Bay View Resort, you will find a variety of suites and amenities that range from the cozy and comfortable to the fun-filled and exciting. Whether you’re looking for a relaxing home away from home or a thrilling holiday packed with entertainment, we have you covered.

Water Adventures

Bay View Resort’s oceanfront poolscape has something for the whole family. Enjoy an indoor heated pool, indoor heated lazy river, indoor heated kiddie pool and indoor heated hot tub, along with an outdoor pool and spa.

  • Indoor Swimming Pool
  • Indoor Lazy River
  • Kiddie Pool
  • Outdoor Pool & Spa
  • Relaxing Hot Tub

Onsite Starbucks™

Grab your favorite cup and stroll along the Myrtle Beach Boardwalk or relax on our outdoor patio enjoying the free wifi and soothing swell of the ocean. Luxury can be as simple as the daily coffee of your choice. Starbucks provides one of the most attractive amenities for Bay View Resort guests and is located on the ground floor of the tower. We make sure your vacation can include your favorite morning ritual.
  • Starbucks Coffee
  • Seasonal Flavors
  • Sit and Enjoy

Bay View Resort Extras

At Bay View Resort you’ll find much more than beautiful beaches and a variety of swimming pools. We have an extensive list of amenities that make it difficult to pack up and leave when it is time to check out. Our towers feature engaging activities for the entire family from a gorgeous lawn to giant chess and checkers games, there is not a minute to be spared from relaxation and entertainment.

  • Boozy Buoy Oceanfront Pool Bar: View Menu »
  • Oceanfront Tanning Lawn
  • Giant Chess & Checkers
  • Fire Pit
  • Shuffleboard
  • Fitness Room

Oceanfront Boardwalk

Bay View Resort is nestled directly in front of the 1.2 miles long Myrtle Beach Oceanfront Boardwalk, a lengthy stretch that provides pristine views of the entire beach line and direct access to numerous shops and restaurants as well as the Myrtle Beach SkyWheel. Along this curving path, guests can enjoy oceanfront views, a refreshing sea breeze and some of the greatest dining and entertainment the beach has to offer.
  • Excitement on the Boardwalk
  • Take a Stroll on the Boardwalk
  • Breathtaking Views
  • Direct Beach Access
