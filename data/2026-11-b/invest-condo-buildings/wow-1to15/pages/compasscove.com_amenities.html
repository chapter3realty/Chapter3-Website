Amenities at Compass Cove Resort

Myrtle Beach is unique for its wide array of diverse resorts, giving any visitor to the Grand Strand the luxury of picking the perfect hotel for their vacation. At Compass Cove Resort, you will find a variety of suites and amenities that range from the cozy and comfortable to the fun-filled and exciting. Whether you’re looking for a relaxing home away from home or a thrilling holiday packed with entertainment, we have you covered.

Fun & Games

Looking for non-stop fun? We’ve got you covered at Compass Cove! Take a break from our thrill-packed water amenities for a laid-back game of shuffleboard, giant checkers or chess. Try your luck in our game room featuring classics like pinball and Pac-Man!

  • Giant Chess and Checkers
  • Shuffleboard

Extra Amenities

Compass Cove also offers a gift shop, fitness center, laundry facilities, fire pits, outdoor lounge areas and grills for added convenience during your stay.
