Landmark Resort

Amazing Amenities

With a variety of year-round and seasonal amenities, Landmark Resort is the perfect place for a family vacation, couple’s retreat, or group meeting any time of the year.

A highlight is our H2OASIS Waterpark! This seasonal amenity features 40-foot tall slides and more.

Water amenities are plentiful at Landmark Resort, and our 17 water features include a giant indoor poolscape with fountains, children’s play areas, and relaxing hot tubs. More fun awaits you oceanfront as the expansive pool deck features a large pool, whirlpool, and a seasonal 300-foot lazy river ride.

H2OASIS seasonal outdoor water park is the place to be for splashing, lounging, and relaxing. Kids and adults alike will love Myrtle Beach’s newest waterpark, featuring three giant slides, a kids’ slide, and a kids’ water play area.

Guests can enjoy the many dining options available at the Landmark Resort. From exceptional dishes to remarkable cocktails, let us prepare your senses for a culinary experience like no other! Sit down to a delicious breakfast at our Gazebo Restaurant or grab a quick bite at our bar and grill. You never have to leave the property to find what you need, as this Myrtle Beach resort also has an exercise room, arcade, and coffee stand serving Starbucks, all on site.
