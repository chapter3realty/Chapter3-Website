Amenities at Grande Cayman Resort

The Grande Cayman Resort is a premier oceanfront property with 720 feet of beautiful beachfront on the quieter north end of Myrtle Beach, SC. Aside from the tranquil beaches, Grande Cayman Resort offers guests a host of on-site amenities from spacious pool decks, delicious food and beverage outlets, and so much more. Outdoor Water Amenities

Whether you are basking in the warm summer sun or enjoying a beautiful Carolina moon dip in our pool, Grande Cayman Resort will present you with a refreshing experience like none other.
  • Oceanfront Pool Deck
  • Hot Tubs & Whirlpool
  • Savoy Private Pool
  • Sun Lounges
  • Silly Sub Waterpark
  • 3 Story Water Slide - Closed for the Winter season as of November 1st.

Please note that the fall hours of operation for the Water Slide will be Monday to Thursday, from 11 AM to 5 PM and Friday to Sunday, from 12 PM to 7PM.

Indoor Water Amenities

Grande Cayman Resort presents an indoor/outdoor complex with a glass front that is removed in warmer seasons and allows you to escape the sun while still enjoying an energizing dip. In cooler seasons the enclosure provides plenty of space to relax in a warm environment while enjoying our water amenities year-round. Enclosure dates vary and are dependent on seasonal weather forecasts.
  • Indoor/Outdoor Pool Deck
  • Kiddie Pool
  • Lazy River

On-Site Restaurants

Games & Extras

We know the pools tend to be the crowd’s favorite, but we offer an array of additional amenities that help make your vacation at Grande Cayman Resort a delightful experience. Like why not form familial teams and enjoy a little friendly competition on your vacation.
  • Shuffleboard
  • Giant Chess & Checkers
  • Ping Pong
  • Fitness Center
  • Gift Shop

Please note that the fall hours of operation for the Gift Shop will be Monday to Thursday, from 9 AM to 8 PM and Friday to Sunday, from 9 AM to 7 PM.
