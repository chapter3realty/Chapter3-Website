Roxanne Towers

  1. Home
  2. Myrtle Beach
  3. Roxanne Towers

Former guests give Roxanne Towers five stars! This oceanfront Myrtle Beach condo is just steps away from the ocean and a short drive from Coastal Grand Mall, Family Kingdom Amusement Park, Broadway at the Beach, Myrtle Beach International Airport, and all the other entertainment, dining, and attractions Myrtle Beach has to offer. Take a relaxing walk along the beach or swim in the oceanfront pools and lazy river. There's even an indoor heated pool during the winter and an outdoor hot tub. Every suite has a fully equipped kitchen, dishwasher, washer/dryer, and oceanfront balcony! Have your Myrtle Beach vacation in this affordable condo with all the best amenities!

 Afordable rental rates.  If you are looking to save a little money on your next vacation, then you will love Roxanne Towers! A great option for anyone on a budget,  you can enjoy all that the beach has to offer, in a great location without breaking the bank.    

 Indoor heated pool.  You wont have to worry about weather or the temparature to have a little splish-splashing fun at Roxanne Towers!  This building features an indoor heated pool, so you can take a swim or just relax poolside, any time of year, rain or shine.  

 Great views of the beach.  At Roxanne Towers, each unit has a private oceanfront balcony.  Wake up and watch the sunrise over the Atlantic, or unwind in the evening on your balcony, no matter what time of day, you will love spending time on your own balcony and taking in the beautiful views of the beach! 

 In the heart of Myrtle Beach.  Located right in the middle of it all, you wont have to worry about tricky directions or long car rides to enjoy some of the area attractions!  Just a short trip to the Coastal Grand Mall for shopping, or a little stroll down the beach to the boardwalk, where you can take a ride on the SkyWheel!
