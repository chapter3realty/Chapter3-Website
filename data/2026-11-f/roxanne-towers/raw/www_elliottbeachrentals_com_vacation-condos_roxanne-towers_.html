General Information

Located just ten minutes from the airport, the Roxanne Towers will be your comfortable home away from home for your dream vacation in North Myrtle Beach, South Carolina. Your vacation rental at Roxanne Towers offers a fully equipped kitchen with dishwasher, comfortable furnishings, cable TV, and Wi Fi connection. Live the life of leisure with a washer and dryer for your convenience.

Your private balcony will quickly become your favorite spot in your condo. Spend your days lounging by the indoor and outdoor pool, or head to the beach. Your North Myrtle Beach rental is just minutes from Broadway at the Beach and the Myrtle Beach Convention Center. You are also within walking distance of the 14th Avenue Pier and Chapin Park.

If entertainment is high on the list for your vacation, there are concerts, theatres, movies, comedy clubs, festivals and more. The shop till you drop crowd will find your paradise at the amazing malls, factory outlets and unique stores and boutiques. The golfer in your group will love the championship courses. If you are looking for an adrenaline rush, try a zip line and ropes course, parasailing, SCUBA diving or a charter fishing trip. You will be close to all Myrtle Beach has to offer when you vacation at Roxanne Towers.
