Atalaya Towers

6 - 9 Guests 3 Bedrooms 2 Bathrooms
Description Amenities Available Rentals
  • Address:912 N Waccamaw Drive

Atalaya Towers is an oceanfront complex comprised of three-bedroom, two-bath units located 0.5 miles north of the Garden City Pier. Standard unit features include a fully-equipped kitchen, central heat and air conditioning, private balcony, and ocean views. Other interior features and amenities vary per unit. Complex amenities include an enclosed swimming pool, hot tub, outdoor swimming pool, and elevators. Limit two parking passes per unit. One car-sized trailer is permitted if parked across the street from the complex and will be counted as one of the two parking passes. Ask for an Internet password when picking up pool and parking passes. No golf carts. No smoking. No pets. Check-out maid service included.
