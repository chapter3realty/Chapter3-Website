Atalaya Towers - Oceanfront Condos for Sale

If you want a seaside location to vacation in the answer may be to invest in a condo at Atalaya Towers. Located along the beach in Garden City Beach, you will find units on the market offering views up and down the beach. You can take your choice among three bedroom, two bath units in this 17 story condominium tower. This complex gives you the amenities you should expect from a beachside condominium development. You also get access to the beautiful beaches of Garden City.

Atalaya Towers Amenities

If you want the ocean side amenities, you get everything you need when you own at Atalaya Towers. This resort offers an outdoor swimming pool along the ocean. For the cooler months you can dip into the indoor pool or take advantage of the hot tub. A sundeck is the perfect place to get a tan. With stunning views from every balcony you will not want to leave your vacation retreat. The Towers are on North Waccamaw Drive in the heart of Garden City Beach. You have access to everything you need within walking distance or a short drive.

Atalaya Towers offers a great investment if you want a unit to rent out and use for vacations.  For anyone looking for a vacation retreat, the Towers are a great place to begin. Some other close by oceanfront condo towers in the Garden City area are Waters Edge and Royal Garden.
