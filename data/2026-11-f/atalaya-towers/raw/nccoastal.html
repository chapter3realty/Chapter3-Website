Description

Wake up to breathtaking ocean views from this 3-bedroom, 2-bath oceanfront condo in the highly sought-after Atalaya Towers of Garden City Beach. Unit 1004 offers a spacious and inviting layout, perfect for a primary residence, vacation getaway, or investment property. Enjoy the sights and sounds of the Atlantic from your private oceanfront balcony, accessible from the living area, creating the ideal space for morning coffee or evening sunsets. The open living and dining area is filled with natural light, while the well-appointed kitchen provides ample cabinet and counter space for entertaining family and friends. The primary suite features ocean views and a private bath, with two additional bedrooms offering comfortable accommodations for guests. Atalaya Towers is a well-maintained oceanfront community with amenities that include indoor and outdoor pools, a hot tub, sun deck, and direct beach access. Conveniently located near dining, shopping, golf, and the Garden City Pier, this condo delivers the perfect blend of relaxation and coastal convenience. Don’t miss the opportunity to own your piece of oceanfront paradise.
