Palms Resort On-Site Rental Management
Palms Resort on-site rental management company protects your investment 365 days a year. You can rest easy when you are in the hands of Palms Resort, the trusted on-site rental management company with over 40 years of service.
We look forward to welcoming you into our rental family here at Palms Resort! Please 
contact Miki McGreevy
, our dedicated Rental Program Specialist for further information.
Looking to make a new reservation or have questions about our resort? Contact our front desk team at (800) 300-1198

By joining Palms Resort’s on-site rental management company you will become part of the Vacation Myrtle Beach group, one of the largest resort groups in the Myrtle Beach area. Vacation Myrtle Beach offers over 40 years of rental management experience that is leveraged on behalf of our owners to successfully operate in a competitive market. With our vast experience in the rental management field and our strength in the Myrtle Beach market, we recognize both the excitement and challenge for an individual who owns a vacation property. We are proud to offer a wide variety of services to each and every owner on our rental management program.
