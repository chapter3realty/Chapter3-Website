Resort Amenities
Palms Resort has all you need for the perfect oceanfront Myrtle Beach vacation. Work out in the fitness room, head out to the beach for an afternoon in the sun, or relax on the oceanfront lawn with a good book. Find out more below.
Prev
Next
Beach Access
When coming to Myrtle Beach, we know that mostly everyone is here to enjoy the beaches. That is why we make accessing the beach easier with our private beach access for our guests. This access is just a short walk through out of our resort and through our oceanfront lawn area
Prev
Next
Oceanfront Lawn
Take in the sun and relax in one of our many beach chairs located on our Oceanfront Lawn. Right by our pool deck, enjoy the ocean view and take in the beautiful ocean scenery as you kick back and relax.
Prev
Next
Fitness Room
If you’re looking to get in a nice workout before hitting the beach, our fitness room is just for you. With state of the art equipment to help you stay in shape, our fitness room provides you with everything you need to get your workout for the day done.
Prev
Next
Free Wireless Internet
No one wants to lose service when on vacation. That is why Palms Resort provides guests with free Wireless Internet while they stay there. Whether you need to check your email, post a picture of your trip, or call a friend; just jump on the Wi-Fi and enjoy your vacation.
Prev
Next
Guest Laundry Facilities
Looking to get a quick load of laundry washed before you head back home from vacation? Our South Tower offers a coin-operated guest laundry equipped with washer and dryers perfect for cleaning up some clothes.
Prev
Next
Keyless Entry
If you pre-register with us before you arrive there is no need to worry about grabbing a room key when you get here. We will provide you with a specific key-code for your room so you can skip the lobby when you first arrive and can get right into vacation mode.
Prev
Next
Additional Amenities
Palms Resort offers additional amenities to ensure that your vacation goes as smoothly as possible. Grab a snack at one of our conveniently located vending machines, make local and 800 calls, and enjoy the luxury of our free parking garage!
