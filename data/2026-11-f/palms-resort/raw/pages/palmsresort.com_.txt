The Palms Resort, located on the wide sandy beachfront of Myrtle Beach, South Carolina, offers guests an oceanfront experience like no other. Our accommodations include one-bedroom suites and three-bedroom condos. You can also enjoy several of our amenities such as indoor and outdoor pools, indoor and outdoor whirlpools, an exercise room, and much, much more. From the moment you arrive, you and your family will be inspired by the views, relaxed by the service, refreshed by our convenient amenities, and enlivened with all the entertainment and excitement of Myrtle Beach, South Carolina: One of America's Favorite Family Vacation Destinations. Book your fun-filled Myrtle Beach oceanfront vacation today at Palms Resort.
