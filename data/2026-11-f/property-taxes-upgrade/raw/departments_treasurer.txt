Treasurer
Intro
The Treasurer’s Office is responsible for collecting all real and personal property taxes, including taxes on vehicles and front-foot assessments. The office also manages and invests funds on behalf of the County, School District, Airport, and Solid Waste Authority. In accordance with state law, the Treasurer’s Office conducts an Annual Public Tax Sale for Delinquent properties. Additionally, the office oversees the collection of Hospitality Tax and Business License fees.

A Message from the Horry County Treasurer
Thank you for allowing me the opportunity to serve as your Horry County Treasurer.  As your Treasurer, customer service, oversight, and fiscal management are among my top priorities. We have assembled a devoted team of professionals, who will work diligently to increase communication with the people of Horry County, while also enhancing accessibility and responsiveness at your Treasurer's Office. 
If we can ever be of assistance, please do not hesitate to contact us. We are here for you! We look forward to serving the citizens of Horry County and making your Treasurer's Office work better for you.
Angie Jones
