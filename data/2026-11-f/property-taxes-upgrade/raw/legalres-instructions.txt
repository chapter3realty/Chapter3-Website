HOW TO REDACT YOUR TAX RETURNS:
Redact the return as shown (SC and other state returns have similar information to the 1040)
using a heavy marker or pen to cover up:
If a line is blank – leave it blank.
If a line is ﬁlled in with a zero –
leave the zero. If a line contains
an N/A – leave the N/A.
The income amounts (dollar ﬁgures) are typically not needed for this process but it is necessary
during the approval process for the Assessor’s Oﬃce to know if some lines contained data.
Therefore, do not cover up the lines with plain paper and do not fold the form when copying to
hide the income lines.

If you have any questions about redacting or about what parts of the returns are needed, call
the Assessor’s Oﬃce at 843-915-5040. Customer service representatives will be happy to assist
you and answer your questions.
