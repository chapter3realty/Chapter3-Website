Amenities

Certainly our beautiful and spacious vacation homes do not in themselves make for the ideal resort experience. Our large oceanfront pool with adjacent wading pool, large sun deck, exercise room and sauna, coupled with all the entertainment options that the Grand Strand has to offer - it all adds up to perfect relaxation no matter what pace you set. If necessities dictate that you mix business with pleasure, we are pleased to offer our Regency Room meeting facility - the perfect spot for larger gatherings. But allow for distractions - the view of the ocean and pool deck from the Regency Room is outstanding!
