MYRTLE BEACH S.C. (WPDE) — 
Margaritaville Vacation Residences Myrtle Beach marked the start of its newest Myrtle Beach development with a groundbreaking launch party held yesterday for a new coastal condominium community.
The event featured a band, food and margaritas as the company celebrated plans for 271 resort-style units in Arcadian Shores. Officials said it is the area’s first new coastal condominium in more than a decade, and 25% of the community has already been sold.
“When the construction is done, that's just the beginning for Margaritaville. that's when the brand, the fun, the escapism. that's what people are buying. so, there'll be pools and they'll be, restaurants and there'll be, barbecues and they'll be. and everything will be themed Margaritaville," said Stuart Schultz, vice president of Margaritaville.
Construction is set to start tomorrow, and officials said the project will take two years to complete.
