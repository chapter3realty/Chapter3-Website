Why Margaritaville Vacation Residences Myrtle Beach?
1. Prime Coastal 
Location
Margaritaville Vacation Residences Myrtle Beach is located in South Carolina just steps from the Atlantic Ocean, in the heart of one of the most popular beach destinations on the East Coast. Enjoy easy access to Myrtle Beach International Airport, top-rated golf courses, dining, shopping, and entertainment options. Whether you’re flying in for the weekend or driving in for an extended stay, everything you need is minutes away. You’re not just close to the beach. You’re close to everything that makes the Grand Strand a top destination. It’s the perfect blend of convenience and coastal charm.
2. Margaritaville Brand’s Excellent Reputation
Owning at Margaritaville Vacation Residences Myrtle Beach means tapping into the globally recognized Margaritaville lifestyle. Known for its carefree, laid-back vibe and signature “no worries” attitude, Margaritaville brings instant familiarity and emotional connection to your ownership experience. This isn’t just a place to stay. It’s a state of mind. From the design to the service, every detail reflects the spirit of escape, relaxation in paradise. It’s a brand people trust, love, and return to year after year.
3.  Exceptional Amenities at Your Fingertips
Margaritaville Vacation Residences Myrtle Beach offers a full suite of amenities designed to elevate your stay, whether it’s for a weekend or the whole season. Enjoy the sparkling pool
, 
tiki hut with food and drinks, a poolside bar and grill perfect for socializing. The lobby restaurant and bar, close by the beach, and curated guest services make every visit effortless. You’ll also have access to a fitness facility, a game room, and more. Everything you need for a first-class, coastal lifestyle is right on-site or close by.
4.  Flexible Rental Program
Margaritaville Vacation Residences Myrtle Beach homeowners can take advantage of the optional all-inclusive on-site rental program. This is a true lock-and-leave program. Enjoy personal use when you want and share your home when you’re not here. The brand strength of Margaritaville enhances our community image and marketing efforts. Additional optional rental programs are available.
5.  Beautifully 
Designed 
Floorplans
Choose from a collection of 1-, 2-, and 3-bedroom fully furnished residences that blend luxury with functionality. Every residence is thoughtfully designed with open living spaces, coastal finishes and private balconies. Floorplans are ideal for entertaining, relaxing, or just soaking up the coastal vibe. Premium furnishings, modern appliances, and turnkey readiness mean you’re ready to enjoy from day one. It’s effortless elegance, wrapped in tropical style.
6.  First New For-Sale condominium Complex This Close to the ocean in over a decade
This is a rare opportunity to select new construction just 0.3 miles from the beach. In a market with very little new inventory, Margaritaville Vacation Residences Myrtle Beach stands out as a one-of-a-kind opportunity that is fresh, stylish, and move-in ready. It combines the best of new quality development with a prime location.
7. Vacation Residences Made Easy
From the day you close, your home is fully furnished and ready to enjoy, or to rent. No furniture shopping, no setup hassles, and no design decisions to stress over. Just show up, drop your bags, and enjoy the coastal life. The property is maintained and operated to be effortless from the start. The way vacation ownership should be.
8.  A Community That Brings People Together
This isn’t just a collection of condominiums. It’s a community built for fun, connection, and shared experiences. On-site events, happy hours, and the Margaritaville spirit create an atmosphere that welcomes everyone. Whether you’re lounging by the pool, sipping cocktails at the tiki hut, or joining neighbors for a night out, you’ll feel right at home. It’s easy to make memories and friendships here. That’s what makes it so special.
9.  Designed for Today’s Lifestyle
Our residences cater to how people live, work, and relax in today’s environment. Work remotely from your private balcony while sipping coffee, host friends in open-concept kitchens, or unplug completely by the pool. Wi-Fi, Smart TVs and in-unit laundry make everyday comfort a given. It’s modern living in a unique resort setting, designed for today’s flexibility and tomorrow’s adventures that only Margaritaville can do. Perfect for a second home, vacation home, full-time residence, or anything in between.
10. A Unique Opportunity. A Special Find
Opportunities like this are rare along the Grand Strand. With only 295 total residences in the entire community, availability is extremely limited compared to the demand for new, well-located coastal properties. Margaritaville Vacation Residences stand out for their thoughtfully crafted architecture and timeless design. It is a boutique offering linked to unique lifestyle brands tailored to an unparalleled location.
