About Ownership at Margaritaville Vacation Residences Myrtle Beach
Whether you’re looking for a vacation home, an investment property, or a place to enjoy the Margaritaville lifestyle, we’ve answered some of the most common questions about ownership below. If you don’t find what you’re looking for, we’re happy to schedule a 
Private Resort Overview
, where we’ll personally walk you through the community, available residences, pricing, ownership costs, resort amenities, construction progress, and answer your questions one-on-one.
																Where is Margaritaville Vacation Residences Myrtle Beach located, and how close is it to the beach?							
Margaritaville Vacation Residences Myrtle Beach is located in the highly sought-after Arcadian Shores section of Myrtle Beach, South Carolina. The community is situated just off Lake Arrowhead Road and is approximately a five-minute walk to the beach, with convenient access near the famous Ocean Annie’s Beach Bar.
Owners enjoy one of the most desirable locations along the Grand Strand, just minutes from Restaurant Row, Barefoot Landing, Tanger Outlets, championship golf courses, entertainment venues, grocery stores, and Myrtle Beach International Airport.
While close to everything Myrtle Beach has to offer, the community is tucked away in a quieter coastal setting that provides the perfect balance of relaxation and convenience. Whether you’re visiting for a weekend, several weeks, or making it your favorite vacation destination year after year, you’ll be just moments from the beach and some of the area’s best dining, shopping, and attractions.
																What is a Margaritaville Vacation Residence Myrtle Beach, and what makes it different from a traditional condominium?							
Margaritaville Vacation Residences Myrtle Beach offers fully furnished, deeded condominium residences designed specifically for vacation home ownership. Unlike a traditional residential condominium, these residences are part of a professionally managed resort community where owners can enjoy their residence for personal vacations while also having the opportunity to participate in a professionally managed vacation rental program when they’re not using it.
Each residence is thoughtfully designed with coastal-inspired interiors and comes fully furnished, making ownership simple and turnkey from day one.
Beyond the residence itself, ownership includes access to an impressive collection of resort amenities, including a resort-style pool, tiki bar, Palmetto Bar & Grill, lobby bar, fitness center, fire pits, and seasonal beach shuttle service. The community is designed to deliver a true resort experience rather than simply providing a place to stay.
Whether you’re looking for a vacation retreat, a second home, or a property with vacation rental potential, Margaritaville Vacation Residences Myrtle Beach offers a unique ownership opportunity that combines the benefits of condominium ownership with the relaxed lifestyle and amenities of a world-class resort.
																What floorplans are available, and how do I choose the one that's right for me?							
Margaritaville Vacation Residences Myrtle Beach offers a variety of thoughtfully designed one, two, and three-bedroom floor plans, including several unique layouts to accommodate different lifestyles and ownership goals.
Whether you’re looking for a cozy beach retreat for weekend getaways, a spacious residence for family vacations, or a floor plan with enhanced vacation rental flexibility, there’s an option designed to fit your needs.
Floor plans range from efficient one-bedroom residences to expansive three-bedroom layouts, including our highly sought-after lockout residences, which offer additional flexibility for personal use and vacation rentals.
Each residence is delivered fully furnished and features an open-concept design, private outdoor living space, modern kitchens, and finishes inspired by the relaxed Margaritaville lifestyle.
During your 
Private Resort Overview

																What resort amenities are included with ownership at Margaritaville Vacation Residences Myrtle Beach?							
Ownership at Margaritaville Vacation Residences Myrtle Beach includes access to a thoughtfully designed collection of resort-style amenities that allow owners and their guests to enjoy the relaxed coastal lifestyle synonymous with the Margaritaville brand.
At the heart of the community is a resort-style pool featuring a Baja shelf, hot tub, and children’s splash pad, surrounded by comfortable seating, private cabanas, and lush tropical landscaping.
Owners will also enjoy the 
Palmetto Bar & Grill

Unlike many condominium communities, Margaritaville Vacation Residences Myrtle Beach was designed from the ground up as a complete resort experience.
During your 
Private Resort Overview

																What does the HOA cover, and what are the monthly ownership costs?							
Every residence is part of a professionally managed homeowners association (HOA) designed to simplify ownership and protect the long-term value of the community.
Monthly HOA dues vary depending on the floor plan and residence size. HOA dues include exterior building maintenance, landscaping and irrigation, resort amenity maintenance, common area maintenance, building insurance, high-speed internet, cable television, trash and recycling service, exterior pest control, professional HOA management, and reserve funding for future capital improvements.
If you choose to participate in the professionally managed vacation rental program, rental management services are separate and will be discussed directly with the rental management company.
During your 
Private Resort Overview
, we’ll provide a detailed breakdown of current HOA dues, explain exactly what is included, discuss estimated ownership costs, and answer any questions you may have.
																Can I rent my residence when I'm not using it?							
Yes. Owners have the opportunity to participate in a professionally managed vacation rental program through 
Luxury Residential Resorts (LRR)
. Participation is completely optional.
LRR manages the day-to-day rental operation, including marketing, reservations, guest communications, check-in and check-out services, housekeeping coordination, maintenance coordination, and guest support, allowing owners to enjoy a hands-off ownership experience.
During your 
Private Resort Overview
, we’ll discuss ownership, available residences, pricing, ownership costs, resort amenities, and construction progress. If you’d like to learn more about the vacation rental program, we’re happy to arrange a phone call or an on-site meeting with a representative from Luxury Residential Resorts.
Please note that neither the developer, the sales team, nor the rental management company can guarantee rental income, occupancy, appreciation, positive cash flow, or investment performance. Those results will vary based on market conditions, owner usage, rental demand, operating expenses, and other factors.

																What is the process for purchasing a residence at Margaritaville Vacation Residences Myrtle Beach?							
Purchasing a residence at Margaritaville Vacation Residences Myrtle Beach is a straightforward process, and our team is here to guide you every step of the way.
The first step is scheduling a

Once you’ve selected your residence, we’ll prepare the Purchase and Sale Agreement and review the contract with you in detail. Our team will guide you through the required documentation, deposit process, and important milestones leading up to closing.

fully furnished

Whether you’re purchasing from across the country or just down the road, our experienced sales team is committed to making the buying process as smooth and enjoyable as possible from your first inquiry through closing day.
