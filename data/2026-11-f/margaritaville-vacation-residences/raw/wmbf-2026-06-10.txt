MYRTLE BEACH, SC (WMBF) - Developers are calling it the area’s first new coastal condominium offering in more than a decade, and it is coming under the Margaritaville name.
Developers broke ground Saturday on the 271-unit Margaritaville Vacation Residences Myrtle Beach, a resort-style residential community at 235 Fins Up Circle in Arcadian Shores.

(BoardroomPR)
Margaritaville is a global lifestyle brand inspired by the late Jimmy Buffett and features restaurants, a cruise line, and hotels.
According to a news release from BoardroomPR, the project received a $60 million construction loan from DLP Capital, a private real estate investment firm.
Art Falcone, the chief executive officer of AmeriCraft Homes, a homebuilding operation that is a co-developer of the project, said he is thrilled about it.
“We’re excited to work with this iconic brand to bring a one-of-a-kind lifestyle community to one of the country’s most beloved beachfront destinations,” Falcone said. “From the fully furnished residences to the hospitality-driven amenities, every aspect of the community has been thoughtfully curated to enhance the coastal living experience.”
According to BoardroomPR, prices start in the high $300,000s and the community is 25% sold so far. Units include fully furnished one-, two- and three-bedroom condominiums with island-inspired decor.
Amenities include a restaurant and lobby bar, a pool with private cabanas and a tiki bar, a fitness center, and a seasonal shuttle service to and from the beach. Owners who wish to rent their units will have access to an on-site rental management program.
Construction on buildings one and two and all resort amenities is expected to be completed by late 2027.
Patrick Marino, Chairman and CEO of M Group Companies, a multidisciplinary investment firm and the other co-developer of the project, said buyers are looking for more than just a home.
“At Margaritaville Vacation Residences Myrtle Beach, we’re delivering a turnkey piece of paradise, complete with onsite rental management services and a robust amenity program that makes every day feel like a vacation,” Marino said.
