Your Ultimate Travel Guide to Living and Playing in Paradise
Welcome to Myrtle Beach, South Carolina—a sun-soaked coastal gem that’s calling your name! Whether you’re dreaming of a relaxing beach getaway, an action-packed family vacation, or even a place to call home, Myrtle Beach has it all. With 60 miles of pristine shoreline, vibrant attractions, and mouthwatering dining options, it’s no wonder the area attracts more than 18 million visitors and generates nearly $13 billion in economic impact. Myrtle Beach isn’t just a place to visit—it’s a place to live, play, and make memories that last a lifetime. Let’s dive into the best things to do in Myrtle Beach and see why this coastal paradise should be your next stop!
Myrtle Beach Visitors Report
Soak Up the Sun and Fun on Myrtle Beach’s Iconic Shores
Picture this: soft, golden sand beneath your feet, the soothing crash of waves, and a salty breeze in the air. Myrtle Beach’s sprawling coastline, known as the Grand Strand, is the heart of this destination. Spend your days swimming, paddleboarding, or lounging with a good book under the sun. Thrill-seekers can rev up with jet skiing or soar above the waves while parasailing, taking in jaw-dropping ocean views. Traveling with kids? The shallow waters and gentle tides are perfect for sandcastle building and carefree splashing. It’s no surprise that over 70% of visitors come for the beach—it’s not just a spot on the map; it’s a lifestyle waiting for you to embrace.
Explore Must-Visit Attractions for All Ages
When it comes to 
things to do in Myrtle Beach
, the fun never stops. Kick things off at the 
Myrtle Beach Boardwalk and Promenade
, a 1.2-mile stretch buzzing with energy. Stroll along the oceanfront, snag a sweet treat from a classic candy shop, or hop on the 
SkyWheel
, a 187-foot Ferris wheel that lights up the night with panoramic coastal views. Families can’t miss 
Family Kingdom Amusement Park
, where roller coasters, carnival rides, and a splashy water park keep everyone smiling.
For a nature fix, head to 
Myrtle Beach State Park
—hike scenic trails, cast a line off the pier, or spot dolphins and sea turtles in their natural habitat. Craving culture? 
Brookgreen Gardens
 dazzles with over 2,000 sculptures set across 9,000 acres of lush greenery. And for a quirky twist, the 
Hollywood Wax Museum
 lets you pose with lifelike celebrity figures—your next Instagram post is calling! From thrills to chill, Myrtle Beach has something for everyone.
Savor the Flavors of Myrtle Beach Dining
Foodies, get ready—Myrtle Beach’s dining scene is a feast for the senses, with visitors spending over $2 billion at restaurants. Whether you’re into fresh seafood or Southern soul food, this coastal haven delivers. Start at 
The Claw House
, where massive lobster rolls and creamy clam chowder steal the show. For a taste of the South, 
Tupelo Honey
 serves up fried green tomatoes, shrimp and grits, and honey-dusted fried chicken that’ll have you licking your fingers.
Craving ocean vibes with your meal? 
Landry’s Seafood House
 on the Boardwalk pairs crab-stuffed flounder and key lime pie with stunning water views. For a laid-back bite, 
River City Café
 is your go-to for juicy burgers and crispy fries—ideal after a day at the beach. And don’t skip dessert! Swing by 
The Crazy Mason Milkshake Bar
 for over-the-top milkshakes that are as fun as they are delicious. With over 60% of visitors prioritizing dining, it’s clear: eating out is one of the tastiest 
things to do in Myrtle Beach
.
Unwind with Entertainment Galore
Myrtle Beach keeps the good times rolling, with entertainment spending increasing to over $340 million. Catch a show at 
The Carolina Opry
, where live music, dance, and comedy light up the stage. For a swashbuckling good time, 
Pirates Voyage Dinner & Show
 blends acrobatics, mermaids, and a pirate feast into one epic night. Golf lovers, you’re in paradise—Myrtle Beach boasts over 80 championship courses, earning its title as the “Golf Capital of the World.” Whatever your vibe, there’s no shortage of fun here.
Why Myrtle Beach is the Perfect Place to Live and Play
Beyond the attractions, Myrtle Beach offers a lifestyle that’s tough to top. With a welcoming community, affordable living, and a mild climate year-round, it’s no wonder people stay longer—or never leave. Imagine waking up to ocean sunrises, spending weekends at local gems like the 
Myrtle Beach Food Truck Festival
, or just kicking back in that laid-back coastal rhythm. With a diverse crowd, with visitors coming from Charlotte, Raleigh, Atlanta, New York, Virginia, Pennsylvania, Ohio and many other cities and towns, this thriving city blends small-town charm with big-time appeal.
