This location served us well for more than 20 years. However, the HOA effectively eliminated an on-site rental office on December 31, 2022.  The Surf Master by the Sea staff has moved to one of our other locations to continue to serve our guests and property owners.  Vacation Specialist, Jackie Alston, joined the rest of our Guest Services team at 608 Atlantic Avenue.  Housekeeping Manager, Maggie Hardee, relocated to our Property Services office at 2571 US Highway 17 South.  And Maintenance Technician, Dan DeFreest, joined the Maintenance team at 576 Cypress Drive.  YOUR SURF MASTER BY THE SEA TEAM jackie alston vacation specialist dan Defreest maintenance technician maggie hardee housekeeping supervisor A luxurious Surf Master by the Sea vacation awaits you in beautiful Garden City Beach, located on South Carolina's Grand Strand just south of Myrtle Beach. Choose an oceanfront one, two, or three-bedroom condo, as well as penthouse suites, all with private balconies and fully-equipped kitchens. Come enjoy a carefree vacation while we take care of all the details. Walk along miles of clean, sandy beaches. We invite you to visit us soon and see how special vacations at Surf Master By The Sea can be.
