      Club Wyndham Towers on the Grove
      2100 North Ocean Boulevard North Myrtle Beach, SC 29582
      (843) 249-7575

      The gem of the Grand Strand, Cherry Grove Beach is the number one beach in South Carolina — and it's where you'll find this modern resort. Here, you're just steps away from the gorgeous coastline. And after you explore the oceanfront, it's easy to float the day away on the lazy river.

      Play the day away with thrilling beachside activities and water sports like Jet Skiing. Watch the sunrise and enjoy a leisurely day of fishing at Cherry Grove Pier, or reserve a tee time at one of Myrtle Beach's premier golf courses. Learn some fun facts as you hunt shark teeth and seashells.

      • Helpful Hints
        • Arrivals and unloading located on the ground floor in breezeway. Right lane for unloading and left lane for through traffic. Lobby and registration desk located on the 3rd Floor/Lobby Level.
        • EV charging stations available on property; charging fees apply.
        • This resort offers alternative suite entry points to provide you the best views. As such you'll experience floor plans unique to this destination, including some suites that include a bedroom as the entry point.
        • Not all suites are oceanfront or oceanview.
        • Most of the one bedroom oceanfront suites have two full size beds.
        • Standard suites have a small galley-style kitchen and a dining room area that accommodates 3 to 4 people (varies per suite).
        • This resort has limited activities available.
        • This is a non-smoking resort; smoking is not permitted in the suites or balconies. Designated smoking areas are available on the ground level of the parking garage.
        • Trailers of any size are not allowed in the parking garage and must be parked off property. For local parking facilities, please reach out to the resort.
        • The Boulevard 2-bedrooms do not have a balcony.
        • A credit card is required for the $250 security deposit requested at check in.
        • This is a cashless resort. A credit or debit card is required for on-site purchases.
        • The water slide is seasonal (May – September).
        • Service animals that are trained to work or perform tasks for the benefit of an individual with a disability are welcome at all Club Wyndham Managed locations and Not Managed locations. All other animals, trained or untrained, whose sole function is to provide emotional support, therapy, comfort, or companionship do not qualify as service animals and are not permitted at Club Wyndham Managed or Not Managed locations.
        • Mobility ADA rooms need lower beds—no higher than 23"—which is lower than standard bedding.
