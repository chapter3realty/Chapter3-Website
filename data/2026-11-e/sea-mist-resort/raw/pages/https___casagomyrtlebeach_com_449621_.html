Space

Welcome to your Myrtle Beach getaway! Located on the 16th floor of the Driftwood Building, this ocean view condo comfortably accommodates up to 4 guests. Inside you’ll find 2 double beds, a dresser, heating and air conditioning, a mini kitchen, and a private bathroom with a tub/shower and vanity—making it a perfect stay for couples, small families, or a group of friends.

The kitchen comes fully stocked with basic cookware, utensils, dishes, and glassware—everything you need to prepare meals at home. Prefer to dine out? Myrtle Beach offers hundreds of fantastic restaurants just a short drive or quick Uber away. Delivery options like Uber Eats and DoorDash are also available.
Travel light—we’ve got you covered! All towels, toiletries, bedding, and linens are provided, along with starter amenities such as toilet paper, paper towels, soap, shampoo, conditioner, and lotion. Each bathroom is also stocked with towels and washcloths for your convenience.

While the Sea Mist Resort is more than 60 years old, we do our best to ensure every condo is cleaned, prepared, and ready for a comfortable stay. That said, please keep in mind the age of the resort and its amenities—our pricing reflects the décor, condition, and character of the complex. We always encourage guests to review details about the resort and the surrounding area so expectations are clear before arrival.

This property is privately owned and managed. We are not affiliated with the resort’s staff, housekeeping, or maintenance. Should you need assistance during your stay, our management team will be happy to help directly.

Additional Features: Wi-Fi and cable included Towels, toiletries, and linens provided Convenient location near dining, shopping, and attractionsPlease Note: Resort pools are currently unavailable for guest use, with no anticipated reopening date.
We’re confident you’ll enjoy this unique vacation rental and its unbeatable location. We look forward to hosting you!
