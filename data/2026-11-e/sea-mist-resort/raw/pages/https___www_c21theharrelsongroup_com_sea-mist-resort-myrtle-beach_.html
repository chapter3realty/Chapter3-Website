Sea Mist Resort Condos For Sale | Myrtle Beach

Sea Mist Oceanfront Resort
1200 South Ocean Blvd.
Myrtle Beach, SC 29577

For those looking for the absolutely lowest priced vacation condos in Myrtle Beach, Sea Mist Resort is the number one choice. Extremely popular with vacationers, these units are one of the best rental income properties around. What they lose in rental prices, they make up in rental quantity, especially in the summer months.

Sea Mist is actually a 15 acre resort, beginning on the oceanfront and going several blocks west towards Hwy 17. The oceanfront units are made of two newly renovated high-rises, and there are "rooms", efficiencies, and one bedroom units. Perfect for couples, singles and small families, the resort caters to kids and is PET FRIENDLY. The resort also boasts one of the largest water parks in Myrtle Beach, including a a 670 foot long, 7 foot wide lazy river.

These are very popular Myrtle Beach condos, and there are some fantastic bargains available at even a lower than normal price - around $75,000. If you're looking for inexpensive vacation fun, Sea Mist Resort is one to consider.

See the available Sea Mist Resort condos for sale below, and give us a call to tour some of them!
