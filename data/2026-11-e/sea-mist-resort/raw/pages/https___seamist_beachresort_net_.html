Overview & Features

1200 South Ocean Blvd, Myrtle Beach, 29577-4540,
-
Close to the beach

Rooms with ocean views

Sea Mist Oceanfront Resort offers rooms and suites with oceanfront, oceanview, or resort views. Options include efficiencies with kitchenettes, 1-4 bedroom suites, and a 4-bedroom house. All rooms have a small fridge and microwave, and some include full kitchens. Pet-friendly rooms are available for dogs and cats under 100 lbs. Private balconies overlook the Atlantic Ocean or resort grounds.

Dining and snacks

Onsite dining includes Pan American Pancake House for breakfast, Manta Steak & Seafood for Japanese cuisine, and Rita's Ice for desserts. A Quick Stop grocery store is available for essentials. The adjacent Three Steps Down Grille and Tavern serves dinner. Outdoor charcoal grills and picnic areas are available under century-old oak trees.

Water park and pools

The resort features Wahoo Water Park, exclusive to guests, with a 232-meter lazy river, 3 tube slides, and mini slides. There are 9 outdoor pools, 2 indoor pools, and 4 hot tubs. The indoor pool is open year-round. Beach volleyball equipment is available at the front desk with a driver's license deposit.

Nearby attractions

Sea Mist is 5 km from Myrtle Beach International Airport. Nearby attractions include Broadway at the Beach (4.5 km), Family Kingdom Amusement Park (1 km), and Ripley's Believe It or Not (2.5 km). Tanger Outlets are a 15-25 minute drive. The Myrtle Beach Boardwalk and Promenade is 2.5 km away.

Services and policies

The resort has an 18,000 sq ft conference center for events. Laundry facilities are available at Bubbles & Suds Laundromat. Parking is included for one vehicle per bedroom; additional parking costs $5. Check-in is at 4 PM, check-out at 10 AM. Pets are allowed with a leash requirement and waste cleanup policy.
