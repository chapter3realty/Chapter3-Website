Sea Mist Resort Myrtle Beach

Oceanfront efficiency condos in a long-running family resort, most priced under $100,000.

Sea Mist Resort is an oceanfront resort at 1207 South Ocean Boulevard, just south of downtown Myrtle Beach, near Coral Beach Resort. The resort has several buildings, such as the Driftwood building. MLS records date the condo units to the late 1970s.

The condos for sale at Sea Mist are efficiency units of about 375 to 450 square feet. Most have a full kitchen or kitchenette, two queen beds or a king bed with a sleeper sofa, and a balcony with an ocean view. A few larger units have a separate sitting and dining area.

Amenities include several outdoor pools, an indoor pool, a lazy river, a water park, a hot tub, mini golf, a fitness center and a game room.

Sea Mist Condos for Sale in Myrtle Beach

Thirty-one Sea Mist condos are listed right now, from about $47,000 to $99,000. Sixty-three sold in the last twelve months at an average of about $59,000, with the best units selling for about $75,000 to $80,000. About 124 have sold in the last three years.

Prices have fallen a long way. In 2023 and 2024 the best Sea Mist units sold for about $108,000 to $129,000. Today the HOA fee alone is more than $7,000 a year, which is a large share of the purchase price. Before you make an offer, ask about any recent or planned special assessments, the building’s insurance and the resort’s reserves, and run the numbers with the HOA fee included.

Sea Mist HOA

The HOA fee runs about $610 to $650 a month. For most units it covers building insurance, internet, water, sewer, trash and pest control, and many listings also include electricity. A few listings do not show the pools as included, so confirm what the fee covers for the unit you are considering.

Sea Mist units are used for short-term rentals, long-term rentals and personal beach getaways. If rental income matters to you, ask for the unit’s rental history before you make an offer.

Why Buyers Choose Sea Mist

Sea Mist is a very low-cost way to own an oceanfront condo in Myrtle Beach, with resort amenities and a location close to downtown. It is best for a buyer who has checked the HOA fee, the insurance and any assessments and is comfortable with the numbers.
