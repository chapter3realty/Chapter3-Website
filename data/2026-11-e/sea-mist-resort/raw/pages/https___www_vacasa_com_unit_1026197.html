Welcome to your laid-back retreat on the 8th floor of the Driftwood Building at Sea Mist Resort. This charming coastal studio is perfect for families or small groups looking to relax by the shore. Designed with comfort in mind, the space features two queen-size beds and comfortably accommodates up to four guests—ideal for unwinding after a sun-soaked day at the beach.

Inside, the convenient kitchenette offers a refrigerator, microwave, and coffee maker—just right for slow mornings and simple meals. The spacious bathroom provides a large vanity, shower/tub combo, and fresh towels, linens, and starter toiletries so you can settle in and unwind.
The main living and sleeping area is calm and comfortable, featuring plush queen beds, a dresser, and a cozy dining table for two. Step onto your private balcony and enjoy soothing Atlantic Ocean views, a perfect place to relax with ocean air after a day at the beach.
While Sea Mist Resort is a classic, 65-year-old oceanfront property, this privately owned unit has been thoughtfully cared for to create a comfortable and relaxing retreat beyond a standard hotel stay. We are not affiliated with resort staff, so please contact us directly with any questions or needs during your stay.
Please note the mini waterpark across the street and movie theater are not available to guests.
**Smoking and vaping are not allowed. All pools are currently unavailable for guest use with no anticipated reopening date.**

You must be 18 years or older to rent this property.
