Mar Vista Grande

Oceanfront three-bedroom residences in the Ocean Drive section, a short walk from Main Street.

Mar Vista Grande is an oceanfront condo tower at 603 South Ocean Boulevard in the Ocean Drive section of North Myrtle Beach, steps from Main Street. Built in 2006, it is newer than most of the oceanfront buildings on this stretch of beach. Nearly every unit is a three-bedroom, three-bath residence of about 1,465 to 1,550 square feet, with a few four-bedroom units of about 1,625 square feet, and many have balconies off both the living room and the primary suite.

Mar Vista Grande is one of two buildings that carry the Luxury tag on the North Myrtle Beach Oceanfront Condos page. Units start around $750,000, well above most of the oceanfront towers in Ocean Drive.

Mar Vista Grande Condos for Sale in North Myrtle Beach

There are usually about half a dozen Mar Vista Grande condos for sale, and right now they list from about $750,000 to $1 million. Seven sold in the last twelve months, from about $660,000 to $895,000, at an average of about $807,000, or roughly $530 a square foot. Direct oceanfront units on the upper floors sell at the top of that range. Units on the south side with an angled ocean view, and those needing updates, sell toward the bottom.

Most units are sold fully furnished, and many come with their rental history and existing vacation reservations. Floor, view and the level of renovation drive the price more than size, since nearly every unit shares the same three-bedroom plan.

Mar Vista Grande Amenities

Lazy River & Pools

Indoor and outdoor pools, indoor and outdoor hot tubs, and a lazy river.

Owners’ Lounge

A private owners-only lounge and a fitness center.

Secure Parking

Gated, secured building with four levels of private parking and owner storage.

The building has interior corridors, elevators, climate-controlled owner storage and a pay-per-use EV charging station. Owners are allowed golf carts, and Main Street’s restaurants and shops are a short walk away.

Mar Vista Grande HOA and Rentals

The HOA fee runs about $1,300 a month and covers a lot: building insurance, water, sewer, trash, internet, pest control, security, grounds, the pools and the recreation facilities.

Mar Vista Grande is not a hotel. Listings describe it as owner-operated, and units are used as second homes, full-time residences and vacation rentals, some sleeping up to ten guests. If rental income matters to you, ask for the unit’s rental history and confirm the current rental rules before making an offer.

Not to be confused with Vista del Mar, the oceanfront building in Grande Dunes in Myrtle Beach.

Why Buyers Choose Mar Vista Grande

Mar Vista Grande gives you a three-bedroom oceanfront residence with resort amenities in the one part of North Myrtle Beach where you can walk to dinner. The building is newer than most on this stretch, owner-run rather than hotel-managed, and the units are large enough to work as a family beach home or a vacation rental.

Frequently Asked Questions About Mar Vista Grande

How much are Mar Vista Grande condos?

Mar Vista Grande condos currently list from about $750,000 to $1 million. Seven sold in the last twelve months, from about $660,000 to $895,000, at an average of about $807,000.

Where is Mar Vista Grande?

Mar Vista Grande is at 603 South Ocean Boulevard, North Myrtle Beach, SC 29582, on the oceanfront in the Ocean Drive section, steps from Main Street.

Is Mar Vista Grande a hotel or a resort?

It is a condo building, not a hotel. Units are privately owned, listings describe the building as owner-operated, and owners use them as second homes, full-time residences and vacation rentals. The resort-style amenities are for owners and their guests.

What amenities does Mar Vista Grande have?

Indoor and outdoor pools, indoor and outdoor hot tubs, a lazy river, a fitness center, a private owners’ lounge, climate-controlled owner storage, four levels of secured parking and an EV charging station.

What is the HOA fee at Mar Vista Grande?

About $1,300 a month. It covers building insurance, water, sewer, trash, internet, pest control, security, grounds, the pools and the recreation facilities.

How big are Mar Vista Grande condos?

Nearly every unit is a three-bedroom, three-bath residence of about 1,465 to 1,550 square feet. A few four-bedroom units run about 1,625 square feet.

When was Mar Vista Grande built?

Mar Vista Grande was built in 2006. Homes are zoned for Ocean Drive Elementary, North Myrtle Beach Middle and North Myrtle Beach High School.
