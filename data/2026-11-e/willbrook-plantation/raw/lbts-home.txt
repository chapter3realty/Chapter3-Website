ABOUT US
A Southern Resort Lifestyle Rooted in Place
Litchfield-by-the-Sea (LBTS) stands as a premier, gated seaside sanctuary nestled in the heart of South Carolina’s iconic Lowcountry. Ideally situated on the Waccamaw Neck between the vibrant energy of Myrtle Beach and the storied history of Georgetown, this community offers an unparalleled lifestyle defined by privacy, natural beauty, and a deep-rooted sense of place.
A Lifestyle Defined by Peace and Place
At LBTS, the timeless charm of the South meets modern resort living, providing residents and guests with a meticulously maintained environment that emphasizes peaceful living and high community standards. The lifestyle is intentionally designed to harmonize with the surrounding environment, creating a coastal experience that feels both refined and deeply connected to its natural setting. 
OUR COMMUNITY
Life at Litchfield-by-the-Sea
	Explore the Community
Beach Club & Coastal Amenities
The community is anchored by private beach access, where over a mile of pristine Atlantic shoreline meets a magnificent oceanfront Beach Club. This central hub features an expansive sundeck and pavilion, creating the perfect backdrop for long summer days. Residents also enjoy championship tennis and pickleball courts, a private pool, and tranquil fishing docks and crabbing piers for a quieter connection to the coastal waters.
Nature & Outdoor Living
Nature remains at the heart of the LBTS experience. Miles of paved scenic trails wind through preserved marshlands and maritime forests, inviting walkers, joggers, and cyclists to explore the property at their own pace. Along the way, frequent encounters with local wildlife — from herons to sun-drenched turtles — keep the surrounding landscape woven into daily life.
Homes with a Sense of Place
This connection to the environment extends to the community’s housing options, which range from luxurious oceanfront estates to charming marsh-view villas and sophisticated condominiums. Each is designed to reflect the unique spirit of coastal living while offering its own perspective on the natural beauty of the property.
The Best of the Hammock Coast
Beyond the gates, the treasures of the Hammock Coast await. Residents are minutes from Brookgreen Gardens, Huntington Beach State Park, and historic Atalaya Castle. The surrounding area also offers dozens of championship golf courses, the dining destination of the Murrells Inlet MarshWalk, the shops of historic Georgetown waterfront, and the distinctive charm of the Hammock Shops Village.

OUR LOCATION
A Prime Hammock Coast Location
Located in Pawleys Island along South Carolina’s scenic Hammock Coast. Nestled between Myrtle Beach and Charleston, it offers a peaceful Lowcountry setting with easy access to shopping, dining, and championship golf. Surrounded by the Atlantic Ocean and beautiful salt marshes, LBTS location provides both stunning coastal views and convenient access to nearby attractions while maintaining a tranquil, gated environment.
