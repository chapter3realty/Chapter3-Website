Become A Member
As a member of Founders Group International, Willbrook Plantation accepts membership applications from all golfers looking to play its exquisite golf course within a country club setting. To learn more about the benefits of becoming a member of Willbrook Plantation, contact a representative with any questions you may have by calling 843-237-4900.
Prime Times Signature Card
Willbrook Plantation is one of 21 courses that are part of this longstanding, popular loyalty program which offers up to 70% savings on tee times and the ability to earn free rounds with frequent play, plus:
$299 first year/$199 annual renewal
FREE rounds – earn points every time you play.
Reduced rates up to 70% off – year-round on 21 of Myrtle Beach’s premier courses
$50 Gift Card on new memberships redeemable at any Founders Collection Golf Shop
Special 9-hole rates
Special savings for up to 3 guests every time you play
