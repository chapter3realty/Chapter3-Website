Ocean Club
The Ocean Club
Contemporary coastal elegance with a casual atmosphere.
Featuring Mediterranean-style architecture, the Ocean Club offers residents private oceanfront access as well as a variety of dining experiences and entertainment opportunities. Whether soaking up the sun by the pool, enjoying exceptional cuisine in the dining room, or hosting your own private function, you will find the Ocean Club is a luxurious beach retreat.
DISCOVER THE OCEAN CLUB
Check out our live Beach Cam!
Casual Dining — In the mood for casual alfresco dining? The Ocean Club features a seasonal menu of light, casual fare, perfect for an afternoon with the family.
Family Fun — The Ocean Club features a resort style pool with towel and bar service, and other family-friendly amenities.
Beach Access — Private access to the beach is one of the many benefits of the Ocean Club, with exclusive cabanas and towel service available to members.
Guests of members must complete a waiver of liability form before arrival to the club. This form is to be completed by adult guests representing themselves and not by members on behalf of their guests.
