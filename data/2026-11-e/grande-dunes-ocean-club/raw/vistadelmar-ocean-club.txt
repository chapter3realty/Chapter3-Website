Ocean Club Access
Residents of Vista del Mar take in the views of the Atlantic from their own oceanside balconies and private amenities within the community's gates. The Grande Dunes Master Association expands on these experiences by providing automatic membership to the Grande Dunes Ocean Club. This social club offers opportunities for additional social activities, private beach access, dining, swimming, cabanas, and towel service.
