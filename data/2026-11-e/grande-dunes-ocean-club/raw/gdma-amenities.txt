Amenities
One of the most amazing facets of living in Grande Dunes are the amenities like golf, tennis and the beach. As a Grande Dunes resident, you have priority access to all of these Myrtle Beach hotspots. Make the most out of this beautiful community and discover why Grande Dunes is truly a great place to live, to thrive and to belong.
Ocean Club
As a Grande Dunes homeowner, you receive a complimentary household membership to the Grande Dunes Ocean Club. This magnificent facility features a large outdoor pool and whirlpool, private beach access and a variety of classes, clubs, dining and entertainment experiences. Whether soaking up the sun on the pool deck, enjoying an exceptional meal in the dining room or mingling with neighbors and new friends at one of the Club’s countless activities, you will always have a home at the Ocean Club. Visit the
Ocean Club website
today for a full list of offerings or call (843) 315-0350.
Members Club
The private Members Club Golf Course is an 18-hole, par-71 golf course gracefully interwoven into some of Grande Dunes’ most luxurious residential neighborhoods. Designed by premier golf architect Craig Schreiner, the course includes nine holes bordered by pristine Mediterranean homes and nine holes of "pure core golf" framed by natural vistas. Amidst it all is the magnificent Members Club, featuring daily fine dining, a pro shop and fun social events. For membership details and pricing, visit the
Members Club website
or dial (843) 913-1355.
Marina
The public Grande Dunes Marina features 126 wet slips and 1,200 linear feet of state-of-the-art floating docks. Onsite maintenance, gift shop, showers and fueling stations make the marina a perfect place to house and launch vessels of up to 120 feet or more. For further information, visit the Grande Dunes
Marina website
dial (843) 315-7777.
Resort Course
The recently renovated Grande Dunes Resort Golf Club is a public course that includes five spectacular holes that play along the stunning Intracoastal Waterway. The layout offers players some of the area’s widest fairways and largest greens, making it perfect for golfers of all levels. The newly remodeled clubhouse also includes a public restaurant, Terrazza 19, with daily lunch and dinner service. For more information and to reserve a tee time, visit the
Resort Course website
or contact (843) 315-0333.
Tennis Club
The 5,000 square-foot Grande Dunes Tennis Club boasts ten lighted Har-Tru courts and a fully stocked pro store. From casual games to competition-level matches, players can enjoy private lessons, camps, clinics and sanctioned tournaments, all hosted by USTA-certified pros. Call (843) 449-4486 today to set up your first match or visit the Grande Dunes
Tennis Club website
.
