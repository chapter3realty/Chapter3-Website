Nestled in the heart of Myrtle Beach, a celebrated coastal town in South Carolina, Grande Dunes offers the ideal balance of tranquility and excitement.
With breathtaking natural beauty, a vibrant arts scene, and a coastal lifestyle infused with incredible dining and entertainment, every day brings a new experience.
