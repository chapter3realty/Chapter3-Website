Ocean Club
Your Perfect Spot at the Beach
The Ocean Club is the social centerpiece of Grande Dunes. Featuring Mediterranean-style architecture, it offers residents private oceanfront access and a variety of dining experiences and entertainment opportunities. Whether soaking up the sun by the pool, enjoying exceptional cuisine in the dining room, or hosting your own private function, you will find the Ocean Club is a luxurious beach retreat.

Members Club
Experience golf like never before at the Members Club, where every round delivers a championship-caliber challenge for players of all skill levels. Designed by PGA Hall of Famer Nick Price and renowned architect Craig Schreiner, this par-71 course features generous fairways, newly renovated bunkers, and undulating bentgrass greens that keep the game fresh every time.
With five sets of tees ranging from 4,300 to over 7,000 yards, it’s the ideal setting whether you’re a seasoned golfer or just getting started. Now open for limited outside play, the Members Club invites everyone to enjoy an elite golf experience—and feel like a Member. Explore flexible membership options that fit your lifestyle and enhance your life in Grande Dunes.

357 Raw Coastal Bar
NOW OPEN!
357 Raw Coastal Bar, the newest standout in Myrtle Beach’s culinary scene, delivers a seamless blend of Carolina coastal charm and modern beachfront sophistication Created by the visionary Vaugh and Spadaccini Restaurant Group, 357 Raw Coastal Bar has quickly become a vibrant community hub where coastal elegance meets culinary excellence, serving as a go-to gathering spot for both locals and visitors.
Guests enjoy an array of fresh, raw seafood, from briny oysters on the half shell to vibrant ceviches, all meticulously sourced and prepared with artisanal techniques by Chefs Rolando and Yoshi. Gourmet burgers, inventive starters, and crisp salads round out the menu, with each dish reflecting a deep commitment to quality and craftsmanship.
357 Raw Coastal Bar also features a carefully curated selection of craft cocktails and locally brewed beers, making every visit a true toast to the spirit of the Carolinas.

Marina & Store
Gateway to Life on the Water
Grande Dunes Marina is the perfect place to begin your adventure on the Intracoastal Waterway. With 126 wet slips and 1,200 linear feet of lay-along floating docks, the marina offers a protected harbor for vessels up to 120 feet or more. Our full-service marina is open to the public and features state-of-the-art floating docks, diesel, and non-ethanol gas, maintenance and marina store with showers and everything you need from tackle to t-shirts. The marina is also the trailhead for future scenic walkways along the Intracoastal Waterway.

Resort Course
Grande Dunes Resort Club is an 18-hole golf course which in 2009 was named the “National Golf Course of the Year” by the National Golf Course Owners Association of America. Its spectacular layout, a Roger Rulewich Group design, is positioned on a high bluff creating a spectacular view of the Intracoastal Waterway, Grande Dunes Marina and the four-diamond resort, Marina Inn at Grande Dunes.

Waterway Trail
The Waterway Trailhead is located on Marina Parkway near the Grissom Parkway overpass across from the rear exit of the Claire Chapin Epps YMCA and connects to the Marina Parkway multi-use trail. The Two Mile Waterway Trail is open to the public from sunrise to sunset.

357 Brewers
Located along the Intracoastal Waterway at the iconic Mile Marker 357, the 357 Brewers campus is more than just a brewery — it’s a three-acre coastal destination designed for bold beer, elevated food, and unforgettable moments with friends, family, and good company. This is where coastal energy meets community spirit — a place to dock it or drive it and stay awhile.

The Claire Chapin Epps Family YMCA
The Claire Chapin Epps Family YMCA is a state-of-the-art facility to take your fitness to the next level with fitness instruction (YMCA memberships offered separately) and is not part of the Grand Dunes amenity plan.

Grand Strand Medical Center
An acute care hospital in Myrtle Beach, SC, within Grande Dunes. Their 403-bed facility offers a diverse range of medical services, including 24/7 emergency care, heart surgery, physical therapy and trauma centers for both adults and children. They have been recognized by several national health organizations and are accredited for stroke treatment and rehabilitation.
