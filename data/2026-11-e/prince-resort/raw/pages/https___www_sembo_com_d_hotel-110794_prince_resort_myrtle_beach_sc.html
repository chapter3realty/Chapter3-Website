With a stay at Prince Resort in North Myrtle Beach (Cherry Grove Beach), you'll be a 1-minute drive from Cherry Grove Fishing Pier and 6 minutes from McLean Park. This beach hotel is 3.1 mi (4.9 km) from Barefoot Church and 6.7 mi (10.8 km) from Barefoot Landing. Make yourself at home in one of the 203 guestrooms, featuring kitchens with full-sized refrigerators/freezers and ovens. Rooms have private furnished balconies. Cable programming along with DVD players are provided for your entertainment. Bathrooms with shower/tub combinations feature deep soaking bathtubs and complimentary toiletries. The preferred airport for Prince Resort is Myrtle Beach Intl. Airport (MYR).

Featured amenities include a business center, a 24-hour front desk, and laundry facilities. Event facilities at this hotel consist of a conference center and meeting rooms. Limited parking is available onsite. Dip into one of the 2 outdoor pools or 3 hot tubs and enjoy other recreational amenities, which include a lazy river. This hotel also features concierge services, wedding services, and a banquet hall. Grab a bite at 3500 Ocean Grill, one of the hotel's 2 restaurants, or stay in and take advantage of the room service (during limited hours). Unwind at the end of the day with a drink at the bar/lounge or the beach bar. Full breakfasts are available daily from 7:00 AM to 10:00 AM for a fee.
