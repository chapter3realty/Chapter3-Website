Description

Check-In: 4:00pm Check-Out: 11:00am
Wake up to breathtaking ocean views and the sound of waves at Prince Resort, a North Myrtle Beach favorite located at Cherry Grove Pier. This oceanfront condo resort features amenities of a full-service property, offering a perfect blend of relaxation and convenience.

Each spacious one-, two-, or three-bedroom condo features a fully equipped kitchen, washer and dryer, and private balcony overlooking the shoreline or marsh. Soak up the sun at one of the resort’s two oceanfront pools, float along the rooftop lazy river, or work out in the fitness center. Dining options include 3500 Ocean Grill & Lounge, known for fresh seafood and coastal cuisine with a view.

Located on the quieter north end of the Grand Strand, Prince Resort places you minutes from the area’s top golf courses, outlet shopping, and entertainment at Barefoot Landing. Whether you’re building sandcastles, fishing off Cherry Grove Pier, or catching a sunset from your balcony, this resort promises a memorable and family-friendly beach vacation experience.

Important Information
  • Resort fee: A resort fee of 17.99% of the room total is collected at Check-In.
  • Pet Policy: Pets are not allowed. ADA-defined service animals are welcome.

All fees mentioned are subject to change without prior notice and applicable taxes.

Frequently Asked Questions about Prince Resort

Is early check-in available at Prince Resort?

The unit will be ready for you at 4 PM when you check in. If you expect to arrive earlier, ask the front desk if the unit can be available before 4 PM. If not, please feel free to use the amenities at Prince Resort until your 4 PM check-in (options include but are not limited to enjoying the pool bar and pool amenities, the beach and much more).

What is the difference between oceanfront and oceanview?

Oceanfront means that your view out to the ocean looks directly onto the water without turning your head. You see the water directly in front of you.Ocean-view means that your view to the ocean looks north or south up the coastline. You see the coastline, the beach, and the ocean in your view.

What appliances and serving utensils are in the kitchen at Prince Resort?

Stove, microwave, oven, dishwasher, refrigerator with freezer, clothes washer and dryer. Pots, pans, mixing bowls, serving bowls, cooking utensils, eating utensils, drinking cups, plates, coffee cups, saucers. Paper products are not provided.

Are linens and towels provided at Prince Resort?

Each guest receives a supply of clean linens and towels in the unit. The housekeeping policy is to replace used linens and towels with fresh ones if a guest stays four days or more. Linens and towels include bath towel, wash cloth, hand towel, bath rug, bed sheet, blanket, pillow, bedspread, mattress pad, shower curtain. Also, a dish towel is included in the kitchen.

Does the resort have laundry facilities?

Yes. There are laundry facilities available in each unit.

Is WiFi included?

Yes, free WiFi is available.

Is breakfast included at Prince Resort?

Prince Resort offers a full breakfast buffet at its restaurant 3500 Ocean Grill, located oceanfront, on the lobby floor of the resort. The breakfast buffet costs $8.50 for adults and $5 for children 3-7 years of age and is free for children 3 and under. Fees are subject to change.

Does Prince Resort accept pets?

Pets are not permitted here. Service dogs are permitted, provided they are with their assigned owners who have on hand the appropriate documentation, immunizations, and equipment that accompanies the animals.

Does the resort provide pool towels or beach towels?

The resort provides pool towels at the pool. Pool towels are not to be taken to the beach, and the hotel does not provide beach towels.

Does the resort provide beach umbrellas?

The resort does not provide beach umbrellas because the City of North Myrtle Beach rents them at the beach on a first-come-first-served basis. During the summer months, look for the dark blue kiosks or see the umbrellas already set up on the shoreline. The City's beach umbrellas are not available during the off-season (before Memorial Day and after Labor Day). The full-day use of an umbrella is $30. They may also be rented by the week for $120. Prices are subject to change.

Does the resort provide beach chairs?

The resort provides poolside chairs but does not provide beach chairs. All resort pool furniture must remain at the pool area and may not be taken to the beach. The City of North Myrtle Beach provides beach chair rentals on a first-come-first-served basis at the beach. Look for the dark blue kiosks or see the chairs already set up on the sand. These beach chairs are not available in the off-season (before Memorial Day and after Labor Day). The full day use of a beach chair is $20. They may also be rented by the week for $80. Prices are subject to change.

Is smoking allowed?

The Prince Resort is designated as a non-smoking property. No smoking is permitted on any balcony in any unit, and persons found doing so may incur a fine.

What is the fee to access the Cherry Grove Pier?

Pedestrians pay $3.00 for all-day access. Fishing is charged at $3.00 per person and $10.00 per rod for all-day access. Season passes, king mackerel passes, and season king mackerel passes are available. Prices are subject to change.

Do I need a license to fish, either on shore or on the pier?

Individuals recreationally fishing from shore (beach, bank, private dock, free public pier, etc.) and those recreationally shrimping and crabbing will be required to have a saltwater recreational fishing license.

How far away is Prince Resort from the airport?

The resort is approximately 22 miles north of the Myrtle Beach International Airport.

Does the resort provide shuttle services to any attractions or to the airport?

No, but front desk agents can help you arrange shuttle service if needed.

Are pools heated?

Prince Resort offers its guests use of the pools, hot tubs, and lazy river. In mid-fall, the outdoor pool and hot tub are heated; the heat is turned off when the warm weather returns.

How can I secure my valuables in my condominium unit while I am a guest at Prince?

You may request a condominium unit with a safe, subject to availability. Otherwise, for securing valuables, ask your front desk agent. Please note that the Prince Resort is not responsible for lost or stolen items while staying at the resort or parking in the Prince parking garage. Please make sure that upon vacating your condominium unit you have thoroughly checked for any misplaced items.

What is the minimum age to check in?

The reservation holder must be at least 23 years of age and must present a matching, valid photo ID and credit card to check in at Holiday Inn Hasbrouck Heights-Meadowlands.

Does the resort have accessible units/rooms?

Yes. The resort offers accessible amenities in public entrances, pool areas, and dining facilities. Each unit/condo is individually owned and managed and some units are not handicap accessible. Should you have specific accessibility needs, or need an accessible unit, please contact the resort directly before your arrival date to ensure your needs are met. The resort contact number is listed on your confirmation email and voucher.
