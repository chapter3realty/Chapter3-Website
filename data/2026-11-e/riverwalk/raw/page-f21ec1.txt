With two bedrooms, two baths, and 992 square feet of single-level living, this turnkey, move-in ready condo pairs a split-bedroom floor plan with an HOA that covers many everyday services. The living and dining area features crown molding, a ceiling fan, and sliding glass doors that open to the balcony overlooking the fairway, a relaxing spot for morning coffee or watching the last tee times of the day. The kitchen offers white raised-panel cabinetry, granite countertops, a mosaic tile backsplash, stainless steel appliances, and a breakfast bar, with a full-size stacked washer and dryer close at hand. The split-bedroom design places the primary suite and the guest bedroom on opposite sides, each with a ceiling fan, green-space views, and its own full bath with a tub-shower combination. The primary bedroom also opens directly to the golf-view balcony. Luxury vinyl plank flooring, central heat and air, and a foyer entry complete the interior. Riverwalk residents enjoy indoor and outdoor pools, a clubhouse, and a nature trail along the Intracoastal Waterway. The HOA covers water, sewer, trash, cable, internet, landscaping, pool service, pest control, exterior maintenance, and insurance. Set in the Arrowhead golf corridor near Carolina Forest, the community is close to shopping, dining, Highway 501, and Highway 31, with Atlantic Ocean beaches a short drive away. Golf course views, a furnished end unit, and resort-style amenities come together in Riverwalk at Arrowhead, Myrtle Beach, SC 29579. Measurements not guaranteed; buyer responsible for verifying.
