Resort Accommodations

Choose the right accommodations for your vacation.  We offer both traditional style hotel rooms, as well as one and two bedroom condos.  All of our rooms and condos are 100% Smoke Free.

Our traditional hotel rooms are clean, modern, and convenient. All rooms feature a spectacular view from their private balconies and include all the comforts of home. Several different room styles and moderate pricing will keep your family satisfied during the perfect Myrtle Beach vacation.

Our beautiful 1 & 2 bedroom condominiums feature the comfort and privacy of separate sleeping, living, and dining areas. Decorated to reflect the colorful seaside atmosphere, these completely modern accommodations are among the very first in Myrtle Beach. And all feature a private balcony overlooking the blue Atlantic Ocean. Oceanfront vacations just don't get any better, so join us this year and experience a condominium vacation you will remember all year!
