                                                        OPERATING RESTRICTIONS
   • The Permit Registration and a valid driver’s license must be in the operator’s possession while the golf cart is being operated.
   • An operator must be at least sixteen years of age.
   • To be operated during daylight hours only (except where municipalities and counties have enacted ordinances to allow golf carts to
     operate in designated areas within their jurisdictions at night).
   • May be driven on a secondary highway or street for which the posted speed limit is thirty-five miles an hour or less, within 4 miles of
     residential or additional address noted above.
   • May cross a highway or street at an intersection where the highway has a posted speed limit of more than thirty-five miles an hour.
   • Passengers under the age of 12 years old must wear a seatbelt when golf carts are being operated on public streets and secondary
     highways.
   • Upon sale of the golf cart, immediately notify the Department of Motor Vehicles in writing giving the name and address of the new
     owner, with date of sale. Seller must remove the assigned permit decal from the golf cart upon sale.
