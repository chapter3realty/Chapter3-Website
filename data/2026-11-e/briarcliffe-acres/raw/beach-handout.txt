                        Town of Briarcliffe Acres
Motor vehicles, including golf carts, all illegal year round on the beach in the town limits.

The beach between Ocean Creek Resort 10600 North Kings Hwy to the north and the
Meher Spiritual Center 10200 North Kings Hwy to the south is within the town limits of
Briarcliffe Acres.

The beach is open to pedestrian and bicycle users; however, all motor vehicles are
prohibited with the only exception for official police, EMS, fire, rescue, and beach
service vehicles.

The ordinance prohibiting motor vehicles on the beach is police enforced and violators
and their vehicles are subject to all the penalties allowed by law.

Horry County may permit golf cart operation during certain off season months on the
beach in the unincorporated areas outside of any town or city. The sign pictured above is
posted on the sand dune where unincorporated Horry County ends and the town limit of
Briarcliffe Acres begins.
