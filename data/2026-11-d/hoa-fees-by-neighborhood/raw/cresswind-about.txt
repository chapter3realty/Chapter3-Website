About Us Acreage : 248 acres Home Types: Single-family homes Amenities at a Glance: 12,000 sq. ft. clubhouse Endless recreational activities Range of classes and programs: from fitness and nutrition to crafts and cards Volunteer and enrichment opportunities Miles of walking trails Amphitheater Clubhouse Live life to its fullest at the only fully amenitized, active adult community within Market Common. The Club at Cresswind offers resort-style amenities and an unparalleled lifestyle in a stunning setting. Here, within this beautiful 12,000-square-foot clubhouse, you can take part in fitness classes, enjoy social gatherings and even discover new hobbies. The Club is complete with an aerobics/yoga studio, arts and crafts room, and a state-of-the-art demonstration kitchen. In celebration of the great outdoors, The Club offers an array of al fresco amenities. From the sparkling pool to winding trails, tennis courts, and pickleball courts, it’s all here to help you relax, play and stay fit. Fitness & Recreation Pickleball/Tennis: Four pickleball courts are located at the site as well as 1 tennis court. Fitness: A fully equipped fitness center offers a yoga/aerobics studio, professional equipment and a full schedule of group classes. Recreation: Additional recreational amenities include bocce courts, horseshoes, an outdoor amphitheater for movies and concerts, and a network of walking trails throughout the property. Golfing The Myrtle Beach area is home to an exceptional variety of year-round golf courses, with over 30 courses located within 10 miles of the community. Top choices include the Legends Resort (54 holes designed by Pete Dye and Tom Doak), Myrtlewood (36 holes designed by Arthur Hills), and the Grand Dunes Golf Club (36 holes overlooking the Intracoastal Waterway). Boating & Fishing The property is convenient to boating, sailing, and kayaking excursions on the Atlantic Ocean and the Intracoastal Waterway, as well as excellent coastal and deep-sea fishing. Healthcare 24-hour emergency care and other medical services are available at the Grand Strand Regional Medical Center, a 269-bed acute care hospital located about 15 minutes from the community. Minutes away from Tidelands Health Medical Park at The Market Common. Shopping & Dining The entrance to Cresswind Myrtle Beach is less than a mile from The Market Common, a major commercial center offering many shopping, dining, and entertainment options. In addition, Cresswind Myrtle Beach is about 20 minutes from the famous seafood restaurants of Murrells Inlet. Area Attractions The Myrtle Beach area is a major resort destination with miles of wide Atlantic Coast beaches, boardwalk and amusement park attractions, theater and concert venues, nightlife, and nature areas. Cresswind is about 15 minutes from the Myrtle Beach Boardwalk, 90 minutes from Wilmington and two hours from Charleston. © Copyright 2026 Cresswind Myrtle Beach | Community Website by HOA Sites | Terms of Use | Admin
