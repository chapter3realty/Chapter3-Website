WHAT’S THE DIFFERENCE?
Although the terms “assisted living” and “nursing home” are often
used interchangeably, there is a big difference between the two types
of facilities. Knowing the difference can empower you and your family
to make the best decision possible regarding your or a loved one’s
health. In South Carolina, assisted living facilities are also known as
Community Residential Care Facilities (CRCFs).
