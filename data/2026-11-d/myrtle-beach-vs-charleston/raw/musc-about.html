Changing what’s possible for South Carolina—and the world.

Founded in 1824 in Charleston, MUSC is the state's only comprehensive academic health system, with a unique mission to preserve and optimize human life in South Carolina through education, research and patient care.

Each year, MUSC educates more than 3,200 students in six colleges – Dental Medicine, Graduate Studies, Health Professions, Medicine, Nursing, and Pharmacy – and trains more than 900 residents and fellows in its health system. 

Here for you

We are invested in transforming how health and care are defined today and in the future. Our mission and values are at the forefront of what we do, combining innovation with compassion to make a real difference for you and your family.
