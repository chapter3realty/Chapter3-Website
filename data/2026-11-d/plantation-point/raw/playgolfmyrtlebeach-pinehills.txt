Rain check guidelines. 18 holes paid: 0-3 holes played = 18-hole rain check; 4-13 holes played = 9-hole rain check; 13+ holes played = no rain check. 9 holes paid: 0-3 holes played = 9-hole rain check; 4+ holes played = no rain check. What strain of turf do you have on your greens and fairways? Greens: Sunday Bermuda; Fairways: 419 Bermuda. What is the dress code? Proper golf attire; collared shirt required, no denim. Can I walk the course? Yes, walking is permitted. What practice facilities do you offer? Putting green, chipping green, and sand bunker. Are club rentals available? Yes, club rentals are available. What amenities do you offer for non-golfers? Food and beverage service and a patio by the waterway. Do your dining facilities offer breakfast? Yes, our dining facilities serve breakfast. Do your dining facilities offer lunch? Yes, our dining facilities serve lunch. Do your dining facilities offer dinner? No, our dining facilities do not serve dinner. Does your facility host weddings or special events? No, our facility does not host weddings or special events. Does your course participate in a Kids Play Free program? Yes, our course participates in a Kids Play Free program. What are the top awards or rankings your course has received? Ranked among GolfDay's America's Top 100 Public Layouts and Links Magazine's Top 30 on the Grand Strand. The PineHills Course (redesigned by Arthur Hills) was nominated by Golf Digest for Best New Resort Course and is a regular in its 'Places to Play' guide. How far is the course from MYR (Myrtle Beach International Airport)? Approximately 6 miles from MYR (about 14 min drive). Are golf packages available? Yes, view the golf packages page on www.playgolfmyrtlebeach.com to see all available package options. When does aerification occur at the course? Aerification varies year to year.
