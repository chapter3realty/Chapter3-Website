TO PROTECT YOUR PROPERTY FROM FLOOD DAMAGE…
•     DEMOLISH THE BUILDING OR RELOCATE IT OUT OF HARMS WAY.

•     ELEVATE THE BUILDING ABOVE THE FLOOD LEVEL.

•     ELEVATE DAMAGE– PRONE COMPONENTS, SUCH AS THE FURNANCE OR AIR CONDINTION-
      ING UNIT.

•     DRY FLOODPROOF THE BUILDING SO WATER CANNOT GET INTO IT.

•     WET FLOODPROOF PORTIONS OF THE BUILDING SO WATER WONT CAUSE DAMAGE.

•     CONSTRUCT A BERM OR REDIRECT DRAINAGE AWAY FROM THE BUILDING.

•     MAINTAIN NEARBY STREAMS. DITCHES, AND STORM DRAINS SO DEBRIS DOES NOT OB-
      STRUCT THEM.

•     CORRECT SEWER BACKUP PROBLEMS.
