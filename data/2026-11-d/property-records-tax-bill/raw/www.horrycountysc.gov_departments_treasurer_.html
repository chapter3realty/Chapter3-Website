Treasurer

Intro

The Treasurer’s Office is responsible for collecting all real and personal property taxes, including taxes on vehicles and front-foot assessments. The office also manages and invests funds on behalf of the County, School District, Airport, and Solid Waste Authority. In accordance with state law, the Treasurer’s Office conducts an Annual Public Tax Sale for Delinquent properties. Additionally, the office oversees the collection of Hospitality Tax and Business License fees.

Thank you for allowing me the opportunity to serve as your Horry County Treasurer.  As your Treasurer, customer service, oversight, and fiscal management are among my top priorities.
