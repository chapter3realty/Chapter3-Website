Myrtle Beach Rent Ordinance
Myrtle Beach has no rent-control ordinance. South Carolina state law preempts local rent control, so cities and towns cannot cap rent increases. Landlords may raise rent by any amount at the end of a lease term with proper notice, subject to federal fair-housing and anti-retaliation protections.

Fun Facts about Myrtle Beach
Did you know South Carolina bans local rent control? No city in the state, including Myrtle Beach, can set its own cap.

South Carolina-specific questions, answered.
Common operator questions about running rentals on Hemlane. Each answer ties back to the state-specific workflow Hemlane runs.
Do I need a property management license to use Hemlane?
You do not need a license to manage your own rental properties through Hemlane. If you manage rentals on behalf of others for compensation, your state may require a real estate broker’s license. Hemlane’s local agents are licensed professionals, so owners using the Agents service are always working with a licensed local expert.
Does my state require a written lease agreement?
Most states strongly recommend or require written lease agreements for all rental terms. Hemlane provides state-specific lease templates that include required disclosures and legally recognized terms which are ready to send with eSign in minutes.
How does online rent collection work for landlords?
Hemlane automates rent collection from end to end. Tenants pay online, and funds are deposited directly to the owner and not held in a third-party account. 100% of the rent goes directly to you. Automated reminders and late fee notices reduce manual follow-up so you get paid on time without chasing tenants.
How do I handle late rent fees through Hemlane?
Hemlane lets you configure automated late fees that align with your state’s legal limits. The platform sends automated reminders before rent is due and late notices after. Once the process is set-up, it runs without you having to make a single call.
