Myrtle Beach South Vacation Property Management Services
iTrip® Myrtle Beach South – owned and managed by Jay and Kara Dail – proudly serves Myrtle Beach, Garden City, Murrell's Inlet and Surfside Beach. Homeowners who list with iTrip receive:
Worldwide Exposure on 80+ Listing Sites, Including VRBO, TripAdvisor and Airbnb
Ongoing Support From Local Property Managers
Low Management Fees
Careful Guest Screening
Quick-response Systems
No Rotation That Prioritizes Listings
Owners' Portal with Online Statements and Booking
No Restrictions on Owner Usage
Expansive Advertising and Marketing Efforts
The iTrip Myrtle Beach South team delivers quality and satisfaction to their rental owners and guests. Listing with iTrip can increase owners' rental income by at least 25 percent, while guests enjoy affordable vacation rentals, an easy booking process, and custom apps that enhance their experience.
