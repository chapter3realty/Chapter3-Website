Locations and amenities

Myrtle Beach, South Carolina is a popular destination for Airbnb rentals due to its beautiful beaches, warm climate, and variety of attractions. The city is renowned for its wide, sandy beaches, high-end shopping and dining, and access to outdoor activities such as golfing, fishing, and boating. The combination of relaxation and entertainment makes Myrtle Beach a desirable vacation spot for many travelers.

Seasonality for Airbnb stays and tourism in Myrtle Beach generally peaks during the summer months of June through August, when visitors come to enjoy the warm weather and the beach. The shoulder seasons of spring and fall are also popular with visitors looking to enjoy milder weather and less crowds. The winter months of December through February can be quieter but, there are still visitors who come to enjoy the city's Christmas and New Year celebrations, and the city's other events.

Myrtle Beach has specific short-term rental regulations in place to ensure the safety and comfort of guests. These include obtaining a short-term rental permit, adhering to zoning and parking regulations and paying a transient lodging tax. Hosts are also required to provide certain safety features such as smoke detectors and emergency evacuation plans.

Managing an Airbnb property in Myrtle Beach can be challenging due to the high competition and the need to maintain the property's cleanliness and upkeep. A professional property manager can help by handling the day-to-day operations such as cleaning, maintenance, and guest communication. They can also assist in pricing, marketing, and booking to help increase occupancy rates.

The most sought-after amenities and locations in Myrtle Beach for Airbnb rentals include properties with beachfront access, private outdoor spaces, and proximity to downtown Myrtle Beach and the beach. Properties with views of the Atlantic Ocean are also highly desirable. Additionally, locations near golf courses and marinas are popular with guests looking to enjoy the city's outdoor attractions.

FAQ

What is vacation rental property management?

Vacation rental property management is a full-service offering that handles everything required to operate a short-term rental — listing optimization, guest communication, dynamic pricing, cleaning, maintenance, and owner reporting — on behalf of the owner. Awning provides this service nationwide, managing 20,000+ properties across all 50 states starting at 10% of revenue. Learn how it works with Awning’s vacation rental management services.

How does Airbnb management work?

Airbnb management covers optimizing your listing, managing bookings and calendars, messaging guests, coordinating cleaning and repairs, and adjusting pricing to drive revenue. A professional manager handles these tasks so owners can stay passive.

What are the benefits of hiring a professional Airbnb manager?

Professional Airbnb managers handle everything from guest inquiries and pricing to maintenance and compliance, resulting in higher efficiency, more consistent occupancy, and better reviews—even while you focus on other priorities. Explore more at Awning’s owner resources.

What is full-service vs half-service vacation rental management?

Full-service management covers everything from listing creation and pricing to guest support, cleaning, and maintenance. Half-service plans handle fewer operational tasks, often leaving cleaning and guest messaging to the owner. See full-service benefits.

Are there restrictions on personal use for properties under Awning management?

Owners can block dates on the calendar for personal use as long as it doesn’t conflict with existing bookings. Discuss your calendar preferences directly with your Awning advisor. Start with Awning contact & advisor scheduling.

How much are Awning property management fees?

Awning’s full-service vacation rental management fees start at 10% of monthly revenue, depending on property type, location, and services needed. This competitive pricing includes core management but may vary with additional offerings. See the full breakdown at Airbnb management fees explained.

Do I need to use a property manager, or can I self-manage my vacation rental?

You can self-manage, but most owners find that the time spent on guest communication, price optimization, and coordination with cleaners outweighs the management fee. Owners using a full-service manager typically earn more net revenue after fees because of better pricing, higher occupancy, and fewer operational mistakes. Awning starts at 10%, well below the 25–40% industry average.

How much does vacation rental property management cost?

Full-service vacation rental management costs between 10% and 40% of booking revenue, depending on the provider and the scope of service. Awning starts at 10% of revenue with no setup fees, no cancellation penalties, and a 90-day notice period to cancel. Most competitors charge 25–40% and require annual contracts.

Is Airbnb management different from vacation rental management?

The terms are often used interchangeably, but "vacation rental management" is broader — it includes listings on Airbnb plus Vrbo, Booking.com, direct-booking sites, and 50+ other channels. Awning manages as a vacation rental manager, meaning your home earns from every channel, not just Airbnb. This typically increases total bookings by 20–30% versus Airbnb-only listings.
