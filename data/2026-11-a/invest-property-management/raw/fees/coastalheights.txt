Fee Structure
Property Owners
Our Property Management Fees are:
50% of first months rent
and 10% of monthly rent.
Please be aware you are also required to cover any maintenance & repairs performed on your property which are not the fault of the tenant
Tenants-
$50 applications fee per person on the lease and when permitted
Security Deposit – typically equal to 1 months rent
If the property you are renting allows pets then a $400 pet fee will be required(or what is agreed upon by the Property Owner).
Rent is due by the 5th of the month. NO partial payments excepted.
If the rent is not received by the 5th there will be a $100 late fee.
After the 10th the eviction process will start. There will be an addition fee for this process based on the court cost.

Myrtle Beach Property Management
Coastal Heights Property Management protects property values and the legal interests of property owners who have invested in second homes, condominiums, condo-hotels, patio homes, high-rise residences and detached housing in themed communities. Coastal Heights's extensive experience in the Carolinas, commitment to community service, ability to hire and train the best staff in the Southeast and desire to please homeowners' associations makes Coastal Heights Property Management the first property management choice on the coast from North Myrtle Beach to Georgetown. Professional homeowners association management requires a broad range of skills, extensive knowledge about the marketplace and the ability to work efficiently on behalf of absentee owners serving as board members for their respective homeowners' association in addition to receiving the services of an assigned community manager. Your Homeowners Association also accepts receiving the advantages of Coastal Heights's collective knowledge and experience of its executive staff, other certified managers and support personnel. Coastal Heights Property Management, a Homeowners Association management company experienced in serving a variety of residential and condominium communities, sets the standard for quality property/association management.
