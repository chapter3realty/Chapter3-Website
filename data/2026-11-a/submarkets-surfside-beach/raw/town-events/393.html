Surfside Beach Kite Festival

Get Ready for the 2nd Annual Surfside Beach Kite Festival! 
Join us for a fun-filled day at the Surfside Beach Fishing Pier on Saturday, April 25, from Noon - 3 PM!
No Registration Needed

Grab your kite (or purchase one at the festival), family, and a chair, and join the fun!

*******PLEASE NOTE: IMPORTANT INFORMATION*******
The Surfside Beach Pier Parking Lot will be closed from 5 AM to 5 PM on Saturday, April 25, 2026
KITE ZONE: 4th Ave North to 4th Ave South on Saturday, April 25, 2026 - Please do not set beach tents in this area during the festival

Enjoy!
Kite flying on the beach
Live Band: Rivermist
Music on the beach with DJ Louie
Delicious food trucks & sweet treats
Face painting & bounce houses
Foam zone & beach games
Hula and Fire Show  
Arts & Crafts Vendors
Official Surfside Beach Kite Festival t-shirts will be sold at the Town Merch Tent OR at the Town Hall, 210 US HWY 17 South


Parking: Grand Palms Resort provides free shuttle service to and from the Town of Surfside Beach parking lots (the Pier parking lot will be closed).
No registration is needed!
