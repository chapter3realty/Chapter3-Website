Yuletide Golf Cart Parade

Yuletide Golf Cart Parade: Saturday, December 20, 2025, at 1:00 p.m. 

Get ready to rev up your golf carts for the highly anticipated 2025 Yuletide Christmas Golf Cart Parade! 
The parade kicks off on Saturday, December 20, 2025, at 1 p.m.

If you're joining in on the parade fun, make sure to check in with the Keep Surfside Beach Committee upon arrival. 
Don't forget to register before 4 p.m. on Thursday, December 18, to be considered for sweet prizes. Bring LOTS of candy to throw out to the attendees watching you roll by!

Link to register - click here Version OptionsYuletide Golf Cart Parade Headline Version OptionsYuletide Golf Cart Parade Headline

After the parade wraps up, head over to Neal & Pam's for the announcement of the Yuletide Golf Cart Parade winners. Please note that only registered participants are eligible for prizes. Let's roll!

For more information, visit https://www.surfsidebeach.org/178/Events
For parking information, visit https://www.surfsidebeach.org/202/Parking
For town merchandise, visit https://www.surfsidebeach.org/341/Town-of-Surfside-Beach-Merchandise

Vendors: 
There are no vendors at this event. 
Please note that the only way to sign up or make payment for ANY town event is through our website, www.surfsidebeach.org, or in person at Surfside Beach Town Hall, located at 210 U.S. HWY 17 South. Surfside Beach, SC 29575. If you have any questions, please call 843-913-6111 ext. 390. We will never ask you to make payments via social media. Be aware of scammers. 

Roads closed during the parade: 12:30 pm to 2:00 pm 
Ocean Blvd S from 16th Ave S to Surfside Drive
Surfside Dr from Ocean Blvd to Hollywood Drive
All side streets along Ocean Blvd S
All side streets along Surfside Drive to Hollywood Drive
