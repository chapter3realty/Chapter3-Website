AGENCY’S DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS 
C
oastal Carolina University is a leading public institution located in Conway, South Carolina, just nine miles from 
the resort destination of Myrtle Beach. With more than 11,000 students, CCU is known for its vibrant campus 
community, dedication to student success, and NCAA Division I championship-winning athletics. Founded in 
1954, CCU is a designated Sea Grant and Space Grant university, reflecting its commitment to innovation in 
STEM education as well as its role in addressing key challenges impacting the coastal region. Providing a 
transformative educational experience through research, hands-on learning, and strong academic support, the 
University also holds a Research Universities designation from the Carnegie Classification of Institutions of 
Higher Education.  
A
 recognized leader in innovation, CCU is home to South Carolina’s first space satellite program and the state’s 
only accredited Professional Golf Management program. As a comprehensive institution, CCU comprises seven 
academic colleges and offers more than 100 undergraduate and graduate programs across a broad range of 
disciplines. 
D
uring the 2024-25 fiscal year, CCU was: 
• O
ne of 16 institutions nationwide named to The Princeton Review’s inaugural Mental Health Services
Honor Roll.
• Ranked among the top 10 best colleges for undergraduate teaching in the South by U.S. News & World
Report.
• Recognized as a Top Producing Institution of Fulbright U.S. students.
• Named a 2024 Best for Vets College by Military Times.
• Designated a Silver Status Military Friendly School.
C
CU’s strategic plan, CCU Reach – Our Tealprint for the Future, was launched in January 2024. Inspired by the 
words of the University’s alma mater, the plan is grounded in three pillars – learning, living, and legacy – built on 
the keystones of driving efficiency and sustainability. Since its inception, CCU has been taking bold steps toward 
accomplishing each established goal. 
T
he learning pillar is rooted in advancing student success. To achieve this, the University is designing programs 
that connect classroom learning with real-world skills, expanding flexible learning options, and improving 
recruitment and retention efforts. In the Fall 2024 semester, CCU set a total enrollment record of 11,348 
students. That total figure is boosted by a record first-year retention of 77.4%. Since Fall 2021, the University has 
increased its retention rate by 6.8%, partially as a result of improved recruitment and retention efforts on 
campus, emphasizing CCU’s dedication to maintaining its positive enrollment trend. For the Fall 2024 semester, 
the University received more than 21,000 freshmen applications, up 15.4% compared to 2023. In August 2024, 
CCU reaffirmed its commitment to bolstering graduation rates by joining Complete College America, a coalition 
of states, systems, institutions of higher education, and other organizations committed to increasing the number 
of students who complete college and attain degrees and credentials.  
Th
e six-year graduation rate saw a small decrease to 49%, falling short of the 60% target value. Throughout the 
next few years, CCU will transition to a four-year professional advising model to allow for more dedicated 
support for students. Professional advisors complete individual progress evaluations for students each semester 
to ensure credit benchmarks are met for a timely graduation. This process includes targeted outreach to the 
students who are not on track to create updated graduation plans for upcoming semesters. CCU has also

The living pillar celebrates our thriving campus community. CCU offers programs and spaces that promote 
physical, emotional, and social wellness; creates opportunities to attend cultural arts events, athletic games, and 
lectures; and intentionally welcomes more international students, veterans, first-generation students, and adult 
learners. During the 2024-25 academic year, combined total attendance at CCU athletic and cultural arts events 
was 338,107. As of Spring 2025, 21% of CCU students self-identify as first-generation students. To further 
support the enhancement of the campus, throughout the fiscal year, plans were developed to renovate CINO 
Grille, a popular dining hub in the Lib Jackson Student Union. Renovations began in May 2025 to prepare for a 
new semester.             
 
The final pillar, legacy, focuses on making a difference beyond campus. CCU is expanding opportunities for civic 
and community engagement, partnering with regional organizations to reach underserved communities, and 
supporting innovation, workforce development, and entrepreneurship to solve local economic challenges. To 
support this, the University has partnered with the City of Myrtle Beach to develop a $22.3 million theater arts 
facility that will serve the Grand Strand community. A groundbreaking ceremony for the project took place in 
July 2024. The University’s CORE Center, a Conway Medical Center College of Health and Human Performance 
initiative, opened in Jan. 2025 and aims to advance community health through valuable health assessments, 
fitness testing, and wellness consultations designed to guide clients toward optimal health and wellness. From 
January to June, the center welcomed 596 community members, with 502 participating in health education 
programs. The University was designated a Voter Friendly Campus by the Fair Elections Center’s Campus Vote 
Project and NASPA – Student Affairs Administrators in Higher Education and was recognized by the ALL IN 
Campus Democracy Challenge (ALL IN) as a 2024 ALL IN Most Engaged Campus for College Student Voting. 
 
Another part of the legacy pillar surrounds the development of innovative research that makes a real-world 
impact. CCU earned a Research Colleges and Universities designation in the most recent report by the Carnegie 
Classification of Institutions of Higher Education. This designation reflects CCU’s commitment to research efforts 
and academic mission. During 2024-2025, CCU was awarded $5.6 million in federal, state, municipal, and 
foundation grant funding. This is an increase of 12% over the base, despite being short of the target value due to 
cuts in federal grant funding. These awards allow for important research to be conducted by faculty and for 
experiential learning for students.  During the 2024-25 academic year, enrollment in undergraduate research 
courses was 898, and 27% of faculty presentations had student co-authors. The number of students 
participating in internships rose 62% to 2,007, exceeding the target of 1,500. Moving forward, on-campus 
internships will be a strategy focus of the Office of Career Services. In addition, Career Services will be expanding 
opportunities for internship coordinators across campus to share best practices, network, and discover ways to 
improve internships and experiential learning at CCU. 
 
To achieve the goals outlined by the keystones, driving efficiency and sustainability, the University is 
strengthening operations and resilience, promoting a unified brand that boosts CCU’s visibility, and growing the 
University’s endowment and external funding. With a steadfast commitment to innovation, sustainability, and 
collaboration, CCU is not only preparing the next generation of leaders but also enriching the region and the 
world around it. In June 2025, the Chanticleer Baseball team competed in the Men’s College World Series 
(MCWS) in Omaha, Nebraska, giving the institution an opportunity to increase brand exposure on a national 
level. During the MCWS, CCU earned more than 25,000 new followers across the three major social media 
platforms (Facebook, Instagram, and X). The dedicated work by the University Marketing and Communication 
and Chanticleer Athletics departments in sharing organic social media content resulted in 22.8 million 
impressions, more than 3.5 million video views, and 1.2 million engagements. The University’s merchandise

The University has made measurable progress toward the strategic objectives established in the Campus Master 
Plan, formally adopted by the board of trustees during the 2023-24 fiscal year. In June 2024, CCU unveiled its 
state-of-the-art Indoor Practice Facility. The $20 million, 90,000-square-foot structure is poised to elevate the 
future of Chanticleer Athletics and enrich the broader campus experience by offering a dynamic, multipurpose 
space for training, events, and collaboration. The University also advanced planning for the Highway 544 
pedestrian overpass, a critical infrastructure initiative designed to enhance campus safety and accessibility. 
During the past year, CCU completed a comprehensive feasibility study and worked closely with the State of 
South Carolina and federal agencies to secure $10 million in funding to support the project’s development. This 
effort reflects the institution’s ongoing commitment to improving the campus environment while fostering 
stronger connections with the surrounding community. 
  
In fiscal year 2025, Coastal Carolina University reported total revenues of $318,621,336, reflecting a $21.2 
million increase over fiscal year 2024. Student tuition and fees comprised 53 percent of total revenue 
($169,645,890), followed by sales and services at 15 percent ($47,714,395), state appropriations at 12 
percent ($36,325,205), scholarships, contracts, and grants at 8 percent ($26,429,196), and the remaining 12 
percent derived from gifts, grants, and other operating and non-operating sources. 
 
Financial Highlights 
 
• Viability ratio is a financial metric that assesses the institution’s ability to meet long-term obligations 
using expendable net assets. A ratio above 1.00 indicates financial strength. CCU concluded fiscal year 
2025 with a viability ratio of 1.52, a 13% increase from the prior year’s ratio of 1.34, signifying continued 
financial stability. 
• Diversifying income sources is essential for mitigating risks associated with fluctuating state funding and 
enrollment trends. The decline in tuition revenue as a percentage of total revenue — from 55.3% in 
2024 to 53.2% in 2025 — demonstrates progress toward a more resilient and balanced financial 
structure. 
• Prime reserve ratio, calculated by dividing expendable net position by total expenses, indicates the 
institution’s capacity to manage financial uncertainty. With a benchmark of 40%, the 
University’s 77% reserve ratio at the end of fiscal year 2025 reflects a robust financial position.

appropriations. In fiscal year 2025, the State of South Carolina approved $36,325,205 in non-capital

C
oastal Carolina University remains committed to managed growth and financial sustainability. Total enrollment

rate for South Carolina’s four-year public institutions. Out-of-state tuition remains at $14,814 per semester.

isk Assessment and Mitigation Strategies 
C
oastal Carolina University is committed to providing a high-quality, student-centered education that promotes 
academic excellence, personal growth, and career readiness. Fulfilling this mission requires thoughtful risk 
management and strategic resource allocation. 
I
n today’s dynamic and competitive higher education landscape, the University continues to address financial 
pressures associated with a concentrated revenue base. To ensure long-term sustainability and support student 
success, Coastal Carolina employs a comprehensive strategy focused on operational efficiency, cost 
management, and targeted investment. Key priorities include: 
• E
xpanding enrollment and maintaining strong net tuition performance to enhance brand visibility and
market position.
• Strengthening institutional liquidity and building long-term financial resilience.
• Aligning planning and budgeting processes to reinforce student outcome.
T
o further promote institutional sustainability and student success, Coastal Carolina University respectfully 
encourages consideration of the following actions by the South Carolina General Assembly: 
• S
cholarship Cap Reform: The General Assembly enacted a one-year proviso (117.194) for FY2026 that
temporarily increases the institutional scholarship allowance for in-state undergraduate students from
4% to 8%. The University is grateful for this added flexibility and respectfully requests that this
temporary increase be made permanent. This change would significantly enhance access and
affordability for in-state students.
• Equitable State Funding: In FY2026 proviso 11.23, the General Assembly tasked the South Carolina
C

higher education institutions. Coastal Carolina University requests that as the State moves forward with
allocation formulas, the State consult individual institutions of higher education for input and feedback
on formula determination.

Agency Mission
Coastal Carolina University is a public comprehensive liberal arts institution that seeks to develop students 
who are both knowledgeable in their chosen fields and prepared to be productive, responsible, healthy citizens 
with a global perspective. To deliver on this commitment, Coastal Carolina recruits highly qualified and 
motivated students, faculty, and staff from the region, state, nation, and world to create a diverse and dynamic 
student-centered learning environment.
Because Coastal Carolina embraces the teacher-scholar model, it places primary emphasis on high quality 
teaching and engaged learning, and it supports faculty research, creative activities, and expert collaboration in 
the community, state, nation and world. This focus enables faculty and staff to mentor students in collaborative 
research, creative opportunities, and internships. To nurture this active learning community, Coastal Carolina 
maintains a broad range of contemporary technologies, programming, support services, and innovative course 
offerings and delivery methods. The result is alumni who are well prepared for professional careers or graduate 
programs in their chosen fields and who continue to be connected to Coastal Carolina.
Inspired by its founding in 1954 to serve the educational needs of the region, Coastal Carolina has a tradition 
of a strong liberal arts core. As such, Coastal Carolina commits its resources to building undergraduate and 
graduate degree programs of national and/or regional significance in the arts and sciences, business, 
humanities, education, and health and human services. Coastal Carolina fully embraces its leadership role as a 
regional center of economic and intellectual resources, lifelong learning, cultural and recreational 
opportunities, and athletic programs.
As Coastal Carolina executes this mission, it recognizes its responsibility to be a role model to the community 
and to the professions by assuring fair and honest treatment of people with whom it interacts and sustainable 
stewardship of resources entrusted to it, adopting the highest standards of integrity and accountability, and in 
committing itself to excellence through continuous assessment and improvement.
