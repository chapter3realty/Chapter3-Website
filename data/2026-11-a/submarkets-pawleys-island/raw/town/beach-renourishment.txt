NEWSLETTER – ISSUE #2 10-11-2019 – PROJECT NEWSLETTER – ISSUE #1 9-19-2019 – The Town’s request to amend the Renourishment project permits was approved. The permit modification authorizes expanding the beach placement footprint an additional 2,100 feet north to 3rd Street. Its also moves up the allowable dredge window to October 1, 2019. 9-12-2019 – The Town held the pre-construction meeting with representatives from the Town’s Coastal Engineer, Coastal Science & Engineering, and Marinex Construction, Inc. The contractor will begin mobilizing their equipment on 9-16-2019 and plans to begin pumping sand on 10-1-2019. 8-27-2019 – PROJECT LIFE SCENARIOS 8-15-2019 – PUBLIC NOTICE OF RENOURISHMENT PROJECT PERMIT MODIFICATION 8-12-2019 – Signed contract with Marinex Construction for renourishment project 8-4-2019 – BEACH PROJECT UPDATE 7-27-2019 – BEACH PROJECT UPDATE Project Info: PAWLEYS ISLAND BEACH RENOURISHMENT PROJECT BROCHURE 2018 PAWLEYS ISLAND BEACH PROJECT PLAN MARINEX PROJECT COST BREAKDOWN (2019) MARINEX PROJECT COST BREAKDOWN (2018) HOW BEACH NOURISHMENT WORKS PROJECT PRESENTATION (4-20-2019) PI PROJECT MAP PI PROJECT BORROW AREAS BEACH RENOURISHMENT UPDATE (MARCH 2019) Helpful Links: Play Video Pawleys Island Beach Renourishment 11-25-2019 Play Video Pawleys Island Meeting 4-20-2019 Play Video Beach Renourishment Meeting Facebook-f Twitter Instagram Town of Pawleys Island 323 Myrtle Avenue Pawleys Island, SC 29585 843-237-1698 843-237-7083 Mon - Fri 9:00 am - 5:00 pm Town of Pawleys Island 323 Myrtle Avenue Pawleys Island, SC 29585 843-237-1698 843-237-7083 Mon - Fri 9:00 am - 5:00 pm Pawleys Island weather Facebook-f Twitter Instagram Town of Pawleys Island 323 Myrtle Avenue Pawleys Island, SC 29585 843-237-1698 843-237-7083 Mon - Fri 9:00 am - 5:00 pm Pawleys Island weather Copyright © 2026 Town of Pawleys Island.
