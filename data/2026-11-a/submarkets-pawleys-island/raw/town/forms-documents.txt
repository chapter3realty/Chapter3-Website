Golf Cart Registration Nature Park Rental Accommodations Tax Building Department Beach Wheelchair Beach Fire Permit Golf Cart Registration Golf Carts Golf carts must display valid SC Department of Motor Vehicles and Town of Pawleys Island decals. The Town of Pawleys Island decal can be obtained at Town Hall by completing the Town’s golf cart permit application and paying the annual $25 fee. To register an existing golf cart with the Town of Pawleys Island you must submit a copy of the following forms: A filled-out Golf Cart Permit Application Valid SCDMV Golf Cart Permit Registration This must show a registration address within a 2-mile radius of the Town limits located on the North and South Causeway Valid Insurance Policy Golf Cart Rules Depending on staff availability it could take 2 to 7 business days for you to receive approval after applying. Please plan accordingly. If you need assistance with your application or have questions about what is required please contact Town Hall. Low-Speed Vehicles The operation of low-speed vehicles on all primary and secondary roads within the town is prohibited. Please view this link on what is defined by the State as a Low-Speed Vehicle . Nature Park Rental The Pawleys Island Nature park is available for public gatherings of any kind. All applicants must have a property owner of the Town of Pawleys Island to sign for use of the Nature Park. For events smaller than 25 people there are no charges of any kind. If you plan to have an event with more than 100 people please contact the Town Clerk for assistance. Application forms are listed below: Pavilion Use Policy Park Use Application Hold Harmless Agreement Accommodations Tax Follow this link to find out information about the A-Tax and revenue subject to the Accommodations Tax Please use this form to report revenue subject to the Town of Pawleys Island accommodations tax. Reports should be submitted monthly to Town Hall. Local A-Tax Reporting Form This form covers the period through the last day of the month and becomes DELINQUENT if payment is POSTMARKED by the US Postal Service after the 20th day of the following month. Building Department The Building Department is located at Town Hall at 323 Myrtle Ave. This is a division of the Town Administration and is accessible during normal business hours which are 9:00 AM until 5:00 PM Monday through Friday. The primary goal of the Building Department is to provide professional, courteous, and efficient customer service to contractors, builders, and architects assisting property owners of the Town of Pawleys Island. Please bring completed building permit applications to the Town Hall or email applications to the Town Clerk, Corey Higdon at chigdon@townofpi.com and/or the Town Administrator, Dan Newquist at dnewquist@townofpi.com . Click here for all permits and forms associated with the Building Department. Beach Wheelchair The Town of Pawleys Island has a beach wheelchair that can be used on the beach by persons who have difficulty walking in sand. It is available free of charge on a first-come, first-served basis by calling the Pawleys Island Town Hall at 843-237-1698. The chair is located at the south end beach access and may only be used on the beach in the vicinity of that access. Please contact Town Hall for additional information and rules for use of the chairs. Please complete the form below and email it to chigdon@townofpi.com to reserve a time slot. Beach Wheelchair Release Form Beach Fire Permit NOT AVAILABLE FOR USE OUTSIDE OF TOWN LIMITS. If you are unsure what are the Town limits please contact Town Hall.
