From its largest facilities, like Vereen Memorial Gardens, Socastee Recreation Park, and the Bike and Run Park, to its smallest facilities like Frink Park, Horry County Parks and Recreation has something to offer everyone. Parks are open from dawn til dusk, unless special events are occurring. The following map and table identify all County parks and recreation centers. The Resources page will provide you to links to other public recreation opportunities available in the County Parks Parks of Note Horry County Bike & Run Park Vereen Memorial Historical Gardens Socastee Recreational Park The Bike & Run Park  is quickly becoming a park of regional significance, attracting both local users and vacationers. This facility is located along the Intracoastal Waterway near River Oaks Drive in Carolina Forest. It includes 7 miles of mountain biking and hiking trails on 70 acres of open space. The park was developed through the vision and labor of volunteers on a county-owned drege spoil basin that provides drastic changes in elevation that cannot be found in other parts of the county. In addition to providing a venue for a variety of races, its parking area serves as the first official East Coast Greenway trailhead in the State of South Carolina. Vereen Memorial Historical Gardens is located along the Intracoastal Waterway in Little River on more than 115 acres of forest and marshland. This facility includes approximately 3 miles of nature trails and boardwalks that meander through its botanical gardens and along its waterfront. A small segment of the original Kings Highway, a colonial route between Boston and Charleston, still remains at the front of the property, behind the CB Berry Community Center. Beach Wheelchairs available by reservation for Vereen Memorial Historical Gardens.
