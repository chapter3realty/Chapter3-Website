Barefoot Resort & Golf is one of the premier Myrtle Beach Golf Resorts, nestled in the scenic Lowcountry of North Myrtle Beach, South Carolina. Thoughtfully designed to deliver an unmatched golf getaway, Barefoot stands apart from other East Coast golf destinations with four award-winning courses, exceptional practice facilities, and on-site accommodations.
