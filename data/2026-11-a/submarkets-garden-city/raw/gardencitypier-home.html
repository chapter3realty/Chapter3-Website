Welcome to Pieradise.

Summer Entertainment

Come alive with the sounds of summer at The Pier at Garden City! Our beach deck bar hosts afternoon karaoke sessions where you can belt out your favorite tunes with the ocean breeze as your backdrop. As the sun sets, the energy shifts to live bands performing every night (weather permitting) at both ends of the pier. Whether you're jamming to classic rock or dancing to country classics our 668-foot pier transforms into the Grand Strand's most unique music venue. Grab a cold drink, feel the salt air, and let the music move you. Find the latest band schedule on our Facebook page.

Fishing

Cast your line into adventure at The Pier at Garden City. Adults just $12, children 12 and under $6. Keep your wristband on and come and go as you please throughout your pass period. Best of all? No fishing license required—your pier pass covers everything you need to drop a line and hook a big one! More details here: bait, gear, hours and more!

Café

Fuel your  adventure at our beachfront café! Start your morning right with a hot breakfast served fresh from 7 AM to 10:30 AM. Craving something hearty later in the day? Our classic menu of juicy burgers, all-beef hot dogs, and crispy fries is available all day long. Whether you're grabbing a quick bite between catches or settling in for a night of family fun and entertainment, we’ve got something for everyone. Check out our signature Hugo burger, named after the monster hurricane that destroyed the pier in 1989.

Arcade

Step into family fun at our classic arcade, packed with beloved games that bring out the kid in everyone. Test your skills at skeeball, race to victory in our driving games, or try your luck at dozens of other family favorites. With plenty of prizes waiting to be won, the kids (and kids-at-heart) will find endless entertainment between fishing trips and beach walks. It's the perfect rainy-day backup or sunny-day addition to your pier adventure.
