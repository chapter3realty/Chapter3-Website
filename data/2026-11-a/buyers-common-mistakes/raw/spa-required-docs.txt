Legal Residence Documents Checklist 
To apply for the Special Assessment Based on Legal Residence (4%) go to 
www.Horrycountysc.gov, Online Services and under the Tax Column: Legal Residence Application 
REQUIRED DOCUMENTS: 
□ SC DRIVER'S LICENSE OR STATE ID: For all owner occupants AND spouse's if 
married and must reflect CURRENT address 
□ SC VEHICLE REGISTRATION: For all vehicles owned and must reflect 
CURRENT address 
□ STATE AND FEDERAL TAX RETURNS FOR SC OR SSA-1099: Federal returns 
must include a Schedule 1 and/or E if applicable; State Returns must include 
a Non-Resident (NR) Schedule if applicable. 
IF APPLICABLE: 
□ Recorded Certificate ·of Trust or Full Trust, Land Sales Contract, LLC Operating 
Agreement 
□. Divorced/Separated: A divorce decree if divorced for 5 years or less o.r court filed 
documents requesting a divorce; Legal separation papers or Court Filed Separation 
Maintenance Agreement 
□ Own another home: If another property is owned elsewhere, a copy of the property tax 
bill showing no exemptions or a letter of exemption removal from that county 
□ Letter of explanation if any of the above mentioned documents cannot be supplied 
REMINDERS: 
□ Missing documents can be submitted via mail, fax, email or brought into the office 
□ Applications missing documents or INCOMPLETE APPLICATIONS will have a delay in 
processing time until such documents are received. 
□ Mobile Homes with ACTIVE TITLES with the OMV must be registered within the county 
and have a yellow sticker in the window. 
Assessor's Office- (Phone) 843-915-5040 (Fax) 843-915-6040 
Email: asktps@horrycountysc.gov to submit supporting documents electronically
