(September 18, 2025) - The N.C. Department of Transportation and the S.C. Department of Transportation will hold two pre-hearing open houses and corridor public hearings for the proposed Carolina Bays Parkway Extension​ ​ project. The same information will be presented at both meetings and will be streamed live (see below). NCDOT and SCDOT are proposing to extend Carolina Bays Parkway (S.C. 31) from its current location at S.C. 9 in Horry County, S.C., across the North Carolina state line to U.S. 17 in Brunswick County near Shallotte. The proposed improvement is a multi-lane full access freeway partially on a new location. The primary purpose of the project is to improve transportation in the area by enhancing mobility and connectivity for traffic moving in and through the project area. The public hearings are scheduled for: North Carolina​ Monday, Sept. 29, 2025 5 to 8 p.m. Sea Trail Convention Center 75-A Clubhouse Road Sunset Beach, NC 28468 Join Meeting ​ South Carolina ​​Tuesday, Sept. 30, 2025 5 to 8 p.m. North Strand Recreation Center 120 State Highway 57 South Longs, SC 29568 ​ Join Meeting ​ NCDOT and SCDOT representatives will be available to answer questions and receive comments regarding the proposed project. The opportunity to provide verbal comments and/or submit written comments and questions will be provided. The public may attend at any time during the above hours. Project details and maps can be found at the NCDO​T public i​nput webpage ​​​​​​​​​​ .​​​​​ Carolina Bays Parkway Extension Alternative Map 4 - Preferred Alternative ​ Public Comment NCDOT representatives will be available to answer questions and receive comments. The comments and information received will be taken into consideration as work on the project develops. The opportunity to submit written comments will be provided at the meeting or can be submitted via phone (855) 925-2801 enter project code 7734, email at Carolina-Bays-Pkwy@publicinput.com or at ​NCDOT's public input portal by Oct 31, 2025.
