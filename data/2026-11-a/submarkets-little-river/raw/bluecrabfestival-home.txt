Save the Date for the 45th Annual World Famous Blue Crab Festival
May 15th & 16th, 2027!

 

 

 

Held along the historic Little River Waterfront in Little River, South Carolina, the 45th Annual World Famous Blue Crab Festival is one of the largest and most beloved festivals in the Southeast.

Spend the day enjoying fresh local seafood, live music, scenic waterfront views, and shopping from hundreds of talented crafters and vendors. Whether you’re here for the food, the music, or the coastal atmosphere, there’s something for everyone.

Proudly voted Best Annual Event/Festival on the Grand Strand by readers the Blue Crab Festival is a tradition you won’t want to miss.

Join us the weekend after Mother’s Day.
Free Satellite parking and shuttle service are available.

NO DOGS OR PETS (Working Service Animals Accepted) | NO CONCEALABLE WEAPONS
