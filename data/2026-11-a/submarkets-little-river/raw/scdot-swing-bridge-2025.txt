(May , 2025) – The United States Coast Guard has authorized a temporary schedule change for the Little River Swing Bridge in Horry County. During this temporary deviation, the bridge will open on signal except from 7 a.m. to 9 a.m. and 4 p.m. to 6 p.m., Monday through Friday, except on federal holidays. The draw will open on the hour and half hour. Public vessels of the United States and tugs with tows, upon proper signal, will be passed through anytime Drivers may experience differing wait times as the schedule change is implemented. The temporary change will be in effect until 11:59 p.m. on October 31, 2025. More information on the bridge's operations can be found here: https://www.navcen.uscg.gov/msi. Comments on the changes can be submitted here: Regulations.gov .
