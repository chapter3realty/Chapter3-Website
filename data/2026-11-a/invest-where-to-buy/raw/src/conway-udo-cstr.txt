Commercial Short-Term Rental (CSTR) 
A conditional use whereby a structure is converted and/or created into a fully functioning, private 
accommodations use, which may include cooking, living, sanitary and sleeping facility within one dwelling unit, to 
be rented to one (1) family for a period o f not more than ninety (90) consecutive days. Hotels, motels, Inns, bed 
and breakfast establishments, and other land uses explicitly defined and regulated in the UDO separately from 
Commercial Short-Term Rentals (CSTR’s) are not considered Commercial Short-Term Rentals.           [ZA2021-06-21(B)]

5.1.5 Commercial Short-Term Rentals (CSTR) 
 
Commercial Short-Term Rentals shall be permitted in the CC, CBD, and WRD Zoning Districts, provided the 
following standards are met: 
 
A. Commercial Short-Term Rentals (CSTR) 
1. The principal use of the property is a legally permitted commercial short-term rental facility. 
2. Accessory structures shall not be used for lodging purposes at any time in conjunction with the 
commercial short-term rental. 
3. The length of the stay shall not exceed ninety (90) days; 
4. No additional off-street parking is required for commercial short-term rental (CSTR); 
5. All signage shall adhere to the requirements set forth in Article 11; 
6. The commercial short-term rental (CSTR) shall comply with all current and applicable building codes; 
7. This section does not supersede nor exempt the requirements in Section’s 6.3.6 – Core Commercial 
District, 6.3.7 – Central Business District or 6.4.1 – Waccamaw Riverfront District of the UDO. 
B. Other general requirements, applicable to commercial short-term rentals: 
 
1. CSTR’s are subject to local, county, and state taxes, including, but not limited to, property, sales, use 
and accommodations taxes, and are liable for the payment thereof as established by state law and 
the City’s Code of Ordinances. 
2. Property owner(s) shall be responsible for ensuring compliance with all federal, state and local laws, 
including but not limited to, tax code, building code, fire code and environmental health regulations
