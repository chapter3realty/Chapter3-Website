608. General Resort Residential District (GRR). 
 
Intent.   It is the intent of this district that  adequate areas be established and appropriate 
land reserved for medium to high density re sort residential purposes.  The regulations 
which apply within the district are designe d to encourage a stab le, healthy, and safe 
environment for single family dwellings in areas that have unique environmental and 
recreational characteristics conduciv e to resort living.  It is the intent of this district to 
encourage enjoyment of the beach areas wit hout creating negative pressures that disrupt 
the environment or quality of life for area residents. (Amended Ord. 2006-106)
