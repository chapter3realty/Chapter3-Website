BrickWood Mortgage & Chapter3 Realty

An agent and a lender, under one roof.

BrickWood Mortgage and Chapter3 Realty are teaming up to give Grand Strand buyers and investors something rare: expert local real estate guidance and an in-house lender that finances the deals other banks decline.

Who Chapter3 is

Chapter3 Realty is the data-forward Myrtle Beach real estate team. Instead of a generic listing search, Chapter3 brings buyers and investors real numbers: rental income analysis, DSCR and cap-rate math, condo-by-condo building data, monthly market reports, and neighborhood-level research across the Grand Strand.

What the partnership means for BrickWood clients

  • Financing that matches the analysis. When a borrower is weighing a rental, condotel, or non-warrantable condo, Chapter3 runs the deal and BrickWood finances it, from the same conversation.
  • A co-branded deal analyzer. A BrickWood-branded investment tool that shows cash flow, cap rate, cash-on-cash, and the DSCR your loans qualify on, ready to embed on the BrickWood site.
  • A real estate referral partner. Borrowers who need an agent get a data-driven local team; buyers who need a lender get BrickWood. One trusted hand-off, both directions.
  • Content worth sharing. The Myrtle Beach market report, Airbnb income data, property-tax and closing-cost calculators, all free for your borrowers to use.
